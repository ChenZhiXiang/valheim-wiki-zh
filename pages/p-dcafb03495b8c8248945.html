矮人法师是迷雾之地的矮人聚落附近的中立生物，体形矮小、蓝皮肤，眼睛浅蓝、头发银色，持魔法法杖。共有三种法师，可按法杖和袍子颜色区分。与矮人刺客不同，只出现在矮人聚落等建筑周围，几乎总与其他矮人成群，而且不会重新生成。

玩家伤害它们、附近矮人，或矮人守护石保护的建筑部分时，会激怒它们，转为敌对并攻击。

矮人与迷雾之地其他敌对生物天然敌对，见到就攻击。推进进度通常需清理至少一处聚落，因为矮人板条箱内有矮人提取器，是树汁提取器的必需部件。也可用板车撞碎箱子、用埃达精华破坏，或等附近怪物破坏，以绕过直接交战。

袭击与能力

矮人战斗策略参见 敌对生物攻略 § 矮人.

许多法师攻击有距离要求，因此紧贴施法者可阻止使用。火法师和冰法师共有一种近战攻击。

近战

用杖柄戳玩家，造成70点钝击伤害。玩家距其0.5-2.5米时可用，冷却4秒。虽然动作是直刺，实际覆盖2.5米、水平90度弧形。

火焰法师

火法师穿金边红袍，持带分叉的火红法杖，有两种独有攻击：

火球( 火球)

绕杖后下挥，发一颗火球，造成130点火焰伤害并施加燃烧中。速度15米/秒，最多飞行10秒，散布角1–10度。玩家距其1–15米时可用，冷却6秒。命中地面时，产生向外扩张的半径6米爆炸，造成100点火焰伤害。

集束炸弹

挥杖数秒后高举，向各方向发九颗火球，每颗100点火焰伤害并施加燃烧中。速度11米/秒，最多飞行10秒，从离地2.5米处发出，散布角40–50度。玩家距其5–30米时可用，冷却35秒。落地形成半径3米火区，造成100点火焰伤害。

冰霜法师

冰法师穿银边蓝袍，杖顶有发蓝光的冰晶，有两种独有攻击：

冰弹

向前刺杖发三颗冰球，造成200点冰霜伤害并施加冰霜。每颗速度15米/秒，最多飞行4秒，散布角8–15度。玩家距其1–15米时可用，冷却6秒。

新星

用杖砸地，产生向外扩张、半径5米的圆，造成150点冰霜伤害并施加冰霜。玩家距其0–7米时可用，冷却7秒。

辅助法师

辅助法师穿金边黄褐袍，杖顶有矮人灯笼，具备三种独有能力：

增益

举杖后砸地，对自身及8米内友方施加矮人之力，持续20秒，所有技能+20%。友方距其1–15米时可用，冷却45秒。

疗愈

先在地面画半圆，再举杖，对自身及4.32米内友方施加矮人疗愈，持续4秒，每0.5秒结算400生命值。友方距其1–15米时可用，冷却10秒。

迷雾法弹

用杖砸地，在自身半径3米内生成1–3个迷雾法弹，最多同时九个。每个有1生命值，追踪玩家，命中造成150点钝击伤害。玩家距其1–15米时可召唤，冷却35秒。迷雾法弹在距玩家1米时，于半径1.5米球形范围造成伤害。

Dvergr Mages are neutral creatures found in the Mistlands around Dvergr settlements. They resemble short, blue humanoids with light blue eyes and silver hair, and they carry Magic staves. There are three different types of Dvergr Mage, which can be differentiated by the color of their staff and robe. Unlike Dvergr Rogues, mages are exclusively found around Dvergr settlements and other structures (almost always in groups with other Dvergr) and do not respawn.

Once provoked by a player (by damaging them, other nearby Dvergr, or any part of a nearby structure that is protected by a Dvergr Ward), they will become hostile and attack.

Dvergr are naturally hostile to other aggressive creatures in the Mistlands and will attack them on sight. Clearing at least one Dvergr settlement is typically necessary to progress in the Mistlands, because the Dvergr Component Crate contains the Dvergr Extractor, an essential component in the Sap Extractor. This can be circumvented by ramming the relevant crate with a cart, using Refined Eitr to destroy it, or hoping that aggressive mobs in the area destroy it.

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating Dvergr, see Aggressive creature strategies § Dvergr.

Due to range requirements, many attacks from every variant of mages are possible to disable by standing directly against the caster. Both the fire mages and the ice mage share a melee attack.

Melee

Pokes the player with the hilt of their staff (70 Blunt damage). This is accessible every 4 seconds when within 0.5-2.5 meters of a player. Despite being a straight thrust, this attack hits a horizontal arc 2.5 meters long and 90 degrees across.

Fire mages

Fire mages wear a red robe lined with gold and carry a pronged, fiery-red staff. They have two unique attacks:

Fireball

Brings their staff around and then sweeps down to launch a single fireball that inflicts the Burning status (130 Fire damage). The fireball travels at 15 meters per second for up to 10 seconds before despawning. It is generated with a spread of 1–10 degrees. This attack can be used every 6 seconds when within a 1–15 meters of a player. If it hits the ground instead of a player, it creates a 6-meter radius circular blast that expands outward (100 Fire damage).

Clusterbomb

Waves their staff for a few seconds before raising it up higher to launch a shower of nine fireballs in various directions that inflict the Burning status (100 Fire damage each). Each fireball travels at 11 meters per second for up to 10 seconds before despawning. They originate from a point 2.5 meters off the ground and have a spread of 40–50 degrees. This attack can be used every 35 seconds when within 5–30 meters of a player. On impact with the ground, these fireballs create 3-meter radius circular fields of fire (100 Fire damage).

Ice mages

Ice mages wear a blue robe lined with silver and carry a staff tipped with a glowing blue ice shard. They have two unique attacks:

Icebolt

Stabs forward with their staff to launch three balls of ice at the player which inflict the Frost status (200 Frost damage). Each ball of ice travels at 15 meters per second for up to 4 seconds before despawning and has a spread of 8–15 degrees. This attack can be used every 6 seconds when within 1–15 meters of a player.

Nova

Slams their staff down to create an outward expanding circle with a radius of 5 meters, which inflicts the Frost status (150 Frost damage). This attack can be used very 7 seconds when within 0–7 meters of a player.

Support mages

Support mages wear a yellow-and-brown robe lined with gold and carry a staff tipped with a Dvergr Lantern. They have three unique abilities:

Buff

Lifts their staff up before slamming it into the ground to apply the Dvergr power status to themselves and allies within 8 meters for 20 seconds (+20% to all Skills). This ability can be used every 45 seconds when within 1–15 meters of an ally.

Heal

Draws a half-circle on the ground before lifting their staff in the air to apply the Dvergr heal status to themselves and allies within 4.32 meters for 4 seconds (400 health ticking every 0.5 seconds). This ability can be used every 10 seconds when within 1–15 meters of an ally.

Mistile

Smashes the ground with their staff to create 1–3 Mistiles within a 3-meter radius around the caster; a max of nine can be active at once. These orbs have 1 health and track the player to deal damage on impact (150 Blunt damage). These can be summoned every 35 seconds when within 1–15 meters from a player. The Mistiles themselves deal damage in a 1.5 meter sphere when within 1 meter of the player.
