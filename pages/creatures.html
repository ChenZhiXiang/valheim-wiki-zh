生物是遍布英灵神殿的非玩家角色，可分为四类：

  • 首领是必须召唤的强大敌对生物。击杀后可解锁放逐之力、提高世界难度，并获得推进游戏进度的战利品。
  • 小头目是特定地牢中具有独特名字的敌对生物。击杀任意一位小头目，可获得一件希尔德的宝箱。
  • 敌对生物警觉后会攻击玩家，被击杀时也会掉落战利品。
  • 击杀或采集被动生物可获得资源，它们从不攻击玩家。
  • 友善角色是无法被杀死的友好生物或角色，可能为玩家提供服务或帮助。

首领

首领又称弃誓者，在对应召唤地点献上指定物品即可召唤，所需祭品由符文石提示。相比普通生物，首领生命值更高，并拥有特殊攻击。

阅读特定生物群系中的符文石，会自动在地图上标记首领地点。每位首领都有多个召唤位置。

每位弃誓者被击败后，必定掉落特殊战利品。将其带回献祭石阵，可解锁新的放逐之力。

敌对生物

某些敌对生物带有表示等级的星级。一星生物生命值翻倍，伤害为150%；二星生物生命值为三倍，伤害翻倍。

行为

  • 连续60秒无法攻击，或连续30秒无法侦测目标后，生物会放弃追赶。
  • 某些生物有最大追击距离。追到该距离后，只要1秒无法侦测目标，就会放弃。
  • 连续15秒无法攻击且无法接近目标时，生物会转而攻击建筑，持续15秒。
  • 只有无法接近目标玩家时，生物才会攻击墙和门。
  • 部分生物惧怕火焰，遇到火焰会逃跑。
  • 某些生物会避开火源，并在火源附近保持被动。
  • 某些生物会在等待攻击时绕目标移动。
  • 水生生物被迫上岸时，会尝试逃回水中。
  • 生物每分钟自然恢复最大生命值的1.67%，60分钟恢复100%。
  • 100 米内每增加一名玩家，生物的有效生命值增加 +30%。例如 3 名玩家 = +60% 有效生命值。
    • 每次生物受到伤害时更新此数值。
  • 100 米内每增加一名玩家，生物的伤害增加 +4%。例如 3 名玩家 = +8% 伤害。
    • 每次生物造成伤害时更新此数值。

Creatures are non-player characters which are found throughout Valheim. They can be split to four categories:

  • Bosses are powerful aggressive creatures that must be summoned. Killing them unlocks Forsaken powers, increases world difficulty and provides loot to progress in the game.
  • Minibosses are unique, named, aggressive creatures that you can find in specific dungeons. Killing any given miniboss will reward with one of Hildir's Chests.
  • Aggressive creatures will attack players when alerted. They also drop loot when killed.
  • Passive creatures provide resources when killed (or harvested). They will never attack players.
  • NPCs are friendly creatures/characters that cannot be killed and may provide some service or help to the player.

Bosses

Bosses, also known as the Forsaken, can be summoned by offering specific items (hinted by the Runestones) at their specific spawn site. They have increased HP and special attacks compared to regular creatures.

A boss site is automatically marked on the player's map by reading the runestone in the specific biomes. There are multiple spawn locations for every boss.

Each Forsaken is guaranteed to drop a special trophy when defeated that can be returned to the Sacrificial Stones to unlock a new Forsaken power.

Aggressive creatures

Some aggressive creatures can have stars representing their level. One-star creatures have double the health and deal 150% damage, while two-stars have triple the health and deal double damage.

Behavior

  • Creatures will give up chasing after unable to attack for 60 seconds or unable to detect the target for 30 seconds.
  • Some creatures have a max chase range. After chasing that far they will give up if unable to detect the target for 1 seconds.
  • After unable to attack for 15 seconds, creatures will focus structures for 15 seconds if they can't reach the target.
  • Creatures only attack walls and doors if they aren't able to reach the target player.
  • Some creatures are afraid of fire and will flee from it.
  • Some creatures try to avoid fire and will be passive near it.
  • Some creatures circulate around the target while waiting to attack.
  • Swimming creatures try to flee towards water if forced on ground.
  • Creatures passively regenerate 1.67% of their max health every minute (100% over 60 minutes).
  • Creatures gain +30% more effective health per each additional player within 100 meters. E.g. 3 players = +60% effective health.
    • This is updated whenever the creature takes damage.
  • Creatures gain +4% more damage per each additional player within 100 meters. E.g. 3 players = +8% damage.
    • This is updated whenever the creature deals damage.
