椅子是家具，玩家可以坐下，也可以单纯用于装饰。

共同属性

椅子提供舒适度，但彼此不会叠加；只有玩家附近舒适度最高的椅子生效。

除非另有说明，所有椅子的伤害抗性如下：

  • 较弱: 砍伐
  • 正常: 钝击, 劈砍, 镐击, 火焰, 冰霜, 闪电
  • 抗性: 穿刺
  • 免疫: 毒素, 灵魂

Chairs are furniture. They can be used for the player to sit on, or as simple decorative objects.

Common properties

Chairs provide comfort, but they do not stack with each other: only the highest-comfort chair in a player's vicinity will provide comfort.

Unless noted otherwise, all chairs have the following damage resistances:

  • Weak: Chop
  • Neutral: Blunt, Slash, Pickaxe, Fire, Frost, Lightning
  • Resistant: Pierce
  • Immune: Poison, Spirit
