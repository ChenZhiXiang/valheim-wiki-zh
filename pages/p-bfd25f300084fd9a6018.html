灰烬之地矮人比同族矮人刺客强大得多，能力可与二星矮人刺客相比。它们与迷雾之地的矮人属于不同氏族。

灰烬之地矮人刺客是灰烬之地中的中立生物，尤其常见于矮人聚落周围。它们是矮小的蓝色人形生物，眼睛浅蓝、胡须红色，携带强弩。矮人建筑中生成的刺客通常与其他刺客同行，也可能在灰烬之地任意位置生成及重生。

玩家伤害它们、附近矮人，或矮人守护石保护的建筑部分时，会激怒它们，转为敌对并攻击。

灰烬之地矮人敌视当地其他主动攻击的生物，一见到便会攻击。

袭击与能力

开枪

抬腿支撑并装填弩，再射击玩家，造成210穿刺伤害[说明1 ]。玩家距其1-15米时，每6秒可使用一次。

近战

用弩侧面拍打玩家，造成70钝击伤害。攻击在2.5米、90度弧形内挥动，玩家距其0.5-2.5米时，每4秒可使用一次。

说明

  1. ↑：弩本身属性列出160穿刺伤害，但攻击仅采用攻击文件中列出的110穿刺伤害。

Ashlands Dvergr are much stronger than their cousins Dvergr Rogues, their status can be compared to two-star Dvergr rogues. These Dvergr are a separate clan from those found in the Mistlands.

Ashlands Dvergr Rogues are neutral creatures found in the Ashlands, especially around Dvergr settlements. They resemble short, blue humanoids with light blue eyes and red beard hair, carrying an Arbalest. Rogues that spawn at Dvergr structures will often be accompanied by a mix of other rogues; Rogues may spawn (and respawn) anywhere in the Ashlands.

Once provoked by a player (by damaging them, other nearby Dvergr, or any part of a nearby structure that is protected by a Dvergr Ward), they will become hostile and attack.

Ashlands Dvergr are naturally hostile to other aggressive creatures in the Ashlands and will attack them on sight.

Attacks and abilities

Shoot

Lifts one leg to support and load their crossbow before shooting the player (210 Pierce damage[Note 1]). This attack can be used every 6 seconds when within 1-15 meters of a player.

Melee

Smacks the player with the side of the crossbow (70 Blunt damage). This attack swings in a 2.5 meter 90 degree arc and can be used every 4 seconds when within 0.5-2.5 meters of a player.

Notes

  1. ↑ The crossbow itself has properties listing 160 Pierce damage, but the attack only uses the attack file properties which lists 110 Pierce.
