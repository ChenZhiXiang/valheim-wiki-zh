远古树根是迷雾之地生物群系中的资源，可在其上放置汁液提取器采集汁液。

目录

  • 1 获取方式

  • 4 趣闻

获取方式

远古树根在迷雾之地地面常见，发绿色光芒，可透过迷雾看见。

汁液收获

用汁液提取器提取汁液时，树根逐渐干枯。每个汁液提取器每60秒收集并储存一份汁液，一条远古树根可放置多个提取器。

远古树根每6分40秒恢复1份汁液，最多储存50份，恢复满需5小时33分20秒，因此汁液是可再生资源。

远古树根有饱满、半干及干枯三种视觉状态。

状态 游戏中说明 可收获汁液 图片
已满 此上古之根充满了能量 41 - 50
一半干燥 此上古之根发出的光正在慢慢淡去 5 - 40
干枯 此上古之根看起来已经被抽干了 < 5

Ancient Roots are a resource found in the Mistlands biome. Sap Extractors can be placed on them to harvest Sap.

Contents

  • 1 Availability
  • 2 Sap harvesting
  • 3 Gallery
  • 4 Trivia

Availability

Ancient Roots can be commonly found in Mistlands, at ground level. They emit a green light glow which can be seen through the mist.

Sap harvesting

Each root dries up as Sap is extracted from it using a Sap Extractor. Each Sap Extractor collects and stores one sap every 60 seconds. Multiple sap extractors can be placed on one Ancient Root.

The reservoir of sap in an ancient root regenerates at a rate of 1 sap per 6m 40s, up to a maximum storage of 50 sap (obtained in 5h 33m 20s). Thus, sap is a renewable resource.

Ancient Roots have three visual states: Full, half dried, and dried up.

State In-game description Harvestable Sap Image
Full The ancient root is pulsating with energy 41 - 50
Half dried The ancient root's glow is fading 5 - 40
Dried up The ancient root seems all dried up < 5

Trivia

  • The concept of Ancient roots and by extension, Yggdrasil, serve as the source of magic (its Sap can be refined into Refined Eitr for enchanted equipment such as Staff of Embers or Yggdrasil Porridge which increased the player's Eitr meter) in the game can be compared with how Studio Trigger's Little Witch Academia similarly established Yggdrasil and its roots as the source of special energy required to perform magic.
