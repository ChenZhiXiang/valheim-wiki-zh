镰刀是收割作物的工具，不能作武器。

升级资料

品质 1 2 3
耐久度 200 400 600
制作配方 镰刀把 x1
银 x6 		银 x5 银 x10
熔炉 等级 2 3 4
  • 在潜能熔炉用木制 战斗 神像可将镰刀升至3级以上，每级额外增加200点耐久。

说明

  • 农耕（技能）降低镰刀耐力消耗并增加范围。
  • 镰刀不适用于巨人泡泡及法师帽菇。
  • 用于敌人时不造成伤害，但仍获得耕作经验。

The Scythe is a tool used to harvest crops. It cannot be used as a weapon.

Upgrade information

Quality 1 2 3
Durability 200 400 600
Crafting recipe Scythe Handle x1
Silver x6 		Silver x5 Silver x10
Forge level 2 3 4
  • The Scythe can be upgraded past level 3 at the Forge of Potential by using a Wooden Battle Idol. Each upgrade increases its Durability by an additional 200.

Notes

  • Farming (Skill) decreases scythe stamina consumption and increases its range.
  • Scythe does not work on Jotun Puffs and Magecap.
  • If used on an enemy, it will cause no damage, however, it will still give Farming experience.
