巨熊是黑暗林山中的敌对生物，体形壮硕、毛发浓密，爪牙巨大。

行为

巨熊进入火源3米内时，会避免火并逃跑。

巨熊仍能从火源范围外攻击，但最后一刻会改变攻击方向，可能打空。要躲开所有攻击，玩家须靠近巨熊，使其保持在火源范围内。

火把的作用范围从火焰本身计算，而非玩家中心，因此面朝巨熊最有效。

巨熊会吃地上的蓝莓，但不能被可驯服。虽然与恶骨分属两个敌对阵营，也不会攻击它们。

袭击与能力

咬伤

向前扑咬玩家，造成55点穿刺伤害。范围为4米、水平45度弧形，玩家在3米内时可用，冷却4秒。

爪击

后腿站起，略向前跳，再砸下，造成50点劈砍及40点等级0的砍伐伤害。范围为5.5米、水平45度弧形，玩家在5米内时可用，冷却4秒。

横扫

有左爪、右爪及左右连击三种，各自冷却4秒，每次命中造成25点劈砍及40点等级0的砍伐伤害。范围为5.5米、水平45度弧形，玩家在5米内时可用。

重砸

看起来像爪击，但停在原地，不向前跳，更侧重掌而非爪，造成50点钝击、40点等级0的砍伐及40点等级0的镐击伤害。与爪击不同，范围为半径2.5米的圆，玩家在5米内时可用，冷却12秒。

说明

  • 巨熊所有攻击的工具级均为0，因此无法采集要求工具的资源。
  • 巨熊与恶骨恢复略快于多数生物，50分钟恢复全部生命值，而非60分钟。

Bears are aggressive creatures found in the Black Forest. They are bulky, furry beasts with large claws and teeth.

Behavior

Bears will avoid fire and run away if they come within 3 meters of a fire source.

Bears can still attack from outside the fire's range but will alter their direction of attack at the last moment, which gives them a chance to miss. To avoid all attacks, the player must run close to the Bear to keep it within the fire's range.

For a Torch, the radius is computed from the fire itself, not from the player's center. Therefore, it is most effective for the player to face the Bear.

Bears consume Blueberries on the ground, but they are not tameable. They do not attack Viles despite being in two enemy factions.

Attacks and abilities

Bite

Lunges forward to bite the player (55 Pierce damage). This extends 4 meters in a 45-degree horizontal arc and can be used every 4 seconds when within 3 meters of a player.

Claws

Stands on their hind legs, bounces forward a little, and slams down to attack (50 Slash and 40 Tier 0 Chop damage). This attack has an arched hitbox 5.5 meters long and 45 degrees across horizontally. It can be used every 4 seconds when within 5 meters of a player.

Swipe

This attack has three variations, each with its own 4-second cooldown: a swipe with the left claw, a swipe with the right claw, and both swipes chained together (25 Slash and 40 Tier 0 Chop damage each hit). This attack extends 5.5 meters out in a 45-degree horizontal arc and can be used within 5 meters of a player.

Slam

Looks very similar to the claw attack, but remains stationary instead of bouncing forward and puts slightly more emphasis on their paws instead of claws (50 Blunt, 40 Tier 0 Chop and 40 Tier 0 Pickaxe damage). Unlike the claw attack, this attack hits in a circular area with a radius of 2.5 meters. This can be used every 12 seconds when within 5 meters of a player.

Note

  • All Bear's attacks have tool tier 0, which mean they can not be used to harvest resources that require equipment.
  • Bears and Viles regenerate slightly faster than most creatures, recovering full health over 50 minutes instead of 60.
