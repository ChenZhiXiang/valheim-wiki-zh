工具锤用于建造火、工作台、建筑和家具等物品。配方从游戏开始就已解锁，制作它不需要制作设施。

可建造物品列表见建材。

The Hammer is used to construct objects such as fires, Workbench, buildings, and furniture. The recipe is unlocked from the start of the game, and no crafting station is required to craft it.

To see the list of craftable, refer to Building.
