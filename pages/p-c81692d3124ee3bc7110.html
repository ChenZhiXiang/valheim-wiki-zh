回收灯笼是一种工具，无法制作或升级。

持握时移动速度降低5%。攻击使用棍棒技能，但不造成伤害。它没有耐久度。

获取方式

破坏立式灯笼会固定掉落一个回收灯笼。立式灯笼是非常稀有的建筑，可在青青草原、黑暗林山、泥泞沼泽、凛冽雪山、北境平原、迷雾之地和极北之地中找到，拥有200点生命值。

Salvaged Lantern is a tool. It cannot be crafted or upgraded.

While held, it reduces movement speed by 5%. Its attacks use the Clubs skill but deal no damage. It has no durability.

Availability

A Salvaged Lantern is obtained by destroying a Standing Lantern, which always drops one. Standing Lantern is a very rare structure found in the Meadows, Black Forest, Swamp, Mountain, Plains, Mistlands and Deep North. It has 200 health.
