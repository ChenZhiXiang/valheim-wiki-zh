守护者护甲是一套极北之地级别的护甲。

目录

  • 1 护甲部件
    • 1.1 模具
    • 1.2 品质
  • 2 图片库

护甲部件

这套护甲共有三件，均无法直接制作。玩家必须先在等级 4 的黑熔炉处制作对应的投掷，再在霜铸熔炉中硬化，耗时 50 秒并消耗五份液态冰霜。部件可在等级 4 的黑熔炉处修理。成品在黑熔炉处升级：品质 2 需要设施等级 5，品质 4 需要设施等级 7。

所有部件都不会降低移动速度。守卫者胸甲使穿戴者对冰霜抗性。

模具

每个铸坯都需要对应模具。三种模具由巨人战士掉落，每种概率为3%（一星克里根为6%，二星为12%）。

The Protector Armor is a Deep North-tier armor.

Armor pieces

This set of armor has three pieces. The pieces cannot be crafted directly. Instead the player must craft the corresponding Cast at a level 4 Black Forge and harden it in a Frost Foundry, which takes 50 seconds and five Liquid Frost. The pieces are repaired at a level 4 Black Forge. The finished pieces are upgraded at the Black Forge, from level 5 for quality 2 up to level 7 for quality 4.

None of the pieces reduce movement speed. The  Breastplate of the Protector makes the wearer Resistant VS Frost.

Moulds

Each cast needs the matching mould. The three moulds are dropped by Krigen, each with a 3% chance (6% for one-star and 12% for two-star Krigen).
