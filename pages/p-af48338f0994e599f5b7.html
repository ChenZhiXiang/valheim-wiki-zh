蜂窝是首领，外观像重新贴图、升级的邪骨恶灵，仅能通过控制台命令生成。

蜂巢的攻击以灵魂伤害取代毒素伤害，生成迷雾之地相关随从，掉落类似蛊虫之后的物品。它可能是迷雾之地未完成的占位首领，最终由蛊虫之后替代。

袭击与能力

近战

向玩家进行一次近距离挥击。

范围效果

蜂巢先以浓绿云蓄力，再释放污泥波，前兆是向后倾身。

生成

间歇从腋下取团块投出，从不直接瞄准玩家。落点最多生成四只随机怪物，即邪蛛探索者或壁虱幼虫。某种已有八只后，不再生成该种。

远程

远程攻击，实际外观不明，射程30米。邪骨恶灵没有类似攻击。

说明

  • 虽外观像红色邪骨恶灵，但生成及掉落都是迷雾之地生物和物品，因此可能是未使用的迷雾之地 首领。
This article is about unfinished content
The information exists in the game but in an incomplete state, and may not be playable or require Developer console to spawn.

Hive is a boss resembling a retextured, upgraded version of Bonemass. It can only be spawned via Console Commands.

Hive deals Spirit damage instead of Poison damage with its attacks, spawns Mistlands-related minions, and drops items similar to what The Queen drops. It could be an incomplete placeholder boss for the Mistlands, which was eventually replaced with The Queen.

Attacks and abilities

Melee

A close-range swipe at a player.

Area of Effect

Hive will charge up with a thick green cloud before releasing a wave of ooze. This is signaled when he leans backwards.

Spawn

At intervals, Hive will reach into his armpit to throw out a glob of goop. This is never directed at a player, and will spawn up to four random monsters ( Seekers and/or Ticks) where it lands. No more of a creature type will be spawned once eight of that type are already present.

Ranged

A ranged attack (it is unclear what it looks like in action). It has a 30 meter range. Bonemass does not have an analogue to this attack.

Notes

  • Despite visually resembling a red Bonemass, Hive is probably an unused Mistlands Boss since its spawns and drops are Mistlands creatures/items.
