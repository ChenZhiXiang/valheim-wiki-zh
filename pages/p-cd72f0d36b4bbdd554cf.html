邪煞之根是由古树长老召唤，或位于邪煞之根 黑暗林山替代生物群系中的敌对生物。

它们是巨大凶猛的树根，疯狂挥动，试图伤害目标。

袭击与能力

树根战斗策略参见 敌对生物攻略 § 邪煞之根.

攻击

玩家在4米内时，树根每3秒可向下砸击，造成55点钝击、20点0级砍伐及20点0级镐击伤害，命中4米、垂直90度的弧形范围。

古树长老每次施放树根攻击，会在目标周围15米内生成15条树根，每0.1秒一条。已加载30条古树长老树根时不再生成；野生树根属于另一种生物，不计入上限。召唤树根在18至20秒后消失。

说明

  • 它们会出现在最高处，包括玩家建筑。例如建有3层楼时，树根只在屋顶生成，屋顶被摧毁后才会出现在第一、第二层。

Roots are aggressive creatures spawned by The Elder or found in Root Black Forest alternative biome.

They are enlarged, vicious tree roots that flail around frantically in an attempt to damage their target.

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating Root, see Aggressive creature strategies § Root.

Attack

Every 3 seconds when a player is within 4 meters, a Root can slam down on the player (55 Blunt, 20 Tier 0 Chop and 20 Tier 0 Pickaxe damage). This hits in a 4-meter 90-degree vertical arc.

Each cast of The Elder's root attack spawns 15 Roots within 15 meters of its target, one every 0.1 seconds. No more are spawned while 30 of The Elder's Roots are loaded; wild Roots are a separate creature and do not count. Roots spawned by The Elder disappear after 18 to 20 seconds.

Notes

  • They will appear on the highest area, including on player built structures. For example, if a building with 3 floors is built, roots will only spawn on the roof rather than on the first and second floors until the roof is destroyed.
