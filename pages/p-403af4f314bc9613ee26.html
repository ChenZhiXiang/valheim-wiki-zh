矮人酒杯用于从背包饮用蜜酒，不过没有酒杯也能饮用蜜酒。

获取方式

矮人酒杯在迷雾之地中的矮人守卫塔及矮人灯塔内罕见出现，放在矮人桌上，互动即可拾取。

也可能稀有地作为腐疫矿区宝箱战利品出现。另在腐疫地堡外部下层有七处生成点，虽然仍不常见，但其中五处比其他矮人建筑的生成概率高。

版本0.213.4之前的漏洞使可拾取物体形式的矮人酒杯，与掉落物形式不同，会逐渐下落并穿过建筑，停在地板内部的地形上，仍能够找到并拾取。寻找0.213.4之前生成建筑的酒杯时，应检查地板内部正下方所有矮人桌。

说明

  • 尽管描述不同，矮人酒杯功能与大酒杯及庆祝号角完全相同。

Dvergr Tankards are an item used to consume Mead from within an inventory. Mead can, however, be consumed without the use of a tankard.

Availability

Dvergr Tankards are found rarely inside Dvergr Guard Towers and Dvergr Lighthouses within the Mistlands. They appear as objects atop Dvergr tables and can be picked up by interacting with them.

They can also be found rarely inside Infested Mines, as loot inside treasure chests. Additionally, there are seven spawn locations for Dvergr Tankards in the lower floor of the Infested Citadel exterior; while still uncommon, the Dvergr Tankards have a higher chance of appearing at five of the seven Infested Citadel exterior spawns compared to at other Dvergr structures.

Due to a bug in the game in versions prior to version 0.213.4, Dvergr Tankards in their "pickable"-object form (as opposed to their dropped-item form) will eventually fall and clip straight down through the structures they appear inside, coming to rest upon the terrain inside the floor of the structure; they can still be found there and picked up. Players looking for them in a Dvergr structure generated in versions prior to 0.213.4 should check inside the floors of the structure directly below any Dvergr tables it contains.

Notes

  • Despite its description, the Dvergr Tankard is functionally identical to the Tankard and the Horn of Celebration.
