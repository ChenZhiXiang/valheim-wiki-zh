燃烧中
你被点燃了！
效果
每次结算造成X伤害

燃烧中是受到火焰伤害导致的状态效果，例如走入火中或被火球命中。持续五秒，每秒结算一次。继续受到火焰伤害会在现有燃烧效果上叠加，并重新计算每次伤害。可按以下公式计算：

ToTaL damageduraTion

燃烧造成纯粹伤害伤害。护甲和火抗通过降低初始火焰伤害，间接影响效果，但不影响已获得的燃烧。

说明

  • 处于潮湿时，燃烧消退速度为6x。
  • 燃烧时，潮湿状态消退速度加快至51倍。
  • 燃烧时玩家无法获得休息中效果。
  • 护甲26或以上时，站在营火上不会燃烧，因为火焰伤害被降至低于1。此前提为没有怕火弱点。

Burning
You are on fire!
Effects
X damage per tick

Burning is a status effect caused by taking Fire damage, for example by walking into a fire or getting hit by a fireball. It lasts for five seconds and ticks every second. Taking additional fire damage will stack damage on top of the current burning effect and recalculate the tick damage. Tick damage can be calculated with:

total damageduration

Burning deals Pure damage. Armor and Fire resistance indirectly affect the effect by reducing Fire damage, however they do not affect already acquired effects.

Notes

  • Burning expires 6x faster while being Wet.
  • Being Wet expires 51x faster while burning.
  • The player is unable to receive the Resting Effect while burning.
  • Standing on a Campfire with 26 or more armor will not cause burning, as the Fire damage is reduced to less than 1. This assumes no weakness to Fire damage.
