世界种子是初始化英灵神殿的伪随机数生成器的地图 种子，决定世界生成。同一游戏版本、同种子布局相同。

创建世界可输入名称种子，种子框预填随机值，可换自定义。

  • 42069lolxd — 霍尔德 可沿海岸线前进，并向南穿越至另一座相连岛屿找到。

  • 9934622021 — 全部五个 生态区域 位于第一座岛上。（已过时，现在是一座可爱的小岛，有草原、小片黑暗林山和一座小雪山）

A world seed is a map seed used to initialize Valheim's pseudorandom number generator that determines how a world is generated. All worlds generated from the same seed will have the same layout for any given game version.

When creating a new world in the main menu, the game opens a dialog where the player can enter the world name and seed. The input field for entering the seed is initially filled with a random seed that can be replaced with a custom seed.

List of world seeds

Useful seeds

  • maypoleplz — The starting continent has a maypole to the west.
  • jWM4fqFdSm — The island southwest of spawn has two maypole locations very close to each other.
  • wVJCZahxX8 — Starting in the Meadows with Black Forest east and west, a Mountain west. Eikthyr is close to the spawn but The Elder is on an other island south.
  • 42069lolxd — Haldor can be found by following the coastline and crossing to another attached island on the south.
  • sBIkiadtIh — Haldor can be found on starting island to the West, with Black Forest, Swamp, and Mountains relatively close. Swamp and Mountains to south and west.
  • 9934622021 — All five first biomes on the first island. (outdated, now a cute small island with meadow, small black forest and little mountain)
  • WorldEater — All main biomes on first continent, including Haldor.
  • Gremlin — Over 20 beehives and Haldor on the first continent.
  • Swamp — The Elder, Bonemass, Moder and Yagluth are all located on the same island within a minute's running distance from each other, on the island east of spawn. There are plenty of the first 5 biomes on the island. Both Haldor and Hildir can be found very close to the bosses, and there are several Leviathans surrounding the spawn island. The boss island has a lot of crypts, burial chambers and frost caves.
  • feret2 - First six biomes on spawn island, First three bosses on spawn island. Eikthyr is next to the spawnpoint, Elder,Yagluth, Bog witch,Hildir, and Haldor are relatively close to eachother in a "ring" island directly south of the spawn, along the "stream" along the middle. There is a quadruple leviathan spawn near the world center. Northeast, there is a Moder&elder island. Directly north of the spawn(No boats), There is an Eikthyr, then Bonemass, elder, hildir,haldor, and bog witch.

Hard seeds

  • yfNmtqZ5mh — Starting on a little Meadows island surrounded by a Black Forest. Near a beautiful lake and huge mountain on the other side.
  • lduTRpBHca — Starting on a small-ish island with very little Black Forest and mostly Meadows. No other biomes.
  • nai4qtPKFt — No bosses on starting island, bosses are spread out. This is a very hard seed. (outdated, now elder and Eikthyr on starting island)
  • xV2pLimZea — Hunt for the elusive Frost Queen, tangled starting island with nothing nearby.

Speedrun seeds

  • HHcLC5acQt — Three bosses, five major biomes and Haldor on the same continent, with two bosses each an island away. For maps generated prior to the location changes with version 0.148.6, Moder is still on the starting continent.[1]
  • TxCD10fTIP — Five bosses and biomes on same continent. Two traders close by, one of which is on the same continent, the other can be reached by swimming or rafting.
  • lSB4fI46nV — Five biomes and four bosses on starting continent, Yagluth on continent next to starting island, Haldor on island next to starting continent.
  • kpI0aXHSfz — Five bosses, boss summon items, and Haldor on all starting continent. Current as of update 0.154.1.

External links

  • https://valheim-map.world/
    • The site, created by wd40bomber7, allows generating worlds online by inserting a seed or uploading a save file and previewing them outside of the game. It displays the complete biome map as seen in-game, and locations of all points of interest, such as Forsaken Altars or dungeons in each biome.
