获取方式

在以下兴趣点战利品箱中，与黑金属战斗神像一起出现：

  • 凛冽雪山：废弃小屋、山地塔楼、哀啸之穴。
  • 北境平原：平原宝箱、封印之塔。

用途

每次在潜能熔炉尝试消耗一座神像，不需其他材料。65%概率使品质升一级，可超过4级；失败则摧毁物品，返还部分材料。

The Black Metal Protection Idol is an idol used to upgrade items at the Forge of Potential.

Contents

  • 1 Availability
  • 2 Usage
    • 2.1 Armor
    • 2.2 Shields
  • 3 See also

Availability

It is found in loot chests at these points of interest, together with the Black Metal Battle Idol:

  • Mountain: Abandoned Cabin, Mountain Tower, Howling Cavern.
  • Plains: Plains chest, Sealed Tower.

Usage

Each attempt at the Forge of Potential consumes one idol and costs no other materials. It has a 65% chance to raise the item's quality by one level, even above level 4; otherwise the item is destroyed and part of its materials are returned.

The Black Metal Protection Idol upgrades the following 13 items:

Armor

  •  Padded Helmet
  •  Padded Cuirass
  •  Padded Greaves
  •  Lox Fur Hood
  •  Lox Fur Jacket
  •  Lox Fur Trousers
  •  Vilebone Visage
  •  Vilebone Cage
  •  Vilebone Drapes
  •  Linen Cape
  •  Lox Cape

Shields

  •  Black Metal Shield
  •  Black Metal Tower Shield
