生长方式

砍伐森林不会再生，自然小树，即名称为山毛榉、冷杉或松树的大树苗，也不会长成大树。有人报告偶尔自然出现新树，但非常罕见，不能补回砍掉的森林。

可用耕地耙及部分树种种子种植树苗，随时间长成大树。随机需3,000至8,000秒，即50分钟至2小时13分20秒，约游戏内1.67–4.44天。部分树最长时间较短，部分最短时间较长。种树建议间隔4米，避免拥挤使部分树苗随周围树成熟而死亡。可沿边放木杆参照，临时放两根2米梁测量间距。

古树、世界树嫩枝及灰烬之树不产种子，无法用耕地耙种植。

Trees are the primary source of wood. When destroyed, a full-grown tree will become a giant log that will fall over and collide with any objects in its path. Logs drop various types of wood, depending on the kind of tree. Stumps always drop ordinary wood.

Growing behavior

Cut forests do not regrow, and naturally occurring small trees (large saplings labelled Beech, Fir, or Pine) do not grow into full sized trees. Some have reported trees spontaneously appearing on rare occasions, but this will not replace a cut forest (and is quite rare).

It is, however, possible to plant tree saplings using the Cultivator and seeds from some tree species. Planted saplings grow into large trees over time. A sapling will grow into a tree after a random passage of time, between 3,000 and 8,000 seconds (between 50 minutes and 2 hours 13 minutes 20 seconds, or about 1.67–4.44 in-game days). Keep in mind that some trees have a shorter maximum growth time and some have a longer minimum growth time. For tree farms, it is recommended that you plant each sapling 4 meters apart in order to avoid overcrowding, which will eliminate some saplings as surrounding trees mature. Spacing can be measured by placing poles along one edge of the farm for reference, with the spacing measured by temporarily placing two 2-meter beams.

Ancient trees, Yggdrasil shoots and Scorched Trees do not produce seeds and cannot be planted with the cultivator.
