灰矮人巢穴是黑暗林山中的生物生成点。

巢穴可生成任意灰矮人：

  • 灰矮人 (72%机会)
  • 灰矮人萨满 (14%机会)
  • 灰矮人暴徒 (14%机会)

生成的敌人有15%概率为1星，2.25%概率为2星。

玩家进入60米内时巢穴激活，每12秒尝试在四米内生成生物，直到20米内已有三只灰矮人。

说明

  • 只在无遮盖地面生成敌人。在生成器周围四米半径内搭建屋顶可阻止生成。
  • 保留后，可持续获得敌对生物掉落物，提供无限资源。
  • 可用火盆将其改造成自动刷怪装置。用4x4的覆盖区域遮住巢穴周围地面以阻止生成，再移除边缘一块，寻找灰矮人可生成的位置。确认位置并遮住其他生成点后，稍向下挖，放置火盆并添加燃料，用锄头调整地面，让火焰仍燃烧、火盆却不受燃烧敌人的伤害。将火盆围住，最好用石墙避免被破坏，即可完成。

The Greydwarf Nest is a creature spawner found in Black Forest.

The nest can spawn any Greydwarf:

  • Greydwarf (72% chance)
  • Greydwarf Shaman (14% chance)
  • Greydwarf Brute (14% chance)

Spawned enemies have a 15% chance to be 1-star and a 2.25% chance to be 2-star.

The nest activates when there is a player within 60 meters. The nest attempts to spawn within four meters every 12 seconds until there are three Greydwarfs within 20 meters.

Notes

  • Only spawns enemies on uncovered terrain. Spawning can be disabled by building a roof around the spawner (four-meter radius).
  • If kept alive, these can provide a useful and infinite source of resources that the hostile creatures may drop.
  • You can turn it into an automatic farm using a Standing Brazier. Cover the ground all around the nest in a 4x4 to prevent spawns, and remove a single one on the edge to find a spot where Greydwarfs will spawn. Once you have a spot where they will spawn and everywhere else they can spawn covered, dig down a little, place the brazier, fill it with fuel, and use the Hoe to have the ground in a position where the fire is still lit, but the brazier does not take damage from burning enemies. Block in the brazier (Preferably with stone to prevent the wall from being destroyed) and it's done!
