元素魔法(技能)
说明
使用元素魔法时的伤害和埃达之能消耗。

元素魔法是与元素魔法武器类别相关的技能。

效果

元素魔法技能提高，攻击埃达之能消耗最多降低至-33%，随机伤害和击退倍率从等级0的25–55%提高至等级100的85–100%。

雷霆之杖源质消耗不受元素魔法技能降低。

伤害缩放详情请参阅伤害机制# 技能因素。

Elemental magic (skill)
Description
Damage and Eitr usage when using Elemental magic.

Elemental magic is a skill related to Elemental magic weapon class.

Effects

Higher skill level in elemental magic reduces the Eitr cost to attack down to -33% and uses a random damage and knockback multiplier ranging from 25–55% at skill level 0 to 85–100% at skill level 100.

Dundr Eitr cost is not reduced by Elemental magic Skill.

For the details of damage scaling, see Damage mechanics#Skill factor.

Leveling up

The player gains xp when hitting a creature with a staff. The amount is dependent on the staff used.
