阿斯克之忍是穿戴阿斯克护甲套装获得的状态效果。

奔跑和跳跃耐力消耗-10%，攻击耐力消耗-20%，穿刺 伤害提高+10%。

Ask's Endurance
A lighter armour lets you move more freely, every move requiring less energy.
Effects
Run stamina usage: -10%
Jump stamina usage: -10%
Attack stamina usage: -20%
Pierce: +10%

Ask's Endurance is a status effect granted by wearing the Ask armor set.

It reduces the stamina cost of running and jumping by -10%, attacking by -20% and increases your pierce damage by +10%.
