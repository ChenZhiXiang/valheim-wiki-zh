霜龙是凛冽雪山中的敌对生物，细长无足，鳞片冰蓝，有四只角。霜龙是母亲冰霜龙母的雄性后代。

袭击与能力

击败霜龙的策略见 敌对生物攻略 § 霜龙.

冰霜喷吐

向玩家发射三颗冰球，每颗造成90点冰霜伤害并施加冰霜效果。速度15米/秒，最多飞行10秒，散布角10–20度。玩家距其5–15米时可用，冷却6秒。

说明

  • 最初称为“龙幼崽”，[ 3 ]发布前改名为“霜龙”。[ 4 ]游戏文件中仍称为“Hatchling”。
  • 霜龙最多追到距离生成点200米，超过后更容易放弃追击。
  • 玩家常会在龙蛋附近找到保护它的霜龙。
  • 霜龙掉落物格外有价值：死亡时掉落的蓝色发光霜龙淋巴，是所有冰霜武器及食物的必需材料，战利品则用于制作狼皮护甲的头盔。
  • 外形很像无足翼龙，一种有翼无足的龙，与巨大飞龙般的冰霜龙母明显不同。
  • 霜龙虽然怕火，但以下箭矢的伤害差距很大： 燃烧的箭 与 黑曜石箭，因此黑曜石箭更强，火箭总伤害44，黑曜石箭总伤害52。
    • 这不意味着它们的火焰弱点无用：尚无黑曜石箭或想节省材料时，火箭也是不错的替代。

背景故事

“读到我的人，请小心霜龙，它们是从尤弥尔身体中诞生的最古老族群之一。

龙族最常见的形态是霜龙，即照顾后代的小型雄龙，常伏在岩石巢边守护龙蛋。相比之下，雌龙巨大而罕见，一旦被激怒，就会成为凶残敌人。"

― 符文石

Drakes are aggressive creatures found in the Mountains. They are slender, legless frost dragons with ice-blue scales and four horns. Drakes are the male offspring of their mother, Moder.

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating Drakes, see Aggressive creature strategies § Drake.

Spit cold

Fires three balls of ice at the player, inflicting the Frost effect (90 Frost damage each). Each projectile travels at 15 meters per second for up to 10 seconds before despawning and has a spread of 10–20 degrees. This attack can be used within 5–15 meters of the player and has a 6-second cooldown.

Notes

  • Originally introduced as a "Dragon Hatchling",[3] they were renamed to "Drakes" before release.[4] Drakes are still referred to in game files as a "Hatchling".
  • Drakes have a maximum chase distance of 200 meters from their spawn position. After that, they will drop aggro more easily.
  • Players are likely to find Drakes close to a Dragon Egg protecting it.
  • The drops from Drakes are unusually valuable. Their Freeze Glands, glowing blue objects dropped on death, are necessary for all frost-based weapons and foods, and their trophy is used to make the helmet for Wolf Armor.
  • Appearance-wise, they greatly resemble an Amphiptere, a winged, legless type of dragon, in stark contrast to Moder who resembles a huge wyvern.
  • Drakes may be weak to fire, however, because of the massive damage difference between Fire Arrows and Obsidian Arrows, Obsidian are stronger (Fire totaling 44 and Obsidian totaling 52)
    • This does not make their fire weakness useless, as Fire arrows make a good replacement if you do not have Obsidian arrows yet or just to save materials.

Lore

"Let you who read me be aware of the Frost wyrms, one of the most ancient kins sprung from Ymir's body.

The most common form of the wyrm are the Drakes, the small males that cares for the offspring. Most often they can be seen poised by rocky nests guarding the eggs. In contrast, the females are much larger and a rare sight to behold, but once angered they provide a vicious foe."

― Runestone
