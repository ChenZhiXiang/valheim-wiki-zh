骷髅怪是青青草原、黑暗林山、泥泞沼泽、凛冽雪山及极北之地中的敌对生物。

骷髅的生命、伤害及颜色随所在生物群系而异。

骷髅有80%概率为近战变体，20%概率为远程变体。

近战骷髅有25%概率装备木盾，25%概率装备青铜盾。盾牌仅作装饰，死亡不会掉落。

袭击与能力

骷髅战斗策略参见 敌对生物攻略§ 摇摆.

剑

水平挥砍，覆盖1.8米、90度弧形范围。冷却3秒，玩家在1.6米内时可用。

弓

搭箭射向玩家。箭速度每秒30米，最多飞行4秒后消失，散射角2–20度。攻击冷却4秒，玩家在20米内时可用。

Skeletons are aggressive creatures found in Meadows, Black Forest, Swamp, Mountain, Deep North.

Skeletons have different health, damage and color depending on their biome.

Skeletons appear 80% of the time as melee variants and 20% of the time as ranged variants.

Melee have a 25% chance to equip a Wood Shield and a 25% chance for a Bronze Buckler. Shields are purely cosmetic and don't drop on death.

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating Skeletons, see Aggressive creature strategies § Skeletons.

Sword

Does a horizontal slash that extends 1.8 meters and arcs 90 degrees. This attack has a 3-second cooldown and can be used within 1.6 meters of a player.

Bow

Nocks an arrow and shoots the player. The arrow travels at 30 meters per second for up to 4 seconds before despawning and has a spread of 2–20 degrees. This attack has a 4-second cooldown and can be used within 20 meters of a player.
