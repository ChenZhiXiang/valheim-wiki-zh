    Common properties
    Property Value
    Durability 2000
    Support Iron (from 20 to 1500)
    Loss 1/13 vertical, 1/13 horizontal
    Resistances
    • Weak: Chop
    • Neutral: Blunt, Slash, Pickaxe, Fire, Frost, Lightning
    • Resistant: Pierce
    • Immune: Poison, Spirit
