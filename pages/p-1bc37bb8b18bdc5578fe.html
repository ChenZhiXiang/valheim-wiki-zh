获取方式

在以下兴趣点战利品箱中，与血金战斗神像一起出现：

  • 北方深境：莫克哈拉、废弃村落、维京墓地 (北方深境)、沉船 (北方深境)。

用途

每次在潜能熔炉尝试消耗一座神像，不需其他材料。65%概率使品质升一级，可超过4级；失败则摧毁物品，返还部分材料。

The Bloodgold Protection Idol is an idol used to upgrade items at the Forge of Potential.

Contents

  • 1 Availability
  • 2 Usage
    • 2.1 Armor
    • 2.2 Shields
  • 3 See also

Availability

It is found in loot chests at these points of interest, together with the Bloodgold Battle Idol:

  • Deep North: Mörkhalla, Abandoned Village, Viking Graveyard (Deep North), Shipwreck (Deep North).

Usage

Each attempt at the Forge of Potential consumes one idol and costs no other materials. It has a 65% chance to raise the item's quality by one level, even above level 4; otherwise the item is destroyed and part of its materials are returned.

The Bloodgold Protection Idol upgrades the following 13 items:

Armor

  •  Breastplate of the Protector
  •  Trousers of the Protector
  •  Hood of the Vanguard
  •  Chestpiece of the Vanguard
  •  Trousers of the Vanguard
  •  Headdress of the Caller
  •  Robes of the Caller
  •  Trousers of the Caller
  •  Cape of the Caller
  •  Moose Hide Cape

Shields

  •  Nord Buckler
  •  Nord Shield
  •  Nord Greatshield
