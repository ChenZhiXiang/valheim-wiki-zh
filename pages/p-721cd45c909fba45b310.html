说明

母鸡是可驯服、可繁殖的鸟类。在满足条件时产蛋，包括喂食以下任意一种：蒲公英、山毛榉种子、桦树种子、胡萝卜种子、芜菁种子、洋葱种子或大麦（不会大麦面粉）。

获取

母鸡是成年小鸡。蛋孵化后，小鸡经过一段时间，约两天，即可长大。蛋可向霍尔德以1500枚钱币购买，或在封印之塔中找到。

说明

  • 作为母鸡 为: 属于可驯服动物，玩家可伤害它们 唯一 带一个 屠夫刀.
    • 不过玩家可用湮灭者的范围闪电攻击[ 1 ]，或将河豚放置到与其接触的位置，间接伤害母鸡和小鸡。
  • 母鸡有3种颜色：白色、棕色及白棕相间，每种出现概率似乎各为33%。
  • 母鸡是少数不会直接生出幼体的可驯服动物，而是产下鸡蛋，必须在热源附近保暖才能孵化为鸡。

Hen are passive creatures that can only appear when an Egg hatches. They are the grown stage of Chickens.

Description

Hen are tameable birds that can reproduce and will lay eggs under certain conditions, including being fed with any of:  Dandelion,  Beech Seeds,  Birch Seeds,  Carrot Seeds,  Turnip Seeds,  Onion Seeds, or  Barley (not  Barley Flour).

Obtain

Hens are grown up chickens. A chicken will grow up after a certain amount of time (approximately two days) after an egg has hatched. Eggs can be bought for 1500 Coins from Haldor, or found as loot in Sealed Towers.

Drops

Item 0 Star

Notes

  • As Hens are tameable animals, they can be damaged by players only with a Butcher Knife.
    • Despite this, players can indirectly damage both Hens and Chickens by using an Obliterator's lightning strike AoE[1] or by placing pufferfish in contact with them.
  • Hens have 3 different colors: white, brown, white and brown. The probability of each color to appear seems to be 33% each.
  • Hens are among the few tameable animals that do not directly reproduce a young version; they lay an Egg instead, which must be warmed near a heat source in order to hatch into a Chicken.
