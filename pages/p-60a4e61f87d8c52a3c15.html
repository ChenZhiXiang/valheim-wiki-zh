黑暗林山生态区域是危险的针叶林，植被浓密繁茂。地面铺满蕨类、倒木、树桩、浆果灌木和蓟草，上方覆盖松树与冷杉的树冠。起伏丘陵和宽阔谷地间散布巨大裸岩及沉睡的铜矿源。海岸有许多小湾、入海口甚至湿地，岸边闪亮的锡矿源等待采集。这是唯一可开采铜和锡矿的生物群系；玩家击败赤血灵鹿并获得鹿角镐后即可开采。

大量灰矮人在林海中游荡，偶尔还有巨魔和成群的骷髅怪。这里也有游戏最早的独立地牢诡秘墓穴，是玩家最初获取焰灵之核的地方，可用于建造冶炼场并开启冶金。通常玩家会在青青草原之后，将黑森林作为第二个探索的生物群系。

Black Forest biomes are dangerous, coniferous forests full of dense and lush vegetation. A low blanket of ferns, felled logs, stumps, berry bushes, and Thistle plants is towered over by a canopy of Pine and Fir trees. Rolling hills and spacious dales are broken up by huge exposed boulders and slumbering Copper Deposits. The coastline features many coves, inlets, and even marshlands. The shore is lined with glistening Tin Deposits, ready to be collected. It is the only biome where Copper and Tin ores can be mined, available to the player after defeating Eikthyr and acquiring an Antler Pickaxe.

Large amounts of Greydwarfs amble about this sea of trees, along with the occasional Troll and gaggle of Skeletons. The forest is also home to Burial Chambers, the first instanced dungeon area in the game. These chambers are the player's first source of Surtling Cores, which allow the creation of a smelting yard and usher in the beginnings of metallurgy. Typically, the player will explore the Black Forest as their second biome, after the Meadows.
