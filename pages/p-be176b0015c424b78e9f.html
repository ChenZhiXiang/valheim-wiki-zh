地图提供世界已探索部分的概览。探索新区域时，玩家周围一定范围内的地形会自动揭示。揭示的地形只反映世界生成时高度图上的生态区域分布，不显示建筑或玩家对地形的改动。

地图揭示范围小于世界生成范围，因此玩家亲眼看到的地形可能比地图显示的更远。

按M可打开全屏地图，小地图则始终显示在状态界面中。

地图标记

地图标记分为自动放置的标记和玩家标记两类。

自动放置的标记为:

  • 献祭石阵：从游戏开始就可见。
  • 死亡：显示死亡地点及死亡发生的天数，可帮助定位墓碑并取回物品。
  • 弃誓者祭坛：与对应的导向符文石互动会揭示该首领最近的祭坛。也可与祭坛本身的符文互动，将其标在地图上。
  • 商人：霍尔德、希尔德和沼泽女巫。商人一旦生成便会加入地图，并从所有可能位置中锁定其实际位置。
  • 潜能熔炉：与商人一样加入地图。
  • 希尔德的请求地点：阴燃之墓、嚎叫洞穴和封印之塔。与希尔德面前的桌子互动可将所有地点加入地图。这些地点也有助于寻找特定生态区，因为它们分别必定位于黑暗林山、凛冽雪山或北境平原。
  • 神秘的地点，也可从指路符文获得。
  • 事件：用红色圆圈显示事件发生范围，仅在事件持续期间显示。

玩家可在全屏地图上放置标记。有五种图标可选：火、房屋、锤子、传送门和圆圈。放置时可为标记添加名称，但之后无法改名。标记可放在地图任意位置，包括未探索地形和世界边界之外，并同时显示在全屏地图和小地图上；小地图不显示名称。

左键点击标记会为其加上红色叉号，右键点击会删除标记。左键点击其他玩家的标记，详见§ 多人游戏与 位置共享，会复制该标记，并将其由深灰色变为白色。

小地图

小地图始终显示在右上角，可用句点键（.）和逗号键（,）缩放。

小地图以玩家为中心，显示玩家朝向、当前生态区和风向，但不显示风力。

多人游戏与 位置共享

默认情况下，玩家不会向其他玩家显示自己的位置。可随时通过全屏地图中的复选框开启或关闭位置共享。玩家无需公开自己的位置，也能看见其他已共享位置的玩家。

已揭示的地形和标记默认也不共享。可使用制图台交换地图信息。复制其他玩家的地图会复制其所有已揭示地形和带名称的标记，死亡标记除外，并以较淡的颜色显示。复制的地图数据可关闭显示。

发送地图信号

用鼠标中键点击全屏地图可发出位置提示，所有玩家都能在地图和世界中看见。玩家加入世界时会自动发出所在位置的提示。

地图位置提示对导航特别有用，因为即使关闭地图，也能看见大致行进方向。

无地图

游戏提供禁用地图的世界修改器。无地图玩法极具挑战，导航需要依靠自行设置的标记、地点和世界树，或在游戏之外手绘地图。

迷路可能致命，也会大幅延长轮船旅程。用于揭示弃誓者祭坛位置的指路符文无法正常使用，但仍会将镜头转向首领所在方向。若指路符文石位于地下城中，可用锤子将建筑对象旋转至与镜头方向一致，记录朝向，再在地下城外复现该对象的朝向。

巨人入侵的位置会由一串粒子指示。

获得以下成就需要在禁用地图的情况下游玩：

探路者 • “不使用地图，向四个方向远行。”

The map provides an overview of the explored part of the world. Terrain is revealed automatically in a range around the player as new parts of the world are explored. The revealed terrain only reflects the placement of biomes on a heightmap at world generation, structures or player-made alterations to the terrain are not shown.

The map reveal range is smaller than the world generation range, meaning that it is possible to see terrain in-person farther than what the map shows.

The fullscreen map can be opened using M, whereas the minimap is always visible in the HUD.

Map markers

There are two kinds of markers: automatically placed ones, and player markers.

Automatically placed markers are:

  • Sacrificial Stones, visible from the start.
  • Deaths, showing the place of death and the day it took place. They are useful to determine the location of the tombstone to retrieve.
  • Forsaken Altars. The nearest altar of a given boss is revealed by interacting with its respective Vegvisir. Alternatively, interacting with runes on the altar itself will place it on the map.
  • Traders: Haldor, Hildir and The Bog Witch. They are added as soon as they generate, which locks their location in place out of all possible locations.
  • The Forge of Potential, in the same manner as traders.
  • Hildir's Request locations: Smouldering Tombs, Howling Caverns and Sealed Towers. All locations are added to the map by interacting with a table in front of Hildir. They are useful for locating specific biomes, as each is always located in a Black Forest, Mountain or Plains respectively.
  • Mysterious Locations, also from Vegvisirs.
  • Events, with the range where the event is taking place in a red circle. They are only shown for the duration of the event.

Player markers can be placed using the fullscreen map. There are five icons available: fire, house, hammer, portal and circle; each marker can be labeled as it is placed, but not renamed afterwards. They can be placed anywhere on the map (including in unexplored terrain and outside the world boundary) and are visible on both the fullscreen map and minimap, but without labels on the minimap.

Left-clicking a marker marks it with a red cross, right-clicking a marker erases it. Left-clicking a marker from another player (see § Multiplayer and location sharing) copies it and makes it white instead of dark grey.

Minimap

The minimap is always visible in the top right. It can be zoomed using . (period) and , (comma).

It displays the map centered on the player, the direction the player is facing, the current biome and wind direction (although not wind strength).

Multiplayer and location sharing

Players do not reveal their location to other players by default. It can be enabled or disabled anytime using a checkbox in the fullscreen map. Players can see the location of those who are sharing it without having to reveal their own.

The revealed terrain and markers are also not shared by default. Map information can be exchanged using a Cartography Table. Copying the map from another player will copy all of their revealed terrain and markers (with labels), excluding death markers, and display it faded in color. Copied map data can be toggled off.

Pinging

Middle-clicking the fullscreen map pings the location, which is visible to all players both on the map and in the world. A player joining the world automatically pings their location.

Pinging the map is particularly useful for navigation, as it allows seeing the general direction to travel in even when the map is closed.

No map

A no map World Modifier is available. Playing without the map is particularly challenging; navigating without one necessitates relying on own markers, points of interest and Yggdrasil, or charting a map by hand outside the game.

Getting lost can prove fatal and substantially prolong trips by boat. Vegvisirs, which reveal the location of Forsaken Altars, cannot be used normally, however they will point the camera in the direction of the boss. If the Vegvisir is in a dungeon the camera direction can be recorded using the hammer and rotating an object to match the camera direction, then recording that object direction outside the dungeon.

The location of Jotun Invasions is indicated with a line of particles.

Playing without the map is needed to obtain one achievement:

The Pathfinder • "Travel far in all four directions, without using a map."
