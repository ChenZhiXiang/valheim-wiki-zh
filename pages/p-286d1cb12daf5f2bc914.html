青青草原是温和无害的生态区域，草地覆盖着缓缓起伏的丘陵。作为玩家在英灵神殿,最早接触的生物群系，也最容易生存。宽阔空地与斜坡间散布山毛榉、桦树和橡树林，脚下有蕨类、幼树和偶尔一簇浆果灌木。鹿及野生鬓猪安静吃草，小小灰宝宝则四处窜动，寻找建筑和玩家攻击。溪流河川蜿蜒切过地貌，有时汇成内海和湖泊，敦实的蜥蜴精在岸边晒太阳。整体而言，草原拥有第十世界最宜人的景致之一。

不过这里也有危险。树木及前人留下的许多建筑之间，玩家可能遇到地点，例如维京墓地和尤其致命的尸鬼村庄。

The Meadows are calm, innocuous biomes covered in grass and gentle rolling hills. They are the very first biome the player encounters in Valheim, making them simultaneously the least difficult. Large clearings and sloping hillsides are punctuated by thickets and forests of Beech, Birch, and Oak trees; underfoot is a carpet of ferns, saplings, and the occasional cluster of berry bushes. Deer and wild Boars graze peacefully from the grass while diminutive Greylings scurry about, looking for structures and players to damage. Streams and rivers wind and carve their way through the landscape, sometimes opening up to inland seas and lakes, stout Necks bathing in the sun along their shores. On the whole, the Meadows make for some of the most pleasant vistas the tenth world has to offer.

The biome is not without its hazards, however. Among the trees and numerous structures left behind by their predecessors, the player can find various points of interest while exploring, such as Viking Graveyards and particularly deadly Draugr Villages.

Meadows

100 40-50 In the Meadows, spawns in Draugr Villages, and anywhere on foggy nights after The Elder is defeated.

Spawns with a bow / axe / axe + wood shield / axe + banded shield

After certain bosses are defeated, creatures from other biomes also spawn in the Meadows at night, without stars, and despawn at dawn: Greydwarfs after Eikthyr; Greydwarf Brutes and Greydwarf Shamans after The Elder; Skeletons after Bonemass; Fulings after Yagluth; Seekers after The Queen; and Charred Warriors and Charred Marksmen after Fader.
