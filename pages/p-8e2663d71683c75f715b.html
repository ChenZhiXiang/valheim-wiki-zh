矮人高架桥是迷雾之地中由黑大理石建成的结构，含1至3根柱子，内部由铁牢地板 2x2和铁牢栅栏 2x2构成。

掉落物

每根柱子
资源 数额
 黑大理石 约150
 铁 48
 铜碎屑 8

部分柱体埋在地面中，使某些资源难获取。

提示

  • 破坏柱基后，整个结构倒塌，轻松产出大量黑色大理石和铁。
  • 工具锤配附近切石机和熔炉，可摧毁全部高架桥部件。

Dvergr viaducts are structure made of Black Marble found in the Mistlands. They have from 1 to 3 pillars and an internal structure made of Cage Floor 2x2 and Cage Wall 2x2.

Drops

Per pillar
Resources Amount
 Black Marble around 150
 Iron 48
 Copper Scrap 8

Part of the pillar is buried in the ground, making certain resources difficult to access.

Tips

  • When the base of the pillars are destroyed the full structure collapse and yields large quantities of Black marble and Iron at low effort.
  • A Hammer with a nearby Stonecutter and Forge can destroy all the viaduct parts.
