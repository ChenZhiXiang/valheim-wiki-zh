农耕（技能）
说明
耐力消耗、耕地耙损耗、收割范围及额外产出概率。

耕作是一种技能。

技能效果

耕作技能具有以下效果，随等级提升而增强：

  • 有概率额外收获一份产物，最高为25%。适用于作物、种子及自然生长的蘑菇和浆果。
  • 将镰刀的收割范围由1.5米提高至最高2.5米，耐力消耗最多降低至-33%。
  • 将耕地耙放置作物时的耐久消耗最多降低至-50%，攻击耐力消耗最多降低至-33%。

升级

以下活动提供耕作技能经验： 经验:

  • 使用镰刀获得0.25点经验。
  • 使用耕地耙获得0.25点经验，包括种草和翻耕土地。
  • 手动收获食物或种子获得0.25点经验，也包括采集无法种植的覆盆子、蘑菇和蓝莓。

说明

  • 虽然长柄战戟和大锤等范围攻击武器可以收割某些作物，但不会获得经验；使用镰刀则能获得经验。镰刀还可收割上述武器无法收割的胡萝卜、芜菁和洋葱等作物。
  • 截至补丁0.219.14，镰刀无法收割法师帽菇和巨人泡泡。

Farming (skill)
Description
Stamina use, cultivator wear, harvest radius and chance for bonus yields.

Farming is a skill.

Skill benefits

The Farming skill has the following effect, proportional to the level:

  • A chance to gather one extra product with up to a 25% chance. It works on crops, seeds, mushrooms and berries found in nature.
  • Increases Scythe harvest range from 1.5 meters up to 2.5 meters and decrease stamina usage down to -33%.
  • Decreases Cultivator placement durability usage down to -50% and its attack stamina cost down to -33%.

Leveling up

The following activities give Farming skill experience:

  • Using a Scythe gives 0.25 experience.
  • Using a Cultivator gives 0.25 experience, including growing grass and plowing a field.
  • Harvesting a food or seeds manually gives 0.25 experience. This includes picking foods that cannot be planted such as Raspberries, Mushroom, and Blueberries.

Notes

  • While it is possible to harvest some crops with weapons that have an area of effect (such as Atgeirs and Sledgehammers), these will not give any experience points when doing so, while using the Scythe would. Furthermore, the scythe allows harvesting other crops the previously-mentioned weapons cannot, such as Carrots, Turnips and Onions.
  • As of patch 0.219.14, the scythe cannot harvest Magecaps and Jotun Puffs.
