工作台是基础设施，用于制作、修复物品，以及在20米内建造各种建筑。

建造不需要其他设施。

制作和修复时需要屋顶和70%的遮蔽。修复不消耗材料。范围内建造或阻止怪物生成的功能，则不需要屋顶或遮蔽。

升级

升级工作台可解锁更多配方，提高物品可升级的品质上限。在五米内建造以下设施，最多可升至等级五。每项升级还增加四米建造范围，最大36米。

相同升级设施不会叠加。一件升级设施可同时升级超过1个工作台。

存在要求等级六的配方，暗示未来计划加入更多升级，详见下文。

每件升级设施都需要独立且不重叠的连接路径通往制作台，建造菜单中用发光虚线显示。例如，不能在同侧排成直线，否则会相互挡住连接路径。升级设施可以放在墙或地板另一侧。

升级工具, 武器, 与 护甲

工作台每提高一级，部分物品就能再升级一次，最多升级两次或三次。每次增加耐久度，武器和护甲还会提高伤害或防御。

关于怪物生成的说明

工作台是用于界定玩家基地的基础设施。玩家基地内不会生成怪物，因此可用工作台阻止指定区域刷怪。

  • 若目的是阻止刷怪：
    • 工作台会显示作用半径，因此建工作台是确保覆盖区域没有空隙的最简单方法。
  • 可用工作台范围检查基地设施的覆盖区域。右键锤子并选择工作台，地面会显示白色建造范围圆，其半径也是20米。
  • 未升级工作台的半径为20米，但按40米间距布置方形网格，会因圆形覆盖产生空隙。要完全覆盖，应取能放进每个圆内的最大正方形，此时有效范围为14米。因此，工作台间隔28米可实现完整覆盖。

Workbenches are base structures used for crafting, repairing items and building various structures within 20 meters.

They do not require any other structure to be built.

They must have a roof and 70% cover to enable crafting and repairing. Repairing items does not cost any resources. The ability to build structures within the radius of the workbench, or to suppress monster spawning, does not require the workbench to have a roof or any cover.

Upgrades

Upgrading a Workbench enables additional crafting recipes and higher upgrade levels for items. A Workbench can be upgraded up to level five by building the following structures within five meters. Each upgrade also increases the build radius by four meters, up to a maximum of 36 meters.

Duplicate upgrades do not stack. Upgrade items can be used to upgrade more than 1 Workbench at a time.

Recipes exist calling for level six, which suggests further upgrades are planned for the future (see below).

Each upgrade structure needs its own unique, non-overlapping connection path to the crafting table, visually displayed with a dotted line of light in the building menu. For example, upgrades cannot be placed in a straight line on one side because they will block the connection path of each other. Upgrades can be built on the opposite side of walls and floors.

Upgrading tools, weapons, and armor

With each level of the Workbench gained some items can be upgraded an additional time, to a maximum of two or three upgrades. Each upgrade gives the item more durability. For weapons and armor, upgrades also improve damage or defense ratings.

A note about monster spawning

The workbench is a basic structure used to define the Player's base. Since monster spawning is prevented inside player's bases, the workbench can be used to prevent monster spawns in areas that the player selects.

  • For the goal of preventing spawns:
    • Building workbenches is the easiest way to ensure there are no gaps because the effect radius is shown.
  • Workbench radius can be used to check the covered area of a player base structure. Right-click the hammer and select workbench. A white circle is displayed on the ground to show the build radius which is also 20 meters.
  • Although the build radius of a workbench with no upgrades is 20 meters, a square grid of workbenches spaced 40 meters apart will have unaffected gaps due to the circular nature of the area affected. To have complete coverage, the largest square that can fit into each circular workbench area is needed. This changes the effective range to 14 meters. As such, placing workbenches 28 meters apart provides complete coverage.
