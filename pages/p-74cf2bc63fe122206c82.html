说明

  • 修改世界修改器不影响成就，除非该设置与成就直接相关，例如难度或无地图。
  • 使用作弊命令和模组会禁用成就。可用开发命令yesiuseddevcommandsbutiwantmyachievementsanyway重新启用，意思是“是的，我用了开发命令，但还是想要成就”。
  • 已知问题：若在奥丁之子补丁前已获得生存者成就，某些难度下的“最长连续生存天数”可能锁定。临时切换难度，使计数重新增加，死亡后即可在游戏中再次获得该成就。

Achievements (Trophies on PlayStation) are awarded by performing certain actions, like slaying bosses or exploring the world.

Achievements are based on the player stats visible with the chat command /stats or the developer console command stats.

Note

  • Changing World Modifiers doesn't affect achievements (unless they are directly related to the achievement, like difficulty level and no map).
  • Using Cheat commands and mods prevents achievements. Achievements can be reactivated with the devcommand yesiuseddevcommandsbutiwantmyachievementsanyway ("yes i used devcommands but i want my achievements anyway").
  • Known Bugs: Consecutive Days Max (Requirement for Survivor Achievement) can get locked on certain difficulties if you already had The Survivor achievement before The Child of Odin Patch, switching difficulties temporarily to allow it to increase again will allow you to regain the achievement in game if you died.
