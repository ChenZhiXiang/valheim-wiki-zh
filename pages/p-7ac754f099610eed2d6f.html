蜿蜒隧道是极北之地中的地下城。

蜿蜒隧道入口位于废弃村落的大屋内。每个世界最多放置40座大厅，远离北方深境边缘，海拔至少两米，彼此相隔至少256米。如名称所示，地下城由曲折隧道组成。

内部栖息大量厄妖及一些无眼者。

内部始终昏暗，但既不寒冷，也不冰冻。

布局

每座蜿蜒隧道由32至48个房间生成。房间间许多通道被根墙阻挡，拥有150点生命，破坏后有50%概率掉落一个巨木。地面及墙上较小的树根也可破坏，获得木料。

生物

  • 玩家在30米内时，厄妖之门每10秒生成一只厄妖，直到井口20米内已有五只。三分之一持灯笼，最高可为二星。厄妖之门有300点生命，摧毁后停止生成。
  • 无眼者一直沉睡，直到玩家进入六米内或攻击它们。

资源

  • 破坏碎石堆及厄妖之门，可获得武器、盾牌、法杖用模具或隐秘钥匙。
  • 发光幼虫提供光照，也可采集。
  • 多数隧道末端可找到冰霜之核。每个可悬挂冰霜之核的位置有45%概率出现一个。

The Winding Tunnels is a Dungeon found in the Deep North.

The entrance to the Winding Tunnels can be found inside large houses in Abandoned Villages. Up to 40 of these Great Halls are placed in each world, away from the edges of the Deep North, at least two meters above sea level and at least 256 meters apart. As the name suggests, the dungeon consists of convoluted tunnels.

It is inhabited by large amounts of Elaking and some Eyeless Ones.

Inside, the environment is always dark and neither cold nor freezing.

Layout

Each Winding Tunnels dungeon is generated from 32 to 48 rooms. Many passages between rooms are blocked by walls of roots, which have 150 health and have a 50% chance to drop one  Timberwood when destroyed. Smaller roots on the floors and walls can also be broken for Timberwood.

Creatures

  • Wardrobe Shafts spawn Elaking every 10 seconds while a player is within 30 meters, until five creatures are within 20 meters of the shaft. One in three of the spawned Elaking carries a lantern. Spawned Elaking can have up to two stars. A Wardrobe Shaft has 300 health and stops spawning once destroyed.
  • Eyeless Ones sleep until a player comes within six meters or attacks them.

Resources

  • Moulds for weapons, shields, staffs or the Intricate Key can be obtained by destroying Piles of Rubble and Wardrobe Shafts.
  •  Luminous Larvae provide light, and can be harvested.
  •  Frostcores can be found at the end of most tunnels. Each spot where a Frostcore can hang has a 45% chance to hold one.
