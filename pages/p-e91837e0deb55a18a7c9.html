袭击与能力

焦黑战士战斗策略参见 敌对生物战斗策略 § 焦黑战士.

横扫

垂直挥剑，覆盖前方3米、115度弧形，造成150点劈砍伤害。玩家在1.6米内时可用，冷却3秒。

突刺

向前刺出，延伸3米，略呈10度弧形，造成160点穿刺伤害。玩家在1.6米内时可用，冷却3秒。

佯攻

起手与普通挥击相同，但会突然停顿换姿势，再以3米、垂直77.3度弧形劈砍，造成130点劈砍伤害。玩家在1.6米内时可用，冷却8秒。与普通挥击动画几乎无异。[说明1 ]仍有足够时间反应并再次招架，但翻滚玩家除非完全移出范围，否则会被击中。

佯攻突刺

起手与前刺相同，突然停顿后从当前姿势上撩，造成160点穿刺伤害，覆盖3米、垂直60度弧形。玩家在1.6米内时可用，冷却3秒。与普通前刺动画几乎无异。[说明2 ]仍有时间反应并再次招架，但翻滚者除非完全移出范围，否则会被击中。

说明

  1. ↑普通挥击与假动作的时机差约0.57秒。
  2. ↑前刺与假动作的时机差约0.631秒。

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating Charred Warrior, see Aggressive creature strategies § Charred Warrior.

Swing

A vertical sword slash that creates a 3-meter 115-degree arc in front (150 Slash damage). It can be used every 3 seconds when within 1.6 meters of a player.

Thrust

A forward stab that extends in a 3-meter line which arcs slightly by 10 degrees (160 Pierce damage). This attack can be used every 3 seconds when within 1.6 meters of a player.

Feint

Shares the same windup as the swing, but they will suddenly pause before shifting stances to attack with a different slash that hits in a 3-meter 77.3-degree vertical arc (130 Slash damage). It can be used every 8 seconds when within 1.6 meters of a player. There is practically no difference between the animation for this attack and that of the regular swing[Note 1]; there is more than enough time to react and parry again, but the attack will catch rolling players unless completely moving out of their range.

Thrust feint

Shares the same windup as the thrust, but they will suddenly pause before attacking with an upward swing from their current stance (160 Pierce damage). This attack creates a vertical arc that is 3 meters long and spans 60 degrees. It can be used every 3 seconds when within 1.6 meters of a player. Similar to the regular feint, there is practically no difference between the animation for this attack and that of the regular thrust[Note 2]; there is more than enough time to react and parry again, but the attack will catch rolling players unless completely moving out of their range.

Notes

  1. ↑ The timing difference between the regular swing and the feint is approximately 0.57 seconds.
  2. ↑ The timing difference between the thrust and the feint is approximately 0.631 seconds.

Tips

  • It is difficult to predict whether an attack is a feint or not, to parry the attack effectively the player can try to parry the first part of the attack, if no parry animation is played the attack is a feint, then the player can quickly release and hold the block button again to parry the second part.
  • Feint attacks can be identified by their unique sounds. Both feint attacks share the same set of sounds.
