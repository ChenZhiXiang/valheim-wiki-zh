制作材料

青铜是将两份铜及一份锡在熔炉加工的制作材料。也可破坏凛冽雪山中冰霜洞穴里的火盆，或灰烬之地中废墟及腐朽之穴内的上古之坛获取。另可在冶炼炉直接冶炼少见的青铜废料获得。

说明

  • 青铜目前是游戏唯一合金，即由两种或更多金属组成。
  • 截至灰烬之地更新，青铜与其他金属一样，没有传送门 石头就无法传送。
  • 黑暗林山级基础护甲、武器及工具各制作一件需78份青铜，全部升级再需222份，总计300份。全部生物群系等级基础装备各一件需98份，再需232份升级，总计330份。

Bronze is a crafting material crafted by processing two Copper and one Tin at the Forge. Bronze can also be obtained by destroying the Standing Braziers found in Frost Caves within the Mountain biome, or by destroying Ancient Pots found in ruins and Putrid Holes within the Ashlands biome. Additionally, Bronze can also be obtained by directly smelting the uncommon Scrap Bronze in a Smelter.

Notes

  • Bronze is currently the only alloy in the game. Meaning, its composition is that of two or more metals.
  • Like other metals, Bronze cannot be teleported, without the Portal stone. (As of the Ashlands Update)
  • It takes 78 Bronze to craft one of each base equipment (armor, weapons, tools) in the Black Forest tier. Further 222 Bronze to fully upgrade (total 300 Bronze). 98 Bronze to craft one of each base equipment in all Biomes tiers. Further 232 to fully upgrade (total 330 Bronze).
