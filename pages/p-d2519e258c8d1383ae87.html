拳术（技能）
说明
决定赤手空拳及以下武器的伤害： 拳头 武器.

拳类是与拳类武器类别相关的技能，包括徒手。

效果

拳技能提高，攻击耐力消耗最多降低至-33%，随机伤害和击退倍率从等级0的25–55%提高至等级100的85–100%。

伤害缩放详情请参阅伤害机制# 技能因素。

升级

命中生物时，单手攻击获得1.5经验，双手攻击获得1经验。

说明

  • 技能标识符为未激活，用于raiseskill或resetskill命令。
  • 冰霜洞穴更新之前，拳技能名为徒手。

Fists (skill)
Description
Determines damage with Bare Fists and Fist weapons.

Fists is a skill related to Fists weapon class, including Bare Fists.

Effects

Higher skill level in fists reduces the stamina cost to attack down to -33% and uses a random damage and knockback multiplier ranging from 25–55% at skill level 0 to 85–100% at skill level 100.

For the details of damage scaling, see Damage mechanics#Skill factor.

Leveling up

The player gains 1.5 experience per one-handed and 1 experience per two-handed attack when hitting a creature.

Notes

  • Skill ID is Unarmed (to use with raiseskill or resetskill commands).
  • Fists was previously named Unarmed prior to the Frost Caves update.
