灰巨人是极北之地中的敌对生物，形似冰冻的古树长老。

对多数物理伤害有抗性，但对砍伐为正常抗性。

灰巨人生成时休眠。玩家进入20m内，或50m内玩家发出的噪声传到它时，会醒来。

袭击与能力

灰巨人所有攻击都造成等级3的砍伐和镐击伤害，也会命中友方生物。

重击

在8米、水平90度弧形内造成160点钝击、100点冰霜、60点砍伐及30点镐类伤害。目标距其3-9米时可用，冷却7秒。

冲撞砸击

在9米、垂直50度弧形内造成180点钝击、100点冰霜、50点砍伐及25点镐类伤害，击退力为其他攻击两倍。目标距其10-20米时可用，冷却7秒。

鞭击连打

在10米、水平160度弧形内造成100点钝击、100点冰霜、25点砍伐及10点镐类伤害。目标距其1-10米时可用，冷却7秒。

鞭砸

在9米、垂直50度弧形内造成100点钝击、100点穿刺、40点冰霜、50点砍伐及30点镐类伤害。目标距其4-12米时可用，冷却7秒。

后方砸击

造成190点钝击、80点冰霜和40点砍伐伤害，针对灰巨人后方3-12米内目标，冷却3秒。

The Barka is a hostile creature found in the Deep North. It resembles a frozen The Elder.

It is resistant to most physical damage types, but neutral to Chop.

Barkas spawn dormant. They wake up when a player comes within 20m, or when a player's noise within 50m reaches them.

Attacks and abilities

All of the Barka's attacks deal Tier 3 Chop and Pickaxe damage, and also hit allied creatures.

Heavy swing

Deals 160 Blunt, 100 Frost, 60 Chop and 30 Pickaxe damage in an 8-meter, 90-degree horizontal arc. It can be used every 7 seconds when within 3-9 meters of a target.

Slam drive

Deals 180 Blunt, 100 Frost, 50 Chop and 25 Pickaxe damage in a 9-meter, 50-degree vertical arc, with twice the knockback of its other attacks. It can be used every 7 seconds when within 10-20 meters of a target.

Whip flurry

Deals 100 Blunt, 100 Frost, 25 Chop and 10 Pickaxe damage in a 10-meter, 160-degree horizontal arc. It can be used every 7 seconds when within 1-10 meters of a target.

Whip slam

Deals 100 Blunt, 100 Pierce, 40 Frost, 50 Chop and 30 Pickaxe damage in a 9-meter, 50-degree vertical arc. It can be used every 7 seconds when within 4-12 meters of a target.

Back slam

Deals 190 Blunt, 80 Frost and 40 Chop damage. It is set up for targets behind the Barka, within 3-12 meters, and has a 3-second cooldown.
