砂轮用于升级熔炉。

它是两种不需要金属锭的熔炉升级设施之一，因此可以通过传送门运输；另一种是风箱。

获得铁和磨刀石后解锁磨轮。

若已有切石机可以制作磨刀石，磨轮材料相当常见：五份石头和25份木材。否则，也可能在北境平原的箱子中偶尔找到磨刀石。

The grinding wheel is used as an upgrade for the forge.

It is one of the two upgrades to the forge that does not use metal ingots, and thus can be taken through a portal. (The other is the bellows.)

Building

The grinding wheel is unlocked after acquiring iron and a Sharpening Stone.

Provided that the player has a stonecutter to craft the sharpening stone, the materials for a grinding wheel are fairly common and easy to acquire: five stone, 25 wood. Otherwise, a sharpening stone can be found occasionally in chests in the Plains.
