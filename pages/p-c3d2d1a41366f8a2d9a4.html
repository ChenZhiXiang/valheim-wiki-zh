恩布拉套装是一套灰烬之地级别的护甲，比甲壳护甲和埃达织物套装高一级。其护甲值低于焰金属护甲和阿斯克套装，因此应视为“轻甲”。

这套护甲的埃达之能恢复加成比埃达织物套装更高，因此使用魔法武器时更适合穿戴它。

这套护甲由三件组成，可在古咒法台处制作和升级。

趣闻

  • 在北欧神话中，阿斯克和恩布拉（古诺尔斯语：“Askr ok Embla”）分别为男性和女性，是诸神创造的最初两个人类，参与创造的神之一是奥丁。
  • “embla”的词源尚不确定，通常对“embla”的含义提出两种解释。第一种是“榆树”，通过将*Elm-la追溯至*Almilōn，再联系到“almr”（“榆树”）得出。第二种解释是“藤蔓”，经由*Ambilō得出；它可能与希腊语ἄμπελος（ámpelos）相关，后者意为“葡萄藤、藤本植物”。

The Embla Set is an Ashlands-tier armor. This set is the tier above Carapace Armor and the Eitr-weave Set, and should be considered a "light armor," having a lower armor value than Flametal Armor and the Ask Set.

The armor grants even higher eitr regeneration than the Eitr-weave Set, which makes it the preferred armor when using magic weapons.

This set of armor has three pieces that are crafted and upgraded at the Galdr Table.

Trivia

  • In Norse mythology, Ask and Embla (Old Norse: "Askr ok Embla") — man and woman respectively — were the first two humans, created by the gods(one of those gods responsible being Oden).
  • The etymology of "embla" is uncertain, and two possibilities of the meaning of "embla" are generally proposed. The first meaning, "elm tree" is reached by deriving *Elm-la from *Almilōn and subsequently to "almr" ('elm'). The second suggestion is "vine", which is reached through *Ambilō, which may be related to the Greek term ἄμπελος (ámpelos), itself meaning "vine, liana".
