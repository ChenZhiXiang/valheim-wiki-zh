调料架用于升级铸铁锅。成本较低，通常是大锅的第一项升级。

用途

等级2铸铁锅可制作的物品包括：

  •  黑血汤
  • 敲克力冰沙——在家园与炉火中加入。
  •  洋葱汤
  •  香肠
  •  蛟龙肉羹
  •  芜菁肉汤
  • 其他升级设施见铸铁锅。

必须挂在天花板或架子下，可借助木梁完成。

说明

  • 调料架随家园与炉火于2021年九月16日加入游戏。

The Spice rack is used as an upgrade for the Cauldron. It will typically be the first upgrade for the cauldron due to its low cost.

Usage

Level 2 Cauldron items include:

  •  Black Soup
  •  Muckshake - introduced in Hearth and Home.
  •  Onion Soup
  •  Sausages
  •  Serpent Stew
  •  Turnip Stew
  • For other upgrades, see Cauldron.

Must be placed hanging from the ceiling or a shelf. Can be done using a wood beam.

Notes

  • The Spice rack was introduced in Hearth and Home on September 16th, 2021.
