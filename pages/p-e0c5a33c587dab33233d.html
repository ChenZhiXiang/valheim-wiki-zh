盛宴列表说明
List of feastsNotes

盛宴是持续时间更长、可供多人分享的大份餐食。

盛宴在灶台处制作，需要使用向沼泽女巫购买的香料。

食用前必须用餐盘摆放盛宴。可通过餐盘的建造菜单选择盛宴，将其放在地板、桌子或其他表面上。摆放后，一道盛宴可供食用10份，每份效果持续50分钟。全部吃完后，须使用餐盘的“移除”功能清除空盘。如果盛宴已被吃掉一部分，摧毁或拆除它不会返还任何物品。

盛宴属于均衡型食物，提供相同数值的生命值和耐力。制作时通常消耗不足10份餐食，却能提供10份、持续时间约为多数食物两倍的餐食，相当于20份普通食物，因此很划算。

说明

  • 盛宴的外观会随剩余份量变化，分为剩六份、三份和零份的状态。
  • 虽然每道盛宴都以一种生物群系为主题，但只有沼泽居民限定套餐能在对应生物群系阶段获得。
    • 草原烤全猪和黑暗林山限定套餐需要林地混合香料；只有击败古树长老后，沼泽中的沼泽女巫才会出售，因此这两道盛宴归入沼泽阶段。
    • 海钓套餐需要熟深海蛟龙肉，而蛟龙肉只能在铁烹饪台上烤熟；所需的海员的香草也只在沼泽出售。
    • 山珍佛跳墙及之后的盛宴，都需要先击败对应生物群系的首领。因此，山地主题的盛宴到平原阶段才能获得，后续以此类推。极北阶段的盛宴基本上需要通关游戏后才能获得。

Feasts are large meals that last longer and can be shared among several people.

Feasts are crafted at the Food Preparation Table and require spices bought from The Bog Witch.

Before being eaten, they must be placed using the Serving Tray. They can be placed on any surface, on the floor or on a table, this is done using the Serving Tray build menu to choose which feast to place. Once placed, the feast has 10 servings, and each serving lasts 50 minutes. When all servings are eaten, the empty platter must be removed using the Serving Tray's "Remove" function. If a feast has already been partially eaten, it yields nothing when destroyed or dismantled.

Feasts are balanced foods, providing equal amounts of health and stamina. They are typically crafted from less than 10 meals, but provide 10 servings with twice the duration than most foods, effectively turning them into 20 servings, which makes them a worthwhile investment.

Notes

  • The visual appearance of the feast changes according to the number of remaining portions, those being six, three and zero.
  • Despite each feast being themed after a biome, only the Swamp Dweller's Delight is actually available in its respective biome.
    • Whole Roasted Meadow Boar and Black Forest Buffet Platter require a Woodland Herb Blend only sold by The Bog Witch in the Swamp after The Elder is defeated, thus making them Swamp-tier.
    • Sailor's Bounty requires Cooked Serpent Meat, which can only be made on an Iron Cooking Station, besides the Seafarer's Herbs only sold in the Swamp.
    • Hearty Mountain Logger's Stew and all subsequent feasts require that biome's boss to be defeated, thus making the Mountain-themed feast only available in the Plains tier, etc. The Deep North-tier feast essentially requires the game to be completed to become available.
