无尽之海是由广阔水体组成的生物群系，如现实海洋。实际主要通过船只探索，因为离岸游泳会迅速耗尽耐力并导致溺水。与其说是独立生物群系，海洋更像玩家探索的通道。

目前只有难找且破坏力强的蛟龙，以及更难找的利维坦，后者是覆盖灌木、藻类和藤壶的巨大有壳生物。这两种生物分别是获得蛟龙鳞和甲壳素的唯一途径。

某些天气效果会增加海浪大小及频率。

效果 频率 平均高度 最大高度
晴朗 每分钟2.5 0.5m 1.5m
雨水 每分钟2.5 2m 3m
ThunderStorm 每分钟5 3m 5m

说明

  • 生物在水中死亡或船被毁时，部分掉落物会沉入海底。海上沉船常意味着较重物品永远丢失。
    • 木材和部分战利品会漂浮；钉子、毛皮、金币等会沉底，可能无法拾取。玩家在海洋死亡会留下漂浮墓碑，船只货物则装在漂浮货箱中。
  • 游戏在所有海岸模拟涨潮和退潮。

The Ocean is a biome made up of, much like examples in real life, a vast body of water. It is only realistically accessible by boats, as swimming out from the safety of the shore quickly depletes stamina and leads to drowning. In many ways, the Ocean acts as less its own dedicated biome, per se, and more a vehicle for player exploration.

Currently the biome only features the elusive and destructive Serpent enemy as well as the even more elusive Leviathan, a hulking shelled creature covered in shrubbery, algae, and barnacles. These two creatures are the sole way for players to gather Serpent scales and Chitin, respectively.

Waves

Certain Weather effects will increase the size and frequency of waves within the ocean.

Effect Frequency Avg Height Max Height
Clear 2.5 per min 0.5m 1.5m
Rain 2.5 per min 2m 3m
ThunderStorm 5 per min 3m 5m

Notes

  • Creatures killed and boats destroyed in the water cause some of the flotsam to sink beneath the waves. A ship being sunk at sea often means heavier objects are lost to the briny deep.
    • Examples of floating material include wood and some trophies; nails, pelts, coins, etc. sink to the bottom and can become unreachable. Player deaths in the Ocean biome generate a floating headstone, while a vessel's cargo contents are stored in crates that float above the water.
  • There is a simulation of low and high tide within the game on all coast and shore lines.
