巨魔是黑暗林山中的敌对生物，为巨大笨拙的人形生物，四肢粗壮，长硬质金发。可能生成时手持大树干。

袭击与能力

巨魔有两种变体，1/3持原木，其余徒手。

徒手巨魔有三种攻击。

击打

后仰再挥击玩家，覆盖5米、45度的水平弧形范围，造成60点钝击、100点2级砍伐及40点2级镐击伤害。玩家在4米内时每4秒可用一次。

砸地

举起双拳砸向地面，造成70点钝击、100点2级砍伐及40点2级镐击伤害，覆盖向外4.5米、垂直90度的弧形范围。玩家在5米内时每20秒可用一次，因此玩家始终在范围内时，通常挥拳五次再砸地。命中任何物体或地面后，还产生3米范围伤害，造成50点钝击、50点2级砍伐及30点2级镐击伤害，此范围伤害不随星级变化。

掷出

向距离8–20米的玩家投巨石，造成50点钝击、60点0级砍伐及40点0级镐击伤害。巨石初速度每秒15米，飞行4秒后消失，散射角10–20度。冷却10秒，贴近巨魔可阻止使用。

持原木巨魔有两种攻击。

原木挥击（纵向）

将原木砸向地面，覆盖向外9米、90度范围，造成70点钝击、100点2级砍伐及40点2级镐击伤害。玩家在8米内时每6秒可用一次。

原木挥击（横向）

从侧面挥动原木，覆盖10米、50度弧形范围，造成60点钝击、100点2级砍伐及40点2级镐击伤害。玩家在8米内时每6秒可用一次。

战略

巨魔战斗策略参见 敌对生物攻略 § 巨魔.
  • 巨魔是英灵神殿中最大的敌人之一，力量与体形相称。弓或矛远程攻击最佳，穿过密林引其追逐很有效，因为巨大碰撞体会妨碍寻路。
  • 巨魔攻击强大但缓慢，容易躲避，很难格挡和招架。使用 枯骨塔盾 可有效格挡多数攻击，前提是玩家生命足够。
    • 一名玩家吸引巨魔注意并举塔盾，其他玩家齐射箭，是有效策略。
  • 巨魔有时落入深沟或坑，更常见的是铜矿开采的大坑。糟糕的寻路常使其卡住。
    • 巨魔接近足够深、至少6米的壕沟且未掉入时，不会主动走进去。够不到玩家就会站着怒视坑内，片刻后转而攻击能接触的玩家建筑。
    • 反过来，玩家站在坑沿上，可轻松用箭和矛消灭坑内巨魔。
  • 巨魔生成时可能持树干，也可能徒手。持树干个体的攻击范围和破坏力明显更强。

说明

  • 1星巨魔带浅绿色调，2星则为红褐色。
  • 它们高7米，可穿过宽2米及以上的通道。
  • 灰矮人萨满喷出的治疗花粉也会治疗巨魔，因为它们同属阵营。
  • 它们不会跳跃或游泳，但会在浅水底部行走，有时离岸很远，速度比玩家游泳更快。
  • 除投石外，巨魔全部攻击均具有 工具级 为2，使玩家能利用巨魔采集当前装备等级无法获取的资源。巨魔能击倒并破坏 橡树 与 桦树 用攻击破坏树木，早期尤其有助于获取 细木 无 青铜斧 或更好工具。巨魔也可破坏 铜 与 锡矿源 ，甚至投石也能做到，使玩家可获取 青铜 击杀第一位首领之前， 赤血灵鹿.
    • 将巨魔引入更危险的生物群系后，由于阵营不同，它们会攻击其中多数生物。虽然不太能对付北境平原等强敌，却能消灭大量泥泞沼泽生物，留下内脏及血袋等战利品供玩家拾取。
  • 爱之药提高巨魔生成率及星级概率，并使其警戒。

趣闻

  • 古诺尔斯文献将巨魔与北欧神话巨人约顿混同。直到后期斯堪的纳维亚民俗中，才作为独立存在出现。
  • 早期测试版本中，黑暗林山巨魔为覆绿苔的棕色皮肤，凛冽雪山巨魔则为蓝色。[ 1 ]
  • 早期棕色巨魔战利品可用“spawn TrophyForestTroll”生成，当前巨魔战利品则用“spawn TrophyFrostTroll”。
  • 早期测试版巨魔战利品可放在物品架上，但不可见。

Trolls are aggressive creatures found in Black Forests. They are massive, bumbling humanoids with brutish limbs and wiry blonde hair. They may spawn with a large tree trunk in their hand.

Attacks and abilities

Trolls come in two variants: 1/3rd of Trolls are equipped with a log, while the others are unarmed.

Unarmed Trolls have three attacks.

Punch

Leans back before swinging to attack the player in a 5-meter 45-degree horizontal arc (60 Blunt, 100 Tier 2 Chop and 40 Tier 2 Pickaxe damage). This attack can be used every 4 seconds when within 4 meters of a player.

Ground slam

Lifts both fists up before slamming them down into the ground (70 Blunt, 100 Tier 2 Chop and 40 Tier 2 Pickaxe damage). This slam reaches 4.5 meters out in a 90-degree vertical arc, and can be used every 20 seconds when within 5 meters of a player. This effectively means that the Troll will punch five times before doing a slam, assuming that the player is always in range. When the slam hits anything, including the ground, it also creates a 3-meter area that deals 50 Blunt, 50 Tier 2 Chop and 30 Tier 2 Pickaxe damage. This area damage is the same at every star level.

Throw

Throws a large boulder at players within 8–20 meters (50 Blunt, 60 Tier 0 Chop and 40 Tier 0 Pickaxe damage). The boulder travels at an initial velocity of 15 meters per second and can travel for 4 seconds before disappearing. It has a spread of 10–20 degrees. This has a cooldown of 10 seconds and can be disabled by standing close to the Troll.

Log Trolls have two attacks.

Log swing (vertical)

Slams their log into the ground extending out 9 meters and spanning across 90 degrees (70 Blunt, 100 Tier 2 Chop and 40 Tier 2 Pickaxe damage). This attack can be used every 6 seconds when within 8 meters of a player.

Log swing (horizontal)

Swings their log from the side, creating a 10-meter 50-degree arc (60 Blunt, 100 Tier 2 Chop and 40 Tier 2 Pickaxe damage). This attack can be used every 6 seconds when within 8 meters of a player.

Strategy

For strategies for defeating Trolls, see Aggressive creature strategies § Troll.
  • Among the largest enemies in Valheim, Trolls have a physical strength that reflects their size. Ranged attacks with a Bow or Spear are best and kiting a Troll through dense areas of the forest is very effective, as they have large hitboxes that hinder their pathfinding.
  • Trolls employ massive yet slow attacks that are easy to avoid but very hard to block and parry. Using a Bone Tower Shield is effective at blocking most of its attacks, provided the player has sufficient health.
    • "Pulling" the attention of a Troll and brandishing a tower shield while other players fire arrow volleys is an effective strategy.
  • Trolls will sometimes fall into deep ditches or pits, or more often, the large pit at a copper dig site. Their poor pathfinding often renders them stuck.
    • If a Troll approaches a ditch that is sufficiently deep (at least 6 meters) and does not fall in, it will not willingly walk in. Unable to reach the player, it will stand still, glowering into the pit. After a few moments, it will turn its aggression towards any player structures it can reach.
    • Alternatively, if the positions are reversed, standing atop the ledge of a pit allows for easy dispatch with arrows and spears.
  • Trolls can spawn with or without a tree trunk in their hand. A Troll equipped with a tree trunk has decidedly better range and destructive power than one that is not.

Notes

  • A 1-star Troll has a pale green tint, and a 2-star Troll is tinted a ruddy red.
  • They are tall (7 meters) and can move along corridors 2 meters and wider.
  • The curative pollen ejected by Greydwarf Shamans also heals Trolls, as they share a Faction.
  • They cannot jump or swim, but they will walk along the bottom of shallow water, sometimes getting a considerable distance from the shore. They can walk through shallow water faster than a player can swim.
  • All the Troll's attacks (except its rock throw) have a tool tier of 2, which allows players to use Trolls to harvest resources they are otherwise unable to attain at their current equipment level. Trolls are capable of knocking down and breaking apart Oak and Birch trees with their attacks, which is especially useful in the early game for obtaining Finewood without a Bronze Axe (or better). Trolls can also break Copper and Tin Deposits (even with their rock throw attacks), which can allow the player to get Bronze before killing the first boss, Eikthyr.
    • Additionally, Trolls lured into more hostile biomes will aggressively attack most creatures in that biome, due to them having different Factions. While Trolls are not such a good match for stronger enemies, such as Plains creatures, they are capable of destroying large numbers of Swamp creatures, leaving loot like Entrails and Bloodbags in their wake for the player to collect
  • The Love Potion increases Troll spawn rate, star chances and alerts them.

Trivia

  • In Old Norse sources, Trolls were assimilated to Jötnar, the giants from Norse mythology. Only in later Scandinavian folklore did they appear as separate beings.
  • During the alpha, Trolls in the Black Forest had brown skin covered by green moss, while Trolls in the Mountains had blue skin.[1]
  • The trophy of the brown Troll from the alpha build can be spawned with the command "spawn TrophyForestTroll", and the current troll trophy with "spawn TrophyFrostTroll"
  • The alpha build Troll trophy can be placed on an Item Stand, however, it is invisible.

Lore

"The trolls of Midgard may be fading from your memory, as indeed they are from all the race of man. Few and forlorn, they crouch in damp caves and gnaw on the bones of the weak and foolhardy. The will of Odin has pushed them back into the dark places.

But in Valheim they have flourished. It has been centuries since any in Midgard saw the great Trolls stamp flat the land and bring down the trees for joy. Look upon this great and noble sight, wanderers in a strange land! Then take to your heels and run..."

― Runestone
