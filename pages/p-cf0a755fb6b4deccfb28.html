攻城锤是一种攻城器械，外形有点像板车，但大得多，前方有两根长打击机构，用于近距离摧毁建筑。需要木材、细木、圆木或灰烬之木作为燃料。它可破坏任何能够受到伤害的建筑，包括灰烬亡灵堡垒的门和墙，也可开采开放世界中能受到镐 伤害的资源点，例如岩石、铜矿和锡矿。

用途

玩家须在攻城锤后方的燃料槽装入木材、细木、圆木或灰木，容量上限为25。随后站到前方双锤处，站在两根较小支臂之间时“使用”它，操作类似板车。玩家可控制方向和移动，双锤每隔几秒自动攻击一次。装有燃料但无人使用时，它不会攻击，但仍会继续消耗燃料。

配方

  •  焰金属 x10
  •  焰灵之核 x2
  •  灰烬之木 x20

说明

  • 用攻城锤破坏有守军的要塞门墙时，玩家容易被夹在器械与敌军之间，迅速陷入包围。脱离的方法与板车相同：低头对准攻城锤再次“使用”，或翻滚脱离。破门墙前可能需要用投石机摧毁要塞内部的生物生成点，显著减少守军数量。
  • 攻城锤每次打击造成恰好600点镐 伤害和0点砍伐伤害。
  • 每20秒消耗一份燃料。装满燃料可运行500秒（8.33分钟）。
  • 虽然主要用于攻城，攻城锤也是极高效的采矿器械。每次打击都会在周围较大范围造成大量镐击伤害，因此能迅速摧毁可触及的石材和金属资源点，包括铜矿源、银矿脉、巨人遗骸以及世界各处的巨石。
  • 由于石魔对镐类伤害极弱，攻城锤一次命中就能将其摧毁，造成1200点伤害。
  • 由于巨魔长老对镐类伤害较弱，攻城锤只需四次命中，即两个攻击周期，就能将其摧毁，造成3400点伤害。

Battering rams are siege engines with a slight resemblance to Carts but are significantly larger and have two long punching mechanisms at the front, which are intended to be used to destroy structures at close range. They need Wood, Finewood, Corewood, or Ashwood in order to function. They can destroy any structure that is able to receive damage, including Charred Fortresses doors and walls. But it can also be used to mine any resource nodes located in the open world that are able to receive pickaxe damage, such as rocks, copper and tin veins, etc.

Usage

The player must load the Battering ram with Wood, Finewood, Corewood, or Ashwood in the fuel slot located at the back, which has a maximum capacity of 25. After doing so, the player must operate the ram by standing in front of it (where the dual-hammers are) and "use" it while standing between the pair of smaller arms, very similarly to the Cart. This allows the player to steer and move it while the hammers will automatically attack every few seconds. If a player is not actively using the ram while being loaded with fuel, it will not attack, but the fuel will continue to be consumed.

Recipe

  •  Flametal x10
  •  Surtling Core x2
  •  Ashwood x20

Notes

  • Using the ram to destroy fortress doors or walls while there are enemies inside can easily trap the player between the ram and the army of enemies, where they may be quickly overwhelmed. In order to let go of the ram, the player must use it again just like they would do with the Cart: By looking down at the ram and "use" to drop it, or dodge-roll to break out. A Catapult might be required to destroy the spawners inside the fortress before attempting to breach the doors/walls in order to significantly reduce the amount of defending enemies.
  • Each punch from the Battering ram deals exactly 600 pickaxe damage and 0 chop damage.
  • A unit of fuel is consumed every 20 seconds. A completely fueled ram would last a total of 500 seconds (8.33 minutes).
  • While it is intended to be used against structures during sieges, Battering rams are extremely efficient mining engines. With each punch dealing massive pickaxe damage to a significant area around the punches, it can very easily break down any stone or metal resource node to which the ram has access to in a very short time. This includes Copper Deposits, Silver Veins, Giant Remains, and any giant rock found throughout the world.
  • Due to Stone Golems being very weak to pickaxe damage, the Battering ram can very easily obliterate them in one single hit (dealing 1200 damage)
  • Due to Gammeltrolls being weak to pickaxe damage, the Battering ram can very easily obliterate them in four hits or two battering ram attack cycles (dealing 3400 damage)
