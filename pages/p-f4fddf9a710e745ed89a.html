桦树生长在青青草原和北境平原，树皮白色并带黑斑，至少需要青铜斧才能砍伤。

桦树被毁后掉落木材和细木。两种木材的数量随机，但每棵总共固定掉落20份木材，树桩另掉2份木材。

可故意让倒下的山毛榉撞击桦树，累积足够伤害将其撞倒，再把树干滚下坡，反复撞向障碍物，直到碎成可用木材。巨魔也能破坏桦树。这些方法可让玩家在能制作青铜斧前获得细木。

桦树有4种变体：两种形状各有两种由生物群系决定的配色，配色不影响形状。形状1的大枝较少，最低大枝约离地8米；形状2在约4米高处分出一根粗枝，上方也有更多大枝。每种形状都有绿色叶片（非平原生物群系）和黄色叶片（仅平原）两种。颜色完全由生成地决定，形状随机。玩家种植的桦树始终长绿叶。

生命值

桦树生命值为80。砍倒后掉落生命值60的大原木，再裂成两段各有60生命值的小原木，树桩有80生命值。所有部分只受砍伐伤害，至少需要等级2的工具，例如青铜斧；树桩也能用同等级的铁镐等镐破坏。

种植

使用耕地耙消耗一份桦树种子可种植桦树幼苗。5,000至7,000秒后，长成任一种形状的桦树，随机缩放比例介于0.5与1之间。

北境平原自然生成的桦树叶片呈秋日般的黄色，与青青草原不同。但玩家用耕地耙在平原种植的桦树，仍像草原中的树一样长绿叶。因此，平原自然生成的淡黄色桦树数量有限，被砍后无法替代。

趣闻

  • 桦树树干的木材掉落由多次独立随机判定组成，每次有两个等概率结果，因此每棵树的细木数量服从二项分布，其中n=20、p=0.5。例如，获得至少k份细木的概率，可对介于0与20的整数k用公式∑i=0k(ni)pi(1−p)n−i计算。
  • 获得等级2的斧之前，也可采得桦木。将山毛榉砍倒，使其落在桦树枝上，再劈开倒木，可能造成足够伤害撞倒桦树。桦树原木可滚向其他桦树继续撞倒，再将倒木相互碰撞，直至碎成木材和细木。山毛榉倒向随机，只能略作引导，因此需要运气，也需要大量耐心。
    • 巨魔也能破坏桦树，尤其是持原木的巨魔，横扫可一次放倒数棵树。将巨魔引进密林是大量伐木的高效方法，但非常危险。

Birch is a type of tree that can be found in the Meadows and Plains biomes. It is characterized by its white bark with black spots. A Bronze Axe or better is required to damage it.

Birch trees produce Wood and Finewood when destroyed. The yield of each type of wood is random, however, a birch tree will always drop 20 pieces of wood total (plus 2 Wood from its stump).

It is possible to damage Birch trees by intentionally hitting them with falling Beech trees. If enough damage is done by falling trees, the Birch tree will be brought down. The trunk can then be rolled downslope (ideally) and repeatedly smashed into obstacles until it shatters into usable lumber. Birch trees can also be destroyed by a Troll. These methods allow players to acquire Finewood before being able to craft a Bronze Axe.

Birch trees come in 4 variations: two different shapes, each of which has two biome-dependent variations (which differ in color but not in shape). The two shapes can be distinguished by the number and position of their branches: Variation 1 has fewer large branches, the lowest of which is about 8 meters from the ground, while Variation 2 has a very thick branch which splits from the trunk at only about 4 meters from the ground, and more large branches up higher. Each of these shapes can come in two colors: with green leaves (in non-Plains Biomes) or with yellow leaves (in Plains only). Each Birch tree's color is dependent entirely on which biome it spawns in, but its shape is determined entirely randomly. Player-grown Birch trees always have green foliage.

Health

A birch tree has 80 health. When felled, it drops one large log with 60 health, which breaks into two small logs with 60 health each. The stump has 80 health. All parts can only be damaged with chop damage and require a tool of tier 2 or higher, such as the Bronze Axe; the stump can also be broken with a pickaxe of the same tier, such as the Iron Pickaxe.

Planting

Birch Saplings are planted with the Cultivator for one  Birch Seeds. A sapling grows into a birch of either shape after 5,000 to 7,000 seconds, at a random scale between 0.5 and 1.

Birch trees that grow naturally in the Plains biome have a different "fall-like", yellow color to its leaves compared to the variant of Birch found in the Meadows. However, should the player grow their own Birch trees in Plains by using the Cultivator, the player-cultivated trees will have green leaves like those found in Meadows. This makes the naturally grown Plains birch with the faded yellow hue finite and impossible to replace once chopped down.

Trivia

  • Since the wood drops from the Birch tree's trunk are composed of multiple individual random rolls, each with the same chance and with two possible outcomes, the drop rate of Finewood per tree thus follows a Binomial distribution with n = 20 and p = 0.5. For example, the chance of getting at least k Finewood from a tree (for integer k between 0 and 20) can be calculated with the formula ∑i=0k(ni)pi(1−p)n−i.
  • Birch wood can be harvested before getting a Tier 2 axe. If a Beech tree is felled and lands in the Birch's branches, splitting the tree will cause likely damage to the Birch sufficient to knock it down, creating a Birch log which may then be rolled into another Birch tree to cause it enough damage to fall, and the resulting Birch logs may be rolled into each other to cause damage until they break into Wood and Finewood. This requires a bit of luck, since Beech trees will fall in a random direction, though they can be guided slightly. (It also requires a great deal of patience).
    • Trolls can also destroy Birch trees, especially the log-wielding ones, whose sweep attack can bring down several trees at once. Luring a Troll into a dense grove is a quite effective, though very dangerous, way to cut down a large number of trees.
