摩根是灰烬之地中敏捷的敌对生物，像邪煞之树一样会打破伪装，从地下爬出。

袭击与能力

摩根战斗策略参见 敌对生物攻略 § 摩根.

摩根的所有攻击都会对友方生物造成友军伤害。许多招式伴随大幅移动，攻击判定会随自身移动，从而延伸范围。跳跃落地的范围攻击可招架，招架防御足够高时还会取消随后的冲刺。

咬伤

向前跳跃并咬向玩家，造成160点穿刺、100点3级砍伐及100点3级镐击伤害，形成4米、45度的水平判定范围。玩家在7米内时每4秒可用一次。

向右翻滚

摩根跳起，落地后向右翻滚，造成50点钝击、100点3级砍伐及100点3级镐击伤害，攻击自身周围3米圆形区域。玩家在12米内时每20秒[说明1 ]可用一次。

向左翻滚

摩根跳起，落地后向左翻滚，造成50点钝击、100点3级砍伐及100点3级镐击伤害，攻击自身周围3米圆形区域。玩家在12米内时每12秒[说明1 ]可用一次。

横扫

此攻击有四种变体，各自冷却4秒：右横扫、左横扫、右接左横扫、左接右横扫，造成160点钝击、100点3级砍伐及100点3级镐击伤害。玩家在5米内时可用，命中6米、60度的水平弧形范围。

身体砸击

直立并举起双臂后砸向地面，造成160点钝击伤害。玩家距离5–12米时，摩根每12秒可尝试使用，命中4米长、垂直45度的弧形范围。[说明2 ]

说明

  1. ↑1.01.1尚不清楚左翻滚为何比右翻滚冷却更短。
  2. ↑因动画控制器缺少转换，此攻击实际上无法使用，但摩根仍会尝试，导致短暂停顿，像要攻击一样，却没有后续动作。

Morgens are agile, aggressive foes found in the Ashlands. They rise from the ground after breaking out of their disguise, similar to Abominations.

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating Morgens, see Aggressive creature strategies § Morgen.

All Morgen attacks do friendly fire damage to allied creatures. Many of their attacks feature large movements — these movements shift the attack's hitbox with the user, extending their range. The impact of its jump attacks cause an area of effect hitbox which can be parried, with sufficient parry defense it will cancel the following dash.

Bite

Jumps forward and bites down on the player (160 Pierce, 100 Tier 3 Chop and 100 Tier 3 Pickaxe damage). This attack creates a horizontal 4-meter 45-degree hitbox. It can be used every 4 seconds within 7 meters of a player.

Roll (right)

Morgens jump up and roll to their right upon landing (50 Blunt, 100 Tier 3 Chop and 100 Tier 3 Pickaxe damage). This attack hits in a 3-meter circular area around the user and can be used every 20 seconds[Note 1] within 12 meters of a player.

Roll (left)

Morgens jump up and roll to their left upon landing (50 Blunt, 100 Tier 3 Chop and 100 Tier 3 Pickaxe damage). This attack hits in a 3-meter circular area around the user and can be used every 12 seconds[Note 1] within 12 meters of a player.

Swipe

This attack has four variations that each have their own 4-second cooldown: a right swipe, a left swipe, a right swipe followed by a left swipe, and a left swipe followed by a right swipe (160 Blunt, 100 Tier 3 Chop and 100 Tier 3 Pickaxe damage). These attacks can be used within 5 meters of a player, hitting in a 6-meter 60-degree horizontal arc.

Body slam

Stands upright and lift both arms up before slamming into the ground (160 Blunt damage). Morgens can attempt to use this attack every 12 seconds when within 5–12 meters of a player. It hits in a vertical arc 4 meters long, spanning 45 degrees.[Note 2]

Notes

  1. ↑ 1.0 1.1 It is unknown why the left roll has a shorter cooldown than the right roll.
  2. ↑ Due to a missing transition in the animator controller, this attack cannot actually be used, but Morgens will attempt to do so regardless. This causes them to pause briefly as if they are about to attack, but nothing will happen.

Notes

  • There is no difference in appearance between the 0-star, 1-star and 2-star Morgen.

Tips

  • Ever since the rework of the Forsaken Powers, it is ill-advised to engage a 2-star Morgen, even with maxed armor, maxed health, and a good shield, as its attacks will override a block, unless the player has a very high blocking skill. Parries are effective, but the damage from taking a hit, even with such drastic damage reduction, can nearly two-shot a player.
