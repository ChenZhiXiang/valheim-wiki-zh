废弃村落是在极北之地发现的大型聚落。

村庄中留有昔日居民的影子。靠近时，它们会像矮人一样说话。幽影从不攻击，其他生物也会忽略它们。

家具可破坏以获取先前生态区的各种资源。墙上的战利品被破坏时，有5.5%概率掉落。

位置

每个世界最多放置135处村庄地点（NorthVillage）和40座大厅（thehole01）。两者都仅出现在远离北方深境边缘、海拔至少两米的位置。大厅之间至少相距256米。每处村庄地点位于大厅或另一处村庄地点的85米范围内，同时距其他村庄地点至少70米，因此会围绕大厅形成聚落群。

布局

村庄包含15至20座建筑，分散在半径28至30米的圆形区域内，外围由八段边界结构围绕。建筑仅放置在坡度不超过25度的地面上，彼此不会重叠。

北方深境宝箱相当于范围30米的信标，可被愿望骨探测到。

大厅

大厅内有通往蜿蜒隧道的入口。

每座大厅有四个生成点，玩家进入60米范围时，每个生成一只影子，最高二星。

一楼有两个大厅木桶。

房屋

房屋内有50%概率出现北方深境 宝箱。

入口处有15%概率设置最多6个北方深境 木桶，其中每个各有50%概率出现。

木柱房屋

木柱房屋有50%概率出现隐藏在地板下、木楼梯正后方的北方深境 宝箱。

粮仓

粮仓最多包含九个粮仓木桶，各有50%概率出现。第一层有80%概率出现北方深境 宝箱。

塔盾

塔顶有50%概率出现北方深境 宝箱。

说明

北方深境村庄中，普通房屋和木柱房屋的数量是粮仓和塔楼的12.5倍。

Abandoned Villages are large settlements found in the Deep North.

Shadows of their past inhabitants reside in these villages. They speak when approached, akin to Dvergr. Shadows never attack, and other creatures ignore them.

The furniture can be destroyed for various resources from previous biomes. The trophies on the wall have a 5.5% chance to drop when destroyed.

Location

Up to 135 village sites (NorthVillage) and 40 Great Halls (thehole01) are placed in each world. Both are only placed away from the edges of the Deep North, at least two meters above sea level. Great Halls are at least 256 meters apart. Each village site is placed within 85 meters of a Great Hall or of another village site, and at least 70 meters from other village sites, so the sites form clusters around the Great Halls.

Layout

A village consists of 15 to 20 buildings, scattered within a circle of 28 to 30 meters radius and surrounded by a perimeter of eight sections. Buildings are only placed on ground with a slope of at most 25 degrees, and never overlap.

Deep North Chests act as beacons with a range of 30 meters, which the Wishbone can detect.

Great Hall

Great Halls contain entrances to the Winding Tunnels.

Each Great Hall has four spawn points that each spawn one Shadow, with up to two stars, when a player comes within 60 meters.

There are two Great Hall Barrels on the first floor.

House

Houses have a 50% chance for a Deep North Chest inside.

There is a 15% chance for up to 6 Deep North Barrel at the entrance, with a 50% chance each.

Stave House

Stave Houses have a 50% chance for a Deep North chest hidden under the floor, just behind the wood stairs.

Granary

A Granary contains up to nine Granary Barrels, with a 50% chance each. There is an 80% chance for a Deep North Chest on the first floor.

Tower

The Tower has a 50% chance for a Deep North Chest at the top.

Note

Houses and Stave Houses are 12.5 times more abundant in Deep North villages than Granaries and Towers.
