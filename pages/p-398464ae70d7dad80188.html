亚格鲁斯是第五位首领，是古老丑地精灵体，寄居在腐朽骸骨躯体中，仅剩头、双臂及躯干。头骨上的王冠暗示他曾是或仍是某种统治者。

召唤地点

亚格鲁斯的弃誓者祭坛位于北境平原。可在巨石阵建筑中找到导向符文石，在地图标记最近召唤位置。

在北境平原的弃誓者祭坛放置五个丑地精图腾即可召唤。与所有首领一样，材料足够就能反复召唤亚格鲁斯。

亚格鲁斯的导向符文石生成概率极低，很难寻找。通常直接找弃誓者祭坛比找指引符文石更容易。

袭击与能力

亚格鲁斯战斗策略参见 首领攻略 § 亚格鲁斯.

亚格鲁斯的环境光环会强制黑暗、移除降雨，因此不能利用雨造成的潮湿获得火焰抗性。

射线

亚格鲁斯每15秒可沿约40米直线喷火，玩家移动时会缓慢转向瞄准。吐息由20枚独立弹体组成，每枚造成40点火焰、20点闪电、50点1级砍伐及50点1级镐击伤害[说明1 ]，施加燃烧中及闪电状态。弹体速度每秒40米，飞行3秒后消失。玩家距离10–40米时可用，无散射，意味着完全精准。

陨石

亚格鲁斯每25秒可举起发红光的左拳，几秒后10块燃烧石头落下，在各落点产生半径5米的爆炸。命中者受到燃烧中状态及40点钝击、120点火焰、50点1级砍伐、50点1级镐击伤害。陨石从他后方30米、离地50米、半径15米的球形区域落下，初速度每秒30米，最多飞行8秒后消失。玩家在30米内时可用。

新星

亚格鲁斯举起发蓝光的右拳，再砸向地面，产生从拳头中心缓慢扩张至半径10米的球形爆炸。范围内目标受到燃烧中及闪电状态，承受65点火焰、65点闪电、100点2级砍伐及100点2级镐击伤害。蓝火短暂残留，持续造成100点火焰伤害。初始爆炸可闪避、格挡，残留火焰无法格挡。技能冷却20秒，仅在玩家距离10米内时可用。

遗弃者能力

将亚格鲁斯战利品挂在对应石碑上，即可获得他的放逐之力，为玩家及附近盟友提供以下效果，持续300秒：

  • 伤害修正: 抗性 对闪电
  • 耕作: +25
  • 造成伤害提高：钝击、挥砍、穿刺、伐木、采矿、火焰、冰霜、闪电、毒素及灵魂，均为+10%。

生物 生成

击败亚格鲁斯后，丑地精会在青青草原、黑暗林山及凛冽雪山夜间生成，只能为0星。

商人

击败亚格鲁斯后，可向霍尔德以1500枚钱币购买蛋，也可向沼泽女巫以160枚钱币购买平原草药师的采摘。

Yagluth is the fifth boss. He appears as an ancient Fuling spirit inhabiting a now decomposed skeletal body comprised of a head, two arms and a torso. The crown resting upon his skull implies he was once or still is a ruler of some sort.

Summoning

Yagluth's Forsaken Altar is located in the Plains. His location can be found when visiting Stonehenge structures, where a Vegvisir will reveal his nearest summoning location on the map.

He is summoned by placing five  Fuling Totems in his Forsaken Altar in the Plains. Like all bosses, Yagluth can be summoned as many times as desired, provided the required materials are met.

Yagluth´s Vegvisir has a very small chance of spawning, making it very hard to find. Oftentimes, it is easier to directly find the Forsaken Altar than the Vegvisir.

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating Yagluth, see Boss strategies § Yagluth.

Yagluth has an environment aura that forces darkness and removes raining, which means Wet from rain cannot be used as Fire resistance.

Beam

Yagluth is able to breathe fire in a roughly 40-meter straight line every 15 seconds, slowly rotating to aim at the player if they are moving. This breath is made up of 20 individual projectiles, each dealing 40 Fire, 20 Lightning, 50 Tier 1 Chop and 50 Tier 1 Pickaxe damage[Note 1] and inflicting the Burning and Lightning statuses. Each projectile travels at 40 meters per second and can travel for 3 seconds before disappearing. This ability can be used when a player is within 10–40 meters of Yagluth and does not have any spread: meaning he is perfectly accurate.

Meteors

Every 25 seconds, Yagluth can raise his left fist which glows red. A few seconds later, a volley of 10 burning stones will fall from above and create 5-meter radius explosions wherever they land. Anyone hit is inflicted with Burning and takes 40 Blunt, 120 Fire, 50 Tier 1 Chop and 50 Tier 1 Pickaxe damage. The meteors fall from the sky 30 meters behind Yagluth and 50 meters off of the ground in a 15-meter radius sphere. They fall at an initial velocity of 30 meters per second and can travel for up to 8 seconds before despawning. This attack can be used when a player is within 30 meters of Yagluth.

Nova

Yagluth raises his right fist which glows blue before slamming the ground, creating a 10-meter radius spherical explosion beginning at the center of his fist and slowly expanding out to the full radius. Anyone standing within the explosion will be inflicted with the Burning and Lightning status and take 65 Fire, 65 Lightning, 100 Tier 2 Chop and 100 Tier 2 Pickaxe damage. Blue fire will remain in the area for a short time, dealing 100 Fire damage over time. The initial explosion is both dodgeable and blockable, but the lingering fire is not blockable. This ability has a 20-second cooldown and can only be used when a player is within 10 meters of Yagluth.

Notes

Forsaken power

Mounting Yagluth’s trophy upon his respective stone will allow the player to claim his Forsaken Power. This ability will grant the player and nearby allies the following effects for 300 seconds:

  • Damage modifier: Resistant vs Lightning
  • Farming: +25
  • Damage dealt increase: Blunt, Slash, Pierce, Chop, Pickaxe, Fire, Frost, Lightning, Poison, Spirit: +10%

See Yagluth's power for more details.

Events

Defeating Yagluth affects the following events for that world:

  • Disables: "The horde is attacking."
  • Enables "What's up Gjall?!"
  • Enables "They sought you out."

Creature spawning

Defeating Yagluth unlocks Fulings to spawn at night in the Meadows, Black Forest, and Mountain. They can only have 0 stars.

Traders

Defeating Yagluth unlocks Eggs for sale from Haldor for 1500 Coins. It also unlocks the Grasslands Herbalist Harvest from The Bog Witch for 160 Coins.

Notes

  • At the time of release, Yagluth was the last boss to defeat.
    • His drop was called "Yagluth Thing," and its description said it was a placeholder for his actual drop. The Mistlands update added the Torn Spirit, which is used to craft the Wisp Fountain.
  • After Yagluth spawns in, he breaks through the ground slowly. The player can attack him before he actually begins moving or attacking, racking up some free damage before the boss fight actually begins.
  • In the in-game trophies menu, there is another description of Yagluth, "Sometimes, the eyes still move and the lips mutter silently..."
  • Yagluth's crown is made of Black metal swords.
