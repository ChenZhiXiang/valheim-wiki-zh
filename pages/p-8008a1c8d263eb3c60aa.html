Fenris blessing
The Fenris armor makes you quick on your feet so you can pass through fire, and your fists feel the power of the beast.
Effects
Fists +15
Damage Modifier
Resistant (0.5x) VS Fire

Fenris blessing is a status effects which provides the player with Fire resistance and increases the Fists skill by 15, but does not exceed the skill cap of 100.

Requirements

The effect is granted by wearing all three pieces of the Fenris armor set.
