Notes

  • Except for Rag Armor, every craftable piece of armor when upgraded increases armor value by +2 per level.
  • Every Cape is +1 per level, except for the Ashen Cape and the Moose Hide Cape which are +2 per level.
  • When an equipped armor piece is upgraded, it becomes unequipped. Ensure that the armor piece is re-equipped prior to venturing outside to avoid sudden death.
