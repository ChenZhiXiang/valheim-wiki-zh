冻僵是在进入凛冽雪山、与冰霜龙母战斗或霜龙事件期间施加的状态效果。

只要玩家未处于潮湿，通过以下任一来源获得抗性即可抵消：

  •  抗霜蜜酒
  •  芬里之甲
  •  狼皮护甲 宝箱
  •  狼毛披风
  •  平原牦牛披风
  •  轻羽斗篷
  •  灰烬刚鬣披风
  •  灰烬披风
  •  驼鹿皮披风
  •  召唤者披风
  •  火(效果) （例如来自 营火)
  •  庇护 (减少其为: 寒冷)

此效果停止所有自然生命值恢复，但不影响治疗蜜酒提供的生命值。

Freezing
You are freezing, no health regeneration and significantly lowered stamina.
Effects
Health regen -100%
Stamina regen -60%
Eitr regen -60%
1 damage/s
Damage Modifier
Resistant VS Fire

Freezing is a status effect applied to a character when going in the Mountain, while fighting Moder or during the Drake event.

It is counteracted by gaining resistance from any one of the following, as long as the player is not Wet:

  •  Frost Resistance Mead
  •  Fenris Coat
  •  Wolf armor chest
  •  Wolf Fur Cape
  •  Lox Cape
  •  Feather Cape
  •  Asksvin Cloak
  •  Ashen Cape
  •  Moose Hide Cape
  •  Cape of the Caller
  •  Fire (effect) (such as from a Campfire)
  •  Shelter (which reduces it to Cold)

The effect stops all natural health regeneration, but does not affect health gained from healing mead.
