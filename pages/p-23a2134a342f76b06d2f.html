冥火之光是一件饰品，可在工作台处使用银和冥火制作。首次获得冥火后解锁。

用途

该光源会清除自身周围15米半径内迷雾之地的雾。光球松散地跟随玩家，若玩家改变方向过快，可能短暂置身迷雾中。

它装备在饰品栏位，因此玩家可在使用光源的同时穿戴完整护甲套装。冥火之光与梅金吉奧德腰带和愿望骨共用栏位，无法同时装备。

说明

  • 装备时会显示“你释放了被束缚的精灵”提示。
  • 以下物品也能驱散迷雾： 冥火火炬、 迷雾行者，以及部分 矮人 建筑。
    • 使用迷雾行者驱散迷雾，可让玩家暂时佩戴梅金吉奧德腰带运输铁和铜碎屑。其驱雾范围远小于冥火之光，因此只有确实需要额外负重时才应采用此方法。

趣闻

  • 在1.0更新之前，冥火之光只清除10米半径内的迷雾。

The Wisplight is an accessory made of Silver and Wisp at a Workbench. It is unlocked after acquiring a Wisp for the first time.

Usage

The light clears the Mist of Mistlands in a 15 meter radius around itself. The orb loosely follows the player and can briefly leave the player in a fog if they change direction too quickly.

It is equipped in the accessory slot, and thus allows players to wear a full set of armor in addition to the light. The Wisplight conflicts with the Megingjord and the Wishbone, which occupy the same equipment slot.

Notes

  • When equipped, the message "You release the bound wisp" will appear.
  • The mist is also cleared by Wisp Torches, the Mistwalker, and some Dvergr structures.
    • Using the Mistwalker to clear the mist allows you to temporarily wear the Megingjord to transport Iron and Copper Scrap. Its range is much less than the Wisplight, so this tactic should not be used unless the extra carry weight is needed.

Trivia

  • Before the 1.0 update, the Wisplight used to clear the mist in only a 10 meter radius.
