升级

升级熔炉可解锁更多配方，并提高装备可升级的品质上限。每级还增加三米建造范围，最高38米。在附近建造以下设施，最多可升至等级七：

Forges are base structures used for building, crafting and repairing metal items. They require a roof and 70% cover to be usable. Repairing items does not cost any resources.

Upgrades

Upgrading a forge enables additional crafting recipes and higher upgrade levels for items. Each level also increases the build radius by three meters, up to a maximum of 38 meters. A forge can be upgraded up to level seven by building following structures near it:
