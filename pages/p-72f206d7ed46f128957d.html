弓术强化

  • 弓 +15

树根套装是一套泥泞沼泽级别的护甲，比青铜护甲和巨魔套装高一级。与另一套沼泽级别护甲铁制护甲相比，它提供的护甲值更少，但移动速度惩罚也更小。每个部件都会使穿戴者弱于火焰，但胸甲提供穿刺抗性，面具提供毒素抗性。其独特套装加成使穿戴者的弓技能提高15级(见:树根套装 )。

面具提供50%的毒素抗性，因此这套护甲在有许多毒素攻击敌人的泥泞沼泽中非常有用。此外，穿刺 伤害抗性适合对付北境平原和迷雾之地中的敌人，其中许多敌人具有穿刺攻击。不过，强烈建议使用大麦红酒来抵消任一护甲部件带来的火焰弱点。

这套护甲由三件组成，可在工作台处制作和升级。

Set Effect

Improved archery

  • Bows +15

The Root Set is a Swamp-tier armor. This set is in the tier above Bronze Armor and the Troll Set. It provides less armor points, but also a lower movement speed penalty compared to the other swamp-tier armor, Iron Armor. Each piece provides weakness to fire, but individual parts give resistance to pierce (in the harnesk) and poison (in the mask). Its unique set bonus increases the wearer's Bow skill by 15 levels (see: Improved archery).

The 50% poison resistance from the mask makes this armor very useful in the Swamp, where there are many poison-inflicting enemies. Furthermore, the resistance against pierce damage is useful against enemies in the Plains and Mistlands, many of which have pierce damage attacks. However, Barley Wine is strongly encouraged to counter the fire weakness received from any piece of the armor.
