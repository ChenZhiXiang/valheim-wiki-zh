获取方式

钥匙

  • 在冰霜铸造所中使用隐秘钥匙铸坯获取，需要5份液态冰霜，过程50秒。
  • 由巨人巫师掉落，概率10%。


生物 0星 1星 2星
巨人巫师 10% 10% 10%

铸坯

  • 制作地点 黑熔炉 （4级）需要：
    • 5 血金
    • 1 隐秘钥匙模具

模具

  • 由无眼者及没有灯笼的厄妖掉落。
  • 在蜿蜒隧道破坏厄妖之门，概率13.8%，或碎石堆，概率12.0%，可获取。每处掉落一至三件，每种最多一件。


生物 0星 1星 2星
无眼者 50% 50% 50%
厄妖 20% 20% 20%

用途

  • 开启莫克哈拉大门，进入墨尔克哈拉地下城。钥匙须在背包中，或对门使用。

The Intricate Key is used to open Gates of Mörkhalla in the Deep North.

Availability

Key

  • Obtained by using a Cast: Intricate Key in a Frost Foundry, requires 5 Liquid Frost. The process takes 50 seconds.
  • Drops from Hexen (10% drop chance).


Creature 0 Star 1 Star 2 Star
Hexen 10% 10% 10%

Cast

  • Crafted at a Black Forge (level 4) using:
    • 5 Bloodgold
    • 1 Mould: Intricate Key

Mould

  • Dropped by Eyeless Ones and by Elakings without a lantern.
  • Found by destroying Wardrobe Shafts (13.8% chance) and Piles of Rubble (12.0% chance) in the Winding Tunnels. Each drops one to three items, at most one of each kind.


Creature 0 Star 1 Star 2 Star
Eyeless One 50% 50% 50%
Elaking 20% 20% 20%

Usage

  • Opens the Gates of Mörkhalla and gives access to the Mörkhalla dungeon. The key must be in the player's inventory, or be used on the gate.
