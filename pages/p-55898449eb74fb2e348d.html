生命值决定可破坏物、生物、矿床、玩家、建筑和树木在死亡或被摧毁前能承受多少伤害。

生命值降至0会导致死亡。玩家会留下装有背包物品的墓碑，生物则会化为掉落物。

最大生命值

玩家初始生命值为25，可通过食用食物提高。

不同生物具有不同的基础生命值，并随生物等级及多人游戏缩放而提高。

可破坏物、矿床、建筑和树木的生命值因对象而异。

恢复

玩家

玩家通过食用食物获得生命恢复。每种食物提供1-6生命恢复，每10秒生效一次。

状态效果会进一步修正生命恢复。正面状态效果相加，负面状态效果相乘，因此同时存在正负效果时，应用顺序会影响结果。不过，处于部分负面状态时，无法获得休息好了和休息中。

  • 冻僵: 0x
  • 冷：0.5x。
  • 美味蜜酒：0.5x。
  • 潮湿：0.75x
  • 休息中: +200%
  • 休息好了: +50%
  • 长效治疗蜜酒: +25%

其他来源 :

  • 跑尸形态: 5 生命值每秒钟
  • 冻僵: -1 生命值每秒钟
  • 小份治疗蜜酒：10秒内恢复50生命值
  • 中份治疗蜜酒：10秒内恢复75生命值
  • 长效治疗蜜酒: +25% 生命值再生
  • 狂暴: +30% 生命值再生
  • 邪骨之怒: +20% 生命值再生
  • 森林之心 效果 :+25% 生命值再生
  • 铁制胸针 效果: 治愈50 生命值
  • 铁骨腕饰 效果: 治愈100 生命值
  • 火焰之心 效果: 治愈100 生命值

生物

生物每秒被动恢复最大生命值的0.028%，即6分钟恢复10%，60分钟恢复100%。

其他来源 :

  • 食用物品：鬓猪恢复5生命值，平原牦牛恢复10生命值，狼恢复20生命值。
  • 生命恢复来源 灰矮人萨满: 20 生命值
  • 矮人疗愈：在4秒内恢复400生命值。

建筑

可破坏物、矿床、建筑和树木不会自动恢复生命值。部分建筑可由玩家修理。

无敌

无敌会阻止攻击造成的所有伤害和击退。角色在以下情况下处于无敌状态：

  • 使用时 调试 飞行命令
  • 通过以下对象传送时 传送门
  • 进入或离开地下城时
  • 睡眠时
  • 过场动画期间
  • 何时 躲开 （仅对可闪避的攻击生效）
  • 未开启玩家对战时，仅对玩家攻击生效。
  • 死亡时

例如，即使被巨魔追赶，也可以安全离开巨魔洞穴。

Health determines how much damage destructibles, creatures, ore deposits, players, structures and trees can take before getting killed or destroyed.

Reaching 0 health results in death. Players leave behind a tombstone with their inventory contents whereas creatures burst into loot.

Max health

Players start with 25 health which can be increased by eating food.

Creatures have a different base health per creature that gets increased with Creature Level and multiplayer scaling.

Destructibles, ore deposits, structures and trees have different health per object.

Regeneration

Players

Players gain health regeneration by eating food. Each food gives 1-6 health regeneration that is applied every 10 seconds.

Health regeneration is further modified by Status effects. Positive status effects are additive, while negative status effects are multiplicative. This means the order of effects matters when there are both positive and negative effects. However, Rested and Resting cannot be acquired while having some of the negative status effects.

  • Freezing: 0x
  • Cold: 0.5x
  • Tasty Mead: 0.5x
  • Wet: 0.75x
  • Resting: +200%
  • Rested: +50%
  • Lingering Healing Mead: +25%

Other sources:

  • Corpse Run: 5 health per second
  • Freezing: -1 health per second
  • Minor Healing Mead: 50 health over 10 seconds
  • Medium Healing Mead: 75 health over 10 seconds
  • Lingering Healing Mead: +25% health regeneration
  • Berserk: +30% health regeneration
  • Vilebone Wrath: +20% health regeneration
  • Heart of the Forest effect: +25% health regeneration
  • Iron Brooch effect: heals 50 health
  • Bracelets of the Brave effect: heals 100 health
  • Brimstone effect: heals 100 health

Creatures

Creatures passively regenerate 0.028% of their max health per second (10% over 6 minutes, 100% over 60 minutes).

Other sources:

  • Consuming items: 5 health for Boar, 10 health for Lox and 20 health for Wolf.
  • Healing from Greydwarf Shaman: 20 health
  • Dvergr heal: Heals 400 health over 4 seconds.

Structures

Destructibles, ore deposits, structures and trees do not regenerate health. Some structures can be repaired by players.

Invincibility

Invincibility prevents all damage and knockback caused by attacks. Characters are invincible:

  • When using debug fly command
  • When teleporting through portals
  • When entering or exiting dungeons
  • When sleeping
  • During cutscenes
  • When dodging (only works when the attack can be dodged)
  • When PvP is not enabled (only works against players)
  • When dead

For example, Troll Caves can be safely exited even when chased by a Troll.
