英灵神殿体现多种季节活动，目前包括仲夏、万圣节及圣诞。期间限定物品仅可在该时段制作或购买。

主要装饰，部分提供少量舒适度。季节结束后已制物品保留，但毁坏后无法重制。

  •  仲夏柱
  •  仲夏之冠

  •  巫师帽
  •  芜菁鬼灯

There are a number of seasonal events reflected in Valheim. Currently, these include Midsummer, Halloween, Yule (Christmas). These events are marked by the availability of items that can only be crafted (or purchased) during this time.

These items are mainly cosmetic, but some provide a small amount of Comfort. Items created during these seasons remain once the season ends, but once destroyed cannot be remade.

Midsummer

The following items become available during Midsummer (1 June – 6 July)

  •  Maypole
  •  Midsummer Crown

Halloween

The following items become available during Halloween (1 October – 6 November)

  •  Pointy Hat
  •  Jack-O-Turnip

Yule (Christmas)

The following items become available at Christmas (1 December – 6 January)

  •  Yule Hat (purchasable from Haldor)
  •  Yule Tree
  •  Yule wreath
  •  Yule garland
  •  Mistletoe
  • Yuleklapp ( Yuleklapp (small),  Yuleklapp (medium) and  Yuleklapp (large))
