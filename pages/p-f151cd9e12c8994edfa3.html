寒冷
生命值和耐力值恢复速度变慢。
效果
生命值恢复 -50%
耐力恢复 -25%
埃达之能恢复 -25%

寒冷是在夜间，或同时处于庇护和冻僵时施加的状态效果。使生命值恢复降低50%，耐力和埃达之能恢复降低25%。

站在产生火焰效果的对象附近，例如营火或工作的冶炼炉，可抵消此效果；也可获得冰霜抗性，食用抗霜蜜酒可暂时获得，穿狼毛披风、狼皮护甲 宝箱或平原牦牛披风等保暖衣物则持续获得。

说明

  • 虽然燃烧中效果很“热”，却不会抵消寒冷。因此玩家可能同时燃烧和寒冷，尤其与狂信徒战斗时。

Cold
Lower health and stamina regeneration.
Effects
Health regen -50%
Stamina regen -25%
Eitr regen -25%

Cold is a status effect applied at night, or while Sheltered and Freezing. It reduces health regeneration by 50%, and stamina and eitr regeneration by 25%.

It is counteracted by standing near objects that produce the Fire effect (such as a Campfire or a working Smelter) or by gaining a resistance to frost: temporarily by consuming a Frost Resistance Mead or permanently by wearing warm clothes, such as the Wolf Fur Cape, Wolf armor chest or Lox Cape.

Notes

  • Despite the Burning effect being "hot", it will not negate the Cold effect. This allows for the player to both be on fire and cold at the same time, especially when fighting Cultists.
