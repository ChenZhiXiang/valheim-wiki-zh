The Trailership is a large boat. Currently, it can only be obtained through commands.

Trivia

  • As of March 7th, 2021, the Trailer Ship has no ability to sail, and can only act as a storage and decoration piece.
  • When attempting to repair it at full health, it says, "Longship does not need repairs." This implies it was intended to be the longship in the game now.
