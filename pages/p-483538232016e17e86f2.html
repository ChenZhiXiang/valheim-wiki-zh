冰霜龙母是第四位首领，外形为巨大的冰幼龙，拥有黑鳞、白色毛鬃及破损双翼。多块符文石称她是所有霜龙的母亲。

召唤地点

冰幼龙母的弃誓者祭坛位于凛冽雪山。特定废墟建筑中通常在顶部有导向符文石，可在地图上标记最近的召唤位置。

在凛冽雪山的弃誓者祭坛放置三枚龙蛋即可召唤。与其他首领一样，只要材料足够，就能反复召唤冰幼龙母。

龙蛋重量高达200，因此向霍尔德购买并穿戴梅金吉奧德腰带会有帮助，可增加150点负重上限。

袭击与能力

冰幼龙母战斗策略参见 首领攻略 § 冰霜龙母.

冰幼龙母会飞行，也会降落，在两种状态间交替。她带有环境光环，即使在其他生物群系也会强制产生冻僵效果。

咬伤

在地面时可撕咬，造成120点穿刺、1000点3级砍伐及1000点3级镐击伤害。攻击冷却30秒，仅在玩家距离7米内时使用，命中8米、20度的水平弧形范围。

爪击

在地面时，冰幼龙母可用任一前爪横扫，造成110点劈砍、1000点3级砍伐及1000点3级镐击伤害。每只爪各冷却30秒，玩家在10米内时可用，命中12米、50度的水平弧形范围。

冰刺齐射

在空中时，每8秒可发射16枚冰刺。每枚速度每秒25米，散射范围为瞄准中心外13–20度，造成30点穿刺、200点冰霜、200点3级砍伐及200点3级镐击伤害。击中地面后形成约与玩家等高的晶体，30秒后碎裂。每块晶体有10点生命，可破坏。玩家距离5–25米时可用。

寒冰吐息

在地面时可喷出30米长、略带10度弧度的冰霜射线，最多造成200点冰霜、200点3级砍伐及200点3级镐击伤害，每8秒可用一次，施放时会尝试追踪玩家。玩家距离5–20米时可用，距离从攻击判定区域中心计算，该中心距头部约5米，因此贴近以阻止攻击较难，但仍可做到。

遗弃者能力

将冰幼龙母战利品挂在对应石碑上，即可获得她的放逐之力，为玩家及附近盟友提供以下效果，持续300秒：

  • 航行时始终顺风
  • 伤害修正: 抗性 对冰霜
  • 移动速度: +10%

更多详情见冰霜龙母能力。

事件

击败冰幼龙母会影响该世界的以下事件：

  • 禁用：“寒风从山上吹来。”
  • 启用：“大军来袭！”（若尚未击败亚格鲁斯）。

商人

击败冰霜龙母后，可从沼泽女巫处解锁镰刀把、毒蕈、香料包和山顶胡椒粉，并从霍尔德处解锁加宽口袋。

说明

  • 冰幼龙母是具有典型双足飞龙外形的龙。她的蛋称为龙蛋，眼泪称为龙泪，也能说明这一点。
  • 召唤后，冰幼龙母的光环会暂停或终止正在发生的暴风雪。
  • 游戏战利品菜单中还有一段描述：“布满古老伤疤的头颅，是无数战士生前最后看到的景象。”
  • “Moder”在北欧语言中意为“母亲”。
  • “Móðr”在古诺尔斯语中意为“愤怒、悲痛”。
  • 冰幼龙母与亚当·内维尔2011年英国小说林中小屋及其2017年同名改编电影中的洛基之女巨人同名。
  • 游戏文件将“Moder”称为“dragonqueen”。

Moder is the fourth boss. She appears as a massive frost dragon with black scales, a white fur mane and tattered wings. According to numerous Runestones, she is the mother of all Drakes.

Summoning

Moder's Forsaken Altar is located in the Mountains. Her location can be found when visiting specific ruined structures, typically at the top, where a Vegvisir will reveal her nearest summoning location on the map.

She is summoned by placing three  Dragon Eggs in her Forsaken Altar in the Mountains. Like all bosses, Moder can be summoned as many times as desired, provided the required materials are met.

Considering the extensive weight of dragon eggs (200), purchasing and wearing a Megingjord from Haldor will help, as it increases one's carry weight by 150.

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating Moder, see Boss strategies § Moder.

Moder has the ability to fly but will also choose to land, alternating between the two. She is accompanied by an environmental aura that forces Freezing Effect even in other biomes.

Bite

While grounded, she can bite to deal 120 Pierce, 1000 Tier 3 Chop and 1000 Tier 3 Pickaxe damage. This attack has a 30-second cooldown and can only be used when a player is within 7 meters. This hits in an 8-meter 20-degree horizontal arc.

Claw

While grounded, Moder can perform a swipe attack with either claw which deals 110 Slash, 1000 Tier 3 Chop and 1000 Tier 3 Pickaxe damage. This ability is on a 30-second cooldown for each claw, and she must be within 10 meters of a player to use it. The claws hit in a horizontal arc 12 meters in length and 50 degrees in angle.

Ice Barrage

When airborne, every 8 seconds she can fire a barrage of 16 icicles - each projectile travels at 25 meters per second and spreads within 13–20 degrees from the center of her aim dealing 30 Pierce, 200 Frost, 200 Tier 3 Chop and 200 Tier 3 Pickaxe damage. Upon impact with the ground, a crystal forms about as tall as the player and will fall apart 30 seconds later. Each crystal has 10 health and can be destroyed. This attack can be used when a player is within 5–25 meters.

Cold breath

While grounded, she can let forth an icy beam in a 30-meter line which arcs very slightly by 10 degrees. It deals up to 200 Frost, 200 Tier 3 Chop and 200 Tier 3 Pickaxe damage and can be used every 8 seconds. She attempts to track the player while using this attack. This ability can be used when a player is within 5–20 meters; this distance is measured from the center of her hitbox, which happens to be roughly 5 meters from her head making it difficult - but still possible - to disable the attack by standing close.

Forsaken power

Mounting Moder’s trophy upon her respective stone will allow the player to claim her Forsaken Power. This ability will grant the player and nearby allies the following effects for 300 seconds:

  • Max carry weight: +300
  • Always tailwind while sailing
  • Damage modifier: Resistant vs Frost
  • Movement Speed: +10%

See Moder's power for more details.

Events

Defeating Moder affects the following events for that world:

  • Disables: "A cold wind blows from the mountains."
  • Enables: "The horde is attacking!" (if Yagluth has not been defeated)

Traders

Defeating Moder unlocks the Scythe Handle, Toadstool, Fragrant Bundle and the Mountain Peak Pepper Powder from The Bog Witch. Along with Wider Pockets from Haldor.

Notes

  • Moder is a dragon having the typical appearance of a Wyvern, this is apparent due to both the eggs being dragon eggs, and her tears being dragon tears.
  • Once summoned, Moder's aura will pause or stop ongoing snowstorms.
  • In the in-game trophies menu, there is another description of Moder, "Scored with ancient scars, this head was the last sight of countless warriors."
  • "Moder" translates to "Mother" in Nordic languages.
  • "Móðr" translates to "anger, grief" in Old Norse.
  • Moder shares a name with the jötunn daughter of Loki in Adam Nevill's 2011 British novel The Ritual and its 2017 film adaptation of the same name.
  • In the game files, "Moder" is known as "dragonqueen".
