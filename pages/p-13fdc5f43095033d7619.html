被囚禁的矮人是墨尔克哈拉中的中立极北之地生物。

与其他矮人一样，不主动攻击玩家。玩家伤害其中一名时，它及20米内其他中立生物，包括其他矮人，都变敌对。它们与包含巨人战士和巨人巫师的北方深境阵营敌对，双方互战。

袭击与能力

被囚矮人攻击模式类似普通矮人刺客和灰烬之地矮人，但生命值和伤害更高。

  • 开枪：用强弩发射投射物，造成250穿刺伤害，速度40米/秒。距目标1–15米时，每六秒可用一次。
  • 近战：在2.5米、90度弧形内造成140钝击伤害。距目标0.5–2.5米时，每四秒可用一次。

Imprisoned Dvergr are neutral Deep North creatures found in Mörkhalla.

Like other Dvergr, they don't attack players on their own. When a player damages one, it and all other neutral creatures within 20 meters, including other Dvergr, become hostile. They are enemies of the Deep North faction, which includes Krigen and Hexen, and the two sides fight each other.

Attacks and abilities

Imprisoned Dvergr have similar attack patterns as regular Dvergr Rogue and Ashlands Dvergr but their health and damage are superior.

  • Shoot: a projectile from their Arbalest dealing 250 Pierce damage. The bolt travels at 40 meters per second. This attack can be used every six seconds when within 1–15 meters of a target.
  • Melee: 140 Blunt damage in a 2.5-meter, 90-degree arc. This attack can be used every four seconds when within 0.5–2.5 meters of a target.
