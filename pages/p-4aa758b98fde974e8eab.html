获取方式

在以下兴趣点战利品箱中，与黑金属防护神像一起出现：

  • 凛冽雪山：废弃小屋、山地塔楼、哀啸之穴。
  • 北境平原：平原宝箱、封印之塔。

用途

每次在潜能熔炉尝试消耗一座神像，不需其他材料。65%概率使品质升一级，可超过4级；失败则摧毁物品，返还部分材料。

The Black Metal Battle Idol is an idol used to upgrade items at the Forge of Potential.

Availability

It is found in loot chests at these points of interest, together with the Black Metal Protection Idol:

  • Mountain: Abandoned Cabin, Mountain Tower, Howling Cavern.
  • Plains: Plains chest, Sealed Tower.

Usage

Each attempt at the Forge of Potential consumes one idol and costs no other materials. It has a 65% chance to raise the item's quality by one level, even above level 4; otherwise the item is destroyed and part of its materials are returned.

The Black Metal Battle Idol upgrades the following 12 items:

Weapons

  •  Black Metal Atgeir
  •  Black Metal Axe
  •  Black Metal Battleaxe
  •  Black Metal Knife
  •  Black Metal Sword
  •  Frostfire Axe
  •  Jotun Bane
  •  Nord Axe
  •  Porcupine
  •  Skoll and Hati
  •  Vilebone Maulclaws

Tools

  •  Black Metal Pickaxe

Notes

  • The Nord Axe and Frostfire Axe use this idol, while the other Nord and Frostfire weapons use the Bloodgold Battle Idol.
