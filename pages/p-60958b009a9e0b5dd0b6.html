生成逻辑

区域

地图划分为64x64的方格，0,0位于第一个区域的中心。

有玩家的区域每4秒处理一次生物生成器。

生物生成点

若距上次生成尝试已过去足够时间，生成器就会尝试生成生物。

尝试次数根据经过的时间计算，上限为生物数量上限。

例如，鹿的生成间隔为100秒。经过250秒会尝试生成2次；经过500秒也只尝试4次，因为上限为4。

生成尝试

以下情况下生成尝试失败：

  • 随机判定失败
  • 尚未击杀所需首领。
  • 天气不对
  • 昼夜时段不符。
  • 已装入区域中已有过多同类生物，达到生物数量上限。
  • 尝试20次后仍未找到有效生成位置。
  • 有效生成位置附近已有同类生物，达到抑制限制。

有效生成位置

从区域内随机选择一名玩家，再在距其40 - 80米处随机选择生成位置。以下情况下位置无效：

  • 位置的生态区不符
  • 位置所在区域包含多个生态区，而生成要求完整生态区。
  • 上方被建筑覆盖
  • 被挡住以 玩家基地
  • 错误的高度
  • 地形坡度不符，默认最大坡度为35度。
  • 水量不对
  • 40米内有玩家。
  • 位置不在森林内，而生成要求森林。森林在世界生成时确定，改造地形或种植树木的数量不会影响它。

注意：使用耕地耙、锄头或镐改造地形不会直接阻止生成，但会改变地形高度和坡度。

每组数量

生物数量从群体规模范围中随机选择，并受生物数量上限限制。

只有一只生物时，敌人直接在有效生成位置出现。

多于一只生物时，游戏会为每只生物尝试寻找两次生成位置，在初始位置周围的圆形范围，即群体半径内随机选择，并按相同规则检查有效性。

生成

允许等级提升时，每只生成的生物有10%概率提升等级，其中1星概率为9%，2星概率为1%。部分生物必须距世界中心足够远才能提升等级。

若生成器仅在夜间激活，则生成的生物会设为白天消失，只要其区域仍然启用。

飞行敌人会根据种类在离地一定高度处出现。不过，实际生成位置仍位于地面，可被玩家基地阻止。

示例

例如，蛟龙有两个生成器，仅当区域位于海洋生态区时生效。第一个只在夜间生效，第二个只在暴风雨中生效。因此，夜间暴风雨中两者同时激活，是寻找蛟龙的最佳时机。

蛟龙生成器每1000秒工作一次，成功概率仅5%。若一直在同一地点等待，可能需要数小时才生成蛟龙。若持续进入新区域，则可根据速度轻易做到每12-16秒获得一次生成尝试，但前提是1000秒内未曾到访该区域。

蛟龙最远可在80米外生成，因此最好待在海洋中央。若靠近其他生态区，生成可能因生态区不符而失败。蛟龙永远不会生成有星级的变种，因为其最高等级为1。

因暴风雨生成的蛟龙，即使在夜间生成，也不会在清晨消失，但无法判断它来自哪个生成器。

一条蛟龙会抑制50米内其他蛟龙的生成。由于最远可在80米外生成，仍可能出现更多蛟龙，但概率大幅降低。蛟龙可在50米内察觉玩家，因此大多数时候不会刚生成就发起攻击。

Spawning logic

Zones

The map is split up into 64x64 grid squares with 0,0 being in the center of the first zone.

Every 4 seconds, zones with a player process their spawners.

Spawners

Each spawner tries spawning if enough time has passed since its previous spawning attempt.

Amount of tries is calculated from the elapsed time and capped at creature limit.

For example, Deer have a spawn interval of 100 seconds. After 250 seconds, 2 spawn attempts would be made. After 500 seconds, only 4 spawn attempts would be made because the limit is 4.

Spawn attempts

A spawn attempt fails if:

  • The random roll fails
  • Required boss has not been killed
  • Wrong weather
  • Wrong time of the day
  • Too many same creatures already exist in loaded zones (creature limit)
  • Valid spawn position is not found after 20 attempts
  • There is already the same creature too close to the valid spawn position (suppression limit).

Valid spawn position

A spawn position is randomly selected between 40 - 80 meters of a random player in the zone. Spawn position is not valid if:

  • Position has a wrong biome
  • The zone of the position has multiple biomes (if a full biome is required)
  • Covered by a structure
  • Blocked by player base
  • Wrong altitude
  • Wrong terrain angle (max tilt 35 degrees by default)
  • Wrong amount of water
  • A player exists within 40 meters
  • Position is not inside a forest (if a forest is required). Forests are determined on world generation, terraforming or the amount of planted trees have no effect.

Note: Terraforming with a Cultivator, Hoe or Pickaxes does not directly block spawning but affects terrain altitude and angle.

Group size

The amount of creatures is randomly selected from the group size. The amount is capped at creature limit.

With only one creature, the enemy is spawned at the valid spawn position.

With more than one creature, the game tries finding a spawn position two times for each creature. The spawn position is randomly selected from a circle around the initial spawn position (group radius). Same rules apply for determining the validity.

Spawning

Each spawned creature has 10% chance to level up (9% for 1 star, 1% for 2 stars), if leveling up is allowed. For some creatures, the leveling up requires enough distance from the world center.

If the spawner is only active during the night, then spawned creatures are set to despawn during the daytime (as long as their zone is active).

Flying enemies are spawned off the ground depending on the creature. The actual spawn position is still on the ground and can be blocked by a player base.

Example

For example Serpents have two spawners which only work when zone is located in Ocean biome. The first spawner works only during the night while the second spawner only works during storms. This means both spawners are active during night time storms giving the best chance for finding a Serpent.

Serpent spawners work every 1000 seconds and only have a 5% chance to work. If you just wait on a spot then it can take hours for a Serpent to spawn. But if you keep moving to new zones then you can easily get a spawn attempt every 12-16 seconds (depending on the speed). However this requires that you have not visited the area for 1000 seconds.

Serpents can spawn up to 80 meters away so it is good to be in a middle of the ocean. If you are near other biomes then the spawn can fail because of wrong biome. Serpents never spawn as starred variant because the max level is 1.

Serpents that spawn because of storm will not despawn on morning even when spawned during the night (but no way to tell which spawner spawned it).

A Serpent suppresses other Serpent spawns within 50 meters. It is still possible to spawn more Serpents (since they can spawn up to 80 meters away) but the chances are drastically reduced. Serpents detect you up to 50 meters away so most of the time they will not aggro right away.
