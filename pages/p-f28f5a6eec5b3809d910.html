铁矿脉是不自然生成的资源节点，需青铜镐或更好工具采集。

获取方式

仅可通过控制台命令获取。

掉落物

铁矿脉24节点，每个可能掉落：

数额 物品 机会
1-2 铁矿 33.3%
1-2 石头 66.7%

铁矿脉平均产12份铁矿石及24份石头。

说明

  • 金属矿床不会重新生成。
  • 与其他金属一样，铁矿石无法传送。
  • 节点尺寸固定，放在地形上。降低地形时不会碎裂，而会下落，直到遇到新地面。

An Iron Vein is a resource node that are not naturally found in game and require a Bronze Pickaxe or better to harvest them.

Availability

Only available via console commands.

Drops

An iron vein has 24 nodes, each of which may drop:

Amount Item Chance
1-2 Iron Ore 33.3%
1-2 Stone 66.7%

The average iron vein will yield 12 iron ore and 24 stone.

Notes

  • Metal deposits do not respawn.
  • Like other metals, Iron Ore cannot be teleported.
  • Node sizes are set and rest on top of terrain. If the terrain is lowered they will not crumble, they will fall until they find new ground to rest on.
