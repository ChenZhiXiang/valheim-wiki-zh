腐臭遗骸是诡秘墓穴中的敌对生物。如名称所示，它们是死后复生的人类骸骨。腐臭遗骸也比普通骷髅怪略大且更强，攻击会施加毒素。

横向挥动钉锤，覆盖2.2米、70度的水平弧形范围，造成20点钝击及30点毒素伤害。玩家在2.2米内时每3秒可用一次。

Rancid remains are aggressive creatures found in Burial Chambers. They are, as their name suggests, human skeletal remains which have since been resurrected from the dead. Rancid remains are also slightly larger and stronger than regular Skeletons. Their hits inflict poison.

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating Rancid remains, see Aggressive creature strategies § Rancid remains.

Mace

Swings their mace sideways in a 2.2-meter 70-degree horizontal arc (20 Blunt and 30 Poison damage). This attack can be used every 3 seconds when within 2.2 meters of a player.

Notes

  • Rancid remains carry a green flaming Iron Mace.
  • The Rancid remains trophy used to be required to craft the Dead Raiser, but was likely changed due to how difficult acquiring one of these trophies can be.
