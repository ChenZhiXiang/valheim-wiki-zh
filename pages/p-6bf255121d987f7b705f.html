龙蛋是凛冽雪山的霜龙巢穴中找到的物品，比看起来重得多，并发出微弱嗡鸣。

用途

主条目： 弃誓者祭坛

召唤第四位首领冰霜龙母需要三枚龙蛋。

图片库

说明

  • 龙蛋没有传送门 石头就无法传送，重量为200.0单位，是最重的物品之一。
  • 每枚蛋最多有3只霜龙守护，不会重新生成。
  • 龙蛋会在以下时间后重新生成： 8小时.

提示

  • 无需远距离或用船搬运龙蛋，可直接在冰幼龙母祭坛所在的同片凛冽雪山收集。
    • 唯一例外是数量不足3只，但很少见。

Dragon eggs are items found in Drake Nests in the Mountains. They are far heavier than they look, with a faint humming sound.

Contents

  • 1 Usage
  • 2 Gallery
  • 3 Notes

Usage

Main article: Forsaken Altars

A total of three Dragon eggs are required in order to summon the fourth boss Moder.

Gallery

Notes

  • Dragon eggs cannot be teleported without a Portal stone, and are one of the heaviest items overall at 200.0 units.
  • Up to 3 Drakes protect each egg. They do not respawn.
  • Dragon eggs will respawn after 8h.

Tips

  • It is not needed to haul the eggs long distances or via ship, they can simply be collected in the same Mountain biome as the Moder altar.
    • The only exception is if there are fewer than 3, although that is rather uncommon.
