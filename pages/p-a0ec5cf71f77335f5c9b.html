黑熔炉是用于制作和修复后期装备的基础设施，在迷雾之地阶段解锁。使用时需要屋顶和70%的遮蔽。修复物品不消耗材料。

Black Forges are base structures used for crafting and repairing late-game gear, becoming available in Mistlands. They require a roof and 70% cover to be usable. Repairing items does not cost any resources.
