生物和玩家在步行、奔跑、游泳或飞行时具有不同的移动速度。

击退会造成10米/秒的快速位移，覆盖击退方向上已有的移动。

冰霜效果最多使移动速度降低90%，该效果逐渐消退，平均降速65%。

例如，20%的装备速度惩罚会使步行速度降至3.2米/秒，奔跑速度降至4.9米/秒。

在水中跑动

在深度至少0.4米的浅水中奔跑时，移动速度按以下公式降低：

speed=walkSpeed+0.33⋅(runSpeed−walkSpeed)

换言之，奔跑速度与步行速度的差值减少67%。奔跑技能等级0且没有重甲惩罚时，速度为5米/秒。

转速

玩家每秒以300度的速度转向镜头朝向。

游泳时，转向速度降至每秒100度。

加速

玩家每个更新步增加0.4米/秒的速度。每个更新步为0.02秒，移动速度可按以下公式计算：

speed=maxSpeed⋅20⋅t

例如，达到最大步行速度需要0.2秒，达到最大奔跑速度约需要0.4秒。

游泳时，玩家每个更新步增加当前速度与目标速度差值的5%。游泳速度可按以下公式计算：

swimSpeed=maxSpeed*(1−0.9550⋅t)

例如，0.5秒后达到游泳速度的72%，1秒后达到92%，2秒后达到99%。

生物

生物在步行、奔跑、游泳和飞行时具有不同速度。进入警戒后，生物会快速奔跑或飞行。移动中的生物也会产生噪声，数值与玩家相应移动方式相同。

生物的默认顺序大致遵循预期的生态区推进顺序，可按首列排序恢复此顺序。

Creatures and players have a different movement speeds depending on whether they are walking, running, swimming, or flying.

Knockback causes rapid movement of 10 m/s, overwriting any existing movement on the knockback direction.

Frost Effect slows movement speed by up to 90%, which gradually wears off (on average 65% reduction).

Running in water

Running in shallow water (at least 0.4 meters depth) reduces movement speed with the following formula:

speed=walkSpeed+0.33⋅(runSpeed−walkSpeed)

In other terms, running speed is reduced by 67% towards walk speed, i.e. it's 5 m/s for 0 running skill level and no penalty from heavy armor.

Turn speed

Players turn towards the camera 300 degrees per second.

While swimming, the turn speed is reduced to 100 degrees per second.

Acceleration

Players accelerate 0.4 m/s every tick. With a tick speed of 0.02 seconds, the movement speed can be calculated with the following formula:

speed=maxSpeed⋅20⋅t

For example: reaching maximum walking speed takes 0.2 seconds, and reaching maximum running speed takes about 0.4 seconds.

While swimming, players accelerate 5% of the missing speed every tick. The swimming speed can be calculated with following formula:

swimSpeed=maxSpeed*(1−0.9550⋅t)

For example: it would take 0.5 seconds to reach 72% swim speed, 1 second to reach 92% swim speed, and 2 seconds to reach 99% swim speed.

Creatures

Creatures have different speeds for walking, running, swimming, and flying. Creatures run and fly fast when alerted. Creatures also create noise when moving (same values as with the player movement).

The default ordering of creatures is roughly in the intended biome progression and can be re-sorted back to this using the first column.
