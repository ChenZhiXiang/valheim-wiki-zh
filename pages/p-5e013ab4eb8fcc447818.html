棍棒（技能）
说明
确定 伤害 与 耐力 消耗，由棍棒技能决定。

棍棒是与棍棒武器类别相关的技能。

效果

棍棒技能提高，攻击耐力消耗最多降低至-33%，随机伤害和击退倍率从等级0的25–55%提高至等级100的85–100%。

伤害缩放详情请参阅伤害机制# 技能因素。

升级

命中生物时，每次单手攻击获得1.5经验值，每次双手攻击获得1xp。

Clubs (skill)
Description
Determines damage and stamina cost with Clubs (skill).

Clubs is a skill related to Club weapons class.

Effects

Higher skill level in clubs reduces the stamina cost to attack down to -33% and uses a random damage and knockback multiplier ranging from 25–55% at skill level 0 to 85–100% at skill level 100.

For the details of damage scaling, see Damage mechanics#Skill factor.

Leveling up

The player gains 1.5xp per one-handed attack and 1xp per two-handed attack when hitting a creature.
