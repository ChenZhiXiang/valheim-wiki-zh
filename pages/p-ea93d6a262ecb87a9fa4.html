工具扩展玩家改造周围环境的能力，如地形改造，或协助特定任务，如饮酒、采矿业。

冒险

  • 大酒杯——用于快捷饮用蜜酒。
  • 庆祝号角——与大酒杯相同。
  • 矮人酒杯——也与大酒杯相同。
  • 火把——实用光源，也可造成火焰伤害，但随使用损耗，无法修理。
  • 矮人灯笼——提供光照，不损耗，但基本无法作武器。

建材

  • 工具锤——用于放置、修理物体。
  • 锄头——用于平整或抬高地形，制作路径、道路。
  • 餐盘——用于放置食物及宴席。

耕作

  • 耕地耙——用于种作物及树。
  • 屠夫刀——可屠宰驯服动物，不使其惊恐。
  • 镰刀——同时收割多株作物。

钓鱼

  • 鱼竿——预期的捕鱼方式。
  • 鱼饵——使用鱼竿所必需。

伐木（斧)

  • 石斧——基础斧，仅能砍山毛榉、冷杉及松树树。
  • 燧石斧——砍伐种类与石斧相同，但伤害更高、更耐用。
  • 青铜斧——除此前斧可砍的树外，还可砍古老的、桦树及橡树。
  • 战斧——双手斧，伐木稍弱于青铜斧。
  • 铁斧——砍伐种类与青铜斧相同，但伤害更高、更耐用。
  • 水晶战斧——双手斧，伐木稍弱于铁斧。
  • 黑金属斧——除此前斧可砍的树外，还可砍世界树。
  • 巨人之戮——砍伐种类与黑金属斧相同，但伤害更高、更耐用。

采矿(镐)

  • 鹿角镐——可开采石头、铜、锡、泥泞的废料堆及深渊藤壶。
  • 青铜镐——开采种类与鹿角镐相同，但伤害更高、更耐用。
  • 铁镐——除此前镐可采的资源外，还可采黑曜石、银及发光金属。
  • 黑金属镐——除此前镐可采的资源外，还可采黑大理石、软组织及焰金属。

Tools are items used to extend the player's ability to modify features of the surrounding environment (e.g. terraforming) or help them accomplish a particular task (e.g. drinking, mining).

Adventuring

  •  Tankard – Is used as a shortcut for consuming mead.
  •  Horn of Celebration – Is the same as the Tankard.
  •  Dvergr Tankard – Is also the same as the Tankard.
  •  Torch – Is a useful light source that can also deal fire damage, but degrades over time and cannot be repaired.
  •  Dvergr Lantern – Can provide light and does not degrade, but cannot really be used as a weapon.

Building

  •  Hammer – Is used for placing and repairing objects.
  •  Hoe – Can be used to flatten/raise terrain and create paths/roads.
  •  Serving Tray – Can be used to place Food and Feast.

Farming

  •  Cultivator – Is used for planting crops and trees.
  •  Butcher Knife – Allows the player to butcher tamed animals without frightening them.
  •  Scythe – Used to harvest multiple crops at once.

Fishing

  •  Fishing Rod – Is the intended way of obtaining fish.
  •  Fishing Bait – Is required for using the fishing rod.

Logging (Axes)

  •  Stone Axe – Is a basic axe only capable of chopping down Beech, Fir and Pine trees.
  •  Flint Axe – Chops the same trees as Stone Axe, but does more damage and is more durable.
  •  Bronze Axe – Can chop down Ancient, Birch, and Oak trees, in addition to what previous axes can chop.
  •  Battleaxe – Is a two-handed axe that is slightly less powerful at chopping trees than Bronze Axe
  •  Iron Axe – Chops the same trees as Bronze Axe, but does more damage and is more durable.
  •  Crystal Battleaxe – Is a two-handed axe that is slightly less powerful at chopping trees than Iron Axe.
  •  Black Metal Axe – Can chop Yggdrasil trees, in addition to what previous axes can chop.
  •  Jotun Bane – Chops the same trees as Black Metal Axe, but does more damage and is more durable.

Mining (Pickaxes)

  •  Antler Pickaxe – Can mine Stone, Copper, Tin, Muddy Scrap Pile, Abyssal Barnacle
  •  Bronze Pickaxe – Can mine the same things as Antler Pickaxe, but does more damage and is more durable.
  •  Iron Pickaxe – Can mine Obsidian, Silver, Glowing metal, in addition to what the previous pickaxes can.
  •  Black Metal Pickaxe – Can mine Black Marble, Soft Tissue and Flametal in addition to what the previous pickaxes can.
