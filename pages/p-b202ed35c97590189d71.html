超重
你负重太多并无法跑步。
效果
移动速度：降至2m/s
耐力恢复: -100%
无法奔跑、跳跃、闪避或格挡

超重是角色携带多 重量最大负重时施加的状态效果。穿梅金吉奧德腰带、获得跑尸形态、饮用巨魔之忍蜜酒或冰霜龙母的放逐之力可提高负重上限。

超重玩家耐力降至0后，在解除超重前不会恢复。

超重时无法跳跃，难以爬陡坡，也无法奔跑、闪避或格挡。移动每秒消耗五耐力，速度设为2米/秒，不受奔跑技能或芬里套装、拉塔托斯克药剂等装备效果影响，但仍受被沥青黏住了等其他速度效果影响。

移除超额重量后，效果立即结束。

Encumbered
You are encumbered and cannot run.
Effects
Movement speed: Reduced to 2m/s
Stamina regen: -100%
Removes ability to run, jump, dodge, or block

Encumbered is a status effect applied when a player character is carrying more weight than their maximum capacity. This capacity can be increased by wearing a Megingjord, benefitting from Corpse Run, drinking a Mead of Troll Endurance, and/or Moder's Forsaken power.

Once an encumbered player's stamina reaches 0, they will not regenerate stamina until they are unencumbered again.

Encumbered players can not jump and as such will have difficulty with climbing any steep slopes. Neither can they run, dodge, nor block. Five Stamina is drained per second while moving. Movement speed is set to 2 meters per second, unaffected by the Running skill and equipment effects such as Fenris Set or Tonic of Ratatosk, but still affected by other movement speed effects, such as Tarred.

The effect will end immediately once the excess weight is removed.
