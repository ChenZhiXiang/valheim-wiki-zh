希尔德是青青草原中的角色，与哥哥霍尔德一样属于矮人。她穿红白连衣裙，戴绿红项链和头带。营地最近遭劫，希尔德仍出售一些可用钱币购买的独特物品。

传记

希尔德也像霍尔德一样会说话，例如“我的货物都用最好的布料制作”及“我保证，店里只有品质最好的商品”。她有两只平原牦牛，名为小蓝莓和小红莓。

位置

希尔德的生成机制与霍尔德相似，有10处可能的位置，均在青青草原中，彼此相隔至少1000米，且仅位于距世界中心3000至5100米[ 1 ]处。靠近任意一处后，她便永久定居在那里。发现后，世界地图会出现她的裙子图标。

她与专属地点一起生成，小营地包括大车、多顶帐篷、木桶、专属铁制火炉、玩家可坐的多张躺椅及桌子。

与其他商人一样，营地被闪烁的透明穹顶包围，形成生物无法进入的安全区，且无法摧毁。

任务

希尔德面前有地图桌，指向多个特殊地下城，里面有独特小首领及战利品。与地图桌互动即可将地点添加到玩家地图。地点如下：

  • 阴燃之墓 内 黑暗林山
  • 哀啸之穴 内 凛冽雪山
  • 封印之塔 内 北境平原

每个世界中，每种地点最多生成三处，各类生成规则如下：

  • 阴燃墓穴：彼此相隔3000米，距世界中心3000 - 8000米，海拔至少1米。
  • 嚎叫洞穴：彼此相隔2000米，距世界中心1000 - 8000米，海拔至少200米。
  • 封印之塔：彼此相隔3000米，海拔至少8米。

找到希尔德后，可在附近设置传送门，方便返回。

每个任务地点都有大量战利品、敌人及独特小首领。击败后，各小首领会掉落一件任务物品：

  • 希尔德的黄铜宝箱，由阴燃之墓中的布伦娜掉落。
  • 希尔德的白银宝箱，由哀啸之穴中的盖尔哈法掉落。
  • 希尔德的青铜宝箱，由封印之塔中的吉尔与吞格尔的吉尔掉落。

每个箱子重量为200.0，堆叠上限1，无法传送通过传送门传送，但可用传送门 石头传送。将箱子交还希尔德可解锁更多商品；每种箱子只能交还一个。

击败任一小首领会启用新的事件，使该首领及随从袭击玩家基地。触发事件无需将箱子交还希尔德。

趣闻

  • 希尔德营地的透明穹顶与霍尔德营地相同，似乎由周围四块小符文石供能，每块刻有奥丁幻象符文，这种维京符号用于伪装和欺骗。屏障仅允许希尔德及霍尔德的潜在顾客看见并进入营地，灰矮人等敌对生物无法进入，与其用途相符。
  • 希尔德的平原牦牛小蓝莓与小红莓，名字在瑞典语中分别意为蓝莓和覆盆子。
  • 希尔德对箱子被抢的描述暗示布伦娜、盖尔哈法、吉尔与吞格尔联手偷走货物，但游戏中这些小首领会彼此战斗。
  • 根据希尔德的一句台词，小蓝莓与小红莓均为雌性。

Hildir is a character found in Meadows. Like her brother, Haldor, she is a Dvergr. She wears a red and white dress, a green and red necklace and a headband. At her camp, which was recently raided, Hildir sells several unique items that can be purchased for Coins.

Biography

Hildir also speaks, like Haldor, saying sentences like, "My wares are made out of the finest cloth," and "You'll only find products of the finest quality in my shop, I assure you." She has two Lox, named Blåbär and Hallon.

Location

Hildir has a similar spawning system to Haldor with 10 possible spawn locations that are located in Meadows, each at least 1000 meters apart, and only found between 3000 and 5100 meters[1] from the world center. Approaching any of these locations will permanently place Hildir there. When discovered, her dress icon will appear on the world map.

She spawns with her own point of interest, a small camp consisting of a large wagon, with multiple tents, barrels, her own Iron Fire Pit, and multiple divans the player can sit on, and tables.

Like the other traders, the camp is surrounded by a shimmering transparent dome that acts as a safety zone that Creatures cannot enter. The safe zone cannot be destroyed.

Quests

Hildir has a map table in front of her that leads to various different locations where special dungeons with special minibosses can be fought, and different loot is found. Interacting with the table adds the locations to the player's map. The locations are:

  • Smouldering Tomb in Black Forest
  • Howling Cavern in Mountains
  • Sealed Tower in Plains

Up to three of each type of these locations will generate in each world. The spawning rules for each location type are as follows:

  • Smouldering Tombs: Each 3000 meters apart, between 3000 - 8000 meters from the world center, at an altitude of at least 1 meter.
  • Howling Caverns: Each 2000 meters apart, between 1000 - 8000 meters from the world center, at an altitude of at least 200 meters.
  • Sealed Tower: Each 3000 meters apart, at an altitude of at least 8 meters.

When players find Hildir, it can be a good idea to drop a Portal in the vicinity to make easier returns.

Each of these quest locations is full of loot, enemies, and unique minibosses. Once defeated, these minibosses each drop a quest item:

  •  Hildir's brass chest, dropped by Brenna in the Smouldering Tomb
  •  Hildir's silver chest, dropped by Geirrhafa in the Howling Cavern
  •  Hildir's bronze chest, dropped by Zil of Zil & Thungr in the Sealed Tower

Each of these chests weighs 200.0, has a stack size of 1, and cannot be teleported via Portal. They can, however, be teleported with a Portal stone. Chests can be returned to Hildir, which unlocks additional trades from her; note that only one of each type of chest can be returned.

Defeating one of these minibosses will enable new Events, which will cause that boss and their minions to invade the player's base. It is not required to return the chest to Hildir for these events to be triggered.

Trivia

  • The transparent dome which surrounded Hildir's camp is the same as Haldor's camp and apparently powered by four small runestones placed around it and that each of them carved with Odin's Illusory Rune, a Viking glyph devised to provide camouflage and deception. This is fitting as the barrier only allows Hildir's and Haldor's potential customers to both see their camps and pass through it while hostile beings such as Greydwarfs cannot enter.
  • The names of Hildir's Lox, Blåbär and Hallon, are Swedish for blueberry and raspberry, respectively.
  • How Hildir explains the event of her chests being robbed suggests Brenna, Geirhaffa, Zil & Thunger, all teamed up to steal from her. However, in game the minibosses all fight each other.
  • Blåbär and Hallon are females judging by one of Hildir's lines of text.
