半埋藏墓穴是未使用的黑暗林山地点，是诡秘墓穴的开放世界变体，由半埋墓穴房间组成。

数据

原本可位于海拔5至500米，每世界最多100处。包含骷髅怪生成器，等级1–3，部分60分钟后重生，以及两个骨堆生成器。

包含12间房，都是半埋的墓穴房间副本。

The Half-buried Burial Chambers is an unused Black Forest location. It is an open world variant of the Burial Chambers made from half-buried crypt rooms.

Data

It could have been between 5 and 500 meters altitude, with up to 100 per world. It holds Skeleton spawners (level 1–3, some respawning after 60 minutes) and two bone pile spawners.

It has 12 rooms, half-buried copies of Burial Chambers rooms.
