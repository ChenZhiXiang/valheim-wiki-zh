邪骨恶灵是第三位首领，外形为巨大的类人团块，由毒泥、淤泥和骨骼残骸组成，可能吸收自祭坛附近墓穴。

召唤地点

邪骨恶灵的弃誓者祭坛位于泥泞沼泽。在地下圣堂及部分废墟找到导向符文石，可在地图上标记最近召唤位置。

在沼泽祭坛放10份枯骨即可召唤。与所有首领一样，只要材料足够，就能反复召唤。

袭击与能力

邪骨恶灵战斗策略参见 首领攻略 § 邪骨恶灵.

邪骨恶灵的环境气场会强制下雨，因此将它拉离区域无法消除潮湿。

范围效果

邪骨恶灵周围聚起浓绿云雾，后仰并吐出毒泥，使半径9米内所有目标获得毒素，造成130点毒素伤害。毒云持续15秒，不断刷新留在其中的玩家的中毒时间，无法闪避或格挡。玩家在15米内时可用，冷却30秒。云雾以呕吐落点为中心，受其碰撞箱尺寸影响，不会覆盖身后太远，因此后方有能退避又能继续攻击的安全区域。

击打

近距离横扫对玩家最多造成80点钝击及50点毒素伤害，对命中的树木和岩石另造成1000点等级0的砍伐及1000点等级0的镐击伤害。前方范围为8.5米、水平66.1度弧形，玩家在8米内时可用，冷却7秒。

掷出

每隔50秒，从腋下抓出黏液团，朝玩家大致方向投掷，初速20米/秒，散布角5–20度。落地后尝试生成四只随机怪物，即骷髅怪或软泥怪，概率相同。每种最多同时八只，但每次仍随机选种类：某种达上限，不会强制生成另一种。两种都达上限也仍会尝试。玩家在30米内时才会使用。

战后情景

击败邪骨恶灵可获得愿望骨，方便寻找凛冽雪山地下的银矿脉、沼泽埋藏的泥泞的废料堆及某些地点的其他地下宝藏。

遗弃者能力

将邪骨恶灵战利品挂在对应石碑上，可获得放逐之力，为玩家及附近友方提供以下效果，持续300秒：

  • 伤害修正: 轻微抗性 对钝击
  • 伤害修正: 轻微抗性 对挥砍
  • 伤害修正: 轻微抗性 对穿刺
  • 格挡耐力消耗: -100%
  • 格挡后耐力恢复: +5

更多详情见邪骨恶灵之力。

事件

击败邪骨恶灵会影响此世界的以下事件：

  • 禁用尸鬼和骷髅的“沼泽传来恶臭……”事件
  • 启用：“沼泽飘来恶臭……”中的软泥及软泥怪。
  • 启用: "骷髅惊起"
  • 启用“山间吹来寒风”，前提是冰霜龙母尚未被击败
  • 启用“空气中弥漫着硫磺味”，前提是一只焰灵已经死亡
  • 启用:"你正在被追捕".
  • 启用“你搅动了大锅”，前提是一只邪蝠已经死亡
  • 启用：“你感到脊背发凉……”

注意：其中两种事件解锁飞行敌人，不受基地围墙阻挡，并会攻击驯养动物。例如蝙蝠可能在玩家全部打死前，迅速杀光室外鬓猪。为保护2星鬓猪等珍贵动物，建议挑战邪骨恶灵前先建安全室内畜舍。

生物 生成

击败后，骷髅怪会在草原、黑森林、沼泽（额外）、山地和平原夜间生成，只会有0星。

Bonemass is the third boss. He appears as a gigantic humanoid mass comprised of toxic ooze, mud and skeletal remains, likely absorbed from nearby tombs situated close to his altar.

Summoning

Bonemass' Forsaken Altar is located in Swamps. His location can be found when visiting Sunken Crypts and sometimes ruined structures, where a Vegvisir will reveal his nearest summoning location on the map.

He is summoned by placing 10  Withered Bones in his Forsaken altar in Swamps. Like all bosses, Bonemass can be summoned as many times as desired, provided the required materials are met.

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating Bonemass, see Boss strategies § Bonemass.

Bonemass has an environment aura that forces raining, which means pulling him from the area cannot be used to remove Wet.

Area of effect

Bonemass will charge up with a thick green cloud before vomiting a wave of poisonous ooze, inflicting the Poison status on everything in a 9-meter radius around him (130 Poison damage). This is signaled when he leans backwards. The poison cloud persists for 15 seconds and will continuously refresh the poison duration on any player that stays inside. This effect cannot be dodged or blocked. Bonemass can use this ability when a player is within 15 meters, and it has a cooldown of 30 seconds. The cloud is centered on the impact point of Bonemass's vomit; due to the size of his collision box, this results in the cloud not reaching very far behind him which gives players a safe area to retreat to that is still within range to strike him.

Punch

A close-range swipe at a player dealing up to 80 Blunt and 50 Poison damage to players, in addition to 1000 Tier 0 Chop and 1000 Tier 0 Pickaxe damage to any trees or rocks hit. This attack can be used every 7 seconds when a player is within 8 meters. It hits in a horizontal 8.5-meter 66.1-degree arc in front of Bonemass.

Throw

At 50-second intervals, Bonemass will reach into his armpit to throw out a glob of goop in the general direction of the player at an initial velocity of 20 meters per second with a spread of 5–20 degrees. On impact, this ability will attempt to spawn four random monsters (Skeletons and/or Blobs, with equal chances for both). A maximum of eight summons of each type can be active at once; but the selection of enemies each spawning instance is still random regardless of the status of spawn caps, meaning reaching the spawn cap for one type of summon will not force spawns for the other. Bonemass will still attempt to summon creatures even if both spawn caps are reached. This ability can only be used when a player is within 30 meters.

Aftermath

Defeating Bonemass provides the player with a Wishbone, which allows the player to easily locate Silver Veins beneath the surface of Mountain biomes (as well as buried Muddy Scrap Piles in Swamp biomes, and other miscellaneous buried treasures in certain locations).

Forsaken power

Mounting Bonemass' trophy upon his respective stone will allow the player to claim his Forsaken Power. This ability will grant the player and nearby allies the following effects for 300 seconds:

  • Damage modifier: Slightly resistant vs Blunt
  • Damage modifier: Slightly resistant vs Slash
  • Damage modifier: Slightly resistant vs Pierce
  • Block stamina usage: -100%
  • Block stamina return: +5

See Bonemass' power for more details.

Events

Defeating Bonemass affects the following events for that world:

  • Disables: Draugr and Skeleton "A foul smell from the swamp..."
  • Enables: Blobs and Oozers "A foul smell from the swamp..."
  • Enables: "A skeleton surprise!"
  • Enables: "A cold wind blows from the mountains." (if Moder has not been defeated)
  • Enables: "There's a smell of sulfur in the air." (if a Surtling has died)
  • Enables: "You are being hunted."
  • Enables: "You stirred the cauldron." (if a Bat has died)
  • Enables: "You feel a chill down your spine..."

Note: Two of these events unlock flying enemies that will be uninhibited by base walls and will attack tame animals. For example, bats will quickly wipe out any outdoor tame Boars (if the player has them), most likely before the player can swat them all. Therefore, in order to protect any rare animals (such as a 2-star Boar), it is advisable to build a secure indoor barn area before undertaking Bonemass.

Creature spawning

Defeating Bonemass unlocks Skeletons to spawn at night in the Meadows, Black Forest, Swamp (additional), Mountain and Plains. They can only have 0 stars.
