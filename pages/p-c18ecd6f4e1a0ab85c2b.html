沼泽符文石塔是幽暗沼泽中的地点，像石塔废墟，共两变体。

资源

有25.1%概率出现泥泞沼泽 宝箱，30%概率出现指向最近邪骨恶灵祭坛的导向符文石。由尸体堆守卫，另有32.1%概率尸鬼精英，及两只额外尸鬼，各50%概率。

两只乌鸦始终在塔周围飞翔。

Swamp Runestone Towers are Points of interest found in the Swamp. They look like ruins of stone towers. They come in two variants.

Resources

They have a 25.1% chance for a Swamp chest and a 30% chance for a Vegvisir toward the nearest Bonemass's altar. They are protected by a Body Pile, a 32.1% chance for a Draugr Elite and two additional Draugrs with a 50% chance each.

Two Crows are always flying around the tower.
