吉尔与吞格尔是封印之塔内北境平原中的敌对双人小首领。

袭击与能力

吉尔与吞格尔战斗策略参见 首领战斗策略 § 吉尔与吞格尔.

攻击（吞格尔）

挥动棍棒攻击前方4米、70度的水平弧形范围，造成150点钝击、100点2级砍伐及40点2级镐击伤害。玩家在3米内时每2秒可用一次，有普通及延迟挥击两种变体[ 1 ]。

狂怒攻击（吞格尔）

连续3次将棍棒砸向地面，每次向前迈步，覆盖向外4米、垂直60度的弧形范围，每击造成130点钝击、100点2级砍伐及40点2级镐击伤害。玩家在6米内时每20秒可用一次。

火球（吉尔）

吉尔向玩家发射火球，施加燃烧中状态，造成20点钝击及100点火焰伤害。冷却12秒，玩家在20米内时可用。火球速度每秒15米，最多飞行4秒后消失，从瞄准中心散射4–20度。

防护（吉尔）

为半径6米内所有盟友施加护盾，最多抵消100点伤害后破裂，或持续最多40秒。此技能冷却40秒，玩家在60米内时可用。

吞格尔被杀后，吉尔独自留下，攻击招式恢复为普通丑地精萨满的招式。

说明

  • 吉尔与吞格尔是英灵神殿中首次以双人组合行动的生物。
  • 虽然设定为双人战斗，玩家仍可用各自内部标识符分别生成。
    • 吉尔的内部标识符为 GoblinShaman_Hildir
    • 吞格尔的内部标识符为 GoblinBrute_Hildir
  • 希尔德的青铜宝箱由吉尔掉落。

趣闻

  • 战利品描述显示，吉尔虽比吞格尔小，年龄却更大。
  • 吉尔与吞格尔彼此嫉妒：吉尔嫉妒力量，吞格尔嫉妒魔力。
  • 吉尔游泳时仍能施放元素攻击。
  • 用作弊命令生成吞格尔时，其尸体为普通绿色，而非蓝色。
  • 召唤的有星级吞格尔为棕色，吉尔则保持普通颜色。

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating Zil & Thungr, see Boss strategies § Zil & Thungr.

Attack (Thungr)

Swings his club in a 4-meter 70-degree horizontal arc in front of himself (150 Blunt, 100 Tier 2 Chop and 40 Tier 2 Pickaxe damage). This attack can be used within 3 meters of the player every 2 seconds. It has two variations: a regular swing and delayed swing[1].

Rage attack (Thungr)

Slams his club into the ground 3 times extending out 4 meters and arcing 60 degrees vertically, stepping forward with each swing (130 Blunt, 100 Tier 2 Chop and 40 Tier 2 Pickaxe damage each). He can do this every 20 seconds when within 6 meters of a player.

Fireball (Zil)

Zil shoots a fireball at the player, inflicting the Burning status (20 Blunt and 100 Fire damage). This attack has a 12-second cooldown and can be used when within 20 meters of a player. The fireball travels at 15 meters per second for up to 4 seconds before despawning. It has a spread of 4–20 degrees from the center of his aim.

Protect (Zil)

Casts a protective barrier over all allies within a 6-meter radius, negating up to 100 damage before breaking or lasting for up to 40 seconds. This ability has a 40-second cooldown and can be used within 60 meters of a player.

When Thungr is killed, Zil is left alone and his attack pool is reverted to that of a regular Fuling Shaman.

Notes

  • Zil & Thungr are the first creatures in Valheim that work as a duo.
  • Even though they are supposed to be fought as a duo, players can spawn them separately thanks to their Internal IDs.
    • Zil's Internal ID is GoblinShaman_Hildir
    • Thungr's Internal ID is GoblinBrute_Hildir
  • Zil is the one that drops Hildir's bronze chest.

Trivia

  • According to their trophy descriptions, Zil is older than Thungr, despite the former's size being smaller than the latter.
  • Zil & Thungr have an envy of each other. Zil is jealous of Thungr's strength, and Thungr is jealous of Zil's magical power.
  • Zil can still cast his elemental attacks while swimming.
  • When spawning Thungr with cheats, his corpse is the normal green, instead of blue.
  • Summoned starred variants of Thungr are brown, where as Zil's stay true to his normal color.
