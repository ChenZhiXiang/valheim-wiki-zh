护甲部件

这套护甲由三件组成。

这些部件无法直接制作。玩家必须先在等级 3 的古咒法台处制作对应的投掷，再在霜铸熔炉中硬化。每个铸件硬化需要 50 秒，以液态冰霜为燃料，每 10 秒消耗一份，每个铸件共需五份。

护甲可在等级 3 的古咒法台处修理。由于古咒法台最高等级为 5，目前这些部件无法升级到品质 3 以上。

没有套装加成。

模具

三种模具各有5%的概率由巨人巫师掉落。

The Caller Set is a Deep North-tier mage armor.

  • 1 Armor pieces
    • 1.1 Moulds
    • 1.2 Quality
  • 2 Gallery

Armor pieces

This set of armor has three pieces.

The pieces cannot be crafted directly. Instead the player must craft the corresponding Cast at a level 3 Galdr Table and harden it in a Frost Foundry. Hardening a cast takes 50 seconds and burns Liquid Frost as fuel at one unit every 10 seconds, and five units are required per cast.

The armor can be repaired at a level 3 Galdr Table. Since the maximum Galdr Table level is 5, the pieces cannot currently be upgraded past quality 3.

There is no set bonus.

Moulds

Each of the three moulds has a 5% chance to drop from a Hexen.
