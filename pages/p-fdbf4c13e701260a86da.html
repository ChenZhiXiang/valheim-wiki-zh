Harald是在英灵神殿中提及的角色。

传记

根据英灵神殿中的符文石，哈拉尔德是在米德加尔特死去的战士，被送至炼狱之地。他似乎从世界一端走到另一端，在各生态区留下描述自身生活的符文石。

符文石

好朋友，把手放在这块石头上，记住刻下它的哈拉尔德。我曾在米德加尔特的战场上死去，却在英灵神殿重获生命。然而，我的战斗仍在继续。

在青青草原，我与巨鹿赤血灵鹿交战，被它的鹿角刺瞎了一只眼睛。

在密林深处，我把持盾的手献给了古树长老；一支尸鬼的箭射进了我的肋骨下方，至今仍能用手摸到。

在山腰，我与冰霜龙母交战。她从膝盖处咬断了我的腿，使我从此只能拄杖行走。

如今，我的最后一战想必已近在眼前。这一次睡去，我又会在哪里醒来？

趣闻

  • 历史上，斯堪的纳维亚及北欧人到访的其他地方，人们会立起符文石。它们常用于纪念死者、记录重要事件，甚至标记领地。

Harald is a mentioned character in Valheim.

Biography

According to a runestone located in Valheim, Harald was a warrior who died in Midgard, thus being sent to the purgatory lands. He seemingly traveled the world from end to end, leaving behind him various runestones detailing his life among the various biomes.

Runestone

Good friend, lay your hand on this stone and remember Harald, who carved it. In Midgard I lost my life on the battlefield but in Valheim it was restored to me. Yet still my battle continued.

In the green meadows I fought Eikthyr the Great Stag and lost an eye to his horns.

In the deep woods I gave my shield hand to the Old Man of the Forest, and took a draugr arrow below my ribs that my fingers can still feel.

On the mountainside I fought the Mother Drake and she bit off my leg at the knee so that I must walk always with a staff.

Now my last battle must be at hand. When I sleep this time, where will I wake?

Trivia

  • Historically, runestones were raised by people in Scandinavia and other places visited by the Norsemen. They were often memorials to dead men, written testaments to important events or even used as territorial markers.
