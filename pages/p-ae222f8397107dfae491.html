潜行（技能）
说明
决定潜行时的耐力消耗及被敌人发现的程度。

潜行是一种技能。

效果

最多降低75%潜行时敌人对玩家的可见度及耐力消耗非线性，早期等级下降更明显。

潜行机制

另见: 生物感知

潜行还按以下方式降低可见度：

潜行可见度取决于所在位置光照，以准星下小条表示。越亮白色越满，越暗越低。此值直接降低敌人的有效视距。例如白色填80%，附近敌人判断玩家是否在视野内时，仅使用80%视距。

技能0时，最暗处50%、最亮100%，足够暗时最多使敌人视距减半。技能100时，最暗20%、最亮60%。

潜行时你不会发出声音。潜行判定依据视线，而非随机概率。如果敌人的视线能看到你，而你又处在其修正后的视野范围内，它就会发现你。敌人确实拥有朝向前方的锥形视野，但确切角度尚不明确，并且可能因敌人而异。

在一或多名敌人视距内且都未警戒时，移动每秒获得经验。敌人数、距离、朝向、光照和可见度均不影响。不足一秒也按比例积累。

附近没有敌人时，每移动一秒获得基础经验的10%。即使启用PVP，其他玩家也不算敌人。

利用 T.W.I.G. 可以更轻松地提升技能等级。也可以将一头野猪圈在安全区域内，并在它周围潜行。

Sneaking (skill)
Description
Determines stamina drain and visibility from mobs while sneaking.

Sneaking is a skill.

Effects

It reduces the player's visibility to enemies and stamina drain while sneaking by up to 75% non-linearly, with more noticeable reductions in earlier levels.

Sneaking mechanics

See also: Creature senses

Sneak skill also reduces your visibility to enemies as follows:

While sneaking, your visibility is based on the light level of your position, represented in your HUD as a small bar under the reticle. This bar fills with white as you are in brighter locations, and lowers in darker locations. This value directly reduces the effective view range of enemies when considering if you are seen by them. For example, if that "visibility meter" is filled 80% white, then enemies around you have 80% of their view range when calculating if you're inside it.

With 0 skill, this meter will be at 50% in the darkest dark and 100% in the brightest bright. This means you can cut enemy view range in half at best, if it is dark enough. At 100 skill, the meter will be at 20% in the darkest dark, and 60% in the brightest bright.

While sneaking you make no sound. Sneak considers line-of-sight, but is not chance-based. If the enemy has line-of-sight to you, and you are in their modified view range, they will see you. Enemies do have a frontal view-cone, but it is unknown what are the exact angles, which may vary per enemy.

If you are within one or more enemies' view range and none of them are alerted, you gain experience every second spent moving. It does not matter how many enemies you are sneaking near, how close you are, which way they are facing, how dark it is, or how visible you are. You do not have to sneak for a full second to benefit (fractional seconds of sneaking will accumulate).

You gain 10% of the base experience per second moving if there are no enemies nearby. Players do not count as enemies, even if pvp-enabled.

For an easier way to level up, utilize T.W.I.G.. Alternatively, one could have a wild Boar contained inside a safe area and sneak around it.
