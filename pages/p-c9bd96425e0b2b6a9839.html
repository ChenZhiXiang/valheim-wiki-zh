获取方式

本节为转录，材料来源： 鱼 § 品质.

任何种类的鱼都可在灶台处加工成生鱼肉。生鱼肉的数量主要取决于鱼的品质，详见下表。但某些鱼类还会提供固定额外数量，这部分不会随品质倍增。例如，品质3的金枪鱼可获得8份生鱼肉。哪些鱼有额外产出，详见下一节。

Raw Fish can be made from any caught fish at a Food Preparation Table. It can be cooked into Cooked Fish using a Cooking Station.

Fish can be caught with a Fishing Rod using Fishing Bait or by picking up fish that have become stranded out of water.

Availability

This section is transcluded from Fish § Quality.

Raw Fish can be made in the Food Preparation Table from any type of fish. The number of Raw fish acquired depends mainly on the fish quality, as listed below. However, certain types of fish also add a flat bonus (which is not multiplied per quality level): for example, a quality 3 Tuna will yield 8 raw fish. See the next section for details on which fish give bonus raw fish.
