皇室
英灵神殿的生物不会伤害已掌控它们的人。
效果
阻止非首领生物攻击玩家，并使其逃跑

皇室是穿英灵神殿之冠获得的状态。

阻止非首领生物攻击玩家，使其逃跑。生物须先察觉玩家才逃跑。

Royalty
The creatures of Valheim will not harm the one who has mastered them.
Effects
Prevents non-boss creatures from attacking the player and makes them flee

Royalty is a status effect obtained by wearing the Crown of Valheim.

The effect prevents non-boss creatures from attacking the player and makes them flee. Creatures need to detect the player to start fleeing.
