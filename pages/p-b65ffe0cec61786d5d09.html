连接传送门

建好传送门后，会自动尝试连接其他具有相同区分大小写标签的传送门。标签最多十个字符，可使用特殊字符、数字和字母。默认标签为空，两个空标签传送门会自动配对。多个传送门使用同一标签时，按标签设置顺序两两配对，每个门只连接另一个门。例如四个门都使用“asdf”，前两个配对，第三和第四个配对。

连接后，走进其中一个传送门，即可传送到配对传送门的位置。

阻止传送的物品

某些物品会阻止玩家传送，除非在传送门设置中修改世界修改器。这些物品的图标带有被红线划掉的蓝色传送门。所有矿石及对应金属锭都无法传送，因此常需维京长船，或在陆地长途搬运矿石返回基地。

不过，传送门 石头允许传送普通传送门禁止的所有物品，包括矿石、金属、龙蛋、希尔德的宝箱和机械弹簧。

策略攻略

击败首位首领后，建造传送门的材料较容易获得，可借此穿行地图各处。英灵神殿的进度要求探索散布各地、常隔着大片水域的生物群系；传送门能快速运输包括基础建材在内的大多数物品，减少跑图、拉板车和航海的时间。建立完善的传送门网络可以大幅节省时间。

超重时也可使用传送门，便于运输大量木材、石头或作物，大部分情况下可替代板车。

没有哪个生物群系能稳定提供建造传送门的全部材料。探索时可随身携带一套材料，以便快速返回安全地点。所有传送门材料都能传送，因此可在各处建立传送点，获取资源和进入不同生物群系，无须每次都建造坚固基地。

说明

  • 放置传送门时附近需要工作台，建成后运行则不再需要。
  • 首次各收集一份细木、焰灵之核和灰矮人之眼后解锁传送门配方。
  • 从正面或背面进入都能传送。不过，从背面靠近时，必须更贴近传送门，连接成功的音效和视觉提示才会出现。
  • 放置时，可观察底座向外突出的尖端，以辨认传送门正面。
  • 传送通常耗时八秒，期间玩家无敌。
  • 开始传送两秒后，玩家实际上已移动到目标位置，但传送完成前无法操作移动。
  • 目的地被堵住时，传送最多可能耗时15秒，随后会尝试在传送门上方寻找空位。
  • 传送期间，已有状态效果仍继续计时，但不会对玩家生效，例如中毒伤害在传送过程中会被免除。

Connecting Portals

When two portals are built, they will automatically attempt to connect with any other portal with the same case-sensitive tag. The character limit is ten characters, and special characters, numbers, and letters are permissible. By default, portals are built with no tag, and two portals with a blank tag will be connected to each other. If multiple portals have the same tag, the portals will be connected in pairs in the order that the tags are set. However, each portal will only be linked to one other, paired portal. For example, if four portals are built all with the same tag "asdf": the first two portals will be paired, and the third and fourth portals will be paired.

Once connected, a player simply has to step into one portal to teleport to the position of the paired portal.

Objects denying teleportation

Some objects in the game prevent the player from teleporting (unless the World Modifiers are changed under Portal Settings); such objects are recognizable by a little blue portal crossed in red on their icon. All ores and their smelted counterparts cannot be teleported, often requiring players to use Longships or hauling the ore over long distances across the land to return to base.

However, a Portal stone does allow the teleportation of all items that are not possible with a standard Portal, including Ores, Metals, Dragon Eggs, Hildir's Chests and Mechanical Springs.

Strategies

Portals allow the player to traverse anywhere across the map using materials which are fairly easy to acquire after defeating the first boss. Because progression in Valheim requires the player to explore various biomes which scattered across the world and separated by expanses of water, the use of portals allows the player to quickly transport most materials, including basic building materials, without spending an excessive amount of time running across the map, hauling a Cart, or steering a ship. Setting up a robust portal infrastructure is an incredible time saver.

Portals can be used while encumbered. This is useful for transporting large amounts of wood, stone, or crops, largely rendering the cart obsolete.

No single biome reliably contains all materials to build a portal. When exploring, the player can carry materials to build a portal in order to quickly return to safety. Because all the materials used to create a portal can be teleported, this strategy allows the player to build portals all across the map to access various resources and biomes without having to stop to build a heavily fortified base.

Notes

  • A Portal needs to have a Workbench in range in order to be placed, but does not need one in range to work.
  • The portal recipe is unlocked by collecting one each of Finewood, Surtling Core, and Greydwarf Eye for the first time.
  • Portals work regardless of if you walk in the 'front' or 'back'. However, when approaching from the back, the player needs to be in much closer proximity to the portal before the audio and visual indicator of a connected portal becomes active.
  • When placing the portal, the 'front' of the portal is indicated best by looking for the outward facing point on the base of the portal.
  • Teleporting usually takes eight seconds. Players are invulnerable during the teleportation.
  • Players are physically moved to the target location after two seconds of teleportation but cannot move before the teleportation is finished.
  • Teleporting can take up to 15 seconds if the destination is blocked. After that a free spot is attempted to be found above the portal.
  • Active effects continue to countdown during teleportation, however their application to the player is nullified during the teleportation sequence. (A prime example of this being poison damage being nullified during a teleportation.)
