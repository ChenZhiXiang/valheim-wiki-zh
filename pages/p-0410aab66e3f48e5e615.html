泥泞沼泽是压抑的生物群系，充满巨树、泥沼与敌对生物。巨大裸枝的古树伸向病态阴云，景象骇人。稀少陆地上的湿土蜿蜒穿过遍布此地、深及膝盖的疫水。尸鬼四处蹒跚，等待杀死活着的维京人，向早已不再倾听的诸神证明自己值得升天。

从难度上说，沼泽是刻意设置的障碍。初次进入时，整片土地似乎都在尽快致玩家于死地，所有生物都敌对。不论别处天气如何，这里始终下雨，玩家行进时一直潮湿，耐力负担严酷。夜间还会寒冷，并可能被鬼魅追赶。毒素也无处不在，是大型软泥怪、软泥怪和蚂蟥的主要攻击手段。必须善用前一生物群系解锁的特定蜜酒。

规模稍大的沼泽散布着地下圣堂，需用沼泽密钥开启。墓室近乎废墟，蜿蜒走廊塌陷并被泥泞的废料堆堵住，开采可得铁碎屑、皮革碎片和枯骨。地表还有半浸水的废弃建筑、敌对生物 生物生成点，以及偶尔出现的焰灵 间歇泉。

除墓室外，地下也有废料矿脉。击败邪骨恶灵后可用愿望骨定位这些积水矿脉，但数量少，通常无法提供大量铁。采矿噪声还会吸引当地生物。

The Swamp is an oppressive biome, filled with massive trees, muddy bogs, and hostile creatures. Immense, bare-branched Ancient Trees claw at a sickly cloud-laden sky, making for intimidating vistas. On what small bit of ground exists, tendrils of sodden soil snake around and through the knee-deep, plague-ridden water that permeates the entire biome. Draugr stumble about everywhere, watching and waiting to slay any living Viking they can find; to prove they are worthy of ascension to gods that have long since stopped listening.

From a difficulty standpoint, the Swamps were designed to be a wall of sorts. Entering the biome for the first time, Vikings are met with an unprecedented drive by the very land to kill them as quickly and effectively as possible: every last creature here is hostile to the player. It is always raining in the mire regardless of the weather elsewhere; this means the player will always be Wet when traversing the terrain. The toll taken on stamina in the Swamp is brutal, especially at night, when the player will also be Cold, and likely on the run from Wraiths. The presence of Poison is undeniable as well, being the weapon of choice for Oozers, Blobs, and Leeches. Making express use of a certain mead unlocked in the previous biome is not negotiable.

Sprinkling the landscape of any moderately large Swamp are Sunken Crypts, which require a Swamp Key to unlock. These crypts sit in a state of near ruin, their winding corridors caved in and blocked by Muddy Scrap Piles, which can be mined for Scrap Iron, Leather Scraps, and Withered Bones. Up on the surface, half-submerged abandoned structures and hostile creature spawners can be found, as well as the occasional Surtling Geyser.

In addition to the Crypts, scrap piles can be found buried in the ground here. These waterlogged veins can be located using a Wishbone after defeating Bonemass, though it should be noted that they tend to be uncommon and are generally not viable for providing any significant quantities of iron. Additionally, mining is a noisy activity and will attract the local fauna.

Notes

  • It is uncommon for a Swamp to be present on a world's starting continent; as such, it is likely a Karve is needed to reach it. Once a Swamp is located, it is recommended that an adjacent Meadows or Black Forest is chosen for landfall. This way, it is significantly easier for a forward base to be erected and operated from.
  • There are various ways to glitch (i.e. through unintended ways) into a swamp crypt without using a key. These methods may be patched in later versions of the game.
    • A popular strategy is to place a chair just in front of the crypt entrance, sit in the chair, and then stand up. The player will be pushed through the gate and by extension, the loading barrier.
    • The method listed above can also be utilized if a crypt is found to have an indestructible ancient tree blocking the entrance.
