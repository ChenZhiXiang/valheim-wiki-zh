邪煞之树是在泥泞沼泽出现的敌对生物。它们是由多根树枝组成的巨大树状生物，用四根粗壮树根作腿移动。

由于怕火，使用燃烧的箭击杀它是可行的。劈砍伤害，例如斧、剑和刀，也能快速将其击倒。使用条纹铁盾或更好的装备可招架其攻击，击败它会比想象中容易。

袭击与能力

击败邪煞之树的策略见 敌对生物攻略 § 邪煞之树.

横扫

[ 1 ]：右枝以长11米、水平45度的弧形扫向玩家，造成60点钝击、100点等级2的砍伐及60点等级2的镐击伤害。冷却7秒，只在玩家距其2.5–6米时使用，因此紧贴邪煞之树中心可阻止此攻击。

重砸

[ 1 ]：高举左枝后砸向地面，造成80点钝击、100点等级2的砍伐及60点等级2的镐击伤害。命中范围为枝条周围半径4.3米的圆，冷却8秒，在玩家距其3–5米时使用。因此紧贴邪煞之树中心可阻止此攻击。

砸地

[ 1 ]：稍微抬起身体，再将整个身体砸向地面，造成80点钝击、100点等级2的砍伐及60点等级2的镐击伤害。范围为自身中心周围半径5.4米的圆，冷却6秒，在玩家距其0–3米时使用。

说明

  • 休眠时的邪煞之树很像树桩，通常生成在开阔水域，有时藏在芦苇中。它们也会散发沼泽常见的绿色气体，是明显的辨认线索。
  • 符合生成条件的位置有限，因此常似乎在相同地点反复出现。同一区域内有4000秒、约66分钟的生成冷却，期间不会重新生成。
  • 邪煞之树感知敏锐，但激活范围比侦测范围更大。
  • 若在深水击杀，树脓会沉底并可能丢失，但树根及掉落的战利品会漂浮。
  • 掉落的邪煞之根可用于制作树根套装，制作并完全升级总共需要66份树根。每只固定掉落5份，因此总共需击杀14只邪煞之树。
  • 虽然外形像树，古树长老能力并不将邪煞之树视为树木。
  • 邪煞之树怕火，可将其引到火焰喷口上快速击杀。火焰效果在一定范围内可叠加，持续牵制使其停在火焰上，就能不断增加燃烧效果。
  • 要潜行接近并获得背刺加成，可观察其休眠外形：它有一定倾斜，高的一侧就是面向的方向，应从低的一侧靠近。无论从哪个方向接近，触发起身的范围都一样，但在它转身前不会看见玩家。注意，进入足够深的水会中断潜行。

Abominations are aggressive creatures found in the Swamps. They are large tree-like entities comprised of multiple branches and four sturdy roots, which it uses as legs to move around.

Due to its weakness to fire damage, killing it with Fire Arrows is a valid option. Slashing damage (Axes, Swords, Knives) takes it down quite rapidly. With a Banded Shield or better, its attacks can be parried, and the creature defeated surprisingly easily.

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating Abominations, see Aggressive creature strategies § Abomination.

Swing

[1] Swings their right branch at the player in an 11-meter long 45-degree horizontal arc (60 Blunt, 100 Tier 2 Chop and 60 Tier 2 Pickaxe damage). This has a 7-second cooldown and is restricted to when the player is within 2.5–6 meters, meaning it is possible to disable this attack by standing against the center of the Abomination.

Slam

[1] Raises their left branch high before pounding it against the ground (80 Blunt, 100 Tier 2 Chop and 60 Tier 2 Pickaxe damage). The impact hits in a 4.3-meter radius circle around the branch. It has an 8-second cooldown and can be used within 3–5 meters of a player, meaning it is possible to disable this attack by standing against the center of the Abomination.

Ground slam

[1] Lifts themselves up slightly, before slamming their entire body in the ground (80 Blunt, 100 Tier 2 Chop and 60 Tier 2 Pickaxe damage). This attack hits in a 5.4-meter radius circle around their center. This has a 6-second cooldown and can be used within 0–3 meters of a player.

Notes

  • The idle form of the Abomination looks a lot like a tree stump. They tend to spawn in open water areas, sometimes among reeds. They also give off the Swamp's typical green gas effect, which is a big giveaway.
  • Due to the limited number of locations that match the spawn criteria, they can often appear to spawn in similar locations each time. They will not respawn in a zone during the cooldown period of 4000 seconds or around 66 minutes.
  • Abominations are quite perceptive, but their activation range is greater than their detection range.
  • If they are killed in deep water, the Guck will sink and possibly be lost, but the root, and the trophy if one is dropped, floats.
  • The Root item drop opens access to the Root Set, which requires 66 Root in total to craft and fully upgrade. Thus requiring 14 Abomination kills in all, from the fixed 5 Root per drop.
  • Despite appearances, Abominations are not considered trees by the Elder power.
  • As Abominations are weak to fire, players can lure them over Fire geysers for a speedy kill. Fire stacks up to a point, so keeping them over the flame by kiting them will keep adding fire stacks.
  • To sneak up on them and get the backstab bonus, look closely at the idle form. You'll notice it has a slant to it. The taller side of it is the direction the Abomination is facing, so sneak up on the lower side. Though they will pop up with the same radius regardless of where you are facing, they won't see you unless they turn around. Do note that entering deep enough water stops the player from sneaking.
