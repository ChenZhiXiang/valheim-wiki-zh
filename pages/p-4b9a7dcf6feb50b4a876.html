说明

玩家未装备武器或工具进行攻击，或使用血肉撕裂者等专用拳类武器时，会进行拳击。主要攻击为拳击，次要攻击为具有额外击退和6x失衡效果的踢击。拳击具有2连击，第二拳（左手）造成双倍伤害。

空手适合对付最弱的敌人，尤其是玩家希望避免消耗武器耐久度时。其耐力消耗也低于大多数武器。

Description

When a player attacks without a weapon, tool equipped, or use a dedicated Fist weapon such as the Flesh Rippers, they do a Fist strike. The primary attack is a punch, and the secondary attack is a kick that does additional knockback with 6x staggering. The punch has a 2-hit combo where the second punch (left hand) does double damage.

Bare fists can be useful against the weakest of enemies; especially when the player wants to avoid the cost of weapon durability. It also costs less stamina than most weapons.

See also: Fists (Skill)
