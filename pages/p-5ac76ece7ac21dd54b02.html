休息好了

休息好了是一种状态效果，在获得休息中效果 20 秒后生效。它提供 +50% 生命值恢复、+50% 耐力恢复、+50% 埃达之能恢复和 +50% 经验值获取量。

效果持续时间取决于玩家周围环境的舒适度等级。休息好了的基础持续时间为 7 分钟，每级舒适度额外增加一分钟。

玩家角色退出游戏时，此效果会被移除，不会保留到下一次游戏。

提示

  • 休息好了对所有活动都很重要，玩家应尽量始终保持该增益。
    • 主要方法是建造庇护并摆放家具，提高玩家周围的舒适度等级，以获得持久的休息好了增益。
    • 没有遮蔽时，只要天气允许营火燃烧，也可在营火旁坐下（PC 默认按键为 X），获得 8 分钟的休息好了增益。
    • 在地下城的入口大厅放置营火，可快速恢复 10 分钟的休息好了增益。
  • 虽然休息充分会提升玩家属性，但也应反过来看：缺少此效果会使战斗和赶路明显处于劣势，不应忽视。
    • 与潮湿或寒冷效果叠加时尤其如此，这些效果会使玩家极为脆弱。

Rested
You feel rested. Health and stamina regeneration are higher.
Effects
Health regen +50%
Stamina regen +100%
XP gain +50%
Eitr regen +100%

Rested is a status effect applied after having the Resting effect for 20 seconds. It provides +50% Health regeneration, +50% Stamina regeneration, +50% Eitr regeneration and +50% Experience gain.

The duration of the effect depends on the Comfort level from the player's surroundings. The base duration for the Rested effect is 7 minutes plus one additional minute per comfort level.

The effect is removed when the player character logs out and does not persist into new game sessions.

Tips

  • Rested is an important buff for all activities, and players should always try to keep it active.
    • The main method is by building a Shelter and placing furniture to increase the Comfort level around the player to gain a long standing Rested buff.
    • Without shelter, it is possible to sit (the default keybind on PC being X) next to a campfire and gain an 8-minute Rested buff, given that the weather allows the campfire to burn.
    • Placing the campfire in the entrance hall of Dungeons can quickly restore a 10-minute Rested buff.
  • While acting as a "buff" to player stats, the opposite should be considered: the lack of having the Rested effect provides a huge disadvantage in combat and traversal and should not be overlooked.
    • This especially applies when combined with the Wet or Cold effects, which can make the player extremely vulnerable.
