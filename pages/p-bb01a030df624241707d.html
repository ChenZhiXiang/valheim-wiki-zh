蛟龙是无尽之海中的敌对生物，目前也是海洋唯一自然生成的敌对生物。它们是大型水生蛇状爬行动物，有六只发光眼睛、带刺背鳍，巨口中布满锋利牙齿。

狩猎蛟龙主要为了肉，可用于中后期食物。

袭击与能力

击败蛟龙的策略见 敌对生物攻略 § 蛟龙.

咬伤

蛟龙先缩回头，再向前咬玩家，造成70点劈砍伤害。玩家在3米内时可用，冷却10秒；即使已在攻击范围内，也会继续靠近。范围为3米、水平80度弧形。

说明

  • 独行玩家使用木筏通常太慢、太脆弱，难以抵挡蛟龙。考虑蛟龙威胁，探索海洋最低建议使用维京船，至少携带细木弓。
  • 只有雨天生成的蛟龙可以圈养，夜间生成的会在白天消失。
  • 蛟龙被拖上内陆后，会尝试逃回海中。
  • 蛟龙肉会漂浮，其他掉落物则会沉底。深水击杀时，战利品会沉入海底，目前无法潜水捞取。因此可用深渊之枪先将蛟龙拖到浅水甚至陆地，再击杀收集。

趣闻

  • 蛟龙可能取材于北欧神话约尔穆干德，即游荡在米德加尔特水域的巨型蛟龙。
  • 最初发布时，蛟龙肉可用烹饪台烹饪，如今必须使用铁烹饪台。

Serpents are aggressive creatures found in Oceans, and currently the only hostile creature spawning in Oceans. They are large, aquatic snake-like reptilian creatures with six glowing eyes, spiked dorsal fins and a gaping mouth filled with razor-sharp teeth.

Serpents are hunted primarily for their meat, which can be used in mid-late game food.

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating Serpents, see Aggressive creature strategies § Serpent.

Bite

Serpents will draw their head back before lunging forward to bite the player (70 Slash damage). This attack can be used every 10 seconds when within 3 meters of a player; even when a player is within attacking range, Serpents will continue moving toward them to stay as close as possible. It extends 3 meters out and hits in an 80-degree horizontal arc.

Notes

  • A Raft is generally too slow and too fragile for a solo player to survive a Serpent attack. Because of Serpent attacks, the minimum recommended setup for ocean exploration is a Karve with at least a Finewood Bow.
  • Only the Serpents that are spawned in rainy conditions can be held in captivity. Serpents that spawn at night will despawn during the day.
  • Serpents will attempt to flee back into the ocean if dragged inland.
  • Serpent meat floats, but all other drops do not. Killing a Serpent in deep water will result in the drops sinking to the bottom of the ocean and there is currently no way to dive to retrieve them. Therefore, an Abyssal Harpoon can be used to drag the Serpent to shallow water or even on land before it is killed, to collect the drops.

Trivia

  • The Serpent is likely based on Jörmungandr from Norse mythology, the giant sea Serpent that roams the waters of Midgard.
  • Upon release, serpent meat could be cooked on a Cooking Station. Now, the Iron Cooking Station is required to cook serpent meat.
