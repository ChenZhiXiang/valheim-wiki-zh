召唤地点

凌冽·芬布尔使者被囚禁在监狱，可经由阿萨神族通道抵达。在通道外的奇怪碗中献上三份邪恶之血可打开通道大门。献血后，监狱的大门开启，玩家即可进入。初次抵达时，凌冽仍被锁链束缚，只有受到玩家伤害后才会开始攻击，因此战斗开始前有时间准备。再次召唤时，应在监狱内的碗中再献上三份血液。在监狱外献血只会消耗血液，不会使凌冽在里面出现。

袭击与能力

凌冽·芬布尔使者战斗策略参见 首领攻略 § 凌冽·芬布尔使者.

战斗分三个阶段，各有独立生命值：阶段1为10,000，阶段2为7,000，阶段3为30,000，总计47,000。阶段1死亡后由阶段2替代，阶段3同样替代阶段2。凌冽·芬布尔使者在任何阶段都无法被打出踉跄。

首次发现玩家时，所有玩家会看到“他的仇恨腐蚀万物！”消息。击败阶段3后，会显示“世界归于平静”。

阶段1

凌冽·芬布尔使者直接攻击玩家。此阶段抵抗穿刺、火焰、冰霜及闪电伤害，免疫灵魂伤害。

  • 锁链砸击：造成160点钝击、300点砍伐及300点镐击伤害。左、右各有单次和双次砸击变体，伤害相同，每种在12米内每三秒可用一次。单次砸击仅在生命值至少50%时使用，左侧双次仅在不高于75%时使用，右侧双次仅在不高于50%时使用。
  • 锁链旋转：范围攻击，造成150点钝击、20点冰霜、300点伐木及300点采矿伤害。在8米内每10秒可用一次。
  • 锁链冲刺：造成125点钝击、125点穿刺、300点伐木及300点采矿伤害。在1–12米内每10秒可用一次。
  • 双次横扫：造成150点钝击、200点伐木及200点采矿伤害。在2–12米内每三秒可用一次。

阶段2

凌冽·芬布尔使者藏入无法摧毁的冰块中。此阶段免疫玩家造成的所有伤害类型。

此阶段会召唤之前首领的灵体，称为化身。它们拥有原首领的大多数能力，但并非全部。首先单独出现雷霆雄鹿化身（赤血灵鹿）。击败后，生息森林化身（古树长老）与蠕动亡灵化身（邪骨恶灵）一起出现。此后两条序列分别推进：生息森林之后是龙母化身（冰霜龙母），再是爬行族母化身（蛊虫之后）；蠕动亡灵之后是扭曲灵魂化身（亚格鲁斯），再是翡翠之焰化身（青焰龙王）。

凌冽·芬布尔使者在阶段2仅会在化身死亡时损失生命。每个化身死亡时爆炸，对他造成1,000点伤害。单人且默认世界设置下，七次爆炸共造成7,000点伤害，因此所有化身被击败后该阶段结束。

阶段3

凌冽·芬布尔使者直接攻击玩家，攻击比阶段1更强。此外，玩家在30米内时，他每45秒可召唤触须。触须有80点生命，9–12秒后消失，不会移动，每五秒向40米内目标发射与巨人巫师相同的闪电弹，造成55点钝击、20点伐木及20点采矿伤害。触须弱火、抗冰霜、免疫毒素。

抗性与阶段1相同。

  • 锁链砸击：造成120点钝击、50点火焰、50点冰霜、300点伐木及300点采矿伤害。左、右双次砸击变体伤害相同，各在1–12米内每三秒可用一次。
  • 锁链旋转：范围攻击，造成120点钝击、50点火焰、50点冰霜、300点伐木及300点采矿伤害。仅在生命值至少75%时使用，在8米内每20秒可用一次。
  • 击打：范围攻击，造成150点钝击、300点伐木及300点采矿伤害。在8米内每八秒可用一次。
  • 锁链连击：造成250点钝击、200点伐木及200点采矿伤害。仅在生命值不高于50%时使用，在2–12米内每三秒可用一次。
  • 双次横扫：造成150点钝击、200点伐木及200点采矿伤害。在2–12米内每三秒可用一次。
  • 尖刺雨：造成100点钝击、100点冰霜（x22）、100点伐木及100点采矿伤害。仅在生命值不高于35%时使用，在20米内每25秒可用一次。
  • 召唤触须：召唤触须。

Summoning

Kall Fimbulbringer is imprisoned in The Prison, reached through the Aesir Passage. The door to the passage is opened by offering three Malicious Blood at the Strange Bowl outside the passage. After offering the blood, the door to The Prison will open and the player can enter. Kall is chained when the player first arrives, and only starts to attack after the player causes him damage. This gives some time to prepare before the fight starts. To re-summon, three more blood should be offered at the bowl inside the prison. Offering the blood outside the prison will destroy the blood but Kall will not appear inside.

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating Kall Fimbulbringer, see Boss strategies § Kall Fimbulbringer.

The encounter has three phases, each with its own health pool: 10,000 health in phase 1, 7,000 in phase 2 and 30,000 in phase 3, for a total of 47,000. When phase 1 dies, phase 2 takes its place, and phase 3 replaces phase 2 in the same way. Kall Fimbulbringer can't be staggered in any phase.

When he first notices a player, the message "His hatred corrupts all!" is shown to all players. When phase 3 is defeated, the message "Peace settles over the world" is shown.

Phase 1

Kall Fimbulbringer attacks the player directly. In this phase it is resistant to Pierce, Fire, Frost and Lightning damage and immune to Spirit damage.

  • Chain Slam: 160 Blunt, 300 Chop and 300 Pickaxe damage. There are left and right variants, each as a single and a double slam, all with the same damage. Each variant can be used every three seconds within 12 meters. The single slams are only used at 50% health or more, the double left slam only at 75% health or less, and the double right slam only at 50% health or less.
  • Chain Whirl: an area attack dealing 150 Blunt, 20 Frost, 300 Chop and 300 Pickaxe damage. It can be used every 10 seconds within 8 meters.
  • Chain Rush: 125 Blunt, 125 Pierce, 300 Chop and 300 Pickaxe damage. It can be used every 10 seconds within 1–12 meters.
  • Double Sweep: 150 Blunt, 200 Chop and 200 Pickaxe damage. It can be used every three seconds within 2–12 meters.

Phase 2

Kall Fimbulbringer hides inside an invincible block of ice. In this phase it is immune to all damage types that players deal.

During this phase, spirits of the previous Bosses, called aspects, are summoned. They have most of the abilities of the original bosses, although not all of them. The first aspect is the Aspect of the Lightning Stag (Eikthyr) alone. When it is defeated, the Aspect of the Living Forest (The Elder) and the Aspect of the Writhing Dead (Bonemass) appear together. Each of these two is followed by another aspect when defeated: the Living Forest by the Aspect of the Dragon Mother (Moder) and then the Aspect of the Crawling Matriarch (The Queen), and the Writhing Dead by the Aspect of the Twisted Soul (Yagluth) and then the Aspect of the Emerald Flame (Fader).

Kall Fimbulbringer's phase 2 health only goes down when an aspect dies. Each aspect releases an explosion on death that deals 1,000 damage to him. With one player and default world settings, the seven explosions add up to the full 7,000 health, so the phase ends when all aspects have been defeated.

Phase 3

Kall Fimbulbringer attacks the player directly, with more powerful attacks than phase 1. In addition, he can summon Tendrils every 45 seconds when a player is within 30 meters. Tendrils have 80 health and disappear after 9–12 seconds. They don't move, and they shoot lightning bolts, the same projectile Hexen use, dealing 55 Blunt, 20 Chop and 20 Pickaxe damage every five seconds at targets within 40 meters. Tendrils are weak to Fire, resistant to Frost and immune to Poison.

Its resistances are the same as in phase 1.

  • Chain Slam: 120 Blunt, 50 Fire, 50 Frost, 300 Chop and 300 Pickaxe damage. There are left and right double-slam variants with the same damage, each usable every three seconds within 1–12 meters.
  • Chain Whirl: an area attack dealing 120 Blunt, 50 Fire, 50 Frost, 300 Chop and 300 Pickaxe damage. It is only used at 75% health or more, every 20 seconds within 8 meters.
  • Punch: an area attack dealing 150 Blunt, 300 Chop and 300 Pickaxe damage. It can be used every eight seconds within 8 meters.
  • Chain Flurry: 250 Blunt, 200 Chop and 200 Pickaxe damage. It is only used at 50% health or less, every three seconds within 2–12 meters.
  • Double Sweep: 150 Blunt, 200 Chop and 200 Pickaxe damage. It can be used every three seconds within 2–12 meters.
  • Spike Rain: 100 Blunt, 100 Frost (x22), 100 Chop and 100 Pickaxe damage. It is only used at 35% health or less, every 25 seconds within 20 meters.
  • Tendril spawn: summons Tendrils.
