奥丁是英灵神殿中的角色。他是身披黑斗篷的神秘身影，只有一只发蓝光的眼睛，右手拄杖，现身时周围始终有微弱蓝色光环。

传记

奥丁的具体情况不详，但强烈暗示他就是英灵神殿背景中经常提及的北欧神祇。在北欧神话中，他通常被描述为长须、持矛、独眼的男子。

北欧神话中，奥丁想悄然穿行世界树诸界时，通常装扮成披斗篷、拄杖的旅人。游戏暗示他不时现身，观察玩家在英灵神殿世界中的进展。

北欧神话中，奥丁也以渡鸦作为密探和信使。引导阵亡维京人迈出英灵神殿中第一步的渡鸦福金，就是神话中奥丁两只具名密探渡鸦之一。

对待其他种族

符文石描述奥丁针对整个种族而非个体采取行动，原因是他们的傲慢或对抗：

  • 他“推翻华纳神族、巨人及比其他一切更古老的生物”，将其中最强者放逐至英灵神殿，成为弃誓者。[ 1 ]
  • 其战士成为尸鬼的族群，“挑战诸神，与阿萨和华纳神族开战，彻底毁灭自身。伟大的奥丁夷平了他们的城市”。[ 2 ]
  • 丑地精“曾建起媲美人类的高塔和城市，直到奥丁惩罚他们的傲慢。他践踏丑地精军队……摧毁他们的城市。”[ 3 ]
  • 谈及米德加尔特的巨魔：“奥丁的意志将他们逼回幽暗之地。”[ 4 ]
  • 巨人是“奥丁所有亲族中最古老者，也是他的所有敌人中最凶猛者”。[ 5 ]

位置

玩家击败古树长老后，奥丁才会在夜间短暂出现。玩家靠得太近时，他会提前化为蓝色烟雾消失。

说明

  • 游戏内有关奥丁的背景故事描述他化为拄杖老人，还称他戴宽檐旅行帽。但游戏中的奥丁实际戴遮脸兜帽。
  • 使用控制台命令“spawn odin”，该身影会短暂出现在角色前方，随后消失。
  • 出现300秒后、任一玩家进入20米内，或所有玩家都在50米外时，他会消失。
  • 奥丁不会受到玩家或敌对生物造成的任何伤害。
  • 奥丁是具有实体的完整对象，在光滑地形生成时会滑动。
  • 部分玩家将他误认成死神。
  • 1.0正式版发布前，Oden使用更常见的名称Odin。

Oden is a character found in Valheim. He is a mysterious black cloaked figure with a single glowing blue eye, holding a walking stick in his right hand. A faint blue aura persists around him as long as he remains.

Biography

Nothing is known about Oden, however, he is heavily implied to be the Nordic deity that is heavily referenced in Valheim's lore. His appearance in Norse mythology is often described as a man with long beard, a spear, and a single eye.

According to Norse mythology, when Oden wants to travel unnoticed through the realms of Yggdrasil, he typically disguises himself as a cloaked traveler carrying a walking stick. It is implied that he appears from time to time to observe the progress of the player through the world of Valheim.

Oden is also known in Norse mythology to use ravens as his spies and couriers. Hugin, the raven guiding slain Vikings through their first steps in Valheim, is one of the two named ravens that were Odin's spies in Norse mythology.

Treatment of other races

Runestones describe Oden acting against whole races rather than individuals. The texts give their pride or their opposition to him as the reason:

  • He "threw down the Vanir, the giants and those creatures older than any others", exiling the greatest of them to Valheim as the Forsaken.[1]
  • The race whose warriors became the Draugr "challenged the very Gods and went to war against the Aesir and the Vanir, destroying themselves completely. Great Oden levelled their cities".[2]
  • The Fuling "once built towers and cities to rival those of men until Oden punished them for their pride. He trampled the Fuling armies [...] and tore down their cities."[3]
  • Of the Trolls of Midgard: "The will of Oden has pushed them back into the dark places."[4]
  • The Jotunn were "most ancient of all Oden's kin and fiercest of all his adversaries".[5]

Location

Oden only appears once players have defeated The Elder, at night and for a short period of time. Should the player get too close to Oden, he will also disappear prematurely into a blue aura of smoke.

Notes

  • In-game lore regarding Oden describes him as taking on the form of an old man leaning on a stick. However, the lore also describes him as wearing a wide-brimmed traveler's hat, whereas as the Odin in-game wears a hood that obscures his face.
  • Using the console command "spawn odin" the figure will briefly appear in front of the character before vanishing.
  • He will despawn after 300 seconds, if any player gets within 20 meters, or if all players are more than 50 meters away.
  • Oden does not take damage of any kind from the player or aggressive creatures.
  • Oden is a solid and proper entity, if he spawns on slippery terrain, he will slide.
  • Some players mistake him for The Grim Reaper.
  • Prior to 1.0 launch, Oden was referred to as Odin, his more common name.
