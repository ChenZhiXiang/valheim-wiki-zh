获取方式

在以下兴趣点战利品箱中，与青铜防护神像一起出现：

  • 青青草原：废弃房屋、废弃农场、维京墓地。
  • 黑暗林山：巨魔洞穴、诡秘墓穴、废弃前哨、废弃小棚。

用途

每次在潜能熔炉尝试消耗一座神像，不需其他材料。65%概率使品质升一级，可超过4级；失败则摧毁物品，返还部分材料。

The Bronze Battle Idol is an idol used to upgrade items at the Forge of Potential.

Availability

It is found in loot chests at these points of interest, together with the Bronze Protection Idol:

  • Meadows: Abandoned House, Abandoned Farm, Viking Graveyard.
  • Black Forest: Troll Cave, Burial Chambers, Abandoned Outpost, Abandoned Hut.

Usage

Each attempt at the Forge of Potential consumes one idol and costs no other materials. It has a 65% chance to raise the item's quality by one level, even above level 4; otherwise the item is destroyed and part of its materials are returned.

The Bronze Battle Idol upgrades the following 11 items:

Weapons

  •  Bronze Atgeir
  •  Bronze Mace
  •  Bronze Spear
  •  Bronze Sword
  •  Copper Knife
  •  Finewood Bow
  •  Flint Axe
  •  Nord Atgeir
  •  Paws of the Bear
  •  Wooden Sword

Tools

  •  Bronze Pickaxe

Notes

  • Unlike the other Nord weapons, which use the Bloodgold Battle Idol, the Nord Atgeir uses this idol.
