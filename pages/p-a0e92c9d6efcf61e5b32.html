焰金属护甲是一套灰烬之地级别的护甲，比甲壳护甲和埃达织物套装高一级。同级套装包括恩布拉套装和阿斯克套装。

胸甲和护腿具有耐热效果，可降低熔岩造成的伤害，并延长在沸水中游泳而不受伤害的时间。此效果不影响火焰伤害或燃烧中造成的伤害。

这套护甲由三件组成，可在黑熔炉处制作和升级。

Flametal Armor is an Ashlands-tier armor. This set is the tier above Carapace Armor and Eitr-weave Set. Equal level sets include Embla Set and Ask Set.

The breastplate and greaves have a heat resistance effect which reduces the damage done by Lava and extends the time that you can swim in Boiling water without taking damage. It does not affect fire damage or damage from burning.

This set of armor has three pieces which are crafted and upgraded at the Black Forge.
