玩家基地是特定建筑周围生成的隐形区域，具有以下效果：

  • 阻止生物生成，包括敌对和被动生物，也包括来自事件的生物，但不包括实体生成器。
  • 防止掉在地上满1小时的物品消失，但位于2米水深以下的物品除外。
  • 玩家40米内有3个玩家基地建筑时，允许触发事件。

具有玩家基地效果的地点

部分自然生成的地点含有玩家基地区域，尤其是灰烬之地。通常，带有灰石屋顶或灰烬藤蔓的自然生成建筑会有1个玩家基地区域，神秘的地点除外。潜能熔炉周围具有半径20米的玩家基地区域。

说明

  • 可用工作台范围检查基地设施的覆盖区域。右键锤子并选择工作台，地面会显示白色建造范围圆，其半径也是20米。
  • 能够寻路到达玩家基地建筑的敌对生物，大多会一见到它们就攻击。仅篝火、营火和两种火盆例外。
  • 篝火、火把等可点燃建筑，即使没有点燃，也仍然提供玩家基地效果。
  • 若目的是阻止刷怪：
    • 工作台会显示作用半径，因此建工作台是确保覆盖区域没有空隙的最简单方法。
    • 需要覆盖大面积区域时，木制火炬可能是最便宜的选择。
    • 营火造价低，无需防御敌人，体积也小，必要时容易隐藏，例如用锄头将其上方地形整平。注意，此时锄头必须使用“平整地面”功能；使用“抬高地面”会使营火消失。
  • 玩家基地不影响浆果和其他可采集物的重新生成。

Player base is an invisible area created around certain structures. The effects are:

  • Prevents creatures from spawning (both hostile and passive). This also includes creatures from events. This does not include physical spawners.
  • Prevents items from despawning after being 1 hour on the ground (unless under 2 meters of water).
  • Allows events to trigger if the player has 3 player base structures within 40 meters.

Note: The range of these crafting station is briefly reduced to 20 meters when a zone is reloaded. This allows creatures to spawn within the extended player base radius shortly after a zone is reloaded.

Location with player base

Some naturally-generated locations include an instance of player base, notably in the Ashlands. As a rule of thumb, any naturally-generated building with Grausten Roofs or Ashvines has 1 instance of player base, except for Mysterious Locations. The Forge of Potential has a 20 meters radius player base around it.

Notes

  • Workbench radius can be used to check the covered area of a player base structure. Right-click the hammer and select workbench. A white circle is displayed on the ground to show the build radius which is also 20 meters.
  • Most player base structures are attacked on sight by hostile enemies that can path to them. The only exceptions are campfires, bonfires, and both kinds of brazier.
  • Structures that can be lit, such as campfires and torches, still provide the player base effect when they are not lit.
  • For the goal of preventing spawns:
    • Building workbenches is the easiest way to ensure there are no gaps because the effect radius is shown.
    • Standing Wood Torch may be the cheapest option if a large area needs to be covered.
    • Campfires are cheap, do not need to be protected from enemies, and they are small so they can be hidden from player view easier if that is desirable (such as terrain levelled over it with a Hoe). Please note that in this case the Hoe needs to be used with "Level ground" ability. When "Raise ground" is used the Campfires will disappear.
  • Player base has no effect on respawning of berries and other pickables.
