邪蛛士兵是迷雾之地及腐疫矿区中的敌对生物。

邪蛛侦察兵是大型耐打虫状生物，兼具甲虫、蚂蚁和蜜蜂特征。普通邪蛛探索者像蚂蚁，侦察兵则像锹甲，有强劲口钳及圆润甲壳。与普通邪蛛不同，侦察兵没有翅膀。

袭击与能力

邪蛛侦察兵战斗策略参见 敌对生物战斗策略 § 邪蛛侦察兵.

撞击

先后伸出两只前爪，再用头部向前撞击，造成100点钝击、100点4级砍伐及100点4级镐击伤害。冷却8秒，玩家在4米内时可用，命中4米、45度的水平弧形范围。

咬伤

后仰再向下挥动口钳夹击玩家，造成100点劈砍伤害，覆盖4米、45度的水平弧形范围。玩家在4米内时每8秒可用一次。

砸地

举起一只前肢砸下，再用另一只重复，每击造成120点钝击、100点4级砍伐及100点4级镐击伤害。命中每只爪周围半径4米的圆形区域，玩家在5米内时每12秒可用一次。

说明

邪蛛侦察兵能破坏玩家通常需要黑金属工具才能采集的资源，包括黑色大理石矿、世界树嫩枝、上古之剑及远古护甲。撞击和砸击有此能力，撕咬没有。因此理论上无需黑金属即可大量获得黑大理石和世界之树木，也能在地下圣堂外获取比其他方法更多的废铁。

与邪蛛侦察兵战斗时，要记住其碎石能力。玩家在岩石顶部远程攻击时，它常会直接摧毁岩石。

Seeker Soldiers are aggressive creatures found in the Mistlands and in Infested Mines.

Seekers soldiers are large, highly durable insect-like creatures with traits similar to beetles, ants, and bees. While ordinary Seekers resemble ants, Soldiers bear a resemblance to stag beetles, with powerful mandibles and a rounded, armored carapace. Unlike ordinary Seekers, Soldiers do not have wings.

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating Seeker soldiers, see Aggressive creature strategies § Seeker soldier.

Ram

Brings one claw forward, followed by the other, before slamming forward with their head (100 Blunt, Tier 4 100 Chop and 100 Tier 4 Pickaxe damage). This has an 8-second cooldown and can be used with 4 meters of a player. It hits in a 4-meter 45-degree horizontal arc.

Bite

Leans back before sweeping downward to clamp the player with their pincers (100 Slash damage). This creates a 4-meter 45-degree horizontal arc and can be used every 8 seconds when within 4 meters of a player.

Ground slam

Brings one arm up and slams it down, followed by the same motion with the other arm (120 Blunt, Tier 4 100 Chop and 100 Tier 4 Pickaxe damage for each hit). This hits in a 4-meter radius circle around each claw and can be used every 12 seconds when within 5 meters of a player.

Notes

Seeker Soldiers are capable of breaking various resources that would otherwise require a Black Metal tool for the player to break, including black marble deposits, Yggdrasil shoots, and Ancient Sword and Ancient Armor. Both their tackle and slam attacks have this property, but not their bite attack. This means that it is theoretically possible for the player to obtain large quantities of both Black Marble and Yggdrasil Wood without Black Metal, and it is possible to get much larger quantities of Scrap Iron outside Sunken Crypts than with other methods.

This ability to smash rocks should be remembered when fighting Seeker Soldiers. If a player is sniping them from the top of a rock, the Seeker Soldier will often simply demolish the rock.
