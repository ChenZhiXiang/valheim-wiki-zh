击败各首领并将其战利品挂在献祭石阵上，即可解锁放逐之力。默认按按钮 F激活当前放逐之力。同一时间只能选用一种，可在献祭石碑处切换。

所有放逐之力持续5分钟，共用20分钟冷却。退出游戏、更换当前放逐之力或睡觉均不会重置或缩短冷却。

激活放逐之力时，玩家会进入持续数秒的动画，无法移动或执行其他操作，但可装备或卸下工具、武器。

激活放逐之力时，10米内所有玩家也会获得效果。因此四名玩家依次使用各自的放逐之力，几乎可无限维持效果。

赤血灵鹿 力量

“你跑和跳的技能更强了。”

减少 60% 的 耐力 消耗，适用于奔跑、跳跃和游泳。

古树长老 力量

“伐木速度加快。”

砍伐和镐击伤害提高+60%，生命恢复提高+30%。

邪骨恶灵 力量

“减少受到的物理伤害。”

对穿刺、挥砍及钝击伤害获得轻微抗性（-25%），格挡耐力消耗-100%，每次格挡恢复+5点耐力。

冰霜龙母 力量

“航行时总是顺风。”

航行时始终顺风，负重提高+300.0，移动速度提高+10%，对冰霜获得抗性（-50%）。

说明

  • 此力量会在航行时改变风方向，但不改变风力。微风的助航效果不如强风。
  • 只有操舵时风向才会改变。没有操控船只时，风仍保持正常方向。
  • 风向改变仅作用于各自拥有增益的玩家，不影响其他玩家的风向。
  • 此力量最好留到逆风时使用，因为侧风，即垂直于航行方向的风，与顺风的航行效果大致相同。
  • 太靠近世界边缘时，此力量不会改变风向。

亚格鲁斯 力量

“减少受到的魔法和闪电伤害。”

对闪电获得抗性（-50%），农耕（技能）增加+25，伤害提高+10%。

蛊虫之后的 力量

“采矿速度加成以及埃达之能恢复速度加成。”

埃达之能恢复提高+100%，潜行耐力消耗-100%，对毒素获得抗性（-50%）。

青焰龙王 力量

“翡翠之焰永不退让。即使脚下燃烧，也须坚守，利用战场提供的一切优势。”

血怒提高+100%，硬直计量条修正值降低50%，对火焰获得抗性（-50%）。

说明

  • 邪骨恶灵之力的伤害抗性不会与水晶之心叠加。
  • 将凌冽·芬布尔使者的战利品挂在献祭石碑上不会获得放逐之力，而会结束游戏。

Forsaken powers are unlocked by defeating each boss and hanging their trophy on the Sacrificial Stones. By default, the player's set forsaken power is activated by pressing F. Only one forsaken power can be set at a time, which is changed out at the Sacrificial Stones.

All forsaken powers have a duration of 5 minutes. They also share the same 20-minute cooldown, which does not reset or reduce even when the player logs off, changes their active forsaken power, or sleeps.

Activating a power locks the player into an animation for a couple of seconds. The player is unable to move or perform any action (with the exception of equipping or unequipping tools/weapons).

When activating a forsaken power, all players within 10 meters will also receive the benefit of the forsaken power activated. Thus, a party of four players can keep a forsaken power activated virtually indefinitely by consecutively using each player's forsaken power.

Eikthyr Power

"The grace of the mighty stag flows through you. Your ability to move around is improved, be it on land or in water."

60% reduced stamina usage for running, jumping, and swimming.

The Elder Power

"The power of the forest invigorates you. You shall gather its bounties faster, just as you shall regain your own vitality should you lose it."

+60% Chop and Pickaxe damage increase, +30% health regeneration increase.

Bonemass Power

"The mass takes the brunt of your blows. The effort required to raise your shield lessens, and you can withstand harder hits."

Slightly resistant (-25%) VS pierce, slash, and blunt damage, -100% block stamina cost, +5 stamina returned per block.

Moder Power

"The dragon's wings urge you on. You shall always have tailwind while sailing, and you shall swiftly carry heavy burdens, even over the coldest mountains."

Always tailwind when sailing, +300.0 increased carry weight, +10% increased movement speed, Resistant (-50%) vs frost.

Notes

  • This power changes the wind direction while sailing, but not wind intensity. A calm wind will not aid in travel as much as a fast wind.
  • The wind direction is only changed while steering the ship. While not in control of a ship, the wind will remain at its regular angle.
  • The wind change is local to each player carrying the buff and does not affect each other's wind direction.
  • This power is best saved for when there is headwind since crosswind (perpendicular to travel direction) and tailwind are about equally good for sailing.
  • This power will not affect the wind if you are too close to the World's Edge.

Yagluth Power

"The fallen king lends you his might. A king must have the power to vanquish his foes, just as he must be able to support his people."

Resistant (-50%) vs lightning, +25 Farming (skill), and +10% increased damage.

The Queen's Power

"Her Majesty blesses you. In her shadow both stealth and magic may flourish, and no poison shall harm you."

+100% Eitr regeneration increase, -100% sneak stamina usage, and resistant (-50%) vs poison.

Fader Power

"The Emerald Flame never relents. You shall stand your ground, even when it is aflame, and you shall use all the advantages the battle may offer you."

+100% Adrenaline increase, 50% reduced stagger meter modifier, and resistant (-50%) vs fire.

Notes

  • Bonemass Power damage resistance does not stack with Crystal Heart.
  • Kall Fimbulbringer does not provide a Forsaken Power upon hanging his trophy on the Sacrificial Stones, as doing so ends the game instead.
