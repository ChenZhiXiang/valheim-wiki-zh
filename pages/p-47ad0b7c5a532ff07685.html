说明

入口与正常模式黑暗林山地下城诡秘墓穴很相似，但两侧各有两座点燃的常设铁火炬。

虽然由骷髅怪和幽灵守卫，最大威胁仍是小首领布伦娜。

可找到箱子、焰灵之核、黄蘑菇、白骨碎片和钱币。

战略

布伦娜战斗策略参见 首领攻略 § 布伦娜.

说明

  • 可在希尔德的的地图桌获取这座特殊地下城及独特小首领的位置。
  • 地下城通常很暗，带上火炬或矮人头环。
  • 地牢内可放营火和烹饪台，供玩家休息。
  • 出口互动判定有错位漏洞。若无法离开，将准星对准白色矩形右上角，即可出现互动提示。

Description

The entrance looks quite similar to normal Black Forest dungeons, the Burial Chambers, but with two lit Standing Iron Torches on each side.

Even guarded by Skeletons and Ghosts, the biggest threat is the miniboss Brenna.

You may find chests, Surtling Cores, Yellow Mushrooms, Bone Fragments and Coins.

Notes

  • This special dungeon, with its special miniboss, can be obtained at Hildir's map table.
  • Bring Torches or a Dverger Circlet, as dungeons are usually very dark.
  • Campfire and Cooking Station can be placed inside dungeons to allow the player to rest.
  • There is a bug where the exit hitbox is misaligned. If you cannot leave, aim towards the top right corner of the white rectangle for the interaction prompt to appear.
