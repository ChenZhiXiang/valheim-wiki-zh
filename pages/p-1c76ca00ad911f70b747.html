灰烬长船是大型高速船只，储物容量极大，但操纵性较差。它也是唯一能穿越灰烬之地沸腾海水而不受损的船只。

灰烬长船是建造成本最高的船只，速度几乎与维京长船相同，但以灵活性换取更高耐久度和储物空间，适合携带大量货物并承受较多伤害。

规格

建码头时可参考尺寸：船长35.2米、宽12.5米，横桁宽15.2米，从船底到桅杆顶高19.7米。

储物空间有32个物品格。

除舵位外，灰烬长船还有17个座位，包括九个凳位和八个长椅座位。玩家也可在桅杆和船首处“抓紧”。

Drakkars are large, high-speed boats with exceptional storage capacity but poor maneuverability, and the only boats capable of traversing the boiling waters of the Ashlands without being damaged.

Drakkars are the most expensive boats to construct compared to other boats. They can move nearly as fast as Longships, but trade agility for greater durability and storage space, which makes them suitable for withstanding significant damage while carrying large loads.

Specifications

This boat is 35.2 meters long, 12.5 meters wide (15.2 meters across the sail yard) and 19.7 meters tall from the bottom of the hull to the top of the mast, for those who wish to build a dock.

The storage space has 32 item slots.

The Drakkar has 17 seats (nine stools and eight bench seats) besides the helm, and players can also "Hold Fast" at the mast and at the bow.
