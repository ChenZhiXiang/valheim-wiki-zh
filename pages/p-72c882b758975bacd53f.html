用途

完整命令如下： 

spawn [ItemName] [Amount] [Level]
  • ItemName：要生成的物品或非玩家角色名称，不区分大小写。
  • Amount：要生成的物品或非玩家角色数量
  • Level：仅用1-4数字，表示生物或物品等级。所有生物初始为等级1，因此无需显式输入1。仅要生成等级2或3时需要，血条下分别显示一星或二星。

实例: 

spawn AxeFlint
  • 上述命令会生成1把燧石斧。
spawn AxeFlint 3
  • 上述命令会生成3把燧石斧。
spawn Wood 50
  • 上述命令生成50份独立木材，并非一堆。
spawn Deer 1
  • 上述命令会生成一只鹿。
spawn Deer 1 3
  • 上述命令生成一只等级3的鹿，血条下有二星。

移除生成商人的唯一* 已知*方法是forcedelete命令，会清除玩家5米内全部建筑、物品和实体。

生成器说明：多数仅使用一次，生成生物首次被杀后便永久失效。持续生成生物或物品的，在类别列标为重生生成器。

Usage

The full command is as follows: 

spawn [ItemName] [Amount] [Level]
  • ItemName - the name of the item or NPC you want to spawn (case insensitive)
  • Amount - the number of items or NPCs you want to spawn
  • Level - (only numbers between 1-4) the level of the mob or item you want to spawn. All mobs start at level 1, so using "1" for the level is unnecessary. This is only needed if you want a mob to spawn that is level 2 or 3 (one or two stars beneath the health bar, respectively).

Examples: 

spawn AxeFlint
  • The above will spawn 1 flint axe.
spawn AxeFlint 3
  • The above will spawn 3 flint axes.
spawn Wood 50
  • The above will spawn 50 individual pieces of wood (not a stack).
spawn Deer 1
  • The above will spawn a single Deer.
spawn Deer 1 3
  • The above will spawn a single level 3 Deer (a Deer with two stars beneath its health bar).

The only *known* way to remove spawned in traders is through the command 'forcedelete' clearing all buildings, items, and entities within a 5 meter radius of the player.

Spawners note: Most mob spawners are single time use thus once the mob they spawn is killed once the spawner is done forever. Spawner that will continuously spawn new mobs/items are labeled as Respawners in the category column.
