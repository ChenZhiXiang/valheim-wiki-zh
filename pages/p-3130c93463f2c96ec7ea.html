符文台用于升级古咒法台，与永恒之烛和轻羽花环一起，是目前古咒法台的三项升级之一。建造材料可通过传送门运输。

与其他可任意放在范围内的制作设施升级不同，此设施必须连接古咒法台才能有效放置。

The Rune table is used as an upgrade for the Galdr Table. It is one of the three upgrades currently available to the Galdr table, along with Unfading Candles and Feathery wreath. Its building materials can be taken through a portal.

Also, unlike the other craft station upgrades which can be placed anywhere and provides buffs within range, this upgrade building's placement is only valid connected to the Galdr Table.
