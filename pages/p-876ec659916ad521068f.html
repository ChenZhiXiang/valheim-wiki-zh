连锁闪电
效果
75伤害，有概率弹射至附近敌人

连锁闪电是用堇青石或雷血精华升级的武器触发的命中效果。

效果

普通攻击有25%概率触发连锁闪电，次要攻击有20%概率。

对目标造成75闪电伤害，不受技能影响，无法格挡或闪避。

触发后有80%概率弹向附近敌人或玩家。若发生，8米内每个目标有30%概率被选中，每次最多弹向2–4个目标，上限随机确定，再从最近目标开始处理。随后以新目标为中心重复，每次弹跳概率乘0.75，即80% → 60% → 45% → 33.75%，依此类推，可无限重复。

一次连锁闪电不会对同一目标生效多次。

说明

  • 附近有中立灰烬之地矮人时，连锁闪电可能误伤，使其立即敌对。

Chain lightning
Effects
75 damage, chance to bounce off nearby enemies

Chain lightning is an on-hit effect triggered by weapons upgraded with Iolite or Thunderblood Essence.

Effect

Chain lightning has a 25% chance to trigger on a normal attack and a 20% chance to trigger on a secondary attack.

It deals 75 lightning damage to the target, the amount is not affected by skills, and it cannot be blocked or dodged.

Once triggered, it has an 80% chance of bouncing off nearby enemies or players. If so, each nearby target within 8 meters has a 30% chance of being targeted, up to a maximum of 2–4 targets per bounce (the maximum is chosen at random then targets are processed starting from the nearest). The process repeats indefinitely, centered on each new target, but with the chance of rebound multiplied by 0.75 each time (i.e., 80% → 60% → 45% → 33.75%, etc.).

A single activation of chain lightning cannot affect the same target more than once.

Notes

  • Chain lightning within the vicinity of neutral Ashlands Dvergr has the potential of damaging them, immediately turning them hostile.
