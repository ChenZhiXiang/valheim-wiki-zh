丑地精是北境平原中的敌对生物，尤其常见于地精村落。它们形似小地精，似乎有自己的部落文化。符文石和图腾暗示它们崇拜早已死去的国王亚格鲁斯。

丑地精的持棍、持矛、持剑及持火把变体，出现概率分别为33%、17%、33%和17%。

所有丑地精生成时都有一件护甲，提供60护甲值；头盔、臂环、肩甲和腿环各有25%概率。另有33%概率持木盾，与玩家可制作的相同。死亡时不会掉落装备。

袭击与能力

击败丑地精的策略见 敌对生物攻略 § 丑地精.

除只能投掷的矛外，各种武器都覆盖水平90度、距离2.4米的弧形。

棍棒

造成85点钝击伤害，冷却2秒，玩家在1.6米内时可用。

矛

向玩家投矛，造成85点穿刺伤害；无须拾回即可再次攻击。矛速20米/秒，最多飞行10秒，散布角4–20度。玩家在14米内时可用，冷却4秒。

剑

造成85点劈砍伤害，冷却2秒，玩家在1.6米内时可用。

火把

造成45点钝击和45点火焰伤害，施加燃烧中状态。冷却2秒，玩家在1.6米内时可用。

说明

  • 丑地精听觉格外敏锐，能从比其他敌人远得多的距离发现玩家。砍树和采矿很可能吸引它们。
  • 丑地精比较吵，独特的聊天声或笑声通常是附近有它们活动的最早线索。
  • 丑地精速度极快且灵活，但有时来不及识别玩家挖掘的沟边，导致跌入。
  • 丑地精游泳很慢。玩家可利用这一点，例如跳过天然或人工河流，再射击水中变慢的目标。
  • 多数英灵神殿生物取材于北欧神话，丑地精似乎是例外。最接近的是地精，这种生物因龙与地下城而普及，常出现在奇幻作品中。游戏文件将它们称为“Goblin”，也支持这一联系。
  • “Fuling”在现代瑞典语中可粗略译为“丑家伙”。

    背景故事

    “在平原上，你会发现丑地精的居所。这个古老种族曾建造可与人类媲美的高塔和城市，直到奥丁因它们骄傲而惩罚它们。他践踏军队，护甲在脚下如蜗牛壳般碎裂，又摧毁它们的城市。

    唯有伟大的术士亚格鲁斯，不会在他的怒火前屈服或折断。于是奥丁将其身体撕成两半，将残骸抛入英灵神殿。他的血渗入大地，身体崩解成尘，最后的族人为他哭泣。如今丑地精统治平原，野蛮凶残，心中只有仇恨和恐惧。但亚格鲁斯仍与它们同在，萨满从这股无形力量汲取扭曲魔法；族人遍布世界时，他的力量也再次增长……"

    ― 符文石

    符文石描写奥丁因丑地精的骄傲而惩罚整个族群，践踏军队、摧毁城市。对幸存者的描写带有种族偏见，将整个族群刻画为“野蛮凶残，心中只有仇恨与恐惧”。[ 1 ]

    Fulings are aggressive creatures found in Plains, especially within Fuling Villages. They resemble small, goblin-like beings that appear to possess their own tribalistic culture. It is implied through Runestones and totems that they worship Yagluth, a long dead king.

    Fulings appear 33% of the time as club variants, 17% of the time as spear variants, 33% of the time as sword variants and 17% of the time as torch variants.

    All Fulings spawn with one piece of armor each providing 60 armor value and having an equal 25% chance of appearing: helmet, armband, shoulders, and legband. Additionally, they have a 33% chance to spawn with a Wood Shield identical to the one players can craft. They do not drop equipment on death.

    Attacks and abilities

    For strategies for defeating Fulings, see Aggressive creature strategies § Fuling.

    Besides the spear, which can only be thrown, each weapon arcs horizontally by 90 degrees and extends 2.4 meters out.

    Club

    Does 85 Blunt damage. This attack has a 2-second cooldown and can be used within 1.6 meters of a player.

    Spear

    Throws a spear at the player, which does not need to be retrieved for Fulings to attack again (85 Pierce damage). The spear travels at 20 meters per second for up to 10 seconds before despawning and has a spread of 4–20 degrees. This has a 4-second cooldown and can be used within 14 meters of a player.

    Sword

    Does 85 Slash damage. This attack has a 2-second cooldown and can be used within 1.6 meters of a player.

    Torch

    Does 45 Blunt and 45 Fire damage, which applies the Burning status. This attack has a 2-second cooldown and can be used within 1.6 meters of a player.

    Notes

    • Fulings have unusually sharp hearing and will be able to detect the player from a much longer distance than other enemies. Chopping down trees or mining ore is likely to attract their attention.
    • Fulings are relatively noisy. The distinctive sound of their chatter or laughter will often be the first sign that they are active in an area.
    • Fulings are extremely fast and agile, but they sometimes fail to register the edge of a player-dug ditch until it is too late to avoid falling in.
    • Fulings are particularly slow swimmers. Adventurers may use this to their advantage, for instance, by jumping over a river (natural or player-made) while firing arrows at their now slow targets.
    • While most creatures in Valheim take inspiration from counterparts in Norse mythology, the Fuling seem to be an exception. The closest parallel would be the Goblin, more traditionally used in fantasy works since their popularization within the Dungeons & Dragons setting. This is further indicated by them being named "Goblin" in the game files.
    • "Fuling" can be roughly translated into "ugly one" in modern Swedish.

      Lore

      "Here on the plains you will find the dwellings of the Fuling, that ancient race who once built towers and cities to rival those of men until Oden punished them for their pride. He trampled the Fuling armies, their armour cracking like snail shells beneath his feet, and tore down their cities.

      Only Yagluth, the great sorcerer, would not bend or break before his fury. So Oden tore his body in half and cast the remains into Valheim, where he bled into the earth and crumbled to dust, wept over by the last of his people. Now the Fuling rule over the plains of Valheim, savage and wild with only hatred and fear in their hearts. But Yagluth is with them still, an unseen force that the Fuling shamans draw upon for their crooked magic, growing again in strength as his people spread across the world..."

      ― Runestone

      The runestone describes Oden punishing the Fuling as a whole people for their pride, trampling their armies and tearing down their cities, and its portrayal of the surviving Fuling is racially motivated: it stereotypes the entire race as "savage and wild with only hatred and fear in their hearts".[1]
