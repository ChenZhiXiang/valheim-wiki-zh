灰烬刚鬣蛋由灰烬之地生物群系中的灰烬刚鬣产下，详情参见灰烬刚鬣——繁殖。

孵化

置于火或热源附近30分钟后，蛋孵出灰烬刚鬣幼崽，之后长成成年灰烬刚鬣。蛋必须单个放置，不可堆叠，才能孵化。

与鸡蛋不同，灰烬刚鬣蛋有不同品质。图标右上角品质数字表示幼崽星级：品质“1”孵出普通幼崽，品质“2”孵出1星幼崽，品质“3”孵出2星幼崽。

Asksvin eggs are laid by Asksvin in the Ashlands biome. See Asksvin - Breeding for more details.

Hatching

When placed near a fire or heat source for 30 minutes, the egg will hatch and an Asksvin hatchling will spawn, which will then grow into an adult Asksvin. The egg must be individual - not in a stack - in order to hatch.

Unlike chicken eggs, Asksvin eggs come in different qualities. The number (quality) at the top right of the icon notes the level (stars) of the hatchling:
Quality "1" hatches into regular Askvin hatchlings
Quality "2" hatches into 1-star Askvin hatchlings
Quality "3" hatches into 2-star Askvin hatchlings.
