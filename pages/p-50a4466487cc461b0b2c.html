基本信息

铺平是地面装饰，在锄头菜单中选择该功能后对地面使用即可。

修整路面消耗耐力，但不需要材料或工作台。

小路仅有外观作用，在上面奔跑不会加速，也不会减少耐力消耗。

General information

Pathen is a ground decoration applied by selecting the option in the Hoe menu and using it on the ground.

Building sections of the path costs Stamina, but require neither materials nor a Workbench.

Paths are purely cosmetic, they do not give any speed boost or stamina reduction when running on them.
