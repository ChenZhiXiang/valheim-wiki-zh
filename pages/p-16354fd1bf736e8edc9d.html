铁制火炉是可放在任何表面上的光源。

用途

铁制火炉具有与营火相同的功能，属于基础设施。

铁制火炉使用木材作为燃料。装入1/10容量的燃料可燃烧约1小时23分钟，装满可燃烧约13小时53分钟。也可用于烹饪，但不如营火容易摆放。

铁制火炉的火效果范围约为7–8米。

说明

  • 用工具锤拆除铁制火炉不会返还木材，只返还建造所用的铁火炉。用作燃料和初始建造材料的木材都不会掉落。
  • 可在灰烬之地的矮人焦毁塔楼废墟中找到铁制火炉，摧毁后掉落1个铁制火炉物品，但它们受矮人的守护石保护。
  • 雨天没有屋顶遮蔽时，铁制火炉会熄灭。

趣闻

  • 铁制火炉是随希尔德请求更新加入的物品。

The Iron Fire Pit is a light source that can be placed on any surface.

Usage

The Iron Fire Pit has all the same functions as a Campfire and is a base structure.

Iron Fire Pits are fueled with Wood. With 1/10 fuel, an Iron Fire Pit will last for roughly 1 hour and 23 minutes. At maximum capacity, an Iron Fire Pit should burn for roughly 13 hours and 53 minutes. It can be used for cooking, however, it does not fit easily like a Campfire does.

The Fire Effect for the Iron Fire Pit extends for about 7–8 meters.

Notes

  • Destroying the Iron Fire Pit with a Hammer will not yield back any Wood and will only return the Iron Pit used for its construction. All Wood used as fuel and for the initial construction will not be dropped.
  • Iron Fire Pits can be found in Dvergr Charred Tower Ruins in the Ashlands, 1 Iron pit will drop if destroyed, however, they are protected by the Dvergr's Ward.
  • Iron Fire Pits stop working if exposed to rainy weather without a roof.

Trivia

  • Iron Fire Pit is an item which got introduced in the Hildir's Request update.
