沼泽女巫是泥泞沼泽生物群系中的灰矮人 商人。她在沼泽小屋出售独特食材及物品，可用钱币购买，更多商品随世界进度解锁。她的香料及餐盘是制作、食用宴席所必需的。

位置

世界生成时，会为沼泽女巫确定最多10处位置，均在泥泞沼泽中，彼此至少相隔1000米，距世界中心3000至8000米。靠近任意一处后，她永久定居，其他候选点失效。发现后，地图出现锅图标。

她与专属地点一起生成，小屋摆满各种药剂。入口旁有灰矮人，巫师扫帚不断打扫内部。小屋拥有三级舒适度，为玩家提供全部相关增益。

营地被闪烁的透明穹顶包围，形成生物无法进入的安全区，且无法摧毁。

The Bog Witch is a Greydwarf trader found in the Swamp biome. She sells unique ingredients and items from her swamp hut which can be purchased with Coins, with additional items unlocking as the world progresses. Her spices and serving tray are required to prepare and eat a Feast.

Location

During world generation, up to 10 spawn locations are determined for The Bog Witch. All spawn locations are within Swamp biomes, each at least 1000 meters apart, and only found between 3000 and 8000 meters from the world center. Approaching any of these locations will permanently place The Bog Witch there, nullifying all other potential spawns. When discovered, a cauldron icon will appear on the world map.

She spawns with her own point of interest, a small hut decorated with various potions. A Greydwarf sits next to the entrance, and a Kvastur constantly sweeps inside the hut interior. The hut has Comfort level three and grants the player all relevant buffs.

The camp is surrounded by a shimmering, transparent dome that acts as a safety zone that Creatures cannot enter. The safe zone cannot be destroyed.
