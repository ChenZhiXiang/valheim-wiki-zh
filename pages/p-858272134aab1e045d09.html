跳跃（技能）
说明
确定跳跃高度

跳跃是一种技能。

效果

随着成长，跳跃高度略微增加，最多40%。

升级

每次耐力足够时跳跃，跳跃技能获得0.5经验。

获取经验需支付完整跳跃耐力。零耐力跳动不会提升技能，耐力不足虽显示小跳动画，也无经验。起跳及落地高度、距离或跨越对象均无影响。

武器、护甲及奔跑技能等级不影响跳跃高度。

计算

跳跃100级可离地3米，奔跑100级时可向前跳超过5米。

现实世界跳高和跳远纪录分别2.45及8.95米。玩家跳远与顶尖运动员相比普通，但跳高会远超所有对手，尤其玩家采用并不适合跳高的跨栏姿势。

Jumping (skill)
Description
Determines jump height.

Jumping is a skill.

Effects

As the player progresses, their jump height will slightly increase, up to 40%.

Leveling up

Every time the player makes a jump with enough stamina, they gain 0.5 experience in the jump skill.

Note that to gain skill progression, the player must use the full stamina cost of the jump, hopping at zero stamina will not progress the skill (jumping with insufficient stamina will still show a small jump animation, but the player will not gain exp). The height the player jumps from or land from does not matter, nor does the distance or what the player jumps over.

Weapons, armor, and Run skill level do not affect jump height.

Calculations

At level 100 jump, the player can leap 3 meters off the ground, and at run 100, over 5 meters forward.

The overall world records for jump height and distance are 2.45 and 8.95 meters, respectively. The player's jumping distance would be mediocre against the world's best, but would overwhelmingly beat all competition in the high jump, especially considering the player hurdles, which is a very ineffective high jump strategy.
