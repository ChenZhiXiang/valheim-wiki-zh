材料堆和料堆是由大量单一材料堆成的设施，用于储存材料或装饰家园。

与其他建筑一样，可用工具锤摧毁或拆除，返还全部材料。

Stacks and Piles are structure made from a single materials in large quantity. They are used to store the materials, or simply decorate around a homestead.

Like other structures, they can be destroyed or deconstructed with a Hammer to return all the materials.
