感觉不舒服
“你感觉不太舒服，胃里的食物留不住了。
效果
  • 每2秒移除一种已食用的食物效果
  • 禁用生命值恢复
  • 禁用耐力值恢复
  • 减半移动速度
  • 每2秒造成1点伤害

感觉不舒服是食用兔兔梅、腐坏的肉或毒蕈后获得的负面状态效果。

效果持续15秒，期间生命值和耐力无法恢复，移动速度减半；每2秒受到1点伤害，并移除一种已食用的食物效果。

可用此效果清除当前食物，无需等待到期。

说明

  • 可用puke开发者命令施加此效果。

Feeling sick
"You don't feel so well and can't hold your food down.
Effects
  • Every 2 seconds undoes one consumed Food
  • Disables health regen
  • Disables stamina regen
  • Halves movement speed
  • Every 2 seconds deals 1 damage

Feeling sick is a negative status effect gained by consuming a Bukeperries, Rotten Meat or Toadstool.

For 15 seconds, it disables health and stamina regen, halves movement speed, and every 2 seconds it deals 1 damage and undoes one consumed Food.

The effect is useful to remove active food without having to wait past theirs expiration times.

Note

  • The effect can be inflicted with the puke dev command.
