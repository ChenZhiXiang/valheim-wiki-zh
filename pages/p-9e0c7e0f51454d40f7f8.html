烹饪(技能)
说明
烹饪速度、上菜托盘损耗和额外产出概率。

烹饪是一种技能。

效果

烹饪技能具有以下随等级成比例变化的效果：

  • 制作时间最多减少60%，适用于铸铁锅、灶台和蜜酒壶。
  • 最多有25%的概率额外制作一件产品。
    • 使用按住Shift，制作x5烹饪选项更划算，因为累积机制会进一步增加额外产出。可能获得+1、+3、+6、+10或+15，而同样5件物品按1件逐1件烹饪，仅额外+1至+5。满级时总体额外产出为+37.5%。
    • 若应产生额外成品，但会超过背包容量，制作会取消，背包不发生变化。此时是否仍获得经验尚未确认。
  • 餐盘耐力消耗最多降低50%。

从烹饪台、铁烹饪台和石砌烤炉取出物品时，也有概率获得额外成品。

升级

以下活动提供烹饪技能经验：

  • 制作一件食物获得0.25经验。
  • 将物品放上烹饪台可获得0.1经验。
  • 从烹饪设施取出物品获得0.15经验，包括木炭。

趣闻

  • 生食材会受到两次额外产出影响，分别是食物准备台制作及石烤炉取出时。因此满级总产出增加+56.25%。
  • 此技能于0.219.13 (沼泽女巫)版本加入。

Cooking (skill)
Description
Cooking speed, serving tray wear and chance for bonus yields.

Cooking is a skill.

Effects

The Cooking skill has the following effect, proportional to the level:

  • Reduces the time to craft by up to 60%; applies to Cauldron, Food Preparation Table and Mead Ketill.
  • Up to a 25% chance to craft one extra product.
    • Using the hold shift x5 cooking option is even more advantageous, as a cumulative mechanism adds even more additional items. Possible yields are +1, +3, +6, +10 and +15 (instead of +1 to +5 by cooking the same 5 items 1 by 1). This results in an overall +37.5% extra product at max skill level.
    • If an additional product should be crafted, but it would overflow the player's inventory, then the crafting is cancelled, and the player's inventory does not change. Whether this still rewards XP has not been confirmed.
  • Reduces Serving Tray stamina consumption by up to 50%.

The chance of an extra product also applies when picking the item from Cooking Station, Iron Cooking Station and Stone Oven.

Leveling up

The following activities give Cooking skill experience:

  • Crafting a food item gives 0.25 experience.
  • Placing an item on a cooking station gives 0.1 experience.
  • Picking an item from a cooking station gives 0.15 experience (Coal included).

Trivia

  • Uncooked foods are affected twice by the extra drop, when crafted at the Food preparation table and when picked from the Stone oven. This results in a +56.25% yield at max level.
  • This skill was added in version 0.219.13 (Bog Witch)
