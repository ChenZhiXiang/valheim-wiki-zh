墓碑

死亡后，玩家留下刻有自己名字的墓碑。

  • 地图用骷髅及交叉骨标记最后死亡位置，并注明死亡的天数。
  • 背包为空时死亡，不会出现墓碑。
  • 墓碑包含玩家全部装备，任何发现它的人都能取用。
  • 取回物品途中再次死亡，原墓碑仍会保留，物品不会丢失。
  • 原玩家与墓碑互动时，若背包空间足够，所有物品自动放回背包；否则不会自动转移，需要手动取回。
  • 关闭服务器或放置不管，都不会使墓碑消失。
  • 在深水中死亡会生成漂浮墓碑。
    • 墓碑会直接生成在熔岩顶部，因为它被视为一种地面。

重生

玩家在最后睡过的床铺旁重生。床被摧毁或从未睡过床时，会在献祭石阵重生。

经验损失

默认死亡惩罚下，死亡使每项技能等级降低5%。

由于升级经验需求按指数增长，这大致相当于失去每项技能累计经验的1/8。例如，花八小时练技能，死亡会损失约一小时成果。

效果

死亡后获得没有技能流失，防止再次死亡继续损失经验，持续10分钟。

取回墓碑物品后，获得持续50秒的跑尸形态，增强生存和逃离附近残留敌人的能力。

说明

  • 墓碑存在以下相关问题：
    • 地下城内死亡，可能使墓碑生成在无法到达的位置，例如天花板上方。
    • 多人中死亡时断线，可能导致背包清空却没有生成墓碑。
    • 此时最好加载自动备份，至少恢复部分物品。
  • 即使物品取回，地图骷髅标记也不会消失，会一直标记死亡位置，直到玩家右键手动删除。

Tombstone

Upon death, the player leaves behind a tombstone with the player's name on it.

  • The map is marked with skull and bones to indicate the player's last death location, with a notation of the day that the player died.
  • No tombstone will appear if the player dies with nothing in their inventory.
  • The tombstone contains all of the player equipment and is accessible by anyone who finds it.
  • When dying on the way to retrieve the items, the tombstone will stay and the items will not be lost.
  • When the original player interacts with the tombstone, all items that are in the tombstone are automatically placed back in the player's inventory if they have enough room for it, otherwise nothing will happen and the player will have to manually transfer the items.
  • The tombstone does not expire when the server is closed or if left be.
  • Dying in deep waters will spawn a floating tombstone instead.
    • Tombstones will spawn directly on top of lava, as it is considered a type of ground.

Respawning

Players respawn by the bed they last slept in. If the bed is destroyed or the player has never slept in one, they respawn at the Sacrificial Stones.

Experience loss

Upon death, the player will lose 5% of their total levels in each skill if the world is on the default death penalty.

Due to the exponential cost of levels, this is approximately equal to losing 1/8 of the total experience gained in each skill. For example, spending eight hours grinding the desired skill, death will mean the loss of one hour.

Effects

After a death, the effect No skill drain is obtained and will prevent any further loss of experience due to additional deaths. The effect duration is 10 minutes.

After collecting their gravestone, the player gains Corpse run effect, which lasts for 50 seconds and gives the player a better ability to survive and run away from any enemies that may still be nearby.

Notes

  • There are certain problems related to tombstones:
    • Dying inside dungeons may cause a tombstone to generate outside the accessible area, such as above the ceiling.
    • Being disconnected upon dying in multiplayer may cause the player inventory to empty but no tombstone to generate.
    • In such cases, it is best to load an automatic backup to at least partially recover the inventory.
  • The skull-and-crossbones icon on the map will not disappear even after the player's items are recovered, permanently marking the death location until the player removes it from their map manually (by right-clicking).
