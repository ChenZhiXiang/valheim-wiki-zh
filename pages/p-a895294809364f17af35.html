建材

用工具锤在制作标签中放置大锅。放置时不需要火源，使用和制作时才需要。

玩家首次拾取冶炼得到的锡，并已建造熔炉后，才会解锁大锅配方。

升级大锅可解锁更多配方。在附近建造以下设施，最多可升至等级7：

The Cauldron is a crafting station used to prepare food. It requires a fire source underneath to operate, such as a Campfire or Hearth.

Building

Cauldrons can be placed using the Hammer, found under the Crafting tab. A fire does not need to be present to place a Cauldron, only to use/craft with it.

The Cauldron recipe is unlocked only when the player picks up Tin for the first time after smelting it and has a Forge built.
