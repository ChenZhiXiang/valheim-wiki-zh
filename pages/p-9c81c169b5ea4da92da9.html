屠夫台用于升级铸铁锅。由于成本中等，通常是大锅的第二项升级。

用途

等级3铸铁锅可制作的物品包括：

  • 眼激凌——在家园与炉火中加入。
  • 狼肉干——在家园与炉火中加入。
  • 狼肉串——在家园与炉火中加入。
  • 其他升级设施见铸铁锅。

说明

  • 屠夫台随家园与炉火于2021年九月16日加入游戏。

The Butcher's table is used as an upgrade for the Cauldron. It will typically be the second upgrade for the cauldron due to its medium cost.

Usage

Level 3 Cauldron items include:

  •  Eyescream - introduced in Hearth and Home.
  •  Wolf Jerky - introduced in Hearth and Home.
  •  Wolf Skewer - introduced in Hearth and Home.
  • For other upgrades, see Cauldron.

Notes

  • The Butcher's table was introduced in Hearth and Home on September 16th, 2021.
