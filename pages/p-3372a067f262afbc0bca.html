灰烬之鹫蛋可在灰烬之地生物群系的秃鹫巢穴内找到，也由灰烬之鹫掉落。

与其他蛋不同，灰烬之鹫蛋不会孵出灰烬之鹫。

Volture Eggs can be found inside Volture Nests in the Ashlands biome. They are also dropped by Voltures.

Unlike other eggs, Volture Eggs do not hatch into Voltures.
