传记

根据英灵神殿中的多块符文石，阿斯特丽德是一位盾女，死后被送往炼狱之地。她已在英灵神殿度过至少七年，似乎从世界一端走到另一端，在各个生态区留下描述自身生活的符文石。

符文石

青青草原

我是阿斯特丽德，森林中的盾女。来到这里之前的人生，我已一无所知，但我的手臂仍记得如何挥剑，双眼仍能看清箭矢的轨迹。

如今渡鸦指引着我，我以奥丁之名与巨兽作战。

凛冽雪山

这个石头被我安插,阿斯特丽德 在我第七年里英灵神殿。此时此刻该全能的父亲和我说话。 我醒来了，材料来源：深处睡觉发现他的话散落在我身边该 地面被冰冻为石块该深处冷。 当我在手上取暖时,它们就融化了与他给我讲了他的信息,一个接一个地说。

杀死被遗弃者，把他们的头颅用锁链拴起来。我会到来。

灰烬之地

我是长臂的阿斯特丽德，历经艰难来到此地。我为战兽而来，打破禁锢，乌鸦带回了一段记忆，让我不得安宁。我愿死多少次也罢，只求再见儿子一面。

趣闻

Biography

According to multiple runestones located in Valheim, Astrid was a shield-maiden who died, thus being sent to the purgatory lands. She has spent at least seven years in Valheim and has seemingly traveled the world from end to end, leaving behind her various runestones detailing her life among the various biomes.

Runestone

Meadows

I was Astrid, a shieldmaiden of the forest. I know nothing of my life before I came here but my arm remembers the sword and my eyes see the course of the arrows.

Now the raven guides me and I fight the great beasts in Odin's name.

Mountain

This stone was placed by me, Astrid, in my seventh year in Valheim. At this spot, the Allfather spoke to me. I awoke from a deep sleep to find his words scattered around me on the ground, frozen to pebbles by the deep cold. When I warmed them in my hands they thawed and spoke his message to me, one word after another.

Slay the Forsaken. Chain their heads. I will come.

Ashlands

I am Astrid of the Long Arm. I came here after much hardship to fight the beast and break the curse that keeps me here. The raven has returned a memory to me and it will not let me rest. I will die as many times as I need to die to see my son again.

Trivia

  • Historically, runestones were raised by people in Scandinavia and other places visited by the Norsemen. They were often memorials to dead men, written testaments to important events or even used as territorial markers.
