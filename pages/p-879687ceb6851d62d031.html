蛊虫之后是第六位首领，是巨大的邪蛛探索者族母，外观与其他邪蛛明显不同。躯体像幼虫，类似邪蛛幼体，四条“手臂”均靠近头部，独特口部排列细小尖牙。与其他邪蛛共有的特征包括类似邪蛛士兵的大口钳、普通邪蛛的触角，以及两种邪蛛都有的稀薄细毛。

召唤地点

蛊虫之后的弃誓者祭坛位于迷雾之地。与之前所有首领不同，必须在地下城腐疫地堡中战斗，首次挑战无需献祭。腐疫矿区内的指路符文可定位最近感染堡垒，但进入需要封印破除者，由同样在矿洞找到的碎片制作。

蛊虫之后在堡垒底层生成，被激怒前四处游荡。首次击败后，可在顶部蜂巢座献上3个邪蛛侦察兵战利品再次召唤。

袭击与能力

蛊虫之后战斗策略参见 首领攻略 § 蛊虫之后.

远程传送

生命低于90%时，蛊虫之后最多每60秒可钻入地下，在200米内其他位置出现。此技能仅限腐疫地堡，且玩家需在400米内。

冲刺

生命低于60%、玩家距离5–25米时，蛊虫之后可沿16.24米直线爬行，同时挥臂攻击前方8米、120度的水平弧形范围，造成100点劈砍、300点3级砍伐及300点3级镐击伤害，冷却25秒。

咬伤

生命低于70%、玩家在10米内时，蛊虫之后可前扑撕咬，施加毒素状态，造成140点穿刺、100点毒素、300点3级砍伐及300点3级镐击伤害，覆盖自身体中心向外10米、25度的水平弧形范围。

呼唤

蛊虫之后停下咆哮，触发半径30米内的洞穴生成邪蛛探索者和邪蛛幼雏。每个洞为幼体或普通邪蛛生成器，前者100%孵出幼体，后者33%孵出邪蛛、67%孵出幼体。每洞周围半径50米内最多同时存在四只幼体及两只邪蛛，达到上限就不再生成。此技能仅限腐疫地堡，即使达到上限仍会尝试使用。冷却60秒，玩家在25米内且蛊虫之后生命低于99%时可用。

喷吐

蛊虫之后向玩家发射20枚弹体，施加毒素及被黏住了状态，造成40点钝击、40点毒素、50点3级砍伐及50点3级镐击伤害。弹体速度每秒15米，散射角1–5度，飞行4秒后消失。触及玩家或地形时爆炸，每枚有30%概率生成邪蛛幼雏。腐疫地堡内最多同时存在30只邪蛛幼体，但竞技场的橙色卵达到上限后仍会孵化。玩家距离5–25米、生命低于80%时，每20秒可用一次。

击打

玩家在9米内时，蛊虫之后挥动一条手臂，造成130点劈砍、300点3级砍伐及300点3级镐击伤害，攻击前方10米、145度的水平弧形区域，每4秒可用一次。

穿刺范围攻击

蛊虫之后用四条手臂刺向附近地面，攻击自身周围半径4.5米的圆形区域，造成150点穿刺、300点3级砍伐及300点3级镐击伤害。玩家在4米内时每4秒可用一次。

生物 生成

击败蛊虫之后后，邪蛛探索者会在青青草原、黑暗林山、泥泞沼泽、凛冽雪山及北境平原夜间生成，邪蛛幼雏及壁虱幼虫也会在迷雾之地夜间生成。只有壁虱幼虫可带星级生成。

  • 灰烬之地更新前，壁虱幼虫及邪蛛幼雏可在青青草原、黑暗林山、凛冽雪山和北境平原生成，因此目前尚不清楚这是漏洞还是未公布的修复。

说明

  • 竞技场入口完全不受蛊虫之后攻击，玩家可躲入治疗。已生成邪蛛及幼体会跟进，但玩家离开并重新激怒她之前，不会继续生成。有时她的后部穿墙进入入口，可无伤攻击。躲一段时间再出去时，她总会咆哮，便于快速攻击一两次。
  • 玩家主动离开、死亡离开堡垒，或退出游戏，均不会重置蛊虫之后生命。她会像其他首领、生物一样随时间缓慢恢复。
  • 黏液状态仅由蛊虫之后的喷吐攻击施加。
  • 可将蛊虫之后引至入口，用歼灭者造成范围伤害，轻松击败；她无法命中玩家，会很快死亡。最新补丁已禁用此方法：无法接近玩家时，她会失去敌意并离开。不过仍可利用引诱机制，用弓、劲弩射墙或向墙投矛引来她。生命条消失后约20秒，她无法重新锁定玩家，甚至可用近战攻击。

The Queen is the sixth boss. She is a monstrous Seeker Matriarch, and her appearance differs substantially from other Seekers. Her body is larva-like (similar to Seeker Broods), her four "arms" are all located nearer to the head, and she has a distinctive mouth lined with small, sharp teeth. Some features she shares with other Seekers are a set of large mandibles like the Seeker Soldier, antennae like regular Seekers, and thin, wispy hair like both Seeker types.

Summoning

The Queen's Forsaken Altar rests in the Mistlands. Unlike all previous bosses, she must be fought in a dungeon, the Infested Citadel, and her first fight requires no sacrifice. The nearest Infested Citadel can be located by Vegvisirs found in Infested Mines, but entry requires a Sealbreaker, which can be constructed from fragments also found in the mines.

The Queen will spawn on the lower floor of the citadel and will wander around until aggroed. After her initial defeat, 3 Seeker soldier trophies may be offered on the hive seat at the top of the citadel to summon her again.

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating the Queen, see Boss strategies § The Queen.

Teleport

Below 90% health and up to every 60 seconds, The Queen can burrow herself into the ground and emerge somewhere else within 200 meters. This ability is restricted to the Infested Citadel and to when a player is within 400 meters.

Rush

Below 60% health and within 5–25 meters of a player, The Queen can crawl in 16.24-meter line slashing her arms in an 8-meter 120-degree horizontal arc in front of her (100 Slash, 300 Tier 3 Chop and 300 Tier 3 Pickaxe damage). This attack has a cooldown of 25 seconds.

Bite

Below 70% health and within 10 meters of a player, The Queen can lunge forward and bite to inflict the Poison status (140 Pierce, 100 Poison, 300 Tier 3 Chop and 300 Tier 3 Pickaxe damage). This reaches 10 meters from her center in a 25-degree horizontal arc.

Call

The Queen will stop and roar, which triggers holes within a 30-meter radius to spawn Seekers and Seeker broods. Each hole is either a brood spawner or a regular Seeker spawner. The brood spawners have a 100% chance of hatching into Seeker broods, while the Seeker spawners have a 33% chance of hatching into Seekers and a 67% chance of hatching into Seeker Broods. A maximum of four Seeker broods and two Seekers can active from within a 50-meter radius of each hole - if this cap is reached the holes will not spawn any creatures. This ability is restricted to the Infested Citadel and she can still attempt to use it regardless of spawn caps. This ability has a 60-second cooldown, can be used when a player is within 25 meters, and is accessible while The Queen is below 99% health.

Spit

The Queen shoots 20 projectiles at the player which inflict the Poison and Slimed status (40 Blunt, 40 Poison, 50 Tier 3 Chop and 50 Tier 3 Pickaxe damage). Each projectile travels at 15 meters per second with a spread of 1–5 degrees. They can travel for 4 seconds before disappearing. When these projectiles make contact with the player or terrain, they explode and have a 30% chance each of spawning a Seeker Brood. A maximum of 30 Seeker broods can be active at once within the Infested Citadel (although the orange eggs scattered throughout the arena will not stop hatching after reaching the spawn cap). She can use this ability every 20 seconds when within 5–25 meters of a player and while below 80% health.

Slap

Within 9 meters of a player, The Queen swings one of her arms in front of her (130 Slash, 300 Tier 3 Chop and 300 Tier 3 Pickaxe damage). This hits in a 10-meter 145-degree horizontal arc in front of her. This attack can be used every 4 seconds.

Pierce AoE

The Queen stabs the ground nearby with all four arms dealing damage in a 4.5-meter radius circle around herself (150 Pierce, 300 Tier 3 Chop and 300 Tier 3 Pickaxe damage). She can use this attack every 4 seconds when a player is within 4 meters.

Forsaken power

Mounting The Queen’s trophy upon her respective stone will allow you to claim her Forsaken Power. This ability will grant the player and nearby allies the following effects for 300 seconds:

  • Eitr regen: +100%
  • Damage modifier: Resistant vs Poison

Creature spawning

Defeating The Queen unlocks Seeker to spawn at night in the Meadows, Black Forest, Swamp, Mountain and Plains, as well as unlocks Seeker Brood and Tick at night in Mistlands. Only Ticks can spawn with multiple stars.

  • Prior to Ashlands update, Tick and Seeker Brood could spawn in Meadows, Black Forest, Mountain and Plains, so this is currently unknown as bug or unannounced fix.

Events

Defeating The Queen affects the following events for that world:

  • Disables: "They sought you out."
  • Disables: "What's up, Gjall?!"
  • Enables: "The undead army marches"
  • Enables: "The dead have been summoned"

Traders

Defeating The Queen unlocks the Herbs of the Hidden Hills from The Bog Witch for 180 Coins and Deeper Pockets from Haldor for 2000 Coins.

Notes

  • The entrance to the arena is completely safe from The Queen's attacks, enabling players to duck inside and heal. Note that the spawned Seekers and Broods will follow the player in, but no more will spawn until the player leaves and re-aggros The Queen. On occasion, The Queen's backend may stick through the wall into the entrance, enabling free hits. Upon leaving the entrance after some time, The Queen will always do a roar animation, allowing for a quick hit or two.
  • The Queen's health does not reset when players exit the citadel either voluntarily or through death, or when exiting the game. Instead, her health slowly replenishes over time, identical to other bosses or creatures.
  • The Slimed status effect is exclusive to The Queen's spit attack.
  • The Queen can easily be defeated by luring her to the entrance, then using the Demolisher to deal AOE damage. The Queen cannot hit you, and will die quickly. (Disabled in the latest patch: if the Queen can't reach the player character, she loses aggro and walks away. The luring mechanic can still be used in battle – shooting from a bow or an arbalest or throwing a spear at a wall lures her. Additionally, when her health bar disappears, the Queen can't aggro on the player character for about 20 seconds, so you can even attack her with a melee weapon.)
