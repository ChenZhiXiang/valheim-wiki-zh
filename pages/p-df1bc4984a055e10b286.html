袭击与能力

幽灵战斗策略参见 敌对生物攻略 § 幽灵.

攻击

横向挥手抓击玩家，覆盖1.7米、90度的水平弧形范围，造成25点劈砍伤害。玩家在1.2米内时每2秒可用一次。

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating Ghosts, see Aggressive creature strategies § Ghost.

Attack

Swings their hand sideways in a 1.7-meter 90-degree horizontal arc to scratch the player (25 Slash damage). This attack can be used every 2 seconds when within 1.2 meters of a player.

Notes

  • Like Leviathans, they do not respawn, making them a finite creature when raids are disabled.
