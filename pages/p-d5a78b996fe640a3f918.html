毒素
效果
施加持续伤害

毒素是负面状态效果，对受影响对象造成持续伤害，由毒素伤害引起。

抗毒蜜酒等所有抗性在效果创建时计算，每次伤害结算时忽略。因此效果开始后改变抗性，不会改变该效果。

堆叠

毒素伤害不叠加。效果会计算尚未造成的伤害；若新攻击的毒素伤害超过剩余量，就重新创建效果，否则忽略新伤害。

计算

持续时间根据总伤害计算，公式为⌈(1+X*damage)⌉，更精确数值见下文。对玩家时X为5，对敌人时为1。这意味着玩家中毒持续时间约为两倍，每次结算伤害约减半。技术细节见后续章节。

每次结算伤害计算方式： total damageduration.

额外伤害

理论上全部结算伤害总和应等于初始值，但因舍入，实际总伤害略高。

  • 首先取以下数值的非整数值： duration 作为: 1+X*damage
  • 然后,我们采取 damagePerHit=totalDamage⌊duration⌋
  • 则实际结算次数为 ⌊duration1.02⌋+1.

原因是什么？浮点数特性[ 1 ]及英灵神殿固定50 FPS的物理更新，使每次结算间隔为1.02秒。但首次结算发生在持续时间起点，即时间=0，因此会多结算一次。

Poison
Effects
Applies damage over time

Poison is a negative status effect that applies a damage overtime effect on the affected entity. It is caused by Poison damage.

All resistances like Poison Resistance Mead are applied at the time of creating the effect and ignored on tick. Therefore any change to resistances after the effect start doesn't change it.

Stacking

Poison damage does not stack. The effect calculates how much damage remains to be dealt, and if a new poisonous attacks deals more poison damage than the remaining one, the effect is re-created. Otherwise, that incoming damage is ignored.

Calculations

Duration from total damage is calculated (see below for more precise numbers) as ⌈(1+X*damage)⌉ where X is 5 against players and 1 against enemies. This means Poison lasts roughly twice as long for players and halves the tick damage. See further section for the technical details.

Tick damage is calculated with total damageduration.

Extra damage

Theoretically, the total damage from all ticks should be the same as initial value, but due to rounding the total damage is slightly higher.

  • First we take non-integer value of duration as: 1+X*damage
  • then we take damagePerHit=totalDamage⌊duration⌋
  • then actual number of ticks is ⌊duration1.02⌋+1.

Where does this come from? Due to floating number peculiarities[1] and the fact, that Valheim runs physics on fixed 50 FPS, every tick takes 1.02 seconds, but since first tick happens at the beginning of duration, when time = 0, we have an extra one.
