黑色大理石防护神像是在潜能熔炉升级物品所用的神像。

获取方式

在以下兴趣点战利品箱中，与黑色大理石战斗神像一起出现：

  • 北境平原：平原宝箱、封印之塔。
  • 迷雾之地：矮人守卫塔、岩石尖塔、腐疫矿区。
  • 灰烬之地：灰烬城堡、腐朽之穴。

用途

每次在潜能熔炉尝试消耗一座神像，不需其他材料。65%概率使品质升一级，可超过4级；失败则摧毁物品，返还部分材料。

The Black Marble Protection Idol is an idol used to upgrade items at the Forge of Potential.

Contents

  • 1 Availability
  • 2 Usage
    • 2.1 Armor
    • 2.2 Shields
  • 3 See also

Availability

It is found in loot chests at these points of interest, together with the Black Marble Battle Idol:

  • Plains: Plains chest, Sealed Tower.
  • Mistlands: Dvergr Guard Tower, Rock Spire, Infested Mine.
  • Ashlands: Charred Fortress, Putrid Hole.

Usage

Each attempt at the Forge of Potential consumes one idol and costs no other materials. It has a 65% chance to raise the item's quality by one level, even above level 4; otherwise the item is destroyed and part of its materials are returned.

The Black Marble Protection Idol upgrades the following 10 items:

Armor

  •  Carapace Helmet
  •  Carapace Breastplate
  •  Carapace Greaves
  •  Eitr-weave Hood
  •  Eitr-weave Robe
  •  Eitr-weave Trousers
  •  Feather Cape
  •  Headdress of the Bear

Shields

  •  Carapace Buckler
  •  Carapace Shield
