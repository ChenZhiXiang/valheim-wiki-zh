火龙剑是第七种剑。

  • 主要攻击为3连击，最后一击造成双倍伤害。攻击多个目标时没有伤害惩罚。
  • 次要攻击造成3x伤害，攻击距离略长。

火龙剑碎片

制作火龙剑需要三块独特的碎片。这三块碎片分别位于三处神秘的地点中。

  • 火龙剑柄碎片
  • 火龙剑身碎片
  • 火龙剑刃碎片

每块碎片重量为0.5，不能堆叠。这些碎片不可再生，因此只能制作一把火龙剑。如果玩家提高了物品掉落倍率设置，就会掉落更多碎片，从而可以制作更多剑。

趣闻

  • 火龙剑意为“白柄”，是一把强大的剑，也是不列颠岛十三珍宝之一；这些物品出自中世纪晚期（15-16世纪）的威尔士传统。传说它属于莱德克海尔(Rhydderch Hael)，即威尔士三题集中提到的不列颠三位慷慨者之一。配得上它或出身高贵的人拔剑时，整把剑刃都会燃起火焰。里德赫从不吝于将剑交给任何人，因此获得意为“慷慨者”的绰号Hael；但获赠者一旦得知其特殊性质，总会拒绝这把剑。
  • 虽然属于 灰烬之地级别武器，但在灰烬之地更新前就已存在，并可通过控制台命令获得。它之前的ID是 SwordIronFire，当时造成55点劈砍和30点火焰伤害，耐久度为200，消耗14点耐力。
    • 尽管灰烬之地更新前无法制作它，但内部曾定义其配方和最高至品质4的升级。每次升级增加5点劈砍伤害、6点火焰伤害和50点耐久度。
      • 制作：2细木、20焰金属、20焰灵核心、3皮革碎片
      • 升级至等级2：10焰金属、10焰灵核心、2皮革碎片
      • 升级至等级3：20焰金属、20焰灵核心、4皮革碎片
      • 升级至等级4：30焰金属、30焰灵核心、6皮革碎片

The Dyrnwyn is the seventh sword.

  • The primary attack is a 3-hit combo with double damage on the last hit. No damage penalty against multiple targets.
  • Its secondary attack deals 3x damage with a slightly longer reach.

Dyrnwyn fragments

The recipe to craft the Dyrnwyn requires three unique fragments. Each fragment can be found on each of the three Mysterious Locations.

  • Dyrnwyn hilt fragment
  • Dyrnwyn blade fragment
  • Dyrnwyn tip fragment

Each fragment weighs 0.5 and does not stack. These fragments are not renewable, thus only one Dyrnwyn can be crafted. If the player has increased the setting from drop rate of items, more fragments will drop, enabling more sword to be crafted.

  • Weapons that do spirit damage such as the Mistwalker or Silver Sword may be more effective at dealing with some Ashlands enemies such as the Charred Warrior due to the damage bonus against undead enemies.

Trivia

  • The Dyrnwyn, meaning "white-hilt" was a powerful sword that was part of the Thirteen Treasures of the Island of Britain, a series of items from late medieval (15th-16th century) Welsh tradition. It was said to have been owned by Rhydderch Hael, one of the Three Generous Men of Britain mentioned in the Welsh Triads. When drawn by a worthy or well-born man, the entire blade would blaze with fire. Rhydderch was never reluctant to hand the weapon to anyone, hence his nickname Hael, meaning "the Generous", but the recipients, as soon as they had learned of its peculiar properties, always rejected the sword.
  • While being an Ashlands-tier weapon, it existed and was obtainable before the Ashlands update using console commands. Its previous ID was SwordIronFire, it dealt 55 slash and 30 fire damage, had 200 durability and used 14 stamina.
    • Despite it being uncraftable before the Ashlands update, it used to have a recipe as well as upgrades up to quality 4 defined internally. Each upgrade added 5 slash and 6 fire damage, and 50 points of durability.
      • Crafting: 2 Fine wood, 20 Flametal, 20 Surtling core, 3 Leather scraps
      • Upgrade to level 2: 10 Flametal, 10 Surtling core, 2 Leather scraps
      • Upgrade to level 3: 20 Flametal, 20 Surtling core, 4 Leather scraps
      • Upgrade to level 4: 30 Flametal, 30 Surtling core, 6 Leather scraps
