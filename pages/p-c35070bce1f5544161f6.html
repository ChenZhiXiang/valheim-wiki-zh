抗性和弱点决定受到各类伤害类型时的伤害。生物有固有抗性和弱点，也受部分状态效果影响。玩家可从护甲、蜜酒或状态效果获得抗性和弱点。

伤害机制

抗性及弱点列表：

  • 极弱：承受200%伤害
  • 较弱：承受150%伤害
  • 略弱：承受125%伤害
  • 普通：承受100%伤害
  • 略有抗性：承受75%伤害
  • 抗性：承受50%伤害
  • 极高抗性：承受25%伤害
  • 免疫：承受0%伤害

对玩家而言，由于伤害公式，抗性与护甲会产生协同效果。护甲至少达到原始来袭伤害的一半时，协同效果最大，可将抗性效果翻倍。

类似协同也可能增强弱点效果，但需要更多护甲。

考虑护甲协同：

  • 极弱：承受200%-400%伤害
  • 较弱：承受150%-225%伤害
  • 抗性：承受25%-50%伤害
  • 极高抗性：承受6.25%-25%伤害

以下示例:

示例
  1. 一次攻击造成86.6-100点伤害，生物伤害在最高值向下13.4%的范围内浮动。
  2. 假定攻击随机到100点伤害，玩家有25点护甲。
  3. 无抗性时，实际伤害为100-25=75。
  4. 有抗性时，先减伤50%，即100*0.5 = 50，再计算护甲：
  5. (50/(25*4))*50 = 25（详情： 伤害机制)
  6. 25是75的三分之一。与无抗性相比，抗性效果相当于减伤66.66%。

堆叠

多来源抗性及弱点不会叠加，也不会互相抵消。最强抗性会覆盖所有弱点。

效果选择优先级：极高抗性 > 抗性 > 略有抗性 > 免疫 > 极弱 > 较弱 > 略弱 > 普通。

唯一例外是蛟龙鳞盾会与其他来源叠加，因为格挡单独计算。

实例:

  • 抗霜蜜酒的冰霜抗性与狼毛披风的冰霜抗性共同作用，结果仍为抗性。
  • 狼毛披风的冰霜抗性与潮湿的冰霜弱点共同作用，结果为抗性。
  • 平原牦牛的冰霜抗性与潮湿的冰霜弱点共同作用，结果为抗性。
  • 蛟龙的火焰免疫与潮湿的火焰抗性共同作用，结果为抗性。
  • 树根面罩和树根腿甲均具有火焰弱点，结果仍为较弱。
  • 树根面罩的火焰弱点与抗火大麦酒的火焰抗性共同作用，结果为抗性。
  • 被沥青黏住了的火焰极弱与抗火大麦酒的火焰抗性共同作用，结果为抗性。
  • 树根面罩的火焰较弱与被沥青黏住了的火焰极弱共同作用，结果为极弱。
  • 树根面罩的毒素抗性与抗毒蜜酒的极高毒素抗性共同作用，结果为极高抗性。

Resistances and weaknesses determine how much damage is taken from different damage types. Creatures have innate resistances and weaknesses but are also affected by some status effects. Players may acquire resistances and weaknesses from armor, meads or status effects.

Damage mechanics

List of resistances and weaknesses:

  • Very weak: 200% damage taken
  • Weak: 150% damage taken
  • Slightly weak: 125% damage taken
  • Neutral: 100% damage taken
  • Slightly resistant: 75% damage taken
  • Resistant: 50% damage taken
  • Very resistant: 25% damage taken
  • Immune: 0% damage taken

For players, resistances have a synergy with the armor because of the damage formula. The maximum synergy is reached when armor is at least half of the incoming raw damage which will double the resistance effect.

Similar synergy may also increase the effect of weaknesses but this requires more armor.

With the armor synergy:

  • Very weak: 200%-400% damage taken
  • Weak: 150%-225% damage taken.
  • Resistance: 25%-50% damage taken
  • Very resistant: 6.25%-25% damage taken

Example below:

Example
  1. An incoming attack that deals between 86.6-100 damage (the damage range for a creature is the top 13.4%)
  2. Let's assume it rolls 100 and the player has 25 armor.
  3. Without resistance effect, damage taken is 100-25=75.
  4. With resistance effect, 50% reduction is applied first, 100*0.5 = 50. Then armor is applied:
  5. (50/(25*4))*50 = 25 (for specifics: Damage mechanics)
  6. 25 is a third of 75. The resistance effect may be observed as a 66.66% damage reduction compared to not having it.

Stacking

Multiple sources of resistances and weaknesses do not stack nor do they cancel each other out. The strongest source of resistance will override all weakness.

The selected effect is in the order of: Very Resistant > Resistant > Slightly Resistant > Immune > Very Weak > Weak > Slightly Weak > Normal

The only exception is that Serpent Scale Shield stacks with other sources because blocking is calculated separately.

Examples:

  • Frost Resistance Mead (frost resist) with Wolf Fur Cape (frost resist) results in resist.
  • Wolf Fur Cape (frost resist) with Wet (frost weak) results in resist.
  • Lox (frost resist) with Wet (frost weak) results in resist.
  • Serpent (fire immune) with Wet (fire resist) results in resist.
  • Root Mask (fire weak) and Root Leggings (fire weak) results in weak.
  • Root Mask (fire weak) with Fire Resistance Barley Wine (fire resist) results in resist.
  • Tarred (fire very weak) with Fire Resistance Barley Wine (fire resist) results in resist.
  • Root Mask (fire weak) with Tarred (fire very weak) results in very weak.
  • Root Mask (poison resist) with Poison Resistance Mead (poison very resist) results in very resist.
