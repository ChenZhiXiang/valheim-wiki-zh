金属切割器用于升级黑熔炉。虽然黑熔炉随迷雾之地加入，但金属切割台需要灰烬之地生物群系的材料。

用途

黑熔炉的升级设施，可将其制作的装备升级至更高品质。

The Metal cutter is used as an upgrade for the Black Forge. While the Black forge was introduced in Mistlands, the Metal cutter requires materials from the Ashlands biome.

Usage

An upgrade to the Black forge, allows for Black forge-crafted gear to be upgraded to higher quality levels.

Trivia

  • The Gem Cutter was introduced in patch 0.218.15 on May 14th, 2024 as part of the Ashlands update.
