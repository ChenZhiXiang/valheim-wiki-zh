埃达之能是使用魔法武器所需的个人资源，在状态界面显示为紫色条。

食用蕴含埃达之能的食物可获得该资源，并像耐力一样随时间恢复。穿埃达织物套装或恩布拉套装部件可加快恢复，蛊虫之后的放逐之力也能提高。

恢复

停止使用埃达之能1秒后开始恢复。恢复速度与剩余量及修正系数呈线性关系。

baseRegen=2+2⋅(1−remainingEitrmaxEitr)

剩余为零时，基础恢复最高4；达到上限时，基础恢复最低2。

修正值

某些动作会降低埃达之能恢复，与状态效果分别计算。

  • 游泳：0x
  • 格挡: 0.8×

正面状态效果相加，负面效果相乘，因此两者并存时，计算顺序有影响。护甲加成始终最后加入。

  • 冻僵：0.4x
  • 寒冷：0.75x
  • 潮湿：0.85x
  • 休息好了: +100%
  • 休息中: +300%
  • 持久埃达之力蜜酒: +25%
  • 蛊虫之后之力: +100%
  • 埃达织物兜帽: +20%
  • 埃达织物长袍: +40%
  • 埃达织物长裤: +40%
  • 恩布拉兜帽: +30%
  • 恩布拉长裤: +50%
  • 恩布拉长袍: +50%
  • 脉息耳环 效果 :+25%

This article is about the gameplay mechanic. For the crafting material, see Refined Eitr.

Eitr is a personal resource necessary to use magic weapons. It is represented by a purple bar in the HUD.

Eitr is generated by eating eitr-infused food and will regenerate over time, similarly to stamina. The regeneration rate is augmented while wearing pieces of Eitr-weave Set or Embla Set. It is also augmented by The Queen's Forsaken power.

Regeneration

Eitr starts regenerating after eitr has not been used for 1 second. Eitr regeneration depends linearly on the amount of remaining eitr and modifiers.

baseRegen=2+2⋅(1−remainingEitrmaxEitr)

Maximum base regen of 4 is at zero eitr. Minimum base regen of 2 is at maximum eitr.

Modifiers

Some actions reduce eitr regeneration and are applied separately from status effects.

  • Swimming: 0x
  • Blocking: 0.8x

Positive status effects are added additively while negative status effects use multiplication. This means the order of effects matters when there are both positive and negative effects. Armor bonus are always added last.

  • Freezing: 0.4x
  • Cold: 0.75x
  • Wet: 0.85x
  • Rested: +100%
  • Resting: +300%
  • Lingering Eitr Mead: +25%
  • The Queen's Power: +100%
  • Eitr-weave Hood: +20%
  • Eitr-weave Robe: +40%
  • Eitr-weave Trousers: +40%
  • Hood of Embla: +30%
  • Trousers of Embla: +50%
  • Robes of Embla: +50%
  • Pulsating Earrings effect: +25%
