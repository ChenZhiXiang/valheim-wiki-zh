耕地耙可耕地以种植种子，也可种树。

使用草模式可在泥地恢复草，在水面恢复荷叶，在岸边造沙地，并在岸边特定区域恢复香蒲。

耕地耙消耗耐力，因此希尔德服装适合管理大型农场。

获取方式

在熔炉制作，获得青铜及圆木后解锁。

用途

耕地耙是用于翻土、播种及恢复受损环境的农具。

作物种下至成熟前，名称旁显示状态。"健康"表示正在生长，所有食物作物均需耕地。

满足以下任一条件，作物不生长：

  • 错误生态区域
  • 空间不足
  • 阳光不足

部分作物只能在指定生物群系种植，环境错误时显示"无法在这个环境中成长"。

多数作物周围需1x1空地。其他作物、建筑或物体置于范围内会阻止生长，包括被地形覆盖的岩石。范围内任何物体都会导致“需要更多的空间才能成长"”状态。

作物需无遮挡天空。上方任何建筑都会导致"需要广阔的天空"状态，无论高度多高。

酒莓藤和常春藤与其他作物不同，需要种在距离建造的平整墙面 1 的位置，才能攀附生长。它们可以越过窗户或门洞，但任何凸檐或墙面断口都会阻止其生长。篱笆和装饰面板也可作为供其攀爬的墙面。酒莓藤和常春藤需要更大的株间距。

树也用耕地耙种植，需清除周围树木、树桩及大石，但不需耕地。

现有作物

每次使用耕地耙，无论种植或修改地形，消耗1点耐久。

The Cultivator can be used on a patch of land to ready it for planting seeds, as well as to plant trees.

With the Grass mode, the player can regrow grass on patches of dirt, regrow lily pads on water, create sand on the shoreline and regrow cattails in certain areas around the shore.

Use of Cultivator does use stamina, making Hildir's clothing useful for managing large farms.

Availability

Made at a Forge. It becomes available to craft once the player has obtained Bronze and Corewood.

Usage

The Cultivator is a farming tool for tilling soil, planting seeds, and restoring damaged environments.

Crops will display a status next to their name when planted until they are fully grown. The "Healthy" status means the plant is growing. All food crops need cultivated soil.

Crops will not grow if any of the three conditions are met:

  • Wrong biome
  • Not enough space
  • Not enough sunlight

Some crops can only be planted in certain biome requirements. Planting a crop in the wrong environment will display the "Can't grow in this environment" status.

Most crops require a 1x1 area of free space around them. Other crops, structures, or objects placed within this space will prevent the plant from growing. This is true even for rocks that have been covered by the terrain. Any objects within this free space will cause the "Needs more room to grow" status.

Crops require unobstructed view of the sky. Any structures built over a crop will cause the "Needs an open sky" status, regardless of how high the structure is above the crop.

Unique among crops - Vineberry and Ivy vines need to be 1 away from a flat built wall to climb. It can cross over Window or door openings, but any overhang or break in the wall will stop its growth. Fences and decorative panels count as walls for purpose of trellising. Vineberries and Ivy need a greater spacing between plants.

Trees are also planted with a Cultivator. Needs an area cleared of trees, stumps, and large rocks but does not require cultivated ground.

Available crops

Each usage of the Cultivator, whether to plant a crop or to change the terrain, uses 1 durability.
