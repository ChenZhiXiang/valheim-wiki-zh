蜜酒是可饮用的物品，能暂时提升生命值和耐力，也可防止有害的状态效果。

制作蜜酒时，须先在蜜酒壶处制成蜜酒原液，再使用发酵桶发酵。

无论是否持有大酒杯、矮人酒杯或庆祝号角，都可以饮用蜜酒。

冷却

蜜酒有冷却，期间玩家不能再次饮用同类蜜酒。某些蜜酒属于同一互斥组：

  • 治疗蜜酒组：
    •  小份治疗蜜酒
    •  中份治疗蜜酒
    •  大份治疗蜜酒
    •  长效治疗蜜酒
  • 耐力蜜酒组：
    •  小份耐力蜜酒
    •  中份耐力蜜酒
    •  长效耐力蜜酒
  • 埃达之能蜜酒组：
    •  小份埃达之力蜜酒
    •  持久埃达之力蜜酒

Meads are items which can be consumed for temporary health and stamina boosts, as well as preventing harmful status effects.

Mead must first be crafted into a mead base at a Mead Ketill and then fermented using a Fermenter.

Mead can be consumed with or without a Tankard, Dvergr Tankard or a Horn of Celebration.

Cooldown

Meads have cooldown during which a player cannot drink another mead of the same type. Some meads share the same exclusion group:

  • Healing meads group:
    •  Minor Healing Mead
    •  Medium Healing Mead
    •  Major Healing Mead
    •  Lingering Healing Mead
  • Stamina meads group:
    •  Minor Stamina Mead
    •  Medium Stamina Mead
    •  Lingering Stamina Mead
  • Eitr meads group:
    •  Minor Eitr Mead
    •  Lingering Eitr Mead
