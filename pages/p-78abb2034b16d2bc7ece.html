锅碗瓢盆用于升级铸铁锅。由于金属成本较高，通常是大锅的第三项升级。

用途

等级4铸铁锅可制作的物品包括：

  •  血旺
  •  鱼肉卷
  • 其他升级设施见铸铁锅。

趣闻

  • 锅碗瓢盆随家园与炉火更新于2021年九月16日加入游戏。

Pots and pans are used as an upgrade for the Cauldron. It will typically be the third upgrade for the cauldron due to its high metal cost.

Usage

Level 4 Cauldron items include:

  •  Blood Pudding
  •  Fish Wraps
  • For other upgrades, see Cauldron.

Trivia

  • Pots and pans were introduced in the Hearth and Home update on September 16th, 2021.
