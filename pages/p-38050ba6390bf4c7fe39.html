获取方式

在以下兴趣点战利品箱中，与木制 战斗 神像一起出现：

  • 青青草原：废弃房屋、废弃农场、维京墓地。

用途

每次在潜能熔炉尝试消耗一座神像，不需其他材料。65%概率使品质升一级，可超过4级；失败则摧毁物品，返还部分材料。

  •  碎布裤

  •  庆典帽

盾

The Wooden Protection Idol is an idol used to upgrade items at the Forge of Potential.

Contents

  • 1 Availability
  • 2 Usage
    • 2.1 Armor
    • 2.2 Shields
  • 3 See also

Availability

It is found in loot chests at these points of interest, together with the Wooden Battle Idol:

  • Meadows: Abandoned House, Abandoned Farm, Viking Graveyard.

Usage

Each attempt at the Forge of Potential consumes one idol and costs no other materials. It has a 65% chance to raise the item's quality by one level, even above level 4; otherwise the item is destroyed and part of its materials are returned.

The Wooden Protection Idol upgrades the following 9 items:

Armor

  •  Leather Helmet
  •  Leather Tunic
  •  Leather Trousers
  •  Rag Tunic
  •  Rag Trousers
  •  Deer Hide Cape
  •  Celebratory Cap

Shields

  •  Wood Shield
  •  Wood Tower Shield
