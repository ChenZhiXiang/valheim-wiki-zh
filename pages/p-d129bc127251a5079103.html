海尔司坦是黑暗林山中的角色，是一只驯服的平原牦牛，在霍尔德的贸易营地陪伴他。

传记

除与霍尔德同住营地、从碗里进食的平原牦牛身份外，海尔司坦的其他经历不详。

霍尔德曾遇到海尔司坦，决定将其收为宠物，用来拉运载全部商品的大车。

趣闻

  • “海尔司坦”之名源自古诺尔斯语，由意为“岩石”的hallr及意为“石头”的steinn（石头）构成。

Halstein is a character found in Black Forests. He is a tamed Lox that accompanies Haldor at his trading camp.

Biography

Nothing is known about Halstein beyond the fact he is a Lox that resides with Haldor in his camp feeding from a bowl.

At some point in time, Haldor had come across Halstein and decided to keep him as a pet to pull his large wagon which he uses to transport all his tradable goods.

Trivia

  • "Halstein" is a name having roots in Old Norse origins. It is derived from the elements hallr "rock" and steinn "stone".
