细木是砍伐青青草原或北境平原中的桦树、橡树树获得的制作资源。平原桦树丰富，但敌人与危险使其通常到后期才适合采细木。青青草原森林也散布桦树及橡树。采伐应保留种子，以后可种植补充木材，橡树的橡果尤其稀有。

桦树和橡树至少需青铜斧砍伐，通常意味着先击败赤血灵鹿，制作鹿角镐，才能开采青铜斧所需矿石。

细木也可在黑暗林山沿岸沉船获取，或从废弃废墟塔楼家具回收。仅需石斧就能拆除细木物体，沉船尤其产量可观。也可推倒或滚动附近树木、原木，撞倒桦树或橡树，再反复将原木互撞或撞树造成伤害，岩石无效。这可提前收集细木。类似地，可引巨魔或其他大型生物毁树，虽慢但可行。

Finewood is a crafting resource obtained by chopping down Birch and Oak trees in the Meadows or Plains biomes. The Plains feature plentiful Birch trees, though the dangerous enemies and hazards within tend to make farming Finewood there infeasible until later in the game. Both Birch and Oak trees can be found scattered through forested parts of the Meadows biome. When harvesting them, the player should be careful to preserve any seeds that may be recovered, since they will be able to plant these later for a new supply of wood (Acorns from Oak trees are particularly rare).

Birch and Oak trees require at least a Bronze Axe to chop. This usually means that players must first defeat Eikthyr and craft the Antler Pickaxe, which unlocks the ability to mine the ore required for the Bronze axe.

Finewood can also be looted from Shipwrecks on the coasts of the Black Forest biome, or recovered from furniture in derelict Ruined Towers. A Stone Axe is the only requirement to dismantle objects made of Finewood, and shipwrecks in particular may yield a considerable amount. Alternatively, the player can fell or roll other nearby trees or tree trunks onto Birch or Oak trees to knock them down, then repeatedly roll the logs into each other and other trees to damage them (rocks will not work). In this way, players can collect a quantity of Finewood early on. Similarly, a Troll or other large creature may be kited into destroying the trees (another slow, but viable process).
