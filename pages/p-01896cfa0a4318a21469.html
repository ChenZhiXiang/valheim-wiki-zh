软组织既是资源节点，也是制作埃达精华的资源，遍布迷雾之地，也可在灰烬之地的腐朽之穴中找到。

获取方式

  • 用黑金属镐或更好工具，从迷雾之地巨人遗骸头骨内节点采集。
  • 破坏迷雾之地矮人营地及建筑的矮人板条箱掉落。
  • 矮人刺客及矮人法师有概率掉落。
  • 从灰烬之地腐朽之穴内肉堆采集。

掉落物

作为资源节点，用黑金属镐或更好工具开采时，每段可能掉落：

1 软组织 50%

每个大脑节点布局相同，含118个小节点，各5点生命，完整开采平均产59份软组织。部分头骨靠水面或陡坡，可能无法完全开采。

Soft Tissue is both a resource node and a resource used to craft Refined Eitr. They can be found throughout the Mistlands, and inside Putrid Holes in the Ashlands.

Contents

  • 1 Availability
  • 2 Drops
  • 3 Usage
  • 4 Gallery

Availability

  • Harvested from the resource nodes inside the skulls of Giant Remains in the Mistlands, using a Black Metal Pickaxe or better.
  • Dropped by broken Dvergr Crates in the Mistlands, found in Dvergr camps and structures.
  • Chance of being dropped by Dvergr Rogues and Dvergr Mages.
  • Harvested from mounds of flesh inside Putrid Holes in the Ashlands.

Drops

As a resource node, each segment may drop the following when mined (using a Black Metal Pickaxe or better):

Amount Item Chance
1 Soft Tissue 50%

Each brain-shaped resource node has the same layout with 118 nodes, each of which has 5 health. Thus, a single resource node will drop 59 Soft Tissue on average, if fully mined. Note that as some skulls generate close to the water level or on steep terrain, it may not always be possible to fully mine a resource node.

Usage

  • Crafting
    • Used in combination with Sap in the Eitr Refinery to make Refined Eitr.
