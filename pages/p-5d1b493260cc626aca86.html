冰霜
你为体温过低感到痛苦。
效果
减速移动速度

冰霜是降低移动和转向速度的负面状态效果。速度惩罚在效果持续期间按指数衰减。

初始减速100%，逐渐趋向0%。刷新效果会重置为100%。

持续时间取决于损失生命值。玩家基础持续15秒，生物50秒：

duraTion=base duraTion⋅damagemax heaLTh

例如，最大生命150、护甲50时，受到200冰霜伤害，会损失50生命并获得5秒冰霜效果。

5s=15s⋅50150

Frost
You suffer from hypothermia.
Effects
Slows movement speed

Frost is a negative status effect that applies a movement and turn speed penalty on the affected entity. This speed penalty decays exponentially over the duration of the status effect.

Movement speed reduction starts at 100% and gradually decays towards 0%. Refreshing the effect resets the reduction back to 100%.

The debuff duration depends on the amount of health lost; The base duration is 15 seconds for players and 50 seconds for creatures:

duration=base duration⋅damagemax health

For example, receiving 200 Frost damage while at max 150 health and wearing 50 armor causes 50 damage and 5 seconds of Frost Effect.

5s=15s⋅50150
