银矿脉是高海拔凛冽雪山的资源节点，用铁镐或更好工具开采掉银矿及石头。

装备愿望骨可探测矿脉。靠近时愿望骨发声，玩家周围出现彩色粒子；越近，音调越高、频率越快。

也可用鹿角战锤或铁大锤随机砸地，直到地下显示“太硬了”，称为勘探。在凛冽雪山定位银时，鹿角战锤比铁大锤精准得多。虽慢，但无需击杀邪骨恶灵。

生成

世界生成器在每个区域尝试50次，为单条矿脉寻找合适位置，须满足：

  • 凛冽雪山 生物群系
  • 起码120公尺高度
  • 地形坡度不超过30度
  • 九米内10个随机点的高度差不超过三米

成功时，矿脉放在地下四米处。

掉落物

银矿脉布局始终为Y形，100节点，各40点生命。每节点可能掉落：

数额 物品 机会
2-3 银矿 33.3%
2-3 石头 66.7%

每条银矿脉平均产约83.3份银矿石及166.7份石头。

说明

  • 金属矿床不会重新生成。
  • 与其他金属一样，银矿石无法传送。
  • 银矿脉有时与附近岩石相交，移除岩石后即可开采。
  • 霜龙及黑曜石需海拔100米生成，可据此估计山高。
  • 平坦地形更可能将矿脉完全埋在地下。但随机检查也允许不平地形，可能自然露出。
  • 移除全部支撑地面，再开采一个节点，可瞬间采完整条矿脉。首节点碎后其他随之破坏，立即获得资源，但不额外提供镐经验。

Silver Veins are resource nodes found in tall Mountains. They drop Silver Ore and Stone when mined with an Iron Pickaxe or better.

Veins can be detected by equipping the Wishbone. The Wishbone will emit a tone along with colored particles from the player when a vein is nearby. The tone becomes higher-pitched and faster the closer the player is to the vein.

Alternatively, the player can hit the terrain at random positions with Stagbreaker or an Iron Sledge until a "Too hard" message is seen under the ground, this is known as Prospecting. Stagbreaker is much more accurate than Iron Sledge for locating silver in the Mountains. While slower, this does not require killing Bonemass.

Spawning

The world generator attempts 50 times to find a suitable position for a single vein on every zone. The spawn position must have the following conditions:

  • Mountain biome
  • At least 120 meter altitude
  • Up to 30 degree terrain angle
  • 10 random points within nine meters have up to a three-meter altitude difference

If successful, the vein is placed four meters under the ground.

Drops

Silver Veins always have the same Y-shaped layout with 100 nodes with 40 HP each. Each of the nodes may drop:

Amount Item Chance
2-3 Silver Ore 33.3%
2-3 Stone 66.7%

On average, one silver vein will produce about 83.3 silver ore and about 166.7 stone.

Notes

  • Metal deposits do not respawn.
  • Like other metals, silver ore cannot be teleported.
  • Silver Veins sometimes intersect with nearby stone, but can be mined after the stone is removed.
  • Drakes and obsidian require 100 meter altitude to spawn. These can be used to measure the mountain height.
  • Veins are more likely to appear completely underground on even terrain. However, the random check allows them also to be placed on uneven terrain, which can leave them naturally exposed.
  • Veins can be mined instantly by removing all supporting ground and then mining a single node. Once the first node breaks all others will follow. This will give all resources instantly but will not give additional Pickaxes experience.
