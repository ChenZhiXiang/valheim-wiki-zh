血魔法（技能）
说明
使用血魔法时的伤害、埃达之能和生命值消耗。

血魔法（技能）是与血魔法武器类别相关的技能。

目录

  • 1 效果
  • 2 升级
  • 3 技能效果清单
  • 4 提示

效果

提高血魔法技能等级具有以下效果：

  • 使用法杖的埃达之能消耗和生命值消耗最多降低至-33%。
  • 用招魂之杖召唤的枯骨傀儡伤害最多提高+200%。
  • 佑护之杖产生的魔法屏障可吸收伤害上限提高：等级0时基础值200，每级增加5，等级100时最高700。

巨魔之杖产生的召唤巨魔不受血魔法等级影响。

升级

血魔法获取经验的方式独特，施法本身不提供经验。

  • 使用佑护之杖时，护盾破裂会获得一点经验。经验给予受护盾保护的实体，而非施法者，因此没有法杖也能提升血魔法。
  • 使用亡者召唤杖时，召唤骷髅造成伤害会获得经验：每次近战命中+0.75，每次远程命中+0.5。
  • 使用巨魔之杖或其召唤巨魔均不提供经验。

提示

  • 只要保护生成器不被召唤骷髅破坏，生物生成点就能轻松挂机刷经验。
  • 古树长老也是好选择，因为容易召唤，伤害也不高。
  • 邪骨恶灵战斗很适合练级。在中央头骨顶部用1米木柱搭平台，2米木柱太高，再召唤骷髅弓手；若不是弓手，就将其推下。弓手停止射击时，可能正瞄准无法触及的敌人。毒云仍能伤到玩家，因此带抗毒装备，或为自己再搭更高平台。召唤骷髅免疫毒伤害。
  • 召唤骷髅不会直接攻击练兵傀儡，但误伤仍会提供经验。可将灰矮人幼体、灰矮人或软泥怪等容易圈住的敌人放在T.W.I.G.下方或后方，使骷髅尝试攻击敌人，却只命中T.W.I.G.。若血魔法等级高到足以让骷髅摧毁T.W.I.G.，此方法就无法完全挂机。

Blood magic (skill)
Description
Damage, Eitr and health drain when using Blood magic.

Blood magic (Skill) is a skill related to the Blood magic weapon class.

Contents

  • 1 Effects
  • 2 Leveling up
  • 3 List of skill effects
  • 4 Tips

Effects

Higher skill level in blood magic as the following effects:

  • Reduces the Eitr cost and the Health cost to use staff down to -33%.
  • The damage dealt by Skelett summoned with the Dead Raiser increases up to +200%.
  • The maximum amount of damage which can be absorbed by the Magic Barrier produced by the Staff of Protection increases, with a base value of 200 damage at level 0, increasing by 5 per level to a maximum of 700 damage at level 100.

Summoned Trolls from the Trollstav are not affected by the level of Blood magic.

Leveling up

Experience gain for Blood magic is unique in that casting does not give experience.

  • With the Staff of Protection, one experience is gained when the barrier is broken. The experience is given to the entity protected by the barrier (instead of the caster). Thus, it is possible to level Blood magic without having the staff.
  • With the Dead Raiser, experience is gained when a summoned Skelett deals damage, +0.75 experience per melee hit and +0.5 experience per ranged hit.
  • Neither the use of the Trollstav nor the Summoned Troll gives experience.

Tips

  • Creature spawners provide an easy way to farm experience AFK, as long as the spawner is protected from the Skelett.
  • The Elder is another good option because it is easy to summon and does not deal high amounts of damage.
  • Bonemass fight is an efficient way to level up. Build an elevated platform on top of the skull in the center with a 1-meter wooden pole, a 2-meter pole is too high, and summon Skelett Archers on it, if it is not an archer simply push them off. If the Archers stop shooting, they are likely aiming at an out-of-reach mob. The poison cloud can still reach the player, so bring something with poison resistance or the player can build a second higher platform for themselves. The Skeletts are immune to poison damage.
  • Although Skeletts will not attack T.W.I.G. directly, they still generate experience from collateral damage. The player can make a simple farm by keeping an easily contained enemy such as a Greyling, Greydwarf, or Ooze beneath or behind the T.W.I.G. such that the Skeletts will try to attack the enemy but only hit T.W.I.G. instead. This method is not fully AFK if the player's Blood magic level is high enough such that the Skeletts destroy the T.W.I.G.
