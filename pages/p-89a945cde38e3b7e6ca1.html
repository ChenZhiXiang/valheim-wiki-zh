鱼竿是用于钓鱼的工具。

获取方式

  • 可向霍尔德以350枚钱币购买。
  • 可在冰霜洞穴极罕见生成的大型冰封湖边找到，拾取时也掉落2-4个鱼饵。
  • 无法制作。

用途

背包有鱼饵时，主攻击抛线。按住左键越久，距离越远，最远30米。格挡会收回浮漂及上钩的鱼。

每次抛竿消耗一份鱼饵。0.213.4补丁后，未钓到鱼就收线或重新抛竿时，会返还鱼饵。

The Fishing Rod is a tool used for Fishing.

Availability

  • It can be purchased from Haldor for  350 Coins.
  • It can be found at the edge of large frozen lakes, which (very rarely) can spawn in Frost Caves. When picked up there, 2-4 Fishing Bait will also drop.
  • It cannot be crafted.

Usage

Its primary attack casts the line if the player has Bait in the inventory. The longer the left-click is held, the further the line will go (up to 30 meters away). Blocking will reel in the bobber and pull any caught Fish with it.

One bait is consumed every time the Fishing Rod is cast. As of patch 0.213.4, fishing bait is returned if it is pulled in without catching a fish or when recasting.
