舒适度等级决定休息中效果持续时间。舒适度1时最短8分钟，每额外一级增加1分钟，普通物品最高29分钟，配齐两种罕见季节物品最高31分钟。

通常最高舒适度为22。附近还有仲夏柱或耶鲁树时为23，两者都有则为24。

进入诡秘墓穴等大多数地下城时，可建篝火，轻松获得3舒适度及10分钟休息好了效果。

注意，同类别家具可以放多个，但只有舒适度最高者影响总舒适度。

说明

物品必须在角色十米范围内，才能增加舒适度。

家具中心点：

  • 床铺、龙床、灶：对象底部。
  • 篝火、营火：未点燃对象的中心。
  • 吊火盆：对象顶部。
  • 浴缸：水平方向略偏向烟囱，垂直方向位于烟囱起始处。
  • 耶鲁树：接近对象顶部。
  • 其余所有物体均以物体中心为准。

Comfort level determines the duration of Resting Effect. Minimum duration is 8 minutes (at 1 comfort), with each additional comfort level stacking 1 minute up to 29 minutes with usual items and 31 minutes with both rare seasonal items.

The max comfort normally achievable is 22. If also near a Maypole or the Yule Tree 23, and if both then 24.

When entering most dungeons (such as Burial Chambers), a campfire can be constructed to easily gain 3 comfort and a 10-minute Rested effect.

Note that while the player can add more than one furniture item from the categories below, only the one with the highest comfort will influence the comfort rating.

Notes

Items must be within ten meters of the character to add comfort.

Furniture center points:

  • Bed, Dragon Bed, Hearth: Bottom of the object.
  • Bonfire, Campfire: Center of the unlit object.
  • Hanging Brazier: Top of the object.
  • Hot Tub: Horizontally slightly towards the chimney, vertically where the chimney starts.
  • Yule Tree: Almost top of the object.
  • Center of the object for everything else.
