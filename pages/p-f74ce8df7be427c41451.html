魔法屏障
能够吸收伤害的魔法护盾
效果
可抵挡200-700点伤害（取决于施法者的技能等级）。

魔法屏障是为实体提供球形屏障的正面状态效果。持续一定时间，期间吸收固定上限伤害；超过上限后屏障破裂，同时移除状态。

玩家使用佑护之杖获得，可作用于施法者及小半径内友方玩家和驯服生物。持续60至120秒，取决于法杖品质。吸收上限为200至700伤害，完全取决于施法者血魔法等级，详情见该页。

Magic barrier
A magical shield that absorbs damage
Effects
Blocks 200-700 damage (depending on caster's skill level).

Magic barrier is a positive status effect that surrounds the affected entity in a spherical barrier. This barrier lasts for a certain amount of time, and while active it can absorb up to a fixed amount of damage. Once this damage limit is exceeded, the barrier breaks, which also removes the status effect.

The effect is acquired from a player using the Staff of Protection, which can apply it to the caster and to other allied players and tamed creatures within a small radius of its use. The duration of the effect is between 60 and 120 seconds, as determined by the quality of the staff. The amount of damage that the barrier can absorb is a certain fixed amount between 200 and 700 damage, and is determined entirely by the caster's Blood magic skill level; see that page for more details.
