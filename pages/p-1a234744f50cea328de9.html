石魔是凛冽雪山中的敌对生物，是覆雪的人形石体，身上可见银矿脉。受扰后从沉睡中苏醒，攻击玩家及其他生物。

袭击与能力

石魔像战斗策略参见 敌对生物战斗策略 § 石魔像.

石魔像有两种变体，一半装备巨锤，另一半装备尖刺。

巨锤石魔像有两种攻击。

砸地

举起一条巨锤手臂后砸向地面，造成110点钝击、100点0级砍伐及100点0级镐击伤害。攻击覆盖向外8米、垂直70度的弧形范围，冷却8秒。

双重砸击

举起双锤手臂，稍停后同时砸向地面，造成110点钝击、100点0级砍伐及100点0级镐击伤害，覆盖向外8.66米、垂直70度的弧形范围，冷却15秒。

尖刺石魔像有两种攻击。

尖刺

后仰再向玩家突刺，直线攻击长7米、没有弧度，造成110点穿刺、100点0级砍伐及100点0级镐击伤害。玩家在6米内时每8秒可用一次。

尖刺横扫

先收回尖刺，再横扫7.71米、104.24度的水平弧形范围，造成110点钝击、100点0级砍伐及100点0级镐击伤害。玩家在6米内时每15秒可用一次。

说明

  • 石魔像无法攀登陡峭悬崖或沿狭窄小径前行。因此在山坡开凿折返小路，既能防御它们，又能抵达山顶。
  • 石魔像从沉睡中苏醒时，会使附近玩家画面震动。
  • 石魔像无法破坏凛冽雪山中的黑曜石矿源及银矿脉。
  • 石魔像与霜龙敌对，将两者引至一起，是提前获取水晶的可行方法。

趣闻

  • 石魔像有未使用的“音爆”攻击，会后收双臂再前扑攻击。尚不清楚原本计划用于巨锤、尖刺变体，还是两者都有。

Stone Golems are aggressive creatures found in the Mountains. They are snow-covered stone humanoids with silver veins visible on their bodies. When disturbed, they awake from their slumber, attacking both the players and other creatures.

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating Stone Golems, see Aggressive creature strategies § Stone golem.

Stone Golems come in two variants: half of them are equipped with sledges, while the other half have spikes.

Sledge Golems have two attacks.

Ground slam

Raises one sledge arm up, before slamming it down into the ground (110 Blunt, 100 Tier 0 Chop and 100 Tier 0 Pickaxe damage). The attack extends 8 meters outward in a 70-degree vertical arc. It has an 8-second cooldown.

Double smash

Raises both sledge arms up, holds for a moment, and slams them both into the ground (110 Blunt, 100 Tier 0 Chop and 100 Tier 0 Pickaxe damage). The attack extends 8.66 meters outward in a 70-degree vertical arc. It has a 15-second cooldown.

Spiked Golems have two attacks.

Spike

Leans back before thrusting at the player in a 7-meter-long attack with no arc (110 Pierce, 100 Tier 0 Chop and 100 Tier 0 Pickaxe damage). This attack can be used every 8 seconds when within 6 meters of a player.

Spike sweep

Draws its spike back before slashing it in a 7.71-meter 104.24-degree horizontal arc (110 Blunt, 100 Tier 0 Chop and 100 Tier 0 Pickaxe damage). This can be used every 15 seconds when within 6 meters of a player.

Notes

  • Stone Golems are unable to climb sheer cliffs or follow narrow paths. Therefore, cutting a switchback trail up the mountainside is an effective defense against them while also allowing you access to the mountaintop.
  • Stone Golems when they awake from their slumber will cause nearby players' screens to shake
  • Stone Golems cannot destroy Obsidian Deposits and Silver Veins in their mountain biomes.
  • Stone Golens are enemies of Drakes, making baiting them into each other a valid strategy to obtain Crystals early on

Trivia

  • Stone Golems have an unused attack labelled "Sonic boom" where they draw back both arms before lunging forward to attack. It is unknown whether this would have been added to sledge Golems, spiked Golems, or possibly both.
