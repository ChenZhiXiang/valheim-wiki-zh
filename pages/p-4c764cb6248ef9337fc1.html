影子是阵亡战士灵魂，位于废弃村落，有时夜间游荡极北之地。

不攻击，忽略所有伤害，有时低声说话。

Shadow are the souls of fallen warriors, They are found in Abandoned Villages and sometime roaming in Deep North during the night.

They have no attack and ignore all damage, but sometimes mumble sentences.
