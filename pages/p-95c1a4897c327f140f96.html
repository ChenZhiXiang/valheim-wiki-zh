火焰
舒适的火给你带来温暖。
效果
缩短以下状态的持续时间： 潮湿 效果，并移除 寒冷 与 冻僵 效果。

火焰是靠近热源获得的状态效果，移除寒冷和冻僵，并使潮湿消退速度提高至10倍。

附近无敌对生物时，具有火源效果并坐下，会开始休息中，舒适度等级为1。但处于庇护后，火源效果不再额外提供舒适度；火源与庇护共同允许不坐下也休息，庇护至少提供舒适度2。仅篝火和壁炉会在火源之外额外提供舒适度，分别为1和2。

Fire
Warm from a cozy fire.
Effects
Reduces the time for the Wet effect and removes Cold and Freezing effects.

Fire is a status effect granted when in close proximity to a heat source. It removes Cold and Freezing effects as well as reduces the duration of the Wet effect 10 times faster.

If there are no hostile creatures nearby, sitting while having the fire effect will cause the player to begin Resting, with a comfort level of 1. Once the player is under Shelter however, the fire effect no longer provides additional comfort. Instead however, the fire effect along with the shelter effect both allow the player to experience the resting effect without sitting, whilst gaining at least comfort level 2 due to shelter. Only the campfire and hearth will provide additional comfort along with the fire effect, providing 1 and 2 comfort respectively.
