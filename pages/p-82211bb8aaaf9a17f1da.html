雪铲是用于清除极北之地积雪的工具，必须清雪后才能在当地地面上建造。

尽管凛冽雪山生物群系也覆盖着积雪，但那里不需要雪铲。

主要攻击消耗5点耐力。次要攻击消耗20点耐力，覆盖玩家周围全部方向（360度）。雪铲使用耕作技能。

雪铲只会清除极北之地的积雪。主要攻击清除两米半径内的积雪，次要攻击以更高强度清除三米半径内的积雪。两种攻击也都会清除建筑上积累的雪。

获取方式

  • 在等级3的黑熔炉处使用5x细木、4x焰金属和2x余烬制作。

The Snow Shovel is a tool used to clear snow in the Deep North, which is necessary to be able to build on the ground there.

Despite the Mountain biome also being covered in snow, the Snow Shovel is not needed there.

The primary attack costs 5 Stamina. The secondary attack costs 20 Stamina and reaches all around the player (360 degrees). The Snow Shovel uses the Farming skill.

The Snow Shovel only clears snow in the Deep North. The primary attack clears snow in a two-meter radius, and the secondary attack clears it in a three-meter radius at a greater strength. Both attacks also remove snow that has built up on structures.

Availability

  • Crafted at a level 3 Black Forge using 5x Finewood, 4x Flametal, 2x Embers
