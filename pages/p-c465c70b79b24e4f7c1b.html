邪蛛探索者是在迷雾之地及腐疫矿区中的敌对生物。

邪蛛是大型昆虫状生物，兼具甲虫、蚂蚁和蜜蜂特征。有下颚及三对腿，中间一对较大，用来攻击。背部长出一对大皮翼，可短距离飞行。邪蛛及士兵们似乎都是蛊虫之后的后代，类似现实昆虫群落。

袭击与能力

击败邪蛛的策略见 敌对生物攻略 § 邪蛛探索者.

起飞

落地且距玩家10至100米时，会起飞，冷却8秒。

降落

飞行中距玩家不足5米时，会降落，冷却8秒。

钳击

先后仰，再向下扫，用钳夹住玩家，造成90点穿刺伤害。范围为2.2米、水平45度弧形，玩家在2米内时可用，冷却6秒。

爪击

有左爪和右爪前刺两种，各自冷却10秒，造成120点穿刺伤害。范围为3.5米、水平20度弧形，玩家在3.5米内时可用。

砸地

后腿站起，再双爪砸向玩家，造成100点钝击伤害。前方范围为3.2米、水平60度弧形，玩家在3米内时可用，冷却10秒。

飞行砸地

飞行中后仰，再用双爪向前冲，造成100点钝击伤害。此攻击会使邪蛛落地，可代替降落能力。前方范围为3.2米、水平60度弧形，玩家在5米内时可用，冷却10秒。

说明

  • 邪蛛外形像蚂蚁，但普通邪蛛能飞，而邪蛛侦察兵及蛊虫之后不能。
  • 邪蛛巢产出皇家果冻。现实中，这种物质由工蜂分泌，用来喂幼虫，尤其是培育新蜂后。

Seekers are aggressive creatures found in the Mistlands and in Infested Mines.

Seekers are large insectoid creatures with traits similar to beetles, ants, and bees. They have mandibles and three pairs of legs, the middle of which are larger and used to attack enemies. A pair of large leathery wings sprout from the back and can be used to fly short distances. Seekers and their Soldier counterparts are implied to all be offspring of The Queen, much the same as how real insect colonies function.

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating Seekers, see Aggressive creature strategies § Seeker.

Takeoff

When grounded between 10 and 100 meters of a player, they will launch from the ground and begin flying. This ability can be used every 8 seconds.

Land

When airborne under 5 meters of a player, they will land on the ground. This ability can be used every 8 seconds.

Pincers

Leans back before sweeping downward to clamp the player with their pincers (90 Pierce damage). This creates a 2.2-meter 45-degree horizontal arc and can be used every 6 seconds when within 2 meters of a player.

Claw

This attack has two variants, each with its own 10-second cooldown: a thrust with the left claw and a thrust with the right claw (120 Pierce damage). This hits in a 3.5-meter 20-degree horizontal arc and can be used within 3.5 meters of a player.

Ground slam

Stands on their hind legs before slamming down on the player with both claws (100 Blunt damage). This hits in a 3.2-meter 60-degree horizontal arc in front of Seekers and can be used every 10 seconds when within 3 meters of a player.

Ground slam flying

While airborne, leans back before propelling forward with both claws (100 Blunt damage). Using this attack grounds Seekers and is an alternative to landing with their Land ability. This hits in a 3.2-meter 60-degree horizontal arc in front of Seekers and can be used every 10 seconds when within 5 meters of a player.

Notes

  • Seekers are ant-like in appearance, but unlike ants, basic Seekers are capable of flight while Seeker Soldiers and The Queen are not.
  • Seeker hives produce Royal Jelly, which in real life is produced by worker bees and fed to larvae, particularly to produce new queens.
