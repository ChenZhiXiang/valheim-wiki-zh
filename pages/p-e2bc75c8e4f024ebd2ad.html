铁制战斗神像是在潜能熔炉升级物品所用的神像。

获取方式

在以下兴趣点战利品箱中，与铁制防护神像一起出现：

  • 黑暗林山：巨魔洞穴、废弃前哨、废弃小棚。
  • 幽暗沼泽：地下圣堂、沼泽墓地、沼泽符文石塔。

用途

每次在潜能熔炉尝试消耗一座神像，不需其他材料。65%概率使品质升一级，可超过4级；失败则摧毁物品，返还部分材料。

The Iron Battle Idol is an idol used to upgrade items at the Forge of Potential.

  • 2 Usage
    • 2.1 Weapons

Availability

It is found in loot chests at these points of interest, together with the Iron Protection Idol:

  • Black Forest: Troll Cave, Abandoned Outpost, Abandoned Hut.
  • Swamp: Sunken Crypt, Swamp Grave, Swamp Runestone Tower.

Usage

Each attempt at the Forge of Potential consumes one idol and costs no other materials. It has a 65% chance to raise the item's quality by one level, even above level 4; otherwise the item is destroyed and part of its materials are returned.

The Iron Battle Idol upgrades the following 11 items:
