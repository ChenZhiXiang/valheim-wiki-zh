腐疫地堡是第6个首领蛊虫之后的出生地点和战场。与此前不同，腐疫堡垒是类似地牢的独立区域，可在内部遇见并对战蛊虫之后。

外部

外部入口是迷雾之地中巨岩下方的破损大型矮人制黑大理石建筑。顶部门口通入岩壁，但被矮人金库阻挡，这扇大门带符文和锁定装置，需要封印破除者开启。

通往顶部楼梯的一侧墙内，有破损木墙，可破坏或拆除，进入下层通道。下层有数间房：

  • 两间空房，仅有通向主楼梯入口处的楼梯。
  • 一间有矮人椅子、桌子和架子等家具，以及矮人灯笼。
  • 另一个房间仅有一张矮人桌。
  • 中间房有可破坏矮人灯笼、凳子及三个矮人木桶。

矮人家具，包括木桶，玩家无法使用，但可破坏以获取资源：

  • 椅子、桌子、凳子和架子，每件提供1-3份铜碎屑，概率33.3%，或木材，概率66.7%。
  • 每个矮人木桶恰好提供1份铜碎屑、1份细木和1份美味蜜酒。

外部还饰有蓝色黄麻窗帘和窗帘，可破坏获取蓝黄麻。

内部

内部是带腐疫柱子的大房间，楼梯交错通向顶部，弃誓者祭坛，即蜂巢王座，在那里等待。巢穴周围散布多组卵，可生成邪蛛幼雏，像虫巢矿洞中的卵一样可打破。

蛊虫之后活着并在堡垒内游荡，首次无需祭坛召唤。击败后，祭坛消耗三个邪蛛侦察兵战利品可重新召唤。首次到访时，她可能在任何位置，因此唯一真正安全区是入口附近狭小有顶区域，任何首领攻击都够不到，但召唤物仍可能进入。

The Infested Citadel is the spawn location and boss arena of the 6th boss, The Queen. Unlike the previous boss arenas, the Infested Citadel is an instanced area (much like a dungeon) wherein The Queen can be encountered and fought.

Exterior

The outer entrance of the Citadel is a large, damaged Dvergr-made Black Marble structure found in the Mistlands and situated beneath a large rock. Atop the structure is a doorway leading inside the rock face, which is blocked by the Dvergr vault, a large door bearing a runic inscription and a locking mechanism. In order to open the vault door, a Sealbreaker is required.

In the wall of one side of the staircase leading up to the top of the structure is a damaged Wood Wall, which can be destroyed or deconstructed to access a passage to the lower floor of the structure, which has several rooms:

  • Two rooms are empty (except for stairs leading to the access point at the main staircase).
  • One room contains several pieces of Dvergr furniture (a chair, table, and shelf) and a Dvergr Lantern.
  • Another room contains only a Dvergr table.
  • The middle room has a destroyable Dvergr lantern, a Dvergr stool, and three Dvergr Barrels.

While the Dvergr furniture (including the barrels) cannot be used by players at all, they can be destroyed to provide resources:

  • The chairs, tables, stools, and shelves each provide between 1-3 of: Copper Scrap (33.3%) and/or Wood (66.7%).
  • Each Dvergr Barrel provides exactly 1 Copper Scrap, 1 Finewood, and 1 Tasty Mead.

The structure exterior is also adorned with Blue Jute Curtains and drapes, which can be destroyed for a source of Blue Jute.

Interior

The Citadel interior is a large room with infested pillars and is crisscrossed by staircases stretching upwards to the top, where the Forsaken Altar, the Hive seat, awaits. Sprawled around the lair are several grouped brood eggs, which spawn Seeker Broods and can be broken like the ones found in Infested Mines.

The Queen is alive and roaming inside the Citadel; she need not be summoned at the altar unless she has already been defeated, in which case the altar will consume three Seeker soldier trophies to re-summon The Queen. Since The Queen can be anywhere within the Citadel when the player first arrives there, the only truly safe zone is the small, covered area around the entrance, which cannot be reached by any of her attacks (though her summons may still reach players there).

Trivia

  • The door of the Dvergr vault at the entrance to the Citadel is inscribed with two glowing Proto-Germanic runes from the Elder Futhark runic alphabet: ᛈ perþō ('p') and ᛉ algiz ('z' or, in Old English, 'x').
  • Inside the Infested Citadel, the player map takes on a yellowish-green hue.
