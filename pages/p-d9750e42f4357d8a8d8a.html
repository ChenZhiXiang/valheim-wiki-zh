灰烬刚鬣是灰烬之地中的敌对生物，可驯服并快速骑乘，像巨大的变异蜥蜴精。

袭击与能力

灰烬刚鬣战斗策略参见 敌对生物攻略 § 灰烬刚鬣.

灰烬刚鬣驯服前后都不受熔岩伤害，尽管并不免疫火焰伤害。

咬伤

头部先向后摆，再向前咬玩家，造成75点钝击、75点劈砍、50点等级0的砍伐及50点等级0的镐击伤害。范围为4.5米、水平45度弧形，玩家在4米内时可用，冷却8秒。

头撞

后退后低头冲向玩家，造成120点钝击、100点等级4的砍伐及100点等级4的镐击伤害。范围为4米、水平45度弧形，玩家在6米内时可用，冷却8秒。

扑击

稍微晃动后向玩家扑去，造成150点钝击、50点等级0的砍伐及100点等级0的镐击伤害。范围为5.5米、水平45度弧形，玩家距其3–6米时可用，冷却4秒。类似平原牦牛，对灰石和较弱建筑造成大量伤害，甚至能损伤友方生物。

驯服

一般驯服信息及策略参见“驯服”。

与狼类似，等级3的二星灰烬刚鬣只在夜间生成。若尚未开始驯服，等级3灰烬刚鬣会在白天尝试逃离已加载区域并消失。可将其困在无法逃出的坑或圈栏中，并保持64米内；或开始驯服，之后就不会消失。

喂余烬泡泡、酒莓束或蕨菜花可驯服灰烬刚鬣，装上灰烬刚鬣鞍即可骑乘。骑乘时玩家不能攻击，但可穿越熔岩，骑手和坐骑都不受熔岩伤害。

驯服的灰烬刚鬣不会对玩家、其他驯养生物或中立矮人造成友军伤害。

繁殖

灰烬刚鬣开心，吃饱蕨菜花、酒莓束或余烬泡泡，四米内有另一只，且至少10米内不超过10只时，可繁殖。满足后积累好感直至怀孕。怀孕持续60秒，每30秒检查一次，因此60至90秒后产下灰烬刚鬣蛋。蛋品质由产蛋者决定：背包中品质1对应0星，品质2对应1星，品质3对应2星。

蛋附近有营火等热源时，30分钟后孵成灰烬刚鬣幼崽。热源消失或将蛋放回背包，会重置计时。之后幼崽无须热源，50分钟长成成年。

10米内达到10只时，不会继续繁殖。但目前蛋的数量没有已知上限。

趣闻

  • “Asksvin”在瑞典语中意为“灰烬猪”，单复数形式相同，类似英文swine。
  • 因外形绿色且多疣，Twitch英灵神殿社区常昵称它为“腌黄瓜狗”。

Asksvin are hostile, tameable, and fast rideable creatures found in the Ashlands. They look like huge, mutated Necks.

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating Asksvin, see Aggressive creature strategies § Asksvin.

Both before and after taming, Asksvin take no damage from lava, despite not being immune to fire damage.

Bite

Sweeps their head back before rounding forward to chomp down on the player (75 Blunt, 75 Slash, 50 Tier 0 Chop and 50 Tier 0 Pickaxe damage). This extends 4.5 meters in a 45-degree horizontal arc and can be used every 8 seconds when within 4 meters of a player.

Headbutt

Steps back before bringing their head down and rushing at the player (120 Blunt, 100 Tier 4 Chop and 100 Tier 4 Pickaxe damage). This extends 4 meters in a 45-degree horizontal arc and can be used every 8 seconds when within 6 meters of a player.

Pounce

Wiggles a little before springing forward at the player (150 Blunt, 50 Tier 0 Chop and 100 Tier 0 Pickaxe damage). This attack hit in a horizontal arc of 5.5 meters and 45 degrees. It can be used every 4 seconds when within 3–6 meters of a player. The attack does high damage to grausten and weaker structures, similar to a Lox, and it can even damage allied creatures.

Taming

For general taming information and strategies, see Taming.

Similar to the Wolf, Level 3 (two-star) Asksvin only spawn at night. Like any creatures spawned at night, Level 3 Asksvin will attempt to flee the active area and despawn during daytime hours if the taming process has not begun. This can be prevented by either (A) trapping them in a pit or pen that they cannot escape (and staying within 64 meters of them) or (B) by starting the taming process, after which they will never despawn.

Asksvin can be tamed by feeding them Smoke Puffs, Vineberry Clusters or Fiddleheads. They are rideable by equipping the Asksvin Saddle. When riding an Asksvin, players cannot attack. Players riding an Asksvin can traverse lava, and neither the player nor Asksvin will take damage from the lava.

Tamed Asksvin do not do friendly fire damage to players, any other tamed creatures or neutral Dvergr.

Breeding

Asksvin can breed, provided the Asksvin is happy and well-fed with Fiddlehead or Vineberry Cluster or Smoke Puff, along with there being another Asksvin within four meters, and no more than 10 Asksvin within at least 10 meters. Once the conditions are met, they will accumulate love points with one another until the pregnancy starts. Pregnancy lasts 60 seconds, checked every 30 seconds, so the egg comes 60 to 90 seconds after it starts. They then lay an Asksvin egg. The quality of the Asksvin egg is determined by the Asksvin that laid it. A quality 1 egg in the player's inventory indicates a 0-star Asksvin, quality 2 indicates a 1-star, and quality 3 indicates a 2-star.

Once laid, it would take 30 minutes to hatch into Asksvin Hatchling if there is a heat source nearby (Example Campfire). If the heat source is gone or the player puts the egg into their inventory, the timer is reset. Then the Asksvin Hatchling takes 50 minutes to grow into an adult without the need of heat source.

They will not breed if the cap is reached, which is 10 Asksvin in 10 meters. However, there is currently no known limit to the eggs themselves.

Trivia

  • The word "Asksvin" is Swedish for "ash swine." And like swine, the plural for Asksvin is the same as its singular form.
  • Due to their green and warty appearance, "Pickle-Dog" has gained popularity as a common moniker for Asksvin within the Twitch-Valheim community.
