一个土地面的小区域，中央有树桩祭坛，周围树木环绕，位于青青草原 生态区域。

通常由一群灰矮人保护。击败后，树桩顶部出现随机奖励。

It was a small area with dirt covered ground and a stump-altar in the middle, surrounded by trees. It can be found in Meadows biome.

A group of Greydwarfs is usually protecting it. Upon defeating them, a random reward appears on top of the stump.
