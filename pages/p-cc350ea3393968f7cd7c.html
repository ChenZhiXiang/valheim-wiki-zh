耐力是英灵神殿中的个人资源，以黄色条显示。角色基础耐力为50，每格代表25点耐力。

除步行和与物体互动外，多数动作消耗耐力，包括使用工具、武器及其他移动方式。

停止消耗耐力的动作片刻后，或饮用耐力蜜酒，即可恢复耐力。休息中和休息好了效果、美味蜜酒及长效耐力蜜酒能加快恢复。

处于寒冷、冻僵及潮湿效果时，耐力恢复更慢。

恢复

停止消耗耐力1秒后开始恢复，速率取决于剩余耐力及修正值。

baseRegen=6+6⋅(1−remainingStaminamaxStamina)

regen=baseRegen⋅modifier1⋅modifier2⋅…⋅modifiern

基础恢复

耐力为零时，基础恢复最高为12；耐力满时最低为6。平均基础恢复取决于恢复量：

  • 从0%到100%：每秒8.656
  • 从0%到50%：每秒10.43
  • 从0%到33%：每秒10.97
  • 从0%到25%：每秒11.23
  • 从50%到100%：每秒7.40
  • 从33%到100%：每秒7.83
  • 从25%到100%：每秒8.04

考虑恢复延迟，最优方式是恢复至约50%。有寒冷或其他负面效果时，最优上限约为33%。

修正值

部分动作会降低耐力恢复，与状态效果分别计算。

  • 坠地:0×
  • 游泳：0x
  • 格挡: 0.8×

正面状态加算，负面状态乘算，因此两者同时存在时，顺序会影响结果。不过有任一负面状态时，无法获得休息好了及休息中。

  • 冻僵：0.4x
  • 寒冷：0.75x
  • 潮湿：0.85x
  • 休息好了: +100%
  • 休息中: +300%
  • 美味蜜酒: +100%
  • 长效耐力蜜酒: +25%
  • 青铜坠饰 已激活: +25%
  • 狂暴: +15%
  • 邪骨之怒: +40%
  • 先锋: +10%

实例

例如同时处于精力充沛、休息中并饮用美味蜜酒，恢复提高至+500%，即6x。寒冷再乘以0.75x，降至4.5x。

例如同时处于精力充沛、休息中及寒冷时，恢复为3.75x；饮用美味蜜酒后增至4.75x。

消耗

耐力消耗取决于玩家动作。

攻击

攻击耐力消耗取决于武器本身、主副攻击类型及技能等级，最多降低至-33%。灰烬披风或狂战之酒可进一步降低消耗。

工具

部分工具消耗耐力，对应技能可降低消耗。穿戴希尔德出售的服装可进一步降低，多数连衣裙为-20%，帽子为-15%。

格挡

主条目： 格挡

格挡耐力消耗取决于挡下伤害所需的格挡力量。

闪避

主条目： 闪避

闪避耐力消耗取决于装备速度惩罚及闪避(技能)。成功闪避攻击会返还一半耐力消耗。

跳跃

跳跃耐力消耗取决于装备速度惩罚：jumpStamina=10⋅(1+equipmentSpeedPenalty)。饮用轻盈蜜酒或使用赤血灵鹿之力可进一步降低。

跑尸形态可进一步降低消耗。合计减免100%时不消耗耐力，并可在跳跃、奔跑时恢复耐力。

奔跑

每秒消耗8点耐力，奔跑（技能）每级减免-0.5%，最高-50%。赤血灵鹿之力、穿戴灰烬刚鬣披风及跑尸形态可进一步降低。

合计减免100%时完全不消耗耐力，还能在跳跃、奔跑时恢复耐力。

游泳

每秒消耗6点耐力，游泳（技能）每级减免-0.5%，最高-50%。赤血灵鹿之力、饮用瓦纳蜜酒蜜酒或激活命运之鳍饰品可进一步降低。

潜行

每秒消耗5点耐力，潜行（技能）每级平均减免-0.75%，最高-75%。蛊虫之后之力可进一步降低。

超重

移动时每秒消耗5点耐力，无法改变，也无法跳跃或奔跑。

Stamina is a personal resource in Valheim represented as a yellow bar. Characters have a base 50 stamina, with each bar representing 25 stamina.

Stamina is drained by any actions you perform outside of walking and interacting with objects. These actions include using tools/weapons, and movement that is not walking among others.

Stamina is generated after a few moments of not performing stamina draining tasks or by using Stamina Meads, and can be generated faster with the Resting and Rested Effects, Tasty Mead, and Lingering Stamina Mead.

Stamina will be generated slower while under the Cold, Freezing, and Wet Effects.

Regeneration

Stamina starts regenerating after stamina has not been used for 1 second. Stamina regeneration depends on the amount of remaining stamina and modifiers.

baseRegen=6+6⋅(1−remainingStaminamaxStamina)

regen=baseRegen⋅modifier1⋅modifier2⋅…⋅modifiern

Base regen

Maximum base regen of 12 is at zero stamina. Minimum base regen of 6 is that maximum stamina. Average base regen depends on how much stamina is regenerated:

  • From 0% to 100%: 8.656 per second
  • From 0% to 50%: 10.43 per second
  • From 0% to 33%: 10.97 per second
  • From 0% to 25%: 11.23 per second
  • From 50% to 100%: 7.40 per second
  • From 33% to 100%: 7.83 per second
  • From 25% to 100%: 8.04 per second

With the regeneration delay, the optimal way is to regenerate up to ~50%. With Cold or other negative effects, the optimal limit is ~33%.

Modifiers

Some actions reduce stamina regeneration and are applied separately from status effects.

  • Falling: 0x
  • Swimming: 0x
  • Blocking: 0.8x

Positive status effects are added additively, while negative status effects use multiplication. This means the order of effects matters when there are both positive and negative effects. However, Rested and Resting can't be acquired while having any of the negative status effects.

  • Freezing: 0.4x
  • Cold: 0.75x
  • Wet: 0.85x
  • Rested: +100%
  • Resting: +300%
  • Tasty Mead: +100%
  • Lingering Stamina Mead: +25%
  • Bronze Pendant active: +25%
  • Berserk: +15%
  • Vilebone Wrath: +40%
  • Vanguard: +10%

Examples

For example, being Rested, Resting and having Tasty Mead would increase regeneration to +500% which equals 6x regeneration. Being Cold would multiply this by 0.75x and reduce it to 4.5x.

For example, being Rested, Resting and Cold equals 3.75x regeneration. Drinking Tasty Mead would increase it to 4.75x.

Drainage

Stamina drainage is dependent on the action of the player.

Attacking

The stamina cost of attacks depends on the weapons themselves, the type of attack (primary or secondary) and the Skills level (down to -33%). Attack stamina can be further reduced with Ashen Cape or Berserkir Mead.

Tools

Some Tools consume stamina, reduced by the corresponding skills. It can be further reduced by wearing clothes sold by Hildir, -20% stamina usage for most dresses and -15% for hats.

Blocking

Main article: block

Blocking uses stamina based on how much block power was required to block the damage.

Dodging

Main article: Dodging

Dodging uses stamina based on the speed penalty from equipment, and on the Dodge (skill). Dodging an attack returns half the stamina cost.

Jumping

Jumping uses stamina based on the speed penalty from equipment. jumpStamina=10⋅(1+equipmentSpeedPenalty) It can be further reduced by consuming a Lightfoot Mead or the Eikthyr Power.

It can further be decreased by Corpse run, combining them to 100% reduction makes it take no stamina and allows regeneration while jumping and running.

Running

Stamina is drained 8 per second (-0.5% per Running (skill) level, up to -50%). It can be further reduced by Eikthyr Power and by wearing an Asksvin Cloak, It can further be decreased by Corpse run.

Combining them to 100% reduction makes it take no stamina at all, while additionally allows regeneration while jumping and running.

Swimming

Stamina is drained 6 per second (-0.5% per Swimming (skill) level, up to -50%). It can be further reduced Eikthyr Power, consuming a Draught of Vananidir mead or activating a Fins of Destiny trinkets.

Sneaking

Stamina is drained 5 per second (on average -0.75% per Sneaking (skill) level, up to -75%). It can be further reduced by The Queen's Power.

Encumbered

Stamina is drained 5 per second while moving, this can not be changed. It also disallows jumping and running.
