List of moulds and casts

模具是极北之地各处的制作部件。

在黑熔炉或古咒法台作为材料，制作北方深境武器、护甲及隐秘钥匙的铸坯。再将铸坯投入以液态冰霜供能的霜铸熔炉，获得最终物品。

模具不能复用，制作铸坯时消耗。

Moulds are crafting components found in various places in the Deep North.

They are used as crafting ingredients at a Black Forge or Galdr Table to craft Casts for Deep North Weapons, Armors and the Intricate Key. The Cast can then be placed in a Frost Foundry fueled by Liquid Frost to produce the final item.

Moulds cannot be reused and are consumed to make a Cast.
