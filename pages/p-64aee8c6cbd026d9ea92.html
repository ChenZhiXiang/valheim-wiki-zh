传记

角色进入第10界前的身份几乎未知，但仍有许多猜想空间。

角色只是被送往第10界、奉命对抗弃誓者的无数灵魂之一。第10界遍布废墟村庄，表明此前已有许多人来到此地，之后也很可能继续有人到来。

弃誓者能否真正死亡未知，因此角色可被视为某种看守。

属性

玩家角色默认具有以下属性：

  • 25 生命值
  • 50 耐力
  • 32 物品栏 插槽数
  • 300 负重 容量

食用食物提高基础生命和耐力，并获得埃达之能；穿饰物可获得血怒。负重可通过装备梅金吉奧德腰带等方式提高。背包可用霍尔德的两项永久升级扩容。

每位玩家可在以下各类装备中各穿戴一件：

  • 右手（武器 与 工具)
  • 左手（盾)
    • 某些装备为双手装备，会同时占用两个手部栏位
  • 头部（帽子和头盔）
  • 胸部（上衣和胸甲）
  • 腿部（裤子和护胫）
  • 饰品
  • 饰物

多数能力受学习技能及状态效果影响。

新角色有一个碎布上衣。

Biography

While little is known about whom they were prior to entry into the 10th realm, there certainly is room for speculation.

The player character is only one amongst an unknown number of souls sent to the 10th realm and instructed to battle the Forsaken, as shown by the countless ruins and villages found throughout the 10th realm, many have come before them and many will most likely come after.

Since it is unknown whether the Forsaken can truly die or not, the player character could be seen as a form of jailer.

Attributes

Player characters have these attributes by default:

  • 25 Health
  • 50 Stamina
  • 32 inventory slots
  • 300 carry weight capacity

Consuming food allows increasing the base Health and Stamina, as well as gaining Eitr, Adrenaline can be gained by wearing trinkets. Carry weight capacity can be increased by various methods, such as equipping the Megingjord. The inventory size can be increased with two permanent upgrades from Haldor.

Each player can equip one piece of equipment in each category:

  • right hand (weapons and tools)
  • left hand (shields)
    • some equipment is two-handed and occupies both hand slots
  • head (hats and helmets)
  • chest (tunics and chestplates)
  • legs (pants and greaves)
  • accessory
  • trinket

Most player abilities are affected by learning skills and influenced by status effects.

Newly created characters have a single Rag Tunic.
