乘骑(技能)
说明
动物的速度和耐力消耗。

乘骑是一种技能。

效果

骑术提高，坐骑冲刺速度最多+25%，冲刺耐力消耗最多降低至-50%。

升级

骑平原牦牛、灰烬刚鬣或驼鹿冲刺时，每秒获得0.2经验。

说明

  • 技能标识符为骑行，用于raiseskill或resetskill命令。
  • 于家园与炉火更新加入。

Riding (skill)
Description
Animal speed and stamina drain.

Riding is a skill.

Effects

Increasing the player's riding level increases sprint speed while riding a mount by up to +25%, and reduces stamina usage when sprinting with a mount down to -50%.

Leveling up

The player gains 0.2 experience per second while sprinting when riding a Lox, Asksvin or a Moose.

Notes

  • Skill ID is Ride (to use with raiseskill or resetskill commands).
  • Was added in the Hearth & Home update.
