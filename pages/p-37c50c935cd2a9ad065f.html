鸡蛋是一种物品，也是一种食物。初期可以向霍尔德购买，用来繁殖鸡。开始养鸡需要两枚蛋。

可以在铸铁锅中煮成煎蛋。

获取方式

  • 击败亚格鲁斯后，可向霍尔德花费1,500钱币购买。
  • 可在北境平原的封印之塔建筑底层的箱子中找到。
  • 两只母鸡吃饱、有遮蔽且彼此靠近时，会自然产下。

用途

制作

  •  煎蛋
  •  甜甜包坯子
  •  蘑菇煎蛋卷
  •  松饼

孵化

将蛋从背包中单独丢在火或其他热源附近，并确保上方有屋顶提供遮蔽，才能孵化。将鼠标指向蛋时，会显示是否因过冷而无法孵化（提示可能需要5-10秒才会更新）。30分钟后，蛋会孵化出鸡，随后成长为母鸡。蛋必须单独放置，堆叠的蛋无法孵化。不要放得离火太近，否则幼雏会在孵化后立即死亡。

Egg is an item and a food source, which can be initially purchased from Haldor to be able to breed Chickens. In order to start a chicken farm, two eggs are required.

It can be cooked in a Cauldron to obtain a Cooked egg.

Availability

  • Purchased from Haldor for  1,500 Coins after defeating Yagluth.
  • Found in a chest in the bottom floor of the Sealed Tower structure in the Plains.
  • Naturally spawns if two Hens are well-fed, sheltered, and near each other.

Usage

Crafting

  •  Cooked egg
  •  Unbaked Sweetbread
  •  Mushroom Omelette
  •  Pancakes

Hatching

An egg must be placed (dropped from inventory) near a fire or heat source and under a roof (needs shelter) to hatch. When hovering over an egg, it will indicate whether it is too cold to hatch (note: hovertext may take 5-10 seconds to update). After 30 minutes, the egg will hatch, and a Chicken will spawn and will eventually grow into a Hen. The egg must be placed individually - not in a stack - to hatch. Don't place it TOO close to the fire or it'll immediately die after hatching.
