钓鱼帽是一件可在工作台处制作和升级的护甲部件，为钓鱼和游泳提供+20技能加成。

制作钓鱼帽需要捕获现有所有生物群系（从青青草原到极北之地）中的每一种鱼。制作并将帽子升至等级4，总共需要每种鱼各八条。任何品质的鱼都可用于制作。

The Fishing Hat is an armor piece which can be crafted and upgraded at the Workbench. It provides a +20 skill bonus to Fishing and Swimming.

Players need to catch every single kind of fish from all existing biomes (from Meadows to the Deep North) in order to craft the Fishing hat. A player who wishes to craft and upgrade the hat to rank 4 requires a total of eight of each kind of fish. Any quality level of fish may be used to craft it.
