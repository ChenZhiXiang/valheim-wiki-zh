“这片土地艰险荒野，但被带来的人更坚强。旅人，从眼前馈赠中得到慰藉：优良木石、森林果实与花朵。也留意游荡的鬓猪，它们怕火与人手，却可学会顺从。悄悄靠近，让它们吃你储存的食物，地下根茎是其最爱。”——符文石

野猪是遍布英灵神殿的野生生物，靠近时会表现敌意。状态标签可辨认“野生”或“驯养”，以及“饥饿”“开心”“害怕”“适应中”等情绪。了解这些状态有助于有效互动。

野生鬓猪怕火，包括火把；靠近时会进入“害怕”状态。2星鬓猪极少见，适合通过繁殖获取皮碎片和鬓猪肉，可驯服和繁殖，详见下文。

袭击与能力

鬓猪战斗策略参见 敌对生物攻略 § 鬓猪.

攻击

向上甩头，覆盖1.5米、水平45.2度弧形，造成10点钝击伤害。玩家在2米内时可用，冷却5秒。

驯服

一般驯服信息及策略参见“驯服”。

驯服野生鬓猪时，应潜行靠近，避免惊动。受威胁后会追击，直到玩家离开视野，再恢复“饥饿”状态。它们先冲撞再后退的攻击模式可预测，便于赶入圈栏。(建议:驯养)

应建牢固圈栏，防止逃跑及外界干扰驯服。可喂覆盆子、蓝莓、胡萝卜、洋葱、芜菁或红色蘑菇，其他地面食物都会忽略。在一米内丢食物，只要没有受惊就会进食。（以红色感叹号表示）食物可堆叠放置，每次只吃一份。

使用三份食物，驯服需30分钟现实游玩时间。不间断只要不受惊且地上有足够合适食物，30分钟就能完成。玩家须留在附近以推进进度；途中受惊会延长时间，也可能需要更多食物。

驯养生物

驯服后，鬓猪仍想自由。看到圈栏开口会向那里跑，可用来将它们赶到其他地方。

按左Shift+E可给驯服的鬓猪改名。按E互动会显示“（生物名）爱你”，并出现粉色爱心。它们不再怕火，但自然恢复生命值仍需进食。

驯服后还会解除与生成点的绑定，能更自由地移动。

说明: 废弃农场及符文石的鬓猪保留锚点，始终尝试返回，更难管理。建议原地繁殖后搬运后代，或给原个体命名，繁殖后杀死。原个体常待在圈栏角落盯着出生点，不愿游走，可能妨碍繁殖。

繁殖

鬓猪开心，吃饱浆果、胡萝卜、洋葱、芜菁或红蘑菇，三米内有另一只，且至少10米内不超过五只时，可繁殖。满足后互相积累好感（3），成功时显示粉色爱心。达到要求后怀孕，外观不显示；一分钟后产下（小猪），伴随红色烟雾。任何鬓猪都能怀孕，一只怀孕后仍能继续繁殖，使另一只也怀孕。

出生后，小猪需50分钟成年，继承产下它的亲代的星级。幼崽出生即驯服，须用屠夫刀或友军伤害击杀。成年前不能进行(不重命名或命令)互动。

"This land is hard and wild but we who are brought here are harder still. Take comfort, traveller, in the gifts before you, the good wood and stone, the fruits and flowers of the forest. Look also to the wild boar who roam these lands. They fear fire and the hand of man but they can be taught to obey it. Go quietly to them and let them eat of your stock. Roots of the ground are their pleasure." ― Runestone

Boars are wild creatures found throughout Valheim, known for their aggressive behavior when approached. They can be identified by their status tags indicating whether they are 'wild' or 'tame,' along with their emotional states, such as 'hungry,' 'happy,' 'frightened,' or 'acclimatizing.' Understanding these states can help players interact with them effectively.

Wild Boars are afraid of fire, including torches, and will enter a 'frightened' state when it is nearby. 2-star Boars are extremely rare and are great for acquiring leather scraps and boar meat through breeding. They can be tamed and bred. See the taming and breeding sections below.

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating boars, see Aggressive creature strategies § Boar.

Attack

Swings its head upward and hits in a 45.2-degree horizontal arc with a range of 1.5 meters (10 Blunt damage). This attack can be used every 5 seconds when within 2 meters of a player.

Taming

For general taming information and strategies, see Taming.

To tame a wild Boar, players should approach while sneaking to avoid alarming it. If a Boar feels threatened, it will become aggressive, chasing the player until they are out of sight, at which point it will revert to a 'hungry' state. Wild Boars have a predictable combat pattern, charging and then retreating, which can be used to the player's advantage when herding them into a pen (recommended to tame).

Constructing a secure pen is essential to prevent the Boar from escaping and to avoid disruption of the taming process by outside sources. To successfully tame a Boar, players need to feed it  Raspberries,  Blueberries,  Carrots,  Onions,  Turnips, or red  Mushrooms. They will ignore all other foods dropped on the ground. Just drop the food on the ground within one meter, and the Boar will eat, as long as it is not frightened (indicated by a red exclamation mark). Players can place the food on the ground in a stack, as the Boar will only eat one food from the stack at a time.

The taming speed is 30 minutes of real-life gameplay with three pieces of food, uninterrupted; as long as the Boar does not get frightened and the correct amount of food is on the ground, it should only take 30 minutes. A player must remain in the area for taming to progress. If the Boar becomes frightened at any time during taming, the process will take longer and may require more food.

Tamed

When tamed, Boars seek freedom. If a tamed Boar sees an opening in their fenced area, they will run towards it, which can be used to corral them from place to place.

Tamed Boars can be renamed by pressing LShift + E. Interacting with them using E will display the message "(Creature name) loves you" on-screen along with a cloud of pink hearts. They are no longer afraid of fire and require feeding for passive health regeneration.

Additionally, tamed Boars lose their anchor to their spawn point, allowing them to move more freely.

Note: Boars from abandoned farms and Runestones will retain their anchor point and will always try to return home, making them more challenging to handle. It is recommended to either breed these boars on the spot and move the offspring instead, or to name the original tamed animal and then, once bred, kill it. One issue with using the original animal is that it tends to sit in a corner of a pen, staring at its spawn point, which can interfere with breeding as it does not roam the pen.

Breeding

Tamed Boars can breed, provided the Boar is happy and well-fed with berries, carrots, onions, turnips or red mushrooms, along with there being another Boar at least within three meters, and no more than five Boars within at least 10 meters. Once the conditions are met, the Boar will accumulate love points (3) with one another until the pregnancy starts. Successful love point is indicated by pink hearts. After gaining enough love points, the Boar becomes pregnant, which is not visualized. After one minute, the Boar gives birth to a baby Boar (piglet), visualized by a red cloud. Any Boar can become pregnant, which means that if two Boars breed and one becomes pregnant, they still have the ability to breed and get the other pregnant.

Once born, the piglet will take 50 minutes to mature into an adult. Offspring inherit stars from the parent that gives birth to it. Offspring are tamed at birth and require a Butcher Knife or friendly fire to kill. Offspring cannot be interacted with (no renaming or commanding) until they become an adult.
