制作（技能）
说明
制作速度、锤子损耗和建造耐力消耗。

制作是一种技能。

效果

制作技能具有以下随等级成比例变化的效果：

  • 物品制作耗时最多减少至-60%。
  • 最多有25%的概率额外制作一件物品，仅适用于可在物品栏中堆叠的物品，不包括护甲和手持武器。
    • 使用按住Shift，制作x5制作选项更划算，累积机制会在每次成功额外产出时，使叠加奖励数量增加1。成功1至5次时，额外产出可能为+1、+3、+6、+10或+15；而同样5件物品按1件逐1件制作、没有叠加奖励时，仅额外+1至+5。满级预期额外物品为+37.5%。
    • 若应产生额外物品，但会超过背包容量，制作会取消，背包不发生变化。此时是否仍获得经验尚未确认。
  • 工具锤耐久和耐力消耗最多降低至-50%。

升级

以下活动提供制作技能经验： 经验:

  • 制作物品可获得0.25经验。
  • 建造建筑可获得0.25经验。
    • 拆除建筑会使下一次建造无法获得经验，这种经验欠账可累积至20座建筑。
  • 在制作站修理物品，按恢复耐久比例获得最多0.25经验。

Crafting (skill)
Description
Crafting speed, hammer wear and building stamina use.

Crafting is a skill.

Effects

The Crafting skill has the following effect, proportional to the level:

  • Reduces the time to craft item down to -60%.
  • Up to a 25% chance to craft an extra item, it applies only to items that can be stacked in the inventory. Notably, armors and wielded weapons are excluded.
    • Using the hold shift x5 crafting option is even more advantageous, as a cumulative mechanism increases a stacking bonus amount by 1 for each successful bonus opportunity. Possible yields are +1, +3, +6, +10 and +15 for 1 to 5 successful chances (instead of +1 to +5 by crafting the same 5 items 1 by 1 without the stacking bonus). This results in +37.5% expected extra items at max skill level.
    • If an additional item should be crafted, but it would overflow the player's inventory, then the crafting is cancelled, and the player's inventory does not change. Whether this still rewards XP has not been confirmed.
  • Reduces Hammer durability cost and stamina consumption down to -50%.

Leveling up

The following activities give Crafting skill experience:

  • Crafting an item gives 0.25 experience.
  • Building a structure gives 0.25 experience.
    • Deconstructing a structure prevents the next built from giving experience, the debt can go down to 20 structures.
  • Repairing an item at a crafting station gives up to 0.25 experience, proportionally to the durability repaired.
