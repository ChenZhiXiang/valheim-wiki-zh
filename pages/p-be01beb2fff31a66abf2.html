平原牦牛是北境平原中的敌对生物，形似大型蜥蜴与牛的混合体，长棕色蓬毛，皮肤像爬行动物。平原牦牛可被驯养生物，之后能繁殖及骑行。

袭击与能力

击败平原牦牛的策略见 敌对生物攻略 § 平原牦牛.

咬伤

先后仰，再向前扫咬玩家，造成130点劈砍伤害。范围为5.5米、水平45度弧形，玩家在5米内时可用，冷却4秒。

踩踏

后腿站起再砸下，造成120点钝击、100点等级0的砍伐及100点等级0的镐击伤害。范围为半径2.5米的圆，玩家在6米内时可用，冷却12秒。

驯服

一般驯服说明及策略见“驯服”。

平原牦牛常成群并回到同一位置，因此即使早期，也可定期冲过平原，在牛群聚集处撒云莓，驯服大量牦牛。也可用较高的潜行（技能），留意周围，潜行接近单只或小群再撒云莓。操作时建议保留至少50耐力，被发现后便于逃跑。

多数或全部驯服后，杀人蜂和丑地精常会转而攻击牦牛，而非玩家。牛群较多时，玩家与新生成敌人之间通常会有一只牦牛。但若缺少治疗，在夜间或某些入侵期间持续加载区域，部分牦牛最终可能死亡。

此方法无须建牛栏，但撒食物时死亡风险高。若护甲挡不住杀人蜂，可将除云莓和燧石矛外的物品都留在箱子中。带传送门的简易庇护可用于安全进入平原，并在驯服期间维持区域加载。

乘骑

驯服的平原牦牛可以骑乘。先制作平原牦牛鞍，放入快捷栏，靠近牦牛后使用以装鞍。骑乘时方向键不会使牦牛转向，须转动视角，它始终朝镜头方向前进。可步行或冲刺，无法后退。

骑乘牦牛是强大伙伴，保留高生命值和伤害抗性，冲刺速度比奔跑技能最高的玩家还快，也不受装备护甲的减速影响。它完全忽略骑手负重，即使超重也能移动和冲刺。

骑乘牦牛有240耐力，冲刺消耗速度约与玩家相同。提高乘骑技能可加快速度并降低耐力消耗。但恢复远慢于玩家：最近吃过食物时，约每4秒恢复一点；饥饿时每10秒恢复一点。

目前骑乘时不能使用玩家武器，按攻击键会立即下马。不过，全速冲刺的牦牛能践踏多数其他生物，接触时造成大量伤害。停下不动时，也会像未装鞍时一样攻击附近敌人。

繁殖

平原牦牛满足以下条件时可繁殖：

  • 开心且吃饱以下食物： 云莓, 亚麻,或 大麦
  • 8米内有另一只牦牛
  • 20米内不超过4只牦牛
  • 尚未怀孕
  • 玩家在约64米以内

牦牛需要足够屋内高度，才能寻路并吃到食物。平地上垂直净空8米应已足够，但室内饲养建议10米或更高。

条件满足后，牦牛会互相积累好感，直至怀孕。怀孕至少持续两分钟，之后随机产下幼崽。

幼崽出生后，需要1小时40分钟长成成年牦牛。

说明

  • “Lox”由“lizard”（蜥蜴）和“ox”（牛）组合而成，呼应其外形。
  • 商人霍尔德营地有一只名为海尔司坦的驯养牦牛。希尔德有两只，分别名为小蓝莓和小红莓。
  • 靠近野生牦牛总会使它受惊，并对玩家敌对。
  • 牦牛抬起前蹄砸下时，近战伤害能延伸到前方较远位置。挖沟阻挡时要注意：它无须跨过窄沟，也能破坏另一侧的建筑。
  • 平原牦牛肉营养丰富，是不错的基础生命值食物。平原牦牛肉派的生命值和耐力加成几乎与蛟龙肉羹相同，后者曾是迷雾之地发布前综合最强的生命值食物。牦牛食物比蛟龙食物容易获得，因为牦牛可驯服繁殖，蛟龙则必须寻找。

Lox are aggressive creatures found in Plains. They are an enlarged fusion of both a lizard and an ox, with shaggy brown hair and reptilian skin. Lox can be tamed which allows them to be bred and ridden.

Attacks and abilities

For strategies for defeating Lox, see Aggressive creature strategies § Lox.

Bite

Leans back before sweeping forward to chomp down on the player (130 Slash damage). This extends 5.5 meters in a 45-degree horizontal arc and can be used every 4 seconds when within 5 meters of a player.

Stomp

Stands on their hind legs and slams down to attack (120 Blunt, 100 Tier 0 Chop and 100 Tier 0 Pickaxe damage). This attack hits in a circular area with a radius of 2.5 meters and can be used every 12 seconds when within 6 meters of a player.

Taming

For general taming information and strategies, see Taming.

Because Lox tend to stay in herds and wander back to the same location, it is possible to tame large groups of Lox even early in the game by periodically sprinting through the Plains and scattering the Cloudberries around places where the herds gather. Alternatively, with a decent Sneak (Skill) (and carefully watching the player's surroundings) it is possible to sneak at single Lox (or small herds) and disperse the Cloudberries this way. It is recommended to not go below 50 stamina when doing this so the player can run away if spotted.

Once most or all of them are tamed, Deathsquitos and Fulings will often attack them instead of the player. With a number of tamed Lox, there will usually be one between the player and a new spawn. However, without adequate healing, some of the Lox are liable to eventually die if the area is active at night or during certain invasions.

This strategy saves the player from having to build pens for the Lox, but the risk of death is high while scattering seeds. If the player's armor does not protect them from a Deathsquito attack, it may be ideal to leave everything in chests except Cloudberries and flint spears. A rudimentary shelter with a Portal in it can be used to safely enter the Plains and to keep the area active while the Lox are being tamed.

Riding

Tamed Lox can be utilized as mounts. First, create a Lox Saddle, then equip it to a Lox by assigning the saddle to a hot key and using it while close to the creature. Directional keys will not cause the Lox to turn while riding. Instead, the player must move the camera, and the Lox will always move straight in the direction that the camera is facing. A Lox can move either at walking or sprinting speed - it cannot reverse.

A ridden Lox is a very powerful companion. In addition to retaining their impressive health and damage resistances, a sprinting Lox is faster than even the most high sprint-level players, while also enjoying the benefit of not being slowed down by equipped armor. A ridden Lox also completely ignores a player's carried weight, able to move and even sprint while the rider is encumbered.

A mounted Lox has 240 points of stamina which drain at approximately the same rate as a player's while sprinting, however, leveling the Riding skill both increases the speed of a ridden Lox as well as reducing the amount of stamina drained. It is important to note that a Lox's stamina regenerates much, much slower than a player's, one point is regained roughly every 4 seconds while the creature has been recently fed, and one every 10 seconds while it is hungry.

While it is not currently possible to utilize a player's weapons while riding (trying to do so results in the player instantly dismounting as soon as the attack key is pressed) a Lox at full sprint is capable of trampling most other creatures underfoot, dealing heavy damage when it comes into contact with them. Additionally, when not making the Lox move, it will attack nearby enemies like it would do when unsaddled.

Breeding

Lox can breed provided the Lox is:

  • Happy and well-fed with Cloudberries, Flax, or Barley
  • There is another Lox within 8 meters
  • No more than 4 Lox within 20 meters
  • Not already pregnant
  • Within approximately 64 meters of a player

Additionally, in order for a Lox to be able to pathfind to and eat food, it can't be in a room with a ceiling that is too low. On flat ground, 8 meters of vertical clearance should be enough, but it is recommended to have 10 meters or more if you intend to house your Lox indoors.

Once met, the Lox will accumulate love points with each other until the pregnancy starts. Pregnancy will last a minimum of two minutes, after which the birth of the baby Lox is random chance.

Once born, the baby Lox will take 1 hour and 40 minutes to mature into an adult.

Notes

  • The name 'Lox' is a fusion of the words 'lizard' and 'ox', which reflects their design.
  • The trader, Haldor, has a tamed Lox named Halstein that can be found in his camp. Hildir has two tamed Lox named Blåbär and Hallon.
  • Approaching a wild Lox will always frighten it and cause it to become aggressive to the player.
  • Lox can project their melee damage a considerable distance forward by rearing up and smashing down with their front hooves. When digging ditches to block them, this must be kept in mind; a Lox does not have to be able to cross a narrow ditch to destroy structures on the other side.
  • Lox Meat is very healthy and a good basic HP food. Lox Meat Pie has nearly the same health and stamina gain as Serpent Stew, which were the best overall health foods until the Mistlands release. One advantage Lox-based food has over Serpent-based food is accessibility: Lox can be tamed and bred, whereas Serpents must be found.
