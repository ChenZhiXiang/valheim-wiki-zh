定身
你被卡住了
效果
防止移动与旋转。

定身是阻止移动和转身的状态效应，不阻止攻击、格挡或翻滚。

定身生物无法移动或转向，但面前有范围内目标仍能攻击。摩根等攻击动画自带位移的生物，定身时仍能完成这些位移。

来源

陷阱施加5秒定身，玩家制作及封印之塔中的版本都如此。

用翡翠升级的武器，命中有20%概率施加10秒定身：

  •  丛林之锤
  •  尼德霍格：原始
  •  狂战之斧：原始
  •  原始屠杀者
  •  撕裂者：原始
  •  邪根之牙

野生之杖召唤的邪煞之根命中有10%概率施加10秒定身。

Immobilized
You are stuck
Effects
Prevents movement and rotation.

Immobilized is a Status effect that prevents movement and character rotation, it does not prevent attacking, blocking or rolling.

An immobilized creature cannot move or change the direction that they are facing, but can still attack if they are facing a target within their range. Creatures that have movements that are part of their attack animations, such as the Morgen, can still perform these movements while immobilized.

Sources

 Trap applies a 5-second Immobilized status, both the one crafted by players and those found in Sealed Tower.

Weapons upgraded with Jade apply a 10-second Immobilized status with a 20% chance on hit:

  •  Klossen
  •  Nidhögg the Primal
  •  Primal Berserkir Axes
  •  Primal Slayer
  •  Splitnir the Primal
  •  Root Fang

The Roots invoked by the  Staff of the Wild have a 10% chance to apply a 10-second Immobilized status on hit.
