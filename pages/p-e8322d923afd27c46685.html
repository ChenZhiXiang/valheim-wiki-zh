饰品列表
List of trinkets

饰物是可装备、提供各种战斗收益的物品。

装备饰品可获取血怒，积累足够后自动消耗并触发效果。重复触发同一饰品会重置计时。

同时仅一饰品，卸下不取消已激活效果，因此可切换并逐个触发，累积多种效果。

迷雾之地阶段之前的饰品，可在等级1熔炉制作。迷雾之地及以后需要等级1黑熔炉。

Trinkets are items that can be equipped to provide various benefits during combat.

Equipping a trinket allows the player to gain Adrenaline, which will be spent automatically to trigger the trinket's effect once enough adrenaline has been gained. Triggering twice the same trinket resets the effect timer.

Only one trinket can be equipped at a time. Removing a trinket does not cancel its activated effects, thus it is possible to accumulate multiple trinket effects by switching and activating each trinket.

All trinkets up until Mistlands-tier can be crafted at a Level 1 Forge. Mistlands-tier trinkets and onwards require Level 1 Black Forge.
