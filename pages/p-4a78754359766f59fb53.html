石铺路是地面装饰，在锄头菜单中选择该功能后对地面使用即可。

将地面改为石铺路需要耐力，并消耗1份石头。

铺设石路时，玩家必须位于切石机范围内。石路仅有外观作用，在上面奔跑不会加速，也不会减少耐力消耗。

The Paved Road is a ground decoration applied by selecting the option in the Hoe menu and using it on the ground.

Changing the ground type to Paved Road requires Stamina and costs 1 Stone.

The player is required to be within the radius of a Stonecutter in order to place Paved Roads. Paved Roads are purely cosmetic, they do not give any speed boost or stamina reduction when running on them.
