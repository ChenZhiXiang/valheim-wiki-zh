生成来源

共有五套生成敌人的系统，详情请参阅各自页面。

  • 实体生成器是可破坏实体，玩家在附近时会持续生成敌对生物。
  • 生成点是特定建筑和地下城中的固定位置，大多只能使用一次。
  • 区生成是随玩家移动而反复发生的生物生成。
  • 事件生成器会在袭击事件激活时生成特定生物。
  • 弃誓者祭坛会在玩家献上正确祭品时生成生物。

其他互动

玩家基地是大多数玩家建造的建筑周围形成的隐形区域，阻止生物在其中生成，仅影响事件生成器、生成点和区域生成。

Sources of spawning

There are five systems which spawn enemies. See the individual pages for further details.

  • Physical spawners are destructible entities that continuously spawn hostile creatures when a player is nearby.
  • Spawn points are fixed positions at certain structures and dungeons. Most of them are single use.
  • Zone spawning is the repeatable spawning of creatures that happens as players move around.
  • Event spawners spawn certain creatures when a raid event is activated.
  • Forsaken Altars spawn a creature when a player makes the right offering.

Other interactions

Player base is an invisible area created around most player-made structures that prevents creatures from spawning inside them (only affects event spawners, spawn points and zone spawning).
