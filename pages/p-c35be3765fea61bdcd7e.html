说明

入口由尖锐冰体组成，洞口顶部悬挂巨大的蓝色冰柱。

虽然有大量邪蝠和乌尔夫守卫，最大的威胁仍是小首领盖尔哈法。

可找到箱子、发光的水晶晶簇、芬里之发、芬里之爪及肉堆。

战略

盖尔哈法战斗策略参见 首领攻略 § 盖尔哈法.

Description

The entrance is composed of a spiky ice formation with large, blue icicles hanging from the roof of the cave mouth.

Even guarded by high amount of Bats and Ulvs, the biggest threat is the miniboss Geirrhafa.

You may find chests, glowing Crystal formations, Fenris Hair, Fenris Claws and meat piles.

Strategy

For strategies for defeating Geirrhafa, see Boss strategies § Geirrhafa.

Notes

  • This special dungeon with its special miniboss can be obtained at Hildir's map table.
  • Bring Torches or a Dverger Circlet as dungeons are usually very dark.
