封印之塔是北境平原中的开放式地下城。与其他地下城不同，封印之塔位于地表，进入无需加载画面。

每个世界有3座封印之塔，与希尔德面前的地图互动可揭示位置。

说明

塔由无法破坏的黑石建成，部分窗户封闭，还有箭孔及木板。也有开放阳台可作为入口。塔内部禁止建造与拆除。

真正入口位于塔顶，有重兵守卫。

内部通常有五层。从上往下前四层很相似，由大量丑地精、丑地精萨满守卫，遍布常藏在草堆下的陷阱，也可找到一些平原宝箱。

底层有小首领吉尔与吞格尔及二至三个特殊平原宝箱。吉尔掉落

战略

塔内遍布丑地精与丑地精萨满。携带抗火药剂及治疗药剂会更轻松。可先抵达塔顶，建造传送门，再向下推进至小首领。

对付小首领时，可留在楼梯处，探出身攻击。吉尔施放护盾，吞格尔攻击，但很容易避开全部伤害。杀死吞格尔后，吉尔会跳下，他只是更强的萨满。

可将双人小首领引到门边，再用炸弹或鹿角战锤、铁大锤及歼灭者等范围攻击击杀。

说明

  • 吉尔实际上可以离开封印之塔。杀死吞格尔后，将吉尔引到竞技场入口，他会追赶玩家、走上楼梯。引至顶层后可将其推下塔。生物不受坠落伤害，因此吉尔应能存活。

Sealed Towers are open dungeons found in Plains. Unlike other dungeons, Sealed Towers take place in the surface and do not need a loading screen to enter.

Each world contains 3 Sealed Towers, their locations can be revealed by interacting with the map in front of Hildir.

Description

The Tower is made of unbreakable black stone, some sealed windows, arrow slits and wood panels. There are also some kind of open balconies that can be used as entrances. Building and deconstructing is disallowed in the interior of the Tower.

The real entrance is located at the roof of the tower and is heavily guarded.

Once inside, there are generally five floors. The first four floors (from top to bottom) are very similar, they will be heavily guarded by Fulings, Fuling Shamans and full of Traps, often hidden under straw piles. Some Plains chests can also be found.

The last floor contains the minibosses Zil & Thungr and two or three special Plains chests. Zil drops

Strategy

The Tower is filled with Fulings and Fuling Shamans. Bring Fire resistance potions and healing potions to make the Tower easier. Players may want to work their way to the roof, construct a portal there, then work their way back down to the minibosses.

When fighting the minibosses, it is very easy to stay in the stairway and pop out to hit them. Zil will cast a shield, and Thungr will attack, but it is very easy to avoid all of his damage. Once you kill Thungr, Zil will jump down - he is just a strong shaman.

Players can kill the duo miniboss by dragging them close to the door, then using AOE attacks, such as Bombs or Stagbreaker, Iron Sledge, and Demolisher.

Notes

  • Zil can actually leave Sealed Tower. After killing Thungr, lead Zil to the entrance of the arena. Zil will chase the player, and go up the stairs. Lead him up to the top floor, and then the player can shove him off the Tower. Creatures do not take fall damage, so Zil should survive.
