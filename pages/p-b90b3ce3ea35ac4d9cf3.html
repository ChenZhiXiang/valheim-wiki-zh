魔阁哈拉是位于极北之地的一处地牢。开启其入口“莫克哈拉之门”需要隐秘钥匙，钥匙会在开门后消耗。

说明

地下城约有14层，中央大裂隙将楼层分成两侧，可通过各种桥梁和吊桥穿行。入口靠近顶部，最珍贵的战利品位于底层。地下城由四至五个大型区段生成。

内部始终昏暗，视为冷环境，但不会冰冻。

地下城内部分同样名为莫克哈拉之门的大门，开启时消耗一把精巧钥匙，之后无法关闭。

资源

所有楼层由巨人战士、巨人巫师、丑地精俘虏、被囚禁的矮人和六面浆块守卫。玩家进入生成点60米内时，被俘丑地精和囚禁矮人会生成，最高为二星。

六面浆块有600点生命。玩家在20米内，且附近没有它此前生成的无形浆块时，每5秒生成一个无形黏团。摧毁后从废铁、巨木、越橘及死黏团中掉落二至三个物品。

贵重物品碧渊石、太阳石、紫御石和影渊石可在地面或装饰墙龛找到。有些墙龛改为放置堇青石、翡翠或血石。

碎石堆可破坏，获得一至五个上古金币及灰石。木碎片堆掉落二至三个木材。

火坑可破坏，获得2至4个纪念用炭或木炭。

家具可破坏，获得巨木和锁链。旗帜及地毯掉落红色黄麻。

底部楼层

摧毁底层的邪恶之冰会立即在世界某处触发巨人侵略事件。恶意冰晶有1,000点生命。玩家在120米内时，每50秒生成一名巨人战士或巨人巫师，直到50米内已有六名。其中四分之三为巨人战士，最高可为二星。

训练假人可破坏，获得木料、大麦及亚麻。

武器架可破坏，获得木料、废铁及武器、法杖或盾牌模具。

煤堆可破坏，获得三至五个纪念煤。

卷起的铺位可破坏，获得灰烬刚鬣皮、驼鹿毛皮、平原牦牛皮及狼皮。

战略

玩家可从中央裂隙跳下，快速抵达底层，再用钩绳返回入口。穿戴轻羽斗篷或拥有超过100点生命即可承受坠落，因为坠落最多造成100点伤害。

背景故事

雾尼并未点名，而是统称莫克哈拉住民：“嘎！这里住着奇怪的家伙，由邪恶本身创造……”[ 1 ]

说明

  • 内部可放置营火和烹饪台。
  • 莫克哈拉地下城始终至少有一条无需钩绳就能返回地面的路径，避免玩家被困。

Mörkhalla is a dungeon found in the Deep North. An Intricate Key is required to open its entrance, the Gates of Mörkhalla. The key is destroyed after opening the entrance.

Description

The dungeon consists of around 14 floors, separated by a large central gap that splits the floors into two separate sides. The two sides can be crossed with various bridges and drawbridges. The entrance is near the top of the dungeon and the most valuable loot can be found on the bottom floor. The dungeon is generated from four or five large sections.

Inside, the environment is always dark and counts as cold, but not freezing.

Some gates inside the dungeon, also named Gates of Mörkhalla, consume an Intricate Key when opened and cannot be closed afterwards.

Resources

All floors are protected by Krigen, Hexen, Captive Fuling, Imprisoned Dvergr and Hexahedric Pulp. Captive Fulings and Imprisoned Dvergr spawn when a player comes within 60 meters of their spawn point, with up to two stars.

A Hexahedric Pulp has 600 health and spawns a Shapeless Pulp every 5 seconds while a player is within 20 meters, as long as no other Shapeless Pulp it spawned is nearby. When destroyed, it drops two to three items from  Scrap Iron,  Timberwood,  Lingonberries and  Dead Pulp.

Valuables Grimvarn, Solryth, Veydris and Draumyx can be found on the floor or in decorative wall sockets. Some sockets hold  Iolite,  Jade or  Bloodstone instead.

Rubble piles can be destroyed for one to five Ancient Coins and Grausten. Piles of wooden debris drop two to three  Wood.

Fire Pits can be destroyed for 2 to 4 Memorial Coals or Coals.

Furniture can be destroyed for Timberwood and Chains. Banners and rugs drop  Red Jute.

Bottom floor

Destroying the Malicious Ice on the bottom floor immediately starts a Jotun Invasion event somewhere in the world. The Malicious Ice has 1,000 health. While a player is within 120 meters, it spawns a Krigen or Hexen every 50 seconds, until six of them are within 50 meters. Three in four are Krigen, and they can have up to two stars.

Training dummies can be destroyed for Timberwood, Barley and Flax.

Weapon stands can be destroyed for Timberwood, Scrap Iron, and weapon, staff or shield Moulds.

Coal piles can be destroyed for three to five Memorial Coal.

Rolled sleeping spots can be destroyed for Asksvin Hide, Moose Hide, Lox Pelt, and Wolf Pelt.

Strategy

The player can jump down the central gap to access the bottom floor quickly and ascend back to the entrance with a Grappling Hook. The fall can be survived by either wearing the Feather Cape or having over 100 health (since falling can only deal up to 100 damage).

Lore

Munin describes the inhabitants of Mörkhalla as a whole, without naming them: "Kraa! Strange folk dwell here, created from the very evil itself..."[1]

Notes

  • Campfires and Cooking Stations can be placed inside.
  • Morkhalla dungeons always have at least one route back to the surface that doesn't require a Grappling Hook so that players can't get trapped inside it.
