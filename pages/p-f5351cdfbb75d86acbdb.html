精英苦难之碑是神秘的地点中的生物生成点。

其颜色为紫色，不同于偏红的普通苦难之碑。

精英纪念碑只生成2星版本：灰烬僵尸概率42%，灰烬战士概率33%，灰烬射手概率17%，灰烬术士概率8%。

玩家进入60米内时纪念碑激活，每10秒尝试在4米内生成生物。

The elite Monument of Torment is a creature spawner found at the Mysterious Locations.

They are violet in color, unlike the more red regular Monument of Torment.

The elite Monument can spawn only 2-starred versions of Charred Twitcher (42% chance), Charred Warrior (33% chance), Charred Marksman (17% chance) and Charred Warlock (8% chance).

The Monument activates when there is a player within 60 meters. The Monument attempts to spawn within 4 meters every 10 seconds.
