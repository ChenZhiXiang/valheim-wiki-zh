霍尔德是黑暗林山中的角色。他是一名矮人商人，穿棕色长袍、金色腰带和披风。买家拥有足够钱币且世界进度达到要求后，可在霍尔德营地购买多种独特物品。

传记

除在英灵神殿经商外，霍尔德的经历不详。他曾在黑暗林山扎营，驯服名为海尔司坦的平原牦牛，可看到它在同一营地从碗里进食。

根据Iron Gate博客介绍[ 1 ]及他的一句自述，霍尔德是矮人。

霍尔德会说多种台词，包括“我在矮人里也算矮的”“看看这些东西”“瞧瞧我的货物”和“Radjdadaj……”

霍尔德的妹妹希尔德可在青青草原找到。

位置

霍尔德有10处可能的生成位置，均位于黑暗林山，距世界中心至少1500米。靠近任意一处后，他就会永久定居在那里。相关区域生成时，距离320至453米即可触发，约为默认小地图范围的两倍。发现后，世界地图会出现他的袋子图标。

他与专属地点一起生成。小营地包含由海尔司坦拉动的大木车、撑起的棚布、木桶、桌子及多种展示物，包括矛、剑、盾、头盔和食物。营地被闪烁的透明穹顶包围，形成生物无法进入的安全区，且无法摧毁。

霍尔德旁的熊皮地毯可破坏，掉落巨熊战利品。力场内其他家具均无法破坏。

趣闻

  • “霍尔德”之名源自古诺尔斯语，可能意为“托尔之岩”。[ 2 ]
  • 霍尔德营地周围的透明穹顶似乎由四块小符文石供能，每块刻有奥丁幻象符文，这种维京符号用于伪装和欺骗。屏障仅允许潜在顾客看见并进入营地，灰矮人等敌对生物则无法进入，与其用途相符。

Haldor is a character found in Black Forest. He is a Dvergr trader wearing a brown cloak, a gold belt and cape. At his camp, Haldor sells several unique items that can be purchased if the buyer has a sufficient amount of Coins and world progression.

Biography

Nothing is known about Haldor beyond his occupation as a trader within Valheim. At some point in time, he set up camp within the Black Forest and tamed a Lox named Halstein that can be seen feeding from a bowl in the same camp.

Haldor is a Dvergr or "dwarf", according to his introduction on Iron Gate's blog[1], and how he refers to himself by one of his many quotes.

Haldor says multiple phrases including "I'm short for a dwarf", "Check this stuff out", "Take a look at my wares" and "Radjdadaj…"

Hildir, Haldor's sister, can be found in Meadows.

Location

Haldor has 10 possible spawn locations that are located in Black Forests which are at least 1500 meters away from the center of the world. Approaching any of these locations will permanently place Haldor there. This can happen from 320 to 453 meters away (about twice of the default minimap range) when the related Zones are generated. When discovered, his bag icon will appear on the world map.

He spawns with his own point of interest, a small camp consisting of a large wooden wagon pulled by Halstein, a propped up canopy, barrels, tables and multiple display items including spears, swords, shields, helmets and food. The camp is surrounded by a shimmering, transparent dome that acts as a safety zone that Creatures cannot enter. The safe zone cannot be destroyed.

The Bearskin Rug situated next to Haldor can be destroyed to drop a Bear Trophy. No other furniture can be destroyed inside the force field.

Trivia

  • "Haldor" is a name having roots in Old Norse origins. Its meaning is presumably "Thor's rock".[2]
  • The transparent dome which surrounded Haldor's camp apparently powered by four small runestones placed around it and that each of them carved with Odin's Illusory Rune, a Viking glyph devised to provide camouflage and deception. This is fitting as the barrier only allows Haldor's potential customers to both see his camp and pass through it while hostile beings such as Greydwarfs cannot enter.
