霜铸熔炉是极北之地阶段的制作设施，将铸坯固化为北方深境的武器、护甲、盾牌、法杖及隐秘钥匙成品。

建材

霜铸熔炉位于工具锤的制作类别，建造时附近需要工作台。不能放在水中或斜面上。所需材料：

  •  15 铁
  •  20 石头
  •  10 冰霜之核

耐久度为500，对穿刺和冰霜伤害具有抗性，对砍伐伤害较弱，免疫毒素和精神伤害。

用途

使用寒冰窑制作的液态冰霜作为燃料。最多装20份液态冰霜，每份持续10秒。只有炉内存在未完成铸坯时才消耗燃料。

将铸坯放在中央可制作成品。霜铸熔炉一次容纳一个铸坯，每次消耗五份液态冰霜，耗时50秒。完成后即使留在炉内也不会烧毁，产出物品具有满耐久度。

Frost Foundry is a structure used to craft in the Deep North. It hardens Casts into finished Deep North weapons, armor, shields, staffs and the  Intricate Key.

Building

The Frost Foundry is found in the Crafting category of the Hammer and requires a nearby Workbench to build. It cannot be placed in water or on sloped surfaces. It costs:

  •  15 Iron
  •  20 Stone
  •  10 Frostcore

It has 500 durability. It is resistant to pierce and frost damage, weak to chop damage, and immune to poison and spirit damage.

Usage

It can be fueled with Liquid Frost, which is made in the Frigid Kiln. It holds up to 20 Liquid Frost, and each unit lasts 10 seconds. Fuel is only used while an unfinished cast is inside.

A Cast can be placed at the center to create the final item. The Frost Foundry holds one cast at a time. The process consumes five Liquid Frost and takes 50 seconds. Finished items cannot burn if left inside. Items made in the Frost Foundry come out at full durability.

Casts are made at the Black Forge or the Galdr Table, as listed below.
