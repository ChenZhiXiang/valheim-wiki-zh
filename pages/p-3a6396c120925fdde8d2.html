生成点

本站支持中英文站内搜索。带 ↗ 的链接指向外部网站；各页来源版本见页脚。

生成点分布在地点及地下城，包括诡秘墓穴、巨魔洞穴和地下圣堂附近。大多数生成点仅生效一次，部分生成点仅有一定概率出现。

  • 玩家进入60米范围时，生成点会激活。
  • 除非禁用等级提升，否则生成的生物有10%概率提升等级，其中1星概率为9%，2星概率为1%。
  • 生成的生物被杀死后，重生计时开始；一次性生成点除外。
  • 大多数生成点昼夜都生效，因此白天也可能出现大型软泥怪和鬼魅。

Spawns points are placed near points of interest and dungeons (Burial Chambers, Troll Cave and Sunken Crypts). Most spawn points only work once. Some spawn points only have a random chance of appearing.

  • Spawn points activate when there is a player within 60 meters.
  • The spawned creature has a 10% chance to level up (9% for 1 star, 1% for 2 stars), unless the level up is disabled.
  • Respawn timer starts ticking when the spawned creatures are killed, unless the spawn point is one time.
  • Most spawn points work both day and night (which allows daytime Oozers and Wraiths).
