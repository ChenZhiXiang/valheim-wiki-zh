矮人 灰烬之地 板条箱是矮人板条箱的变体，位于灰烬之地中的矮人焦毁塔楼废墟。

受矮人焦黑塔废墟内的矮人守护石保护。攻击箱子或守护石会惊动附近灰烬之地矮人。

The Dvergr Ashlands crate is a variant of the Dvergr Crate found in Dvergr Charred Tower Ruins in the Ashlands.

They are protected by Dvergr Wards found inside the Dvergr Charred Tower Ruins. Attacking the crate or the Ward will alert nearby Ashlands Dvergr.
