奔跑（技能）
说明
跑步时速度和耐力消耗

奔跑是一种技能。

效果

冲刺速度最多提高+25%，耐力消耗最多降低至-50%。

升级

冲刺每秒获得0.2经验，即使不足一秒也按比例积累。坡度、负重和速度修正不影响经验。

速度

未计装备修正，技能0时冲刺25.2公里/小时，即7米/秒；技能100时31.5公里/小时。还可通过以下方法提高：

  • 已激活 焰灵脚环 护符: +15%
  • 食用 拉塔托斯克药剂: +15%
  • 穿着任何 芬里套装 部件: +3%各项,(+9% 总和)
  • 已激活 冰幼龙母之力: +10%
  • 穿灰烬刚鬣披风：最多+25%。

无论奔跑技能等级如何，角色步行速度均为13.5km/h。

Running (skill)
Description
Speed and stamina drain when running.

Running is a skill.

Effects

Improves sprinting speed by up to +25% and reduces stamina drain down to -50%.

Leveling up

The player gains 0.2 Experience per second while sprinting. Sprinting even a fraction of a second will still accumulate xp proportionally. Slope, weight carried, and movement modifiers do not affect the xp gain.

Speed

Characters sprint at 25.2 km/h (7 m/s) with 0 skill level and at 31.5 km/h with 100 skill levels, before equipment modifiers. Speed can be further increased by various means:

  • An active Nimble Anklet trinket: +15%
  • Consuming a Tonic of Ratatosk: +15%
  • Wearing any Fenris Set pieces: +3% each, (+9% total)
  • Active Moder Power: +10%
  • Wearing Asksvin Cloak: up to +25%.

Characters walk at 13.5km/h no matter the run skill level.
