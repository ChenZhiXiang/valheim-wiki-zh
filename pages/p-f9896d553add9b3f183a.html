烟花是可投进火中，产生观赏爆炸效果的消耗品。使用时显示“你向火中扔了一些X”，X替换为所用烟花名称。

可从希尔德以50钱币购买普通烟花。其他颜色烟花可在任意等级工作台，用基础烟花与材料制作，爆炸颜色与烟花颜色一致。

Fireworks are a type of consumable that can be used on a fire to produce an aesthetic explosion. The message, "You throw some X into the fire" appears when activated, "X" replaced with the firework you use.

Basic fireworks can be purchased from Hildir for  50 Coins. Other colored fireworks are crafted by combining Basic fireworks with materials at a Workbench of any level. The color of the explosion matches the color of the firework.
