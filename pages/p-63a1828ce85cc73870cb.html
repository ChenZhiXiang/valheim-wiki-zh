伐木(技能)
说明
决定用斧头砍树时的伤害。 不影响 斧（技能）.

伐木是与斧伐木伤害相关的技能。

效果

对树、原木和树桩，随机伐木倍率从等级0的25–55%增至等级100的85–100%。挥斧耐力消耗不由伐木降低，仅斧降低。

伐木升级不能砍桦树等硬树，需更高工具等级，详斧。

伤害缩放详伤害机制# 技能因素。

升级

斧打树获1伐木经验，非斧经验。每挥一次最多一次，不论树数。同时打树和非树，两技能都获经验；至少一个为敌人或玩家时，两者各1.5xp。

说明

  • 平地树沿最后挥击时玩家面向倒下。
  • 打太硬树也获伐木经验。
  • 狂战之斧各变体基础伤害相同，但狂战之斧：嗜血随缺失生命缩放。

Wood cutting (skill)
Description
Determines axe damage when hitting trees. Does not impact Axes (skill).

Wood cutting is a skill, related to the chop damage dealt by Axes.

Effect

Higher levels use a random chop damage multiplier ranging from 25–55% at skill level 0 to 85–100% at skill level 100 against susceptible entities (trees, logs, and stumps). Stamina cost to swing an axe is not reduced by the Wood Cutting skill, only by the axes skill.

Leveling the wood cutting skill does not allow you to cut tougher trees, like Birch trees, higher tool tiers are required for that. For more information, see Axes.

For the details of damage scaling, see Damage mechanics#Skill factor

Leveling up

Hitting trees with an axe gives 1 experience for woodcutting, (not axe xp). This can only occur once per swing, regardless of the number of trees hit. If a tree and a non-tree is hit in the same swing with an axe, the player will gain xp for both woodcutting and axe skills. If at least one of those was an enemy or player, the player will gain 1.5xp for both instead.

Notes

  • A tree will fall in the direction the player is facing on the last swing, if the tree is on level ground.
  • You gain Wood Cutting experience from hitting trees, even when the tree is "too hard".
  • The base damage for the Berserkir Axes is the same for all the variants, however, the Bleeding Berserkir Axes do scale their damage with respect to missing health.
