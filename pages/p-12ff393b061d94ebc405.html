睡眠需要床铺、龙床或灰烬之木床榻，仅夜间可进行，会将白天 与夜间循环推进至次日清晨。

要求

认领床后可睡，其他玩家已认领床无法再认领，除非其改认另一床。

仅在满足以下条件时才能睡觉：

  1. 当前是午后或晚间。
  2. 附近没有敌人
  3. 床上方有屋顶，并达到80%的遮蔽。
  4. 存在火附近地区。
  5. 玩家未处于湿。

效果

睡眠快进时间，冶炼炉等制作站继续工作至原料耗尽。睡得越晚，跳过时间越短。

大量加工时，可日落填满冶炼炉并睡觉。烹饪台及石砌烤炉中的生食自然会烧焦。

梦境

床上睡觉可能做梦，有24普通梦及5首领梦，后者仅针对下一首领。

先随机选可用梦，再按该梦概率判定。通过则显示，否则不做梦。全部解锁且排除首领梦时，平均38.33%概率做梦。

Sleeping requires a Bed, Dragon Bed or Ashwood Bed and can only be done at night, which will progress the Day and Night Cycle to the start of the next day (morning).

Requirements

A player can sleep in a bed after claiming it. It is not possible to claim a bed that is already claimed by another player, unless that player claims another bed.

It is only possible to sleep if:

  1. It is afternoon or night.
  2. There are no enemies nearby.
  3. The bed has a roof and 80% cover.
  4. There is a fire nearby.
  5. The player is not wet.

Effect

Sleeping causes time to fast-forward, meaning that crafting stations (e.g. Smelters) will continue working until they run out of resources. Going to sleep late causes less time to be skipped.

It is therefore practical to load Smelters up at sunset when processing large amounts of material and go to sleep at sunset. Naturally, raw food on Cooking Stations and in Stone Ovens will be burned.

Dreams

Dreams may occur while the player sleeps in a bed. Available dreams include 24 standard dreams and 5 boss dreams (which only occur for the next boss).

The sleeping first selects a random available dream and then checks the random chance of that particular dream; if this random roll passes, the dream is shown, otherwise no dream occurs. With all dreams unlocked and excluding boss dreams, a player will have a 38.33% chance to see a dream on average.
