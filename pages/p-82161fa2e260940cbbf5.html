银 防护 神像是在潜能熔炉升级物品所用的神像。

获取方式

在以下兴趣点战利品箱中，与银 战斗 神像一起出现：

  • 幽暗沼泽：地下圣堂、沼泽墓地、沼泽符文石塔。
  • 凛冽雪山：废弃小屋、山地塔楼、哀啸之穴。

用途

每次在潜能熔炉尝试消耗一座神像，不需其他材料。65%概率使品质升一级，可超过4级；失败则摧毁物品，返还部分材料。

The Silver Protection Idol is an idol used to upgrade items at the Forge of Potential.

Contents

  • 1 Availability
  • 2 Usage
    • 2.1 Armor
    • 2.2 Shields
  • 3 See also

Availability

It is found in loot chests at these points of interest, together with the Silver Battle Idol:

  • Swamp: Sunken Crypt, Swamp Grave, Swamp Runestone Tower.
  • Mountain: Abandoned Cabin, Mountain Tower, Howling Cavern.

Usage

Each attempt at the Forge of Potential consumes one idol and costs no other materials. It has a 65% chance to raise the item's quality by one level, even above level 4; otherwise the item is destroyed and part of its materials are returned.

The Silver Protection Idol upgrades the following 9 items:

Armor

  •  Drake Helmet
  •  Wolf Hide Chestpiece
  •  Wolf Hide Trousers
  •  Fenris Hood
  •  Fenris Coat
  •  Fenris Leggings
  •  Wolf Fur Cape

Shields

  •  Silver Shield
  •  Serpent Scale Shield
