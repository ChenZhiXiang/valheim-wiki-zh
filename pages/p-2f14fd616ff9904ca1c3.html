封印之塔

北境平原是开阔的生物群系，丘陵起伏，树木稀疏。岩体多样，既有巨大可破坏石柱等天然岩体，也有当地丑地精建造的结构。用鹿皮和白骨碎片建成、有人居住的地精村落散布各处。

虽然起初看似宁静，平原行进困难且危险。独有资源可用于制作黑金属和亚麻线武器，以及大麦面粉和平原牦牛肉食物。焦油坑及周围还有焦油，可用来制作高级家具、暗木建筑和装饰。

驶近平原时要小心，杀人蜂可能在海滩生成，并追到远处水面。

The Plains are a wide open biome with rolling hills and sparse trees. They are home to many different kinds of rock formations, some appearing to be natural (such as enormous destructible stone monoliths), and some constructed by the native Fulings. Populated Fuling Villages can be seen spread across the Plains, built from Deer Hide and Bone Fragments.

Despite their initially tranquil appearance, the Plains biome is difficult and dangerous to navigate. Resources uniquely found in the Plains enable the player to make weapons out of Black Metal and Linen Thread, and food out of Barley Flour and Lox Meat. Finally, the Plains contain Tar in and around Tar Pits, which allows the player to construct high-tier furniture and darkwood buildings or decorations.

Be careful when sailing near the Plains as Deathsquitoes can spawn on the beach and aggro far into the water.
