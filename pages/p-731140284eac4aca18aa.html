木材是常见材料，用于通过工具锤建造建筑，或在工作台及熔炉制作武器和工具。

不要与其他木材 类型混淆。

获取方式

玩家可通过以下方式获取：

  • 拾取地面散落树枝。
  • 砍伐包括浆果灌木在内的灌木，获得少量木材。
  • 砍伐任意树／树墩。
  • 收集灰矮人掉落物，其中包括少量木材。
  • 拆除自然生成木建筑及箱子。

Wood is a common material. It is used to build structures with the Hammer, or craft weapons and tools from the Workbench and the Forge.

Not to be confused with other wood types.

  • 2 Usage
    • 2.1 Building

Availability

Players can obtain this item by:

  • Scavenging branches scattered on the ground.
  • Chopping bushes, including berry bushes, to yield a small amount of wood.
  • Cutting down any tree/stump.
  • Gathering Greydwarf drops, which includes a bit of wood.
  • Deconstructing naturally-generated wooden buildings and chests.

Usage

Building

  • Wood can be used to place various wooden structures using a Hammer's build menu.
