焰金属防护神像是在潜能熔炉升级物品所用的神像。

获取方式

在以下兴趣点战利品箱中，与焰金属战斗神像一起出现：

  • 迷雾之地：矮人守卫塔、岩石尖塔、腐疫矿区。
  • 灰烬之地：灰烬城堡、腐朽之穴。
  • 北方深境：木材营、废弃村落、莫克哈拉、维京墓地 (北方深境)、沉船 (北方深境)、蜿蜒隧道。

用途

每次在潜能熔炉尝试消耗一座神像，不需其他材料。65%概率使品质升一级，可超过4级；失败则摧毁物品，返还部分材料。

The Flametal Protection Idol is an idol used to upgrade items at the Forge of Potential.

Contents

  • 1 Availability
  • 2 Usage
    • 2.1 Armor
    • 2.2 Shields
  • 3 See also

Availability

It is found in loot chests at these points of interest, together with the Flametal Battle Idol:

  • Mistlands: Dvergr Guard Tower, Rock Spire, Infested Mine.
  • Ashlands: Charred Fortress, Putrid Hole.
  • Deep North: Lumber Camp, Abandoned Village, Mörkhalla, Viking Graveyard (Deep North), Shipwreck (Deep North), Winding Tunnels.

Usage

Each attempt at the Forge of Potential consumes one idol and costs no other materials. It has a 65% chance to raise the item's quality by one level, even above level 4; otherwise the item is destroyed and part of its materials are returned.

The Flametal Protection Idol upgrades the following 13 items:

Armor

  •  Flametal Helmet
  •  Flametal Breastplate
  •  Flametal Greaves
  •  Hood of Ask
  •  Breastplate of Ask
  •  Trousers of Ask
  •  Hood of Embla
  •  Robes of Embla
  •  Trousers of Embla
  •  Ashen Cape
  •  Asksvin Cloak

Shields

  •  Flametal Shield
  •  Flametal Tower Shield
