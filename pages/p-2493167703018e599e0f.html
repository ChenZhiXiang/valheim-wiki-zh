钩绳是用于移动穿行的迷雾之地级工具。

目录

  • 1 获取方式
  • 2 配方
  • 3 用途
  • 4 说明

获取方式

钩爪全部材料都可在迷雾之地获得。尤其钩子可在腐疫矿区、废弃矮人守卫塔及岩石尖塔顶部找到。

无法升级。

配方

钩爪在1级黑熔炉用以下材料制作：

  •  10 世界之树木
  •  10 埃达精华
  •  4 下颌骨
  •  1 钩子

用途

主要攻击：

  1. 向最远 40 米处发射钩爪并附着在任意物体上。消耗 15 耐力，并对目标造成 10 戳刺。
  2. 自动收回，将玩家高速拉向目标。
  3. 钩爪会持续附着在目标上。
    • 按格挡键（鼠标右键）即可使其脱离。
    • 钩爪仍附着时，玩家会缓慢下落，从而减轻坠落伤害。
    • 双脚离地时按跳跃键即可主动收回钩爪（从地面起跳时需连按两次）。
  4. 钩爪未命中或松开后会重新充能。

次要攻击与主要攻击类似，区别如下：

  • 不会自动收回。
  • 未命中或松开后不会重新充能。
  • 钩爪仍附着时也可再次发射。

说明

  • 它特别适合穿越迷雾之地崎岖的地形。
  • 可使用次要攻击连续发射钩爪，攀上没有落脚点的地形。
  • 站在搁浅的船上或拉动卡住的推车时，可将主要攻击当作绞盘使用。
  • 与附着点距离过远时，钩爪会自动脱离。
  • 应小心避免致命的坠落伤害，可穿戴轻羽斗篷来防止这类伤害。

The Grappling Hook is a Mistlands-tier tool used for traversal.

Contents

  • 1 Availability
  • 2 Recipe
  • 3 Usage
  • 4 Note

Availability

All ingredients for a Grappling Hook can be obtained in the Mistlands. Particularly, the  Hook can be found in Infested Mines, ruined Dvergr Guard Towers and on the top of Rock Spires.

It cannot be upgraded.

Recipe

The Grappling Hook is crafted at a level 1 Black Forge with:

  •  10 Yggdrasil Wood
  •  10 Refined Eitr
  •  4 Mandible
  •  1 Hook

Usage

The primary attack:

  1. Fire a hook up to 40 meters and attach itself to any object. It costs 15 Stamina and deals 10 Pierce to the target.
  2. Retract automatically, bringing the player with it at high speed.
  3. The hook stays attached to the target.
    • It can be released with pressing block (right mouse).
    • While the hook is still attached the player falls slowly, mitigating fall damage.
    • The hook can be retracted at will by pressing jump while feet are not on the ground (double jump from the ground).
  4. The grappling hook recharges after missing a hit or being released.

The secondary attack is similar to the primary, except:

  • It doesn't retract automatically.
  • It doesn't recharge if a hit miss or when released.
  • It can be shot while the hook is still engaged.

Note

  • It is particularly useful for traversing the craggy Mistlands terrain.
  • The secondary attack to chain shots together up terrain without footholds.
  • The primary attack may be used as a winch while standing in a stuck boat or pulling a stuck cart.
  • It will release automatically when too far away from where its attached
  • Caution should be taken to avoid lethal fall damage, a  Feather Cape can be worn to prevent it.
